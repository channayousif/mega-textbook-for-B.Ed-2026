# Superseded departmental course guides - provenance only

**Do not treat anything in this folder as authoritative.** Decision `D-2026-0003`
(curriculum owner, 2026-09-20) settles this.

These guides were issued by the **Department of Early Childhood and Elementary Education, Elsa
Qazi Campus, Hyderabad**. They are a departmental variant of the Faculty of Education guides in
`Scheme-and-Course-guides/`, and they are retained here for provenance only.

## Why this file exists

The first intake evaluation (Constitution Art. VII.8) found that two documents in this repository
both present as the EFMP-302 course guide and disagree with each other:

| | Faculty guide (`Scheme-and-Course-guides/1st 2026.pdf`) | This folder |
|---|---|---|
| Credit hours | `3 (3-0)` | `03 (0-3)` |
| `Major:` line | present | absent |
| CLO 1 / CLO 2 order | one order | reversed |
| Unit outlines, reading lists | identical | identical |

EFMP-301 is in the same position, and its Unit 1 is certified and published. Where a file exists
here it states `(0-3)`; the Faculty guides and the revised board Scheme state `(3-0)`.

Nothing had noticed for seven G3 review cycles, because the folder carried no README, no extracted
text and no provenance note.

## What governs instead

1. **`Scheme-and-Course-guides/B.Ed 4 Year 2026 revised after board.docx`** - the revised board
   Scheme of Study, designated final authority by the curriculum owner on 2026-09-10. Credit
   hours are a property of the degree-awarding scheme (`specs/gaps.md` G-2026-05).
2. **The Faculty of Education course guides** in `Scheme-and-Course-guides/` - unit outlines,
   course outcomes and CLO numbering.

A conflict between those two is an Article II.3 escalation and goes to `specs/gaps.md`. A conflict
between either of them and a file in *this* folder is already decided: this folder does not govern.

## Files here

Eight documents, covering EFMP-301, EFMP-302, GQUR-300, GICT-300, GENG-300 (twice) and GNAS-401,
plus a copy of the superseded board scheme. They remain bound as review inputs so that a reviewer
can see what an author may have consulted, not because they carry authority.
