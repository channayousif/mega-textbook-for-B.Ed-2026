# About B.Ed Mega Textbook

This project provides a comprehensive, public, bilingual (English and Urdu) digital textbook for the **B.Ed (4-Year) programme** at the **University of Sindh, Faculty of Education**. 

## Our Mission

Our goal is to make quality teacher education resources accessible to trainee and practising teachers across Pakistan, especially those on mobile networks. We ensure our content is lightweight, fast, and available in both English and Urdu (RTL-correct).

## Scheme of Studies

Our curriculum aligns closely with the University of Sindh scheme of studies. The textbook covers all 8 semesters, organized by course and unit. While some semesters are still being authored, the entire structure is scaffolded to provide a clear roadmap for your B.Ed degree.

## The Team

The project is driven by a dedicated team of curriculum experts, content authors, and engineers who believe in open, high-quality educational materials.
