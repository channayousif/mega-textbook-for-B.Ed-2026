# About Mega Textbook

Mega Textbook is a public, bilingual (English and Urdu) collection of digital textbooks and a learning platform for **Grades 9-12** and **teacher-education degree programmes (ADE, B.Ed Hons)** in Pakistan. It is built for students and their teachers.

## Our Vision

The project is envisioned as a free, open textbooks-and-LMS platform serving two audiences:

- **School students and their teachers** — Grades 9-12 subjects, bilingual and mobile-friendly.
- **Prospective teachers and their instructors** — ADE and B.Ed (Hons) programmes, organised semester-by-semester with a teaching-licence track.

The current release is an MVP aimed at prospective teachers (ADE/B.Ed students) and their teachers. The Grade 9-12 subject tracks are next.

## Our Mission

To make quality educational resources accessible across Pakistan, especially on mobile networks. Content is lightweight, fast, and available in both English and Urdu (RTL-correct).

## The Team

The project is driven by a dedicated team of curriculum experts, content authors, and engineers who believe in open, high-quality educational materials.
