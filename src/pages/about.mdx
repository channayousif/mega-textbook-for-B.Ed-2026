# About Mega Textbook

Mega Textbook is a public, bilingual (English and Urdu) collection of digital textbooks and a learning platform. It is built for ADE and B.Ed(H) students and their teachers, **STEDA teacher-licence aspirants** under the Government of Sindh, and — next — Grades 9-12 students and their teachers.

## Our Vision

The project is envisioned as a free, open textbooks-and-LMS platform serving three audiences:

- **Prospective teachers (ADE / B.Ed Hons students) and their instructors** — organised semester-by-semester.
- **STEDA teacher-licence aspirants** — the full teaching-licence syllabus, free to read, in English and Urdu.
- **School students and their teachers** — Grades 9-12 subjects (next expansion).

The current release is an MVP for audiences 1 and 2. The Grade 9-12 subject tracks are the planned next step.

## Our Mission

To make quality educational resources accessible across Pakistan, especially on mobile networks. Content is lightweight, fast, and available in both English and Urdu (RTL-correct).

## The Team

The project is driven by a dedicated team of curriculum experts, content authors, and engineers who believe in open, high-quality educational materials.
