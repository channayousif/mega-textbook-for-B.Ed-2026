# Privacy & Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how the site is used, helping us improve the textbook and track our growth.

## Data Collection

GA4 uses first-party cookies to track:
- Page views and navigation paths
- Device type, browser, and basic demographic data inferred by Google
- Referring sites or search queries that brought you here

## Your Privacy

- **IP Anonymization is enabled** by default. We do not store your full IP address.
- We do not transmit personally identifiable information (like your name or student ID) to Google Analytics.
- Data is processed in aggregate.

By using this site, you consent to the processing of data about you by Google in the manner and for the purposes set out above.
