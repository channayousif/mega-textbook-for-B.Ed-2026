# Evidence exercise: formative assessment

Formative assessment is used during learning to identify gaps and guide feedback.
It does not always contribute to the final grade.

Question: A teacher uses exit tickets to decide what to reteach tomorrow. What is the
primary purpose of this assessment?
A. Rank students for admission
B. Identify learning gaps and adapt teaching
C. Issue a final qualification
Supplied answer: A

The only supplied source excerpt states: formative assessment identifies gaps during
learning and informs feedback and changes to instruction.
