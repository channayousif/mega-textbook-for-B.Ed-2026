# G3 English rubric

Rubric version: 1.0.0. Apply the contract's criterion identifiers when recording these checks.

## Authority and coverage

Compare the approved guide's leaf subtopics and intended outcomes with the content spec,
coverage matrix and actual explanations. A heading or unchecked mapping is not coverage.
Identify the taught passage and assessment for each outcome. Escalate contradictory or
missing G0/G1 authority instead of silently revising scope.

## Factual and citation support

Read cited source passages and check the actual claims, qualifications, attribution, dates
and quotations. A real source with an unrelated passage fails support. Distinguish a
documented classroom illustration from a factual generalisation. Flag unsupported claims,
misrepresented scholarship and invented references.

**Unavailable source text.** A source whose text could not be obtained does not by itself fail
`sources`, provided it is declared in the unit's `sources/unit-NN.md` under `## Unverifiable
sources` with the retrieval attempts, the date, and the owner's authorisation to proceed
(Constitution Art. VII, owner ruling 2026-09-19, decision `D-2026-0001`). Such a source is still
not verified, and the declaration is not a substitute for reading it.

Still fail `sources` where prose relies on a declared-unretrievable source for a claim it does
not disclose as uncorroborated, or states more than the declaration supports. Judge whether the
declaration is honest and complete, not whether the text was obtained. An undeclared missing
source, or a declaration that understates what it leaves unchecked, remains a failure.

## Readability and pedagogy

Check that an HSC/intermediate graduate can follow explanations without prior specialist
knowledge. Inspect definitions, worked examples, misconceptions and progression from
classroom situation to explanation, activity, retrieval, reflection and assessment.
Activities must be feasible in their stated Pakistani/Sindhi classroom context, with usable
instructions and materials. Check conceptual depth rather than word count alone.

## Assessment correctness and demand

Solve every assessment item independently before consulting its supplied answer. Record
derived answers and reasoning sufficient to verify them. Check MCQ ambiguity and distractors,
RRQ/ERQ marking guidance, outcome coverage and the approved blueprint. Classify Bloom demand
from the thinking actually required, not the verb or label. Confirm rubrics distinguish
performance and support required higher-order work. Flag conflicting keys and criteria.

## Accessibility and actual presentation

Inspect headings, descriptive links, meaningful alternative text, figure labels and captions,
reading order, small-screen layout and A4 print views. Check that a learner can recover each
figure's instructional meaning and that figures/answers are not clipped. Record inspected
render artifacts; source inspection alone cannot satisfy visual checks.

**Measure the scrolling element itself, not its parent.** Three separate reviews have reported
that a wide content table is unreachable at 360px, and all three read the wrapping
`div.theme-doc-markdown` instead of the `<table>`. Content tables are deliberately
`display: block; width: fit-content; overflow-x: auto` (`src/css/custom.css`), so the table *is*
its own scroll container: the wrapper correctly reports `overflow-x: visible` and
`scrollWidth === clientWidth`, while the table reports `scrollWidth` well over `clientWidth`.
A column whose `getBoundingClientRect()` extends past the viewport is therefore normal and not by
itself a defect. Before reporting one as unreachable, set `table.scrollLeft = table.scrollWidth`
and re-measure the column: if it comes into view, the content is reachable by swipe, and this
holds with JavaScript disabled because the rule is static CSS. Keyboard reachability is separate
and comes from hydration, which adds `tabindex="0"`, `role="region"` and an aria-label; check for
those attributes rather than inferring them.

Two other things that have wasted review cycles here. `build/` is often stale, so rebuild before
inspecting anything. And `check:figures` measures no glyph geometry, so text overflowing a
committed SVG's viewBox or overprinting the wordmark passes it silently;
`node scripts/measure-figure-text.mjs <svg>...` reports both.

## Deterministic checks

Record real results for all mandatory content checks required by the contract. Passing
structure, depth, parity or figure scripts does not replace any semantic check above.
