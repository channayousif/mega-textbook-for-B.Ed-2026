# G5 Urdu rubric

Rubric version: 1.0.0. Apply the contract's criterion identifiers when recording these checks.

## English dependency and applicability

Require accepted G3 evidence for the exact English inputs bound to this review. A changed
English digest invalidates the dependency even if an old tracker row says done. For an
explicitly English-only course, report G5 as inapplicable and escalate through the contract;
never invent an Urdu pass.

For shared `authority`, `coverage` and `sources` criteria, verify the same approved guide,
unit outcomes and supporting source passages remain applicable to the Urdu claims. Check
that every required outcome is taught and assessed in Urdu and that citations, quotations
and qualifications retain their supporting meaning. Accepted English review does not
automatically pass these Urdu checks.

## Complete bilingual comparison

Align and compare actual English and Urdu passages across the index, every topic,
assessment, teacher notes, captions and figure labels. Find omissions and additions, including
heading-only stubs. Heading parity and fluent back-translation are insufficient.

Inspect negation, modal force, quantities, percentages, dates, comparisons, causal claims,
examples, pronoun references and instructional sequences. Preserve epistemic uncertainty:
"may" must not become "always". Record paired passage locators and explain each material
semantic divergence. Escalate meanings that cannot be resolved confidently.

## Register and terminology

Use the frozen terminology bank. Check academic-plain Urdu that an entering B.Ed student
can understand, natural sentence order and consistent treatment of English technical terms.
Flag terminology drift and misleading literal calques. Propose disputed or new terms as
findings for the owner; do not change the terminology bank to secure a pass.

## Assessment equivalence

Independently answer the Urdu items, then compare their meaning, answers, options, scoring
and cognitive demand with accepted English items. Check option ordering and answer-key
correspondence, preserved ambiguity/precision and whether translation inadvertently reveals
an answer or lowers an analysis task to recall. Record any changed task or scoring meaning.

## Urdu figures, RTL and print

Inspect actual rendered Urdu in normal and narrow viewports and A4 print views. Check Nastaliq
legibility, shaping, bidi punctuation, numerals, embedded Latin expressions, table order,
wrapping, clipping and reading sequence. Inspect the actual Urdu figure variants and labels;
correct MDX paths cannot prove a usable diagram. Pair screenshots with the reviewed input
identity and inspection notes. Missing render access requires escalation.

## Deterministic checks

Record actual mandatory content, parity and figure check results. Structural success alone
cannot establish completeness, faithful meaning, assessment equivalence or readable RTL.
