## Prompt

build the puppet first

## Response snapshot

Built the puppet from the full-figure layers: a rig builder finds the eye and mouth regions,
crops feathered overlays and records hashes in rig.json; Puppet.tsx cross-fades the line's gesture
over the idle pose, blinks on a seeded 3 to 5 s schedule, breathes, and shows the mouth shape for
each frame. Mouth tracks are computed from each line's audio when props are built (loudness for
openness, syllable peaks for wide open, zero-crossing rate for O versus E). Ustani falls back to
rest/open until her M, E and O mouths arrive. kaggle:pack stages the rigs and the set; production
props refuse to build without rigs. Verified with stills and a 30 s local preview.
