## Response snapshot

Re-ran after `/sp.plan` supplied the missing `plan.md`. Prerequisites resolved this time, since
the branch now follows the `NNN-feature-name` convention.

**Clustered to one ADR, not five.** The tier abstraction, the third docs-plugin instance, the
catalogue key, the Article V.4 gate extension and the navigation choice all serve one thing and
would all revert together if the licence tier were abandoned. Splitting them would be the
over-granular failure the command's flywheel check names.

**Two relationships to existing ADRs**, neither a conflict:

- **ADR-0002** carries a *Navigation* bullet, "fully autogenerated Semester -> Course -> Unit
  sidebar from the folder structure". That is **partially superseded**: the shape still governs
  the degree corpus, but it is no longer the only content hierarchy. Recorded in the same partial
  form ADR-0002 already uses for its Hosting bullet, superseded by ADR-0008.
- **ADR-0004** is **extended**: its validator walks tiers rather than `docs/**` directly, and its
  data-driven catalogue gains `tracks[]`. The contract-and-gate posture is unchanged.
- **ADR-0009** is the precedent followed, not modified.

**Five alternatives recorded with rejection rationale**: pseudo-semester, folding into a degree
course, a separate Docusaurus site, a flat catalogue with a `tier` field, and a generic `/exam/`
route. Two were live options the owner or the research actually rejected rather than
strawmen - folding in was the owner's 2026-09-13 call, and the flat catalogue is explicitly
flagged as worth revisiting once the walker settles.

Negative consequences are stated honestly rather than minimised: every content gate is touched at
once, the additive catalogue key admits a missed-consumer bug that `allCourses()` reduces but does
not remove, two hierarchies are more surface than one, and `/licence/` is a Sindh-specific name.

Status left **Proposed**, since feature 015's spec is still open at PR #46 and unapproved.
