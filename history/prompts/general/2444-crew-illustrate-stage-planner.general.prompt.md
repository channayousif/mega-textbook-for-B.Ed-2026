## Response snapshot

The `illustrate` stage was wired (worker chain agy then codex, a brief) but had never run: 0 jobs on the board. There were four causes. (A) `stagesFor` never returned `illustrate`. (B) Prompt-only counts ignored Kind, so an illustration queued no-op `figures` jobs. (C) The banner sidecar `unit-NN.banner.md` was invisible. (D) The skills still said "Codex only, never invoke a generator" (ADR-0024 wording), so authors stopped writing illustration rows; only 2 exist. Quota was not the cause.

Fix: the planner reads each unit's manifest plus its banner sidecar in crew code. `figure-manifest.mjs` and `report-content-status.mjs` are bound G2 inputs and stay untouched. Schematic prompt-only rows queue `figures`. Illustration rows at prompt-only or generated queue `illustrate`, with at most one in flight. The illustrate brief now names the script, `--banner`, inspection, regeneration via `--id`, EN and UR placement, and gates. generate-figures and author-unit now describe the ADR-0029 flow, including one banner per unit.

The smoke test proved agy 1.4.12 still generates headless. The resulting banner is correct in setting and dress but shows five teachers where the alt text says four, so it stays at `generated` for the illustrate job to reject and regenerate. The banner moved to the gates PR, because its manifest is a bound G2 input.
