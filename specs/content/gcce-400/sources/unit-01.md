## Unverifiable sources

- glencoe-civics-today: print only and edition not named by the guide; only the Open Library record and title were checked. Used for U1-01 for the general idea that civics joins citizenship and government; no page or wording is claimed.
- kymlicka-norman-2000: print only; the editors' introduction could not be read on this host. Used for U1-04 and U1-08 for the distinction between citizenship as legal status and as activity, and the question of shared citizenship in diverse societies; the attribution of the status and activity distinction to this volume should be confirmed by a reviewer with the book.
- mattson-2017: print only; only the Open Library record and title were checked. Used for U1-07 for the book's stated aim of preparing pupils to engage in online communities, as given in its subtitle.

## Notes

- The Council of Europe *Digital citizenship education handbook* (`coe-2019-dce`, content-spec reading list) was not reachable from this host on 2026-11-22 and is not cited; the definition of digital citizenship uses the existing glossary entry.
- Three guide readings (Pike and Selby; Feldpausch and Omilian; Clarke and Steckel) are unbound in the approved content-spec and are not used.
