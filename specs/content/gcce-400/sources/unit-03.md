## Notes

- Constitutional currency: the unit cites the 2012 National Assembly edition, read in the accessible transcription with amendment notes (2026-11-22, re-checked 2026-11-23). Later amendments do change provisions the unit relies on, and the unit follows the current text: the Twenty-sixth Amendment Act, 2024 inserted Article 9A (clean and healthy environment), so Chapter 1 of Part II now has twenty-five articles (8 to 28); the Eighteenth Amendment Act, 2010 omitted "alone" from Article 25(2); the Twenty-seventh Amendment Act, 2025 moved the Article 17(2) reference on proscribed parties to the Federal Constitutional Court, omitted Article 184, and inserted Article 175E, whose clause (3) gives the Federal Constitutional Court the power to make orders on questions of public importance about enforcing fundamental rights, exercised only on an application filed by a person in that Court (checked 2026-11-28 in the Act linked above). Articles 5, 9, 10A, 14, 16, 18, 19, 19A, 23, 24, 25A, 26 and 199(1) as summarised in the unit are unchanged in substance.
