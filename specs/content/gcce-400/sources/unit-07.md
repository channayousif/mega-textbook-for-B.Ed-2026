## Notes

- The census figures quoted in the unit are taken from the National Census Report 2023 and from Table 11 of the detailed results. The report gives a national male-female literacy gap of 16.16 percentage points in its narrative (Section 3.9) while its own summary table shows male literacy 68.00 percent and female 52.84 percent for those aged ten and above. The unit quotes the table values, not the narrative gap.
- The census employment figures in Section 4.7 are described by the report as a snapshot and not directly comparable with the Labour Force Survey. The unit uses them only as an indicator of who works, not as an unemployment rate.
- Three guide readings (Pike and Selby; Feldpausch and Omilian; Clarke and Steckel) are unbound in the approved content-spec and are not used.
