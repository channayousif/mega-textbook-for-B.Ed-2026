## Evidence notes

- Page 14 defines global citizenship: "a sense of belonging to a broader community and common humanity". Topic 1.2 quotes this phrase (under 15 words) with the page.
- Page 15 sets out three core conceptual dimensions, or domains of learning: cognitive, socio-emotional and behavioural. Topic 1.1 uses the behavioural domain (acting effectively and responsibly) to frame civic engagement as action, not only knowledge.
- Pages 23-24 name three learner attributes: informed and critically literate; socially connected and respectful of diversity; ethically responsible and engaged. Topic 1.3 uses these to describe the active citizen.

Support limits: the document concerns global citizenship education. The unit uses it for the general idea that citizenship learning includes action and engagement, and for the wider sense of belonging. It does not define Pakistani citizenship law, active citizenship or participatory citizenship as distinct terms, and the unit does not claim it does.
