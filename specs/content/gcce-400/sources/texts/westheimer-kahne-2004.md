## Evidence notes

Page 243 sets out three kinds of citizen with a food drive example for each:

- Personally responsible citizen: acts responsibly in the community, for example picking up litter, giving blood, volunteering; in the example, "Contributes food to a food drive".
- Participatory citizen: an "active member of community organizations and/or improvement efforts" who organises community efforts; in the example, "Helps to organize a food drive".
- Justice-oriented citizen: critically assesses social, political and economic structures and addresses injustice; in the example, "Explores why people are hungry and acts to solve root causes".

Use in GCCE-400 Unit 1: Topic 1.3 uses the personally responsible and participatory kinds to separate active citizenship in general from its organised, participatory form, and mentions the justice-oriented kind briefly. The unit's three classification questions are the author's teaching device built on this distinction, not the article's own procedure.
