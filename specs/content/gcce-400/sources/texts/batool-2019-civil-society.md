## Evidence notes (abstract; short quotations under 15 words)

- In the democratic arena, civil society "compels the undemocratic elements to follow democratic norms" and keeps a vigilant watch on the activities of the government.
- In Pakistan, civil society is considered an umbrella phrase for a range of "non-state and non-market citizen organisations and initiatives", networks and unions.
- Civil society in Pakistan "has been flawed by the structural dynamics of state consolidation" from the beginning; in recent years civil society organisations have proven stronger and dedicated but are still at a developing stage.
