## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

- The case school is a girls' school of the Aga Khan Education Service in a rural town in the Northern Areas of Pakistan.
- The section on parent and community involvement lists four formal mechanisms: a School Management Committee (SMC), a Parent-Teacher Association, a Mothers' Mobilization Committee, and the Women's Organization of the Aga Khan Rural Support Program.
- The SMC is the most important. It has 15 members, mostly parents of pupils, selected by the community for their role in community leadership; teachers are not eligible. It meets monthly; the principal is secretary.
- In the principal's account, the SMC maintains the buildings, supports good management, helps raise funds and appoints community-based teachers, two of whom it pays.
- Women members contact and visit parents when a child is absent, irregular or late, to find the reason and help solve the problem. The SMC also checks teachers' punctuality and absenteeism and looks into large discrepancies in class test results.

Use in GCCE-400 Unit 4: Topic 4.2 (the school as a community institution) and RRQ 10. The unit presents this as one school's experience, not as a description of all Pakistani schools.
