# Bound source evidence: pbs-census-2023

Source: https://www.pbs.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/National-Census-Report-2023.pdf (Table 11: https://www.pbs.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/census_tables/tables/table_11_national.pdf)

Consulted: 2026-11-22. National Census Report 2023 PDF retrieved and read as text; Table 11 PDF retrieved and read. Both URLs returned HTTP 200.

Locator: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics. (2023). *7th Population and Housing Census 2023: National Census Report*. Government of Pakistan, Ministry of Planning, Development and Special Initiatives. The report describes the census as the first digital census, conducted March to May 2023, and gives the total population as 241,499,431, with detailed characteristics available for 240,458,089 people.

## Evidence notes

### Population by administrative unit (U7-01)

Table 4.2 and Section 4.1.2: Punjab 52.87 percent, Sindh 23.06 percent, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa 16.92 percent, Balochistan 6.17 percent, Islamabad Capital Territory 0.98 percent.

### Mother tongue (U7-01)

Table 11 (all localities, all sexes, population 240,458,089): Punjabi 88,915,544 (about 36.98 percent); Pashto 43,633,946 (about 18.15 percent); Sindhi 34,401,564 (about 14.31 percent); Saraiki 28,849,579 (about 12.00 percent); Urdu 22,249,307 (about 9.25 percent); Balochi 8,117,795 (about 3.38 percent); Hindko 5,590,559 (about 2.32 percent); Brahvi 2,778,670 (about 1.16 percent). The shares are the author's arithmetic on the census counts, rounded to two decimals. The unit gives rounded shares of about 37, 18, 14, 12, 9 and 3 percent.

### Religion (U7-01)

Table 4.13 and the executive summary: Muslim 96.35 percent, Christian 1.37 percent, Hindu 1.61 percent, Qadiani/Ahmadi 0.07 percent, Scheduled Castes 0.56 percent, others 0.04 percent. The unit says "about 96 percent" Muslim.

### Age structure (U7-02)

Section 4.2.2: the 15 to 24 age group is 18.56 percent of the population, the 15 to 29 group 26 percent, and about 66.6 percent of the population is below 29. The population under 15 is 40.56 percent.

### Literacy (U7-02, U7-03)

Summary table and Section 3.9: literacy for those aged ten and above in 2023 was 60.65 percent overall, 68.00 percent male and 52.84 percent female. Table 4.21: Islamabad Capital Territory 83.97 percent, Punjab 66.25 percent, Sindh 57.54 percent, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa 51.09 percent, Balochistan 42.01 percent; rural Sindh 38.14 percent, rural Balochistan 35.74 percent. Section 3.9 states provincial male-female gaps of 27.42 percentage points in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, 17.70 in Balochistan, 14.02 in Sindh, 11.79 in Punjab and 9.10 in Islamabad Capital Territory, and a national gap of 16.16 percentage points.

### Out of school children (U7-02, U7-03)

Section 4.6.2 and Table 4.22: 35.60 percent of children aged five to sixteen were out of school in 2023; 32.44 percent of boys and 38.99 percent of girls.

### Gender parity index (U7-02, U7-03)

Section 4.6.3: the gender parity index in primary education is the ratio of female to male students, and 1 indicates parity. The report gives an average literacy gender parity index of 77.71 percent across 136 districts, with 27 districts above and 109 below.

### Employment (U7-03)

Section 4.7 and Table 4.25: 66.22 million people, or 38.57 percent of the population, were employed during the reference year; 59.68 percent of males and 16.10 percent of females. The report warns that these figures are a snapshot and are not directly comparable with the Labour Force Survey.

Support limits: the report is a national summary. District-level detail, where the unit mentions it, is used only as reported. The unit does not compute any figure not stated in the report except the mother-tongue shares, which are stated here as the author's arithmetic on Table 11 counts.
