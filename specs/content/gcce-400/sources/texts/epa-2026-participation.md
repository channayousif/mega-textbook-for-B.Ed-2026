## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

- The page explains that the International Association for Public Participation (IAP2) designed its Spectrum of Public Participation to help agencies set clear expectations for public participation.
- Inform: give the public the information they need to understand the decision; it offers no real chance to influence it, and must be honest, not persuasion or public relations.
- Consult: the basic minimum; the agency asks for opinions at set points and considers them as it decides.
- Involve: the public is invited in, usually from the beginning, with many chances for input, but the agency still decides.
- Collaborate: includes involve; the public is directly engaged in decision-making, often seeking consensus solutions, though the agency remains the final decision-maker.
- Empower: the public makes the decision, most commonly by voting.

Use in GCCE-400 Unit 4: Topic 4.2 uses the five levels, the warning that informing is not persuading, and the point that the agency keeps the decision at involve and collaborate. The water-tank examples are the author's.
