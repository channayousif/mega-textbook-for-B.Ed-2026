## Evidence notes

The study uses PSLM-HIES survey data (2018-2019) and a logit model to examine ICT use capabilities of individuals in Pakistan. The abstract reports that socioeconomic, demographic, regional and supply-side factors explain variation in ICT use, that "the leading determinants of the digital divide are ICT infrastructure" and access to focal technology, and that the authors "found a sizeable digital divide among the individuals of Pakistan".

Use in GCCE-400 Unit 1: Topic 1.3 and MCQ 10 use only these abstract-level findings (a sizeable divide; infrastructure and access as leading determinants). The unit does not report the paper's coefficients or rankings.
