Consulted: 2026-11-22.

## Evidence notes (from the FAOLEX abstract, paraphrased unless quoted)

- The Act has 161 sections in 17 chapters and 8 schedules, and "establishes an elected local government system" to devolve political, administrative and financial responsibility and authority to elected local representatives.
- It aims at good governance, effective delivery of services and transparent decision making through the institutionalised participation of people at local level.
- The provincial government categorises a district into urban and rural areas, and declares an urban area a Metropolitan Corporation, District Municipal Corporation, Municipal Corporation, Municipal Committee or Town Committee (the abstract is truncated after "Tow...").
- A search-engine excerpt of the Act's text refers to determining "the number of Union Councils, Union Committees and Wards in Municipal Committees and Town Committees".

Use in GCCE-400 Unit 2: Topic 2.1 (U2-02) states only that the Act sets up elected local councils in Sindh, divides districts into urban and rural areas, makes urban areas corporations or committees, and provides for union councils and union committees. The example that street lighting is municipal council work is a general description of municipal services, not a quoted schedule entry; a reviewer with the full Act should confirm it against the Act's schedule of functions.
