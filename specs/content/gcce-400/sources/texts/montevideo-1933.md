# Bound source evidence: montevideo-1933

Source: https://avalon.law.yale.edu/20th_century/intam03.asp (The Avalon Project, Yale Law School: Convention on Rights and Duties of States (inter-American); December 26, 1933).

Consulted: 2026-11-22.

## Checked text

- Article 1 (verbatim): "The state as a person of international law should possess the following qualifications: a ) a permanent population; b ) a defined territory; c ) government; and d) capacity to enter into relations with the other states."
- The page records that the convention was signed at Montevideo on 26 December 1933 and entered into force on 26 December 1934.

Use in GCCE-400 Unit 2: Topic 2.1 (U2-01) paraphrases Article 1 as a widely used modern statement of what a state needs, and links the fourth qualification to the civics-book element of sovereignty. The unit does not claim that Pakistan is a party to the convention.
