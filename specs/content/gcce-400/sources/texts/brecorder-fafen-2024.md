## Evidence notes (paraphrased; figures as reported)

- About 60.6 million voters voted in Pakistan's twelfth general elections on 8 February 2024, about 5.8 million more than the 54.8 million in 2018, according to a Free and Fair Election Network (FAFEN) report.
- Turnout fell from 52.1 percent in 2018 to 47.6 percent in 2024, mainly because registered voters rose from 106 million in 2018 to 128.6 million in 2024, a record addition of 22.6 million.
