# Bound source evidence: constitution-pakistan

Source: https://na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1333523681_951.pdf (National Assembly edition as modified up to 28 February 2012); text checked in the accessible transcription with amendment notes at https://www.pakistani.org/pakistan/constitution/ (Part I, Part II Chapter 1, Part III Chapter 2).

Consulted: 2026-11-22.

## Checked provisions

- Article 4(1): to enjoy the protection of law and to be treated in accordance with law is the inalienable right of every citizen.
- Article 5(1): "Loyalty to the State is the basic duty of every citizen." Article 5(2): obedience to the Constitution and law is the inviolable obligation of every citizen.
- Article 16: every citizen has the right to assemble peacefully and without arms, subject to reasonable restrictions imposed by law in the interest of public order.
- Article 17(1): every citizen has the right to form associations or unions, subject to reasonable restrictions imposed by law.
- Article 19: freedom of speech and expression, and of the press, subject to reasonable restrictions imposed by law on listed grounds.
- Article 25(1): "All citizens are equal before law and are entitled to equal protection of law."
- Article 25A: the State shall provide free and compulsory education to all children aged five to sixteen, in such manner as may be determined by law (inserted in 2010).
- Article 51(2): a person is entitled to vote if a citizen of Pakistan, not less than eighteen years of age, on the electoral roll and not declared of unsound mind by a competent court.

Currency: the unit cites the 2012 edition for these provisions, read in the transcription with its amendment notes on 2026-11-22. Effects of the Twenty-sixth (2024) and Twenty-seventh (2025) Amendments were not checked article by article; none of the articles above is known to be among those they changed, but a reviewer should confirm before stating any of them as current law.
