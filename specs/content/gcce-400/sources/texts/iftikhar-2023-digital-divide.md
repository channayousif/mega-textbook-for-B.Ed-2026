## Evidence notes

- The study assessed the problems university students faced in online education after the Covid-19 shift, using the theoretical background of the digital divide and Heckman's treatment effect model.
- Online education further increased an already existing digital divide between those who have and those who have not. Topic 6.3 uses this to show that the divide is not closed by access alone.
- The barriers named are the scarcity of gadgets, internet access, family income, gender, field of study, age and location. Topic 6.3 uses these to match the four kinds of divide.
- The authors report that rural students faced more problems than urban students, and that connectivity and electricity disruption were barriers. Topic 6.3 uses this for the geographic divide.

Support limits: the study concerns university students during the pandemic, not school pupils. The unit uses it for the barrier list and the widening effect, not for its model or coefficients.
