## Evidence notes

- The report describes a cohesive society as one that "works towards the well-being of all its members", fights exclusion and marginalisation, creates a sense of belonging, promotes trust, and offers its members the opportunity of upward mobility.

Use in GCCE-400 Unit 4: Topic 4.2 quotes the eight-word phrase above (under 15 words) and paraphrases the rest of the definition. The claim that inclusive engagement builds cohesion, and exclusive engagement can weaken it, is the unit's own reasoning from this definition, not a finding of the report.
