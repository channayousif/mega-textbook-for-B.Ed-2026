### MCQ 2
- **Prompt:** A pupil says, "A minority group means a group with fewer people." Which answer best corrects this? (Understand)

- **Comparison:** Agree. Unambiguous conceptual distractor correction.

### MCQ 8

- **Independent solution:** In C. Wright Mills' sociological imagination framework and civic analysis, when an issue forms a pattern affecting a whole collective and breaches a legal/public duty, it constitutes a public issue/injustice rather than an individual private trouble.
