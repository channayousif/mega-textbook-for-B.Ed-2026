## Assessment

The source is declared under `## Unverifiable sources`, so the missing text does not by itself
fail `sources` (Constitution Art. VII, `D-2026-0001`). The problem is that the declaration is
not complete for the use the prose makes of it:

- The prose attributes a specific finding ("demonstrate that civic attitudes are formed through
  multiple agents of socialization") to the book.
- The declaration describes the use as "the role of youth engagement in public discourse and
  civic advocacy" and says "no exact wording or page range is claimed", which does not disclose
  that a socialisation finding is attributed.
- The declaration says the source is used for U5-02, but `topic-02.mdx` never cites Youniss and
  Levine in the prose (only in the further-reading list at `topic-02.mdx:150`).

The declaration also does not name owner ruling `D-2026-0001`, which the unit-3 and unit-4
reviews required for this class of declaration.

Repair: either tighten the prose so the four sources of socialisation are presented as the
unit's own framing (with the book as a general further reading), or expand the declaration to
name the socialisation claim and cite `D-2026-0001`, or both. This is a declaration/prose
alignment defect, not an unretrievable-source failure.
