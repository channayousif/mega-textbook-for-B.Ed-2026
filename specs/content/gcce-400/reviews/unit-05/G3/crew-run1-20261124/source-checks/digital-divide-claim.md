# Source check: the digital-divide generalisation in Topic 5.1

Reviewer: agent:claude (LongCat-2.5), run `crew-gcce-400-u05-review_en-run1-20261124`.
Date: 2026-11-24.

## The claim

`docs/semester-4/gcce-400/unit-05/topic-01.mdx:65`:

> "**The digital divide:** As studies of Pakistan's digital landscape demonstrate, millions of
> citizens, particularly women in rural districts, low-income daily-wage earners, and elderly
> residents, have limited or no access to smartphones, reliable electricity, or high-speed
> internet."

This is a factual generalisation about the Pakistani population, introduced with "As studies ...
demonstrate", but no source is named in the prose and no digital-divide source is listed in the
unit's source table.

## What the unit's bound sources contain

`sources/unit-05.md` lists four keys: `openstax-sociology-3e`, `batool-2019-civil-society`,
`youniss-levine-2009`, `constitution-pakistan`. `coverage/unit-05.md` maps U5-01 only to
`youniss-levine-2009` and `constitution-pakistan`. None of these supports the digital-divide
claim.

The course's sources tree does contain two relevant notes, but they are bound to other units and
are not part of this review's bundle:

- `sources/texts/shair-2022-digital-divide.md` (content-spec reading list: Unit 6) - "sizeable
  digital divide among the individuals of Pakistan"; infrastructure and access as leading
  determinants.
- `sources/texts/iftikhar-2023-digital-divide.md` (Unit 6) - barriers include scarcity of
  gadgets, internet access, family income, gender, field of study, age and location; rural
  students faced more problems.

## Consequence

The claim is plausible and is corroborated elsewhere in the course, but in Unit 5 it is an
uncited generalisation. The repair is to cite a bound source (for example add
`shair-2022-digital-divide` or `iftikhar-2023-digital-divide` to `sources/unit-05.md` and the
prose) or to soften the sentence so it no longer implies a cited study. Adding a source changes
the input bundle, so the parent must prepare a new manifest for the repair attempt.
