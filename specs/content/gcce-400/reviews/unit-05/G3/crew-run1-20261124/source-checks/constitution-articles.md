# Source check: Constitution of Pakistan articles used in Unit 5

Reviewer: agent:claude (LongCat-2.5), run `crew-gcce-400-u05-review_en-run1-20261124`.
Date: 2026-11-24.

The unit cites Articles 16, 17, 19, 25 and 25A as current law. The tracker row for Unit 5 G3
(`specs/content/gcce-400/tasks.md:62`) asks the reviewer to confirm the constitutional articles
against the current text, so the current transcription at
`https://www.pakistani.org/pakistan/constitution/part2.ch1.html` was fetched 2026-11-24 and the
article text read directly (not only the 2012 edition named in the content-spec).

Current text, Chapter 1 of Part II:

- **Article 16 (Freedom of assembly):** "Every citizen shall have the right to assemble
  peacefully and without arms, subject to any reasonable restrictions imposed by law in the
  interest of public order." Matches `topic-02.mdx:42` and `topic-03.mdx:55`.
- **Article 17 (Freedom of association):** "(1) Every citizen shall have the right to form
  associations or unions, subject to any reasonable restrictions imposed by law in the interest
  of sovereignty or integrity of Pakistan, public order or morality." Matches `topic-02.mdx:43`.
  (Article 17(2) on political parties was amended by the Twenty-seventh Amendment Act, 2025 to
  refer a proscribed party to the Federal Constitutional Court; the unit does not state the
  forum, so it is not wrong.)
- **Article 19 (Freedom of speech, etc.):** "Every citizen shall have the right to freedom of
  speech and expression, and there shall be freedom of the press, subject to any reasonable
  restrictions imposed by law..." Matches `topic-01.mdx:38`.
- **Article 25 (Equality of citizens):** "(1) All citizens are equal before law and are entitled
  to equal protection of law. (2) There shall be no discrimination on the basis of sex." The unit
  names Article 25 in the further-reading list (`topic-02.mdx:152`) and the ERQ 2 prompt, but does
  not explain it; no inaccuracy.
- **Article 25A (Right to education):** "The State shall provide free and compulsory education to
  all children of the age of five to sixteen years in such manner as may be determined by law."
  Matches `topic-01.mdx:42`, `topic-02.mdx:45` and `topic-03.mdx:40`.

All five provisions are current and are stated accurately. The bound note
`specs/content/gcce-400/sources/texts/constitution-pakistan.md` records that the 26th and 27th
Amendments were not checked article by article for Unit 5; this check confirms Articles 16, 17,
19, 25 and 25A are unchanged in substance for the unit's use.
