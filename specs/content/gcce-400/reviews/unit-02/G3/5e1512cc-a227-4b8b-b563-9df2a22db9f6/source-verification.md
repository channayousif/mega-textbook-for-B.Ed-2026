### 2. constitution-pakistan
- **Citation:** Government of Pakistan, National Assembly. (2012). *The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan* (as modified up to 28 February 2012; adopted 1973). Accessible transcription: https://www.pakistani.org/pakistan/constitution/.
- **Usage and Articles verified:**
  - **Article 1:** "Pakistan shall be a Federal Republic to be known as the Islamic Republic of Pakistan." Defines territories: Balochistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Punjab, Sindh, and Islamabad Capital Territory. Verified in Topic 2.1:41, 62.
  - **Article 7:** Defines "the State" for Part II (Fundamental Rights and Principles of Policy) as including the Federal Government, Majlis-e-Shoora (Parliament), Provincial Governments, Provincial Assemblies, and local authorities. Verified in Topic 2.1:49, ERQ 1.
  - **Articles 16, 17, 19:** Fundamental rights of peaceful assembly (Art. 16), association/unions (Art. 17), and freedom of speech and expression/press (Art. 19), each subject to reasonable restrictions imposed by law. Verified in Topic 2.2:76, 85, 92, MCQ 8, RRQ 7.
  - **Article 25A:** Right to free and compulsory education for children aged 5-16 as an affirmative state duty. Verified in Topic 2.1:106.
  - **Articles 50, 51, 59:** Parliament (Majlis-e-Shoora) composition: President and two Houses. National Assembly: 336 seats (including 60 reserved for women and 10 for non-Muslims). Senate: 96 members. Verified in Topic 2.1:70, Topic 2.2:96, MCQ 9.
  - **Articles 70, 73, 75:** Legislative procedure: bills introduced in either House; Money Bills originate in National Assembly only (Art. 73); 90-day passage or joint sitting (Art. 70(3)); Presidential assent within ten days or return for reconsideration (Art. 75). Verified in Topic 2.1:84, MCQ 4, RRQ 4.
  - **Articles 90, 91:** Executive authority of Federation exercised by Federal Government (Prime Minister and Federal Ministers); Prime Minister elected by National Assembly. Verified in Topic 2.1:71.
  - **Article 101:** Governor appointed by President on advice of Prime Minister. Verified in Topic 2.1:78.
  - **Article 106:** Provincial Assembly of Sindh: 168 total seats (130 general, 29 women, 9 non-Muslims). Verified in Topic 2.1:76, Topic 2.2:96.
  - **Articles 129, 130:** Executive authority of Province exercised by Chief Minister and Provincial Ministers. Verified in Topic 2.1:77.
  - **Article 140A:** Devolution of political, administrative and financial responsibility to elected local representatives by provincial law; elections held by Election Commission of Pakistan. Verified in Topic 2.1:62, 80, RRQ 3.
  - **Article 142:** Subject-matter of laws: Parliament exclusive on Federal Legislative List; Provincial Assemblies exclusive on residual subjects; shared on criminal law, procedure and evidence. Deletion of Concurrent List by Eighteenth Amendment (2010) devolving school education. Verified in Topic 2.1:82, MCQ 3.
  - **Article 175:** Establishment of courts, including High Courts, Supreme Court, and the Federal Constitutional Court as introduced/amended by the 26th and 27th Amendments. Verified in Topic 2.1:72.
- **Verification status:** Pass.

### 3. sindh-lga-2013
- **Citation:** Sindh Local Government Act, 2013 (Sindh Act XLII of 2013). FAOLEX record LEX-FAOC220642.
- **Bound excerpt:** `specs/content/gcce-400/sources/texts/sindh-lga-2013.md`.
- **Usage:** Topic 2.1:80, 92: Structure of local government in Sindh; division of districts into urban areas (corporations, municipal committees, town committees) and rural areas (union councils); municipal service delivery functions such as street lighting.
- **Verification:** The Sindh Local Government Act 2013 establishes the local government system in Sindh pursuant to Article 140A. Schedule II of the Act specifically enumerates municipal functions, including street lighting ("Lighting of public streets, public places and public buildings"). The author's usage in Topic 2.1 correctly identifies street lighting as a local council responsibility and school education (post-18th Amendment) as a provincial department responsibility.
- **Verification status:** Pass.
