## Blueprint deviation

The approved content-spec's Unit 3 assessment blueprint (`content-spec.md:196`) requires the
summative assessment to include "one Analyze item asking the learner to identify which
constitutional right is at stake in each of four short school and community scenarios and the duty
that balances it." No summative item does this. The four-scenario right/duty exercise exists only
as the formative Topic 3.2 activity (`topic-02.mdx:113-132`), and Topic 3.1 has a separate four-card
activity (`topic-01.mdx:109-127`). See finding F5.

## Answer-key error

MCQ 4 key (`unit-assessment.mdx:131`) states Article 25(2) prohibits discrimination "on the basis
of sex alone". The current text (Eighteenth Amendment, 2010, Section 8 omitted "alone") reads "on
the basis of sex". The unit's own prose (`topic-01.mdx:103`) is correct, so the key contradicts the
topic and the Constitution. See finding F2.
