== glossary terms ==
docs/semester-4/gcce-400/unit-03/topic-01.mdx:47:**<Glossary term="Fundamental Rights">Fundamental rights</Glossary>** are different. They are the supreme class of rights enshrined directly in a country's written constitution. Because the constitution is the supreme law of the land, fundamental rights stand above ordinary laws and administrative decisions. They serve as legal shields that protect individual citizens and minority groups from arbitrary actions by the state, whether by the police, the bureaucracy, or a temporary legislative majority.
docs/semester-4/gcce-400/unit-03/topic-01.mdx:52:2. **Direct judicial enforceability.** Unlike general political promises or policy aspirations, fundamental rights can be directly enforced through the judiciary. Under Article 199, an aggrieved citizen can file a <Glossary term="Writ petition">writ petition</Glossary> in the relevant provincial High Court. Under Article 184(3), the Supreme Court of Pakistan can take notice of questions of public importance involving fundamental rights. The courts can issue binding orders to public authorities to cease unlawful actions or to carry out mandatory constitutional duties.
