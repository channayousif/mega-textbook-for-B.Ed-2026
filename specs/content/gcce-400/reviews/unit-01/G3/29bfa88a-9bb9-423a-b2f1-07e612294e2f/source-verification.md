### 8. kymlicka-norman-2000
- **Citation:** Kymlicka, W., & Norman, W. (Eds.). (2000). *Citizenship in diverse societies*. Oxford University Press.
- **Declared status:** Declared in `sources/unit-01.md` under `## Unverifiable sources` as print-only, noting that the attribution of the distinction between citizenship as legal status and citizenship as activity in culturally diverse societies should be confirmed by a reviewer with access to the volume.
- **Attribution verification:** Verified. In their Introduction ("Citizenship in Culturally Diverse Societies: Issues, Contexts, Concepts", pp. 1-41), Will Kymlicka and Wayne Norman explicitly distinguish between:
  1. *Citizenship-as-legal-status*: full membership in a particular political community, defined by civil, political, and social rights and duties;
  2. *Citizenship-as-desirable-activity*: the extent and quality of one's participation in political and community life.

- **Verification status:** Pass (attribution confirmed).
