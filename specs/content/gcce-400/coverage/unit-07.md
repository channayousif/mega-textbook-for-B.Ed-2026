## Design and assessment trace

- **U7-01**: Diversity runs along language, ethnicity, religion, region, economic class and political view; a minority group is defined by subordination, not numbers; Article 28 protects culture and Article 33 names prejudice as the harm. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 7.1 retrieval 1, 3, 4, 5 and summative; MCQs 1-3; RRQs 1-3; ERQ 1.
- **U7-02**: Young people, women and minorities take part through volunteering, associations, welfare work, politics and public service, and each meets barriers; Articles 25, 26, 27, 34 and 36 place duties on the State rather than grant favours. Target: Understand, Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 7.2 retrieval 1, 3, 4, 5 and summative; MCQs 4-6; RRQs 4-6; ERQ 2.
- **U7-03**: Inequality is systematic and measurable across education, income, gender and region; an injustice has a pattern and an unmet duty; the civic response runs from noticing to evidence, dialogue, advocacy and legal remedy. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 7.3 retrieval 1, 2, 3, 5 and summative; MCQs 7-9; RRQs 7-9; ERQ 3.
- **U7-04**: Inclusive citizenship requires equity as well as equality; Articles 25, 26, 27, 36 and 37 protect equal rights and allow special provision; habits at home, in school and in the community turn rights into facts. Target: Understand, Evaluate, Create. Evidence: Topic 7.4 retrieval 1, 3, 4, 5 and summative; MCQ 10; RRQ 10; ERQ 4.
