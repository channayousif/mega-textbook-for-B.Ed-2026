## Design and assessment trace

- **U4-02**: Non-violence is an active method, rooted in South Asian history, but it is necessary rather than sufficient. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 4.1 retrieval 2-3; MCQ 2; RRQs 2, 4; ERQ 1.
- **U4-03**: A good aim never excuses a harmful method; honesty, respect, inclusion, doing no harm and consent are tested by a four-question check. Target: Apply, Analyze, Evaluate. Evidence: Topic 4.1 retrieval 4 and summative; MCQ 3; RRQ 3; ERQs 1-2, 5.
- **U4-04**: A community needs ties, something shared and joint action, not only a shared place; community development is shared work. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 4.2 retrieval 1, 3; MCQs 5-6; RRQ 5; ERQ 3.
- **U4-05**: Engagement ranges from inform to empower; effective engagement follows six practices and builds social cohesion. Target: Apply, Analyze, Evaluate. Evidence: Topic 4.2 retrieval 2, 4; MCQs 7, 9; RRQ 6; ERQ 4.
- **U4-06**: The Orangi Pilot Project succeeded through community priority, community finance, local leadership and partnership with government, with real limits. Target: Analyze. Evidence: Topic 4.2 summative; MCQ 8; RRQs 7-8; ERQ 3.
- **U4-07**: The school is a community institution; parental involvement takes several forms, and apparent lack of interest usually reflects barriers. Target: Apply, Create. Evidence: Topic 4.2 retrieval 5; MCQ 10; RRQs 9-10; ERQ 5.
