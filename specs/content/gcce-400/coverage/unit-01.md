## Design and assessment trace

- **U1-01**: Civics is wider than politics and needs knowledge, skills and values. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 1.1 retrieval 1, 3; MCQ 1; RRQs 1-2.
- **U1-02**: Civic participation widened step by step, and each step left some people out. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 1.1 worked example and retrieval; MCQs 2-3; RRQ 3; ERQ 1.
- **U1-03**: Civic engagement has many forms, and voting is only one. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 1.1 retrieval 2, 4 and summative; MCQ 4; ERQ 1.
- **U1-04**: Citizenship is a legal status, rights and duties in action, and belonging; a card is evidence, not the whole. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 1.2 retrieval 1-2, 4; MCQs 5-6; RRQs 4-5; ERQ 2.
- **U1-05**: The citizen's role grew from subject to voter to participant, and rights grew from civil to political to social. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 1.2 summative; MCQ 7; RRQ 6; ERQ 2.
- **U1-06**: Civics prepares citizenship, and the practice of citizenship deepens civics. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 1.2 retrieval 3; RRQ 7; ERQ 5.
- **U1-07**: Active, participatory and digital citizenship differ by organisation and tools, can overlap, and digital citizenship depends on access. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 1.3 retrieval 1, 3-4 and summative; MCQs 8-10; RRQs 8-9; ERQs 3, 5.
- **U1-08**: Citizenship supplies law, rights, participation and shared identity to a modern society. Target: Understand, Evaluate. Evidence: Topic 1.3 retrieval 2; RRQ 10; ERQ 4.

MCQs 1-4 and RRQs 1-3 sample Topic 1.1; MCQs 5-7 and RRQs 4-7 sample Topic 1.2; MCQs 8-10 and RRQs 8-10 sample Topic 1.3. ERQ 1 samples Topic 1.1, ERQ 2 Topic 1.2, ERQs 3-4 Topic 1.3, and ERQ 5 integrates the unit.
