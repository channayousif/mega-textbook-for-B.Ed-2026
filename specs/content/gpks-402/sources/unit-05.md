## Verification and limits

The mapped World Bank report is the open-access source satisfying the approved floor of one.
Crossref was queried directly at `https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1596%2F45320` on 2026-10-28.
It returned the title above and publisher Washington, DC: World Bank. The actual report was
opened at the publisher's full-text endpoint:
https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/972c49ee47cc09d4face97b09ea64362-0310012025/original/Pakistan-Development-Update-Staying-the-Course-for-Growth-and-Jobs-October-2025.pdf
The publication identifies its data cut-off as 15 October 2025. It is not presented as the
latest 2026 economic assessment. The Ministry of Finance shares are explicitly a separate,
earlier provisional statistical vintage; the unit does not mix them with later revised growth.

The working PBS Table 11 link, reached through the official index, is:
https://www.pbs.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/census_tables/tables/table_11_national.pdf
The older `/sites/default/files/population/2023/tables/` route returned 404. Province-language
examples are non-exclusive; no share is extrapolated to a region outside a table's coverage.

The approved spec also maps Burki's *Pakistan: Fifty Years of Nationhood* and Rabbani's
*Pakistan Studies*. Their print texts were unavailable in this session. They are not claimed
as consulted and no specific proposition is attributed to them. The topical open-access
substitutes above ground the draft. No leaf topic lacks an external source, so no
`no-external-source` escalation is needed.

All monetary cases, pupils, enterprises, budgets and lesson designs in the unit are original
fictional teaching examples. They are not surveys, quotations, measured intervention effects
or advice about a real borrower. The UNESCO publication page supports the stated broad
language-access principle; it is not used as evidence of a quantified return to schooling.
