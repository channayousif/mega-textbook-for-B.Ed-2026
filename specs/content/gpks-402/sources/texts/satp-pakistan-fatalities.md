## Fatalities in terrorist violence (used by Unit 6, U6-06)

The South Asia Terrorism Portal maintains a year-by-year data sheet of fatalities in terrorist
violence in Pakistan, compiled from open sources. It is a research database rather than an
official government count, and its totals differ from other estimates. The unit therefore states
the post-2002 death toll as an estimate and directs readers to the database for the year-by-year
figures.
