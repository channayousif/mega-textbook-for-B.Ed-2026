## Out-of-school children (used by Unit 6, U6-04)

UNICEF Pakistan reports that an estimated 25.1 million children aged 5 to 16 are out of school, and
describes the number of out-of-school children as over 26 million when counting the wider
school-age population. The page identifies this as one of the highest national totals in the world.
