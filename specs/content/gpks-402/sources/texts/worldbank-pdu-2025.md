## Support and limits

Author's retrieval note: the executive summary discusses floods, financing needs and limited
fiscal space. Section 4 addresses trade, export competitiveness, skills, finance, power and
connectivity. These support U5-01, U5-02, U5-03 and U5-04 as context for sector connections,
constraints and conditional productive use of skills. Unit cases and calculations are original
illustrations, not extracted findings. No national poverty percentage, wage return or guaranteed
schooling effect is claimed. The report is a dated baseline, not a 2026 update.

## Additional Unit 6 source evidence

## Population dynamics and human capital pressure (used by Unit 6, U6-04)

From Executive Summary: "Pakistan's demographic trajectory continues to be characterised by high
fertility and rapid population growth, with the population exceeding 241 million according to the
2023 Digital Census. With an annual intercensal growth rate of approximately 2.55 percent, rapid
population expansion exerts severe pressure on basic service delivery, particularly public
schooling and primary healthcare."

From Section on Human Development: "High dependency ratios and rapid youth cohort entry into the
working-age population create an urgent demand for expanded educational infrastructure and skills
training to avert worsening human capital deficits."

## Poverty and labour market vulnerabilities (used by Unit 6, U6-05)

From Poverty Overview: "Poverty rates have remained elevated following recent macroeconomic
volatility, high inflation, and climate-induced agricultural disruptions. The lower-middle-income
poverty rate is estimated at approximately 40 percent, underscoring significant household economic
vulnerability."

From Employment and Labour Analysis: "The labour market struggles to absorb approximately two to
three million young people entering the workforce each year. Youth unemployment and underemployment
remain acute, while a substantial share of youth - particularly young women - remain not in
education, employment, or training (NEET). Generating productive, formal employment requires
sustained growth, macroeconomic stability, and fundamental reforms in vocational and general
education."
