# Bound source notes - constitution-1962

Government of Pakistan. (1962). *Constitution of the Republic of Pakistan, 1962*.
Historical text with selected later notes, hosted by the Council of Common Interests:
https://cci.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/Constitutions%20of%20Pakistan/Constitution_of_Pakistan_1962.pdf

Retrieved and text-extracted 2026-10-28. Paraphrased notes below identify the sections used.

- Article 1 identifies the original Republic of Pakistan title and East and West Pakistan as provinces. A strong presidency does not imply the absence of provincial constitutional institutions.
- The original Part II Chapter 1 contains Principles of Law-making. Its Article 6 places responsibility on the legislature and prevents a validity challenge merely for inconsistency with those principles. This differs from an enforceable rights chapter.
- The President/executive, National Assembly and provincial chapters establish a presidential design with a single central legislative chamber. The parliamentary history separately confirms cabinet responsibility to the President and presidential appointment of governors.
- Articles 199-206 establish the Advisory Council of Islamic Ideology, its appointment and advisory functions. Thus the original title's omission of Islamic does not establish absence of Islamic institutions.
- The Article 204 notes identify the First Amendment Act, 1963 as Act I of 1964. The official Ministry of Law collection is separately bound to establish the exact title change, new rights chapter and assent date.

## Version caution

This CCI-hosted compilation is not a consistently updated consolidated text: original title/principles remain in its opening, while later portions include amendment annotations. Its first footnote also states that three schedules are omitted. The unit therefore uses the original opening provisions for the original design and the actual Act for the amended scheme. It does not infer current law from this compilation or reproduce the omitted schedules.
