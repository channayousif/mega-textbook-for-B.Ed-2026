# Bound source notes - constitution-1956

Government of Pakistan. (1956). *Constitution of Pakistan, 1956*.
Official historical text hosted by the Council of Common Interests:
https://cci.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/Constitutions%20of%20Pakistan/Constitution_of_Pakistan_1956.pdf

Retrieved and text-extracted 2026-10-28. The following are paraphrases with article/part locators.

- Preamble: chosen representatives, Islamic principles, rights, minority protections, federation and judicial independence are constitutional commitments.
- Article 1: Pakistan is a federal republic with the Islamic Republic title. The text identifies the eastern and western provinces in their historical arrangement.
- Part II: fundamental rights receive a distinct constitutional section, separate from the later policy section.
- Article 37: the cabinet has a Prime Minister at its head, appointed with regard to majority confidence in the National Assembly; the cabinet is collectively responsible to the Assembly.
- Article 43: the Parliament has a National Assembly rather than two Houses. Representation is divided equally between the two wings.
- Part XII: Islamic provisions include the conformity-of-laws requirement. The title alone therefore does not exhaust the constitutional role of Islam.
