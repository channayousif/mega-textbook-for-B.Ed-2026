## Support and limits

Section 3.1 distinguishes learned cultural practices and material/nonmaterial culture.
Section 4.2 examines social interconnection and relationships. U5-05 and U5-06 use these
introductory distinctions. No source passage, image, theory exposition or foreign classroom
case is reproduced. Original Pakistan cases apply a general distinction and avoid assigning
fixed attributes to rural, urban or tribal groups. The source does not establish empirical
traits of Pakistani tribal communities. Those portions of the unit are carefully framed
questions and possible overlaps, not source-derived population findings.
