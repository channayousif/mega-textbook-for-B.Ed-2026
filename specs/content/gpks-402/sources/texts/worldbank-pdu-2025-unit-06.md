## What this report supports (Unit 6, U6-05)

The report's contents are: 1. Executive Summary; 2. Recent Economic Development (Context, Real
Sector: Growth, Inflation, Poverty, Monetary and Financial Sector, External Sector, Fiscal and Debt
Sustainability); 3. Outlook and Risk; 4. Powering Growth through Exports.

* **Poverty (Section 2.b, printed p. 6):** the national poverty line headcount is estimated to have
  fallen to 22.2 percent in FY25 from 25.3 percent in FY24. This is a national-line measure, not
  the lower-middle-income threshold.
* **Growth and inflation (Sections 1 and 2):** the report discusses recent macroeconomic
  stabilisation, inflation and fiscal consolidation. It does not quantify the demographic or
  youth labour-market claims used in this unit.

## Verification note

An earlier bound excerpt for this unit carried purported quotations under invented section names
("Human Development", "Poverty Overview", "Employment and Labour Analysis") and attributed
population, fertility, poverty-threshold and youth labour-market figures to this report. A text
search of the publisher's PDF on 2026-11-05 found no occurrence of "241", "2.55", "40 percent",
"NEET", "two to three million" or "fertility". Those passages are withdrawn. The unit's
demographic figures rest on the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics census; its lower-middle-income
poverty figure rests on the World Bank *Pakistan Poverty and Equity Brief* (October 2024); its
youth labour-market entry figure is stated as an estimate.
