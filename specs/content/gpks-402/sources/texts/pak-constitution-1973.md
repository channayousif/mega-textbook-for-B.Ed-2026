# Bound excerpt - pak-constitution-1973

Government of Pakistan, National Assembly. (2012). *The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of
Pakistan* (as amended to 28 February 2012; original work adopted 1973).
https://na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1333523681_951.pdf

Primary text, open access. PDF retrieved and its text extracted on 2026-10-28; the passages below
are copied from that extraction.

## Preamble (used by Unit 1, U1-05)

"Whereas sovereignty over the entire Universe belongs to Almighty Allah alone, and the authority
to be exercised by the people of Pakistan within the limits prescribed by Him is a sacred trust;"

"Wherein the State shall exercise its powers and authority through the chosen representatives of
the people;"

"Wherein adequate provision shall be made for the minorities freely to profess and practise their
religions and develop their cultures;"

## Article headings and text (used by Unit 1, U1-02, U1-03, U1-05)

Article 2: "Islam shall be the State religion of Pakistan."

Article 2A heading: "The Objectives Resolution to form part of substantive provisions".

Article 20 heading: "Freedom to profess religion and to manage religious institutions".

Article 25(1): "All citizens are equal before law and are entitled to equal protection of law."

Article 31(1): "Steps shall be taken to enable the Muslims of Pakistan, individually and
collectively, to order their lives in accordance with the fundamental principles and basic
concepts of Islam".

Article 33: "The State shall discourage parochial, racial, tribal sectarian and provincial
prejudices among the citizens."

Article 36: "The State shall safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of minorities,
including their due representation in the Federal and Provincial services."

Article 227(1): "All existing laws shall be brought in conformity with the Injunctions of Islam as
laid down in the Holy Quran and Sunnah".

Article 251(1): "The National language of Pakistan is Urdu".

Article 251(3): "Without prejudice to the status of the National language, a Provincial Assembly
may by law prescribe measures for the teaching, promotion and use of a provincial language in
addition to the national language."
