Government of Pakistan, National Assembly. (2012). *The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of
Pakistan* (as amended to 28 February 2012; original work adopted 1973).
https://na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1333523681_951.pdf

Primary text, open access. PDF retrieved and its text extracted on 2026-10-28; the passages below

## Preamble (used by Unit 1, U1-05)

"Whereas sovereignty over the entire Universe belongs to Almighty Allah alone, and the authority
to be exercised by the people of Pakistan within the limits prescribed by Him is a sacred trust;"

"Wherein the State shall exercise its powers and authority through the chosen representatives of
the people;"

"Wherein adequate provision shall be made for the minorities freely to profess and practise their
religions and develop their cultures;"

## Article headings and text (used by Unit 1, U1-02, U1-03, U1-05)

Article 2: "Islam shall be the State religion of Pakistan."

Article 2A heading: "The Objectives Resolution to form part of substantive provisions".

Article 20 heading: "Freedom to profess religion and to manage religious institutions".

Article 25(1): "All citizens are equal before law and are entitled to equal protection of law."

Article 31(1): "Steps shall be taken to enable the Muslims of Pakistan, individually and
collectively, to order their lives in accordance with the fundamental principles and basic
concepts of Islam".

Article 33: "The State shall discourage parochial, racial, tribal sectarian and provincial
prejudices among the citizens."

Article 36: "The State shall safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of minorities,
including their due representation in the Federal and Provincial services."

Article 227(1): "All existing laws shall be brought in conformity with the Injunctions of Islam as
laid down in the Holy Quran and Sunnah".

Article 251(1): "The National language of Pakistan is Urdu".

Article 251(3): "Without prejudice to the status of the National language, a Provincial Assembly
may by law prescribe measures for the teaching, promotion and use of a provincial language in
addition to the national language."

## Unit 3 source-reading notes

Retrieved and text-extracted 2026-10-28. The cover and preface establish the edition cutoff and inclusion of the Eighteenth, Nineteenth and Twentieth Amendments. These are paraphrased article-reading notes for the historical teaching edition, not a present-day legal opinion.

## Navigation and original framework

- The document has a Preamble, numbered Parts, Chapters where used, Articles, Schedules and an Annex. Part I contains Articles 1 and 2; Part II Chapter 1 includes Article 20; Part III Chapter 2 includes Article 50; Part IX includes Article 227.
- Preamble and Annex: authority as a trust, chosen representatives, democracy, freedoms, justice, minority protection, federation and independent judiciary are connected commitments.
- Article 1: federal republic. Articles 41, 48 and 90-91 distinguish the President, advice and federal government led by the Prime Minister with cabinet responsibility.
- Article 50: Parliament consists of the President, National Assembly and Senate; only the two Houses are chambers. Articles 51 and 59 supply their differing bases of representation. Article 73 provides a distinct Money Bill procedure.
- Articles 141-142 and Fourth Schedule concern legislative competence. Articles 153-154 concern the Council of Common Interests and coordination.

## Rights, policy and Islamic provisions

- Article 8 concerns laws inconsistent with fundamental rights. Article 20 addresses religion subject to its qualifications; Article 22(1) protects against required instruction or worship concerning a religion other than one's own in educational institutions; Article 25 addresses equality.
- Articles 29-30 give the Principles of Policy a different mode of constitutional operation from the fundamental rights chapter. Article 31 concerns enabling Muslims' Islamic life and associated policy commitments.
- Article 2: Islam is state religion. Article 227(1)-(2): conformity of laws with Islamic injunctions, given effect in the prescribed manner. Article 227(3): non-Muslim personal laws and citizenship status are protected within that Part.
- Articles 228-230 establish Council composition, reference and advisory functions. Article 230 permits specified legislation before advice and requires reconsideration if later advice finds repugnancy. Article 231 concerns procedure. Advice and enactment are distinct acts.
- Articles 41(2) and 91(3), as printed in this edition, contain Muslim qualifications for the President and Prime Minister respectively.
- Articles 238-239 address amendment; Article 239 requires two-thirds of total membership in each House and additional provincial assembly approval for alteration of provincial limits.

## Amendment notes used

- Article 2A and its footnote date substantive incorporation of the Objectives Resolution to 1985. The preambular inclusion already belonged to the 1973 text.
- Article 25A's footnote identifies insertion by the Eighteenth Amendment of 2010. The same reform removed the Concurrent Legislative List; the edition's Article 142/Fourth Schedule notes show the changed legislative allocation.

The older territory list and amended institutional provisions must not be represented as the latest law. The unit deliberately avoids current chamber totals and current judicial jurisdictions. Commentary is checked against these primary articles where it makes a detailed legal claim.
