# Bound excerpt - pak-constitution-1973

Government of Pakistan, National Assembly. (2012). *The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of
Pakistan* (as amended to 28 February 2012; original work adopted 1973).
https://na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1333523681_951.pdf

Primary text, open access. PDF retrieved and its text extracted on 2026-10-28; the passages below
are copied from that extraction (footnote markers removed).

## The federation (used by Unit 4, U4-01)

Article 1(1): "Pakistan shall be Federal Republic to be known as the Islamic Republic of Pakistan".

Article 140A(1): "Each Province shall, by law, establish a local government system and devolve
political, administrative and financial responsibility and authority to the elected
representatives of the local governments."

Article 142(a): "Majlis-e-Shoora (Parliament) shall have exclusive power to make laws with respect
to any matter in the Federal Legislative List". Article 142(c): a Provincial Assembly shall have
power to make laws "with respect to any matter not enumerated in the Federal Legislative List".

Article 153(2): the Council of Common Interests consists of "the Prime Minister who shall be the
Chairman of the Council; the Chief Ministers of the Provinces; and three members from the Federal
Government to be nominated by the Prime Minister".

Article 155(1): where a Province's interests "in water from any natural source of supply or
reservoir" are prejudicially affected, "the Federal Government or the Provincial Government
concerned may make a complaint in writing to the Council."

Article 160(1): the President shall constitute "a National Finance Commission".

The Fourth Schedule contains a note that the Concurrent Legislative List and its entries 1 to 47
were omitted by the Eighteenth Amendment.

## Parliament (used by Unit 4, U4-02)

Article 50: "There shall be a Majlis-e-Shoora (Parliament) of Pakistan consisting of the President
and two Houses to be known respectively as the National Assembly and the Senate."

Article 51(1) (2012 text): "three hundred and forty-two seats for members in the National
Assembly, including seats reserved for women and non-Muslims." Article 51(2): a person is entitled
to vote if a citizen, "not less than eighteen years of age", on the electoral roll and not
declared of unsound mind.

Article 52: the National Assembly continues "for a term of five years from the day of its first
meeting" unless sooner dissolved.

Article 59(1)(a): fourteen senators "shall be elected by the members of each Provincial Assembly".
Article 59(3): "The Senate shall not be subject to dissolution but the term of its members ...
shall be six years", half retiring every three years.

Article 70(1): a Bill on a Federal Legislative List matter "may originate in either House".

Article 73(1): "a Money Bill shall originate in the National Assembly"; a copy goes to the Senate,
"which may, within fourteen days, make recommendations".

Article 75: the President shall, within ten days, assent or, for a Bill other than a Money Bill,
return it for reconsideration; if passed again in joint sitting, the President "shall give his
assent within ten days, failing which such assent shall be deemed to have been given."

## The executive (used by Unit 4, U4-03)

Article 41(1): the President "shall be the Head of State and shall represent the unity of the
Republic." Article 41(3): elected by an electoral college of "the members of both Houses; and the
members of the Provincial Assemblies."

Article 48(1): the President acts on the advice of the Cabinet or the Prime Minister.

Article 58(1): "The President shall dissolve the National Assembly if so advised by the Prime
Minister".

Article 90: executive authority is exercised in the name of the President by the Federal
Government, consisting of the Prime Minister and the Federal Ministers.

Article 91(1): "There shall be a Cabinet of Ministers, with the Prime Minister at its head".
Article 91(4): "The Prime Minister shall be elected by the votes of the majority of the total
membership of the National Assembly".

Article 130: a provincial Cabinet "with the Chief Minister at its head".

## The courts (used by Unit 4, U4-04)

Article 175(1): "There shall be a Supreme Court of Pakistan, a High Court for each Province and a
High Court for the Islamabad Capital Territory and such other courts as may be established by
law." Article 175(3): "The Judiciary shall be separated progressively from the Executive".

Article 175A(1): "There shall be a Judicial Commission of Pakistan ... for appointment of Judges of
the Supreme Court, High Courts and the Federal Shariat Court".

Article 184(3): if "a question of public importance with reference to the enforcement of any of
the Fundamental Rights" is involved, the Supreme Court may make an order of the nature mentioned
in Article 199.

Article 185(1): the Supreme Court hears "appeals from judgements, decrees, final orders or
sentences of a High Court."

Article 189: a Supreme Court decision on a question of law is "binding on all other courts in
Pakistan."

Article 199(1): where "no other adequate remedy is provided by law", a High Court may, "on the
application of any aggrieved party", direct a person performing public functions "to refrain from
doing anything he is not permitted by law to do, or to do anything he is required by law to do";
Article 199(1)(c): on the application of any aggrieved person, give directions "as may be
appropriate for the enforcement of any of the Fundamental Rights".

## The Armed Forces (used by Unit 4, U4-05)

Article 6(1): any person who "abrogates or subverts or suspends or holds in abeyance" the
Constitution "by use of force or show of force or by any other unconstitutional means shall be
guilty of high treason."

Article 39: "The State shall enable people from all parts of Pakistan to participate in the Armed
Forces of Pakistan."

Article 245(1): "The Armed Forces shall, under the directions of the Federal Government defend
Pakistan against external aggression or threat of war, and, subject to law, act in aid of civil
power when called upon to do so."

## Fundamental rights (used by Unit 4, U4-06)

Article 7: "the State" means the Federal Government, Parliament, a Provincial Government, a
Provincial Assembly, and local or other authorities empowered to impose any tax or cess.

Article 8(1): any law, custom or usage inconsistent with the rights "shall, to the extent of such
inconsistency, be void." Article 8(2): "The State shall not make any law which takes away or
abridges the rights so conferred". Article 8(5): "The rights conferred by this Chapter shall not be
suspended except as expressly provided by the Constitution."

Article 9: "No person shall be deprived of life or liberty save in accordance with law."

Article 10: grounds of arrest, a legal practitioner of choice, production before a magistrate
"within a period of twenty-four hours".

Article 10A: "a person shall be entitled to a fair trial and due process."

Article 11(3): "No child below the age of fourteen years shall be engaged in any factory or mine or
any other hazardous employment."

Article 13(b): no person "shall, when accused of an offence, be compelled to be a witness against
himself."

Article 14: "The dignity of man and, subject to law, the privacy of home, shall be inviolable." "No
person shall be subjected to torture for the purpose of extracting evidence."

Article 16: "Every citizen shall have the right to assemble peacefully and without arms, subject to
any reasonable restrictions imposed by law in the interest of public order."

Article 17(1): "Every citizen shall have the right to form associations or unions"; 17(2): to form
or be a member of a political party.

Article 19: freedom of speech and of the press, "subject to any reasonable restrictions imposed by
law in the interest of the glory of Islam or the integrity, security or defence of Pakistan or any
part thereof, friendly relations with foreign States, public order, decency or morality, or in
relation to contempt of court".

Article 19A: "Every citizen shall have the right to have access to information in all matters of
public importance subject to regulation and reasonable restrictions imposed by law".

Article 22(1): "No person attending any educational institution shall be required to receive
religious instruction, or take part in any religious ceremony, or attend religious worship, if such
instruction, ceremony or worship relates to a religion other than his own."

Article 25: "All citizens are equal before law and are entitled to equal protection of law." "There
shall be no discrimination on the basis of sex". Special provision for women and children is
allowed.

Article 25A: "The State shall provide free and compulsory education to all children of the age of
five to sixteen years in such manner as may be determined by law."

Article 28: any section of citizens "having a distinct language, script or culture shall have the
right to preserve and promote the same".

Article 233(1): during a Proclamation of Emergency, Articles 15, 16, 17, 18, 19 and 24 do not
restrict the power of the State to make laws or take executive action.

## Principles of policy (used by Unit 4, U4-07)

Article 29(1): "it is the responsibility of each organ and authority of the State ... to act in
accordance with those Principles". Article 29(3): "In respect of each year", the President and each
Governor shall cause a report "on the observance and implementation of the Principles of Policy" to
be laid before Parliament or the Provincial Assembly.

Article 30(2): "The validity of an action or of a law shall not be called in question on the ground
that it is not in accordance with the Principles of Policy".

Article 31(2)(a): the State shall endeavour, as respects the Muslims of Pakistan, "to make the
teaching of the Holy Quran and Islamiat compulsory".

Article 32: "The State shall encourage local Government institutions composed of elected
representatives of the areas concerned and in such institutions special representation will be
given to peasants, workers and women."

Article 33: "The State shall discourage parochial, racial, tribal sectarian and provincial
prejudices among the citizens."

Article 34: "Steps shall be taken to ensure full participation of women in all spheres of national
life."

Article 35: "The State shall protect the marriage, the family, the mother and the child."

Article 36: "The State shall safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of minorities".

Article 37(b): "remove illiteracy and provide free and compulsory secondary education within
minimum possible period"; 37(c): "make technical and professional education generally available
and higher education equally accessible to all on the basis of merit"; 37(d): "ensure inexpensive
and expeditious justice".

Article 38(a): raising the standard of living and "preventing the concentration of wealth and
means of production and distribution in the hands of a few".

Article 40: "The State shall endeavour to preserve and strengthen fraternal relations among Muslim
countries" and to promote international peace.

## Citizens (used by Unit 4, U4-08)

Article 5(1): "Loyalty to the State is the basic duty of every citizen." Article 5(2): "Obedience to
the Constitution and law is the inviolable obligation of every citizen wherever he may be and of
every other person for the time being within Pakistan."

Preamble: "the State shall exercise its powers and authority through the chosen representatives of
the people".

## Resources and location (used by Unit 4, U4-10, U4-11)

Article 161(1): the net proceeds of the Federal duty of excise on natural gas levied at well-head,
and of the royalty, "shall be paid to the Province in which the well-head of natural gas is
situated." Article 161(2): the net profits from bulk generation of power at a hydro-electric
station "shall be paid to the Province in which the hydro-electric station is situated."

Articles 40 and 155 (above) are also used in Topic 4.3, on Pakistan's relations with the Muslim
world and on water disputes between provinces.
