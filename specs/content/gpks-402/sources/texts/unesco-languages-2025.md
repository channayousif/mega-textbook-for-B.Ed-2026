# unesco-languages-2025: bounded source evidence for Unit 5

UNESCO. (2025). *Languages matter: Global guidance on multilingual education*.
https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/languages-matter-global-guidance-multilingual-education
Publication page dated 29 January 2025, opened 2026-10-28.

## Bounded excerpt

Page subheading: “Championing all learners’ right to a quality education in languages they understand”.

## Support and limits

The publisher's description recognises multilingualism as an educational approach and describes
inclusive education policies. U5-04 and U5-07 apply the language-understanding principle to
original teaching cases. The cases show a proposed language bridge, familiar representations
and an independent check. They are not outcomes measured by this report. No effect size,
Pakistan language-policy detail or quantified economic return is attributed to the page.
The publication page, rather than the entire guide, was consulted for this bounded principle.
