## Per capita water availability (used by Unit 6, U6-07)

The National Water Policy 2018 reports that per capita surface water availability declined from
5,260 cubic metres per year in 1951 to around 1,000 cubic metres in 2016. The Pakistan Council of
Research in Water Resources later estimated the figure at 948 cubic metres in 2023 and below
1,000 cubic metres in subsequent years. The international benchmark for acute water scarcity is
1,000 cubic metres per person per year.
