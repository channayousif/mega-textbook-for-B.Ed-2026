## Child stunting (used by Unit 6, U6-04)

The 2018 National Nutrition Survey found stunting in 40.2 percent of children under five, wasting in
17.7 percent, and underweight in 28.9 percent. The survey is the fifth national nutrition survey
since 1965 and the first to yield district-representative data.
