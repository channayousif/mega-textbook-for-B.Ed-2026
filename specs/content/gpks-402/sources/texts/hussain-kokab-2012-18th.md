# Bound excerpt - hussain-kokab-2012-18th

Hussain, M., & Kokab, R. U. (2012). Eighteenth Amendment in the Constitution of Pakistan: Success
and controversies. *Asian Social Science, 8*(1), 81-88. CC BY 4.0.
https://doi.org/10.5539/ass.v8n1p81

Open-access PDF (https://ccsenet.org/journal/index.php/ass/article/download/11145/9786) retrieved
and its text extracted on 2026-10-28; OpenAlex W2127299016. The passages below are copied from that
extraction.

## Abolition of the Concurrent List and provincial autonomy (used by Unit 4, U4-01)

"a consensus has emerged on reshaping the precincts of provincial autonomy by abolishing all items
from the Concurrent List"

"The measure of abolition of the concurrent list for the sake of giving more autonomy has produced a
controversy."

"Autonomist parties also are not satisfied with the autonomy given to the provinces with the
abolition of concurrent list."

The paper also reports the opposite worry: that the omission "would deprive the federation of
legislative powers in many areas which had been regulated at the national level in past."

## The President's power of dissolution (used by Unit 4, U4-03)

"the discretionary power of the President to dissolve the National Assembly, as in 58(2)(b), have
been deleted and for dissolution of the Assembly the President will have to follow the advice of the
Prime Minister or Cabinet."

## Appointment of judges (used by Unit 4, U4-04)

"an altogether new procedure has been introduced for the appointment of the judges of Supreme, High
and Shariat courts. A Judicial Commission, consisting of the Chief Justice of Pakistan" and senior
judges, whose recommendation "would be viewed by a parliamentary committee".

On the debate: the parliamentary committee's final meeting was delayed by "the objections of the
PML-N on issues of judges' appointment and renaming of the NWFP".

## Rights added (used by Unit 4, U4-06)

"Rights of education, fair trial and information have been added in the constitution and the
clauses about right of association have been repaired."
