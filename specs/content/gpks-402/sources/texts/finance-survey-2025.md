## Support and limits

This is the unit's provisional FY2024-25 vintage, not the latest revised estimate. U5-01 and
U5-02 use the shares for comparison and distinguish share from growth and employment. Figure
fig-U5-1 retains the year, provisional status, price basis and source. Fictional constraints
in its final column illustrate possible difficulties, not quantified survey findings. GDP
production shares do not establish household income, job counts or individual well-being.
