# unesco-living-heritage-2026: bounded source evidence for Unit 5

UNESCO Pakistan. (2026, July 14; updated July 16). *From the Desert to the Mountains: A Project
Comes Full Circle*.
https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/desert-mountains-project-comes-full-circle
Opened 2026-10-28; opening project description and exhibition account.

## Bounded excerpt

Project objective in the opening description: “capacity building for artisans and craftspeople to support sustainable livelihoods through heritage entrepreneurship”.

## Support and limits

The account names Kalasha communities in KP and Thari communities in Tharparkar, Sindh. It
describes community documentation, pedagogical tools and artisan capacity building. U5-06 and
U5-07 use it to connect living knowledge, transmission and livelihoods. It does not establish
that all residents of either province share those practices. The unit's display, craft,
story and food activities are original fictional designs, not reproduced UNESCO activities
or evidence of measured participation gains.
