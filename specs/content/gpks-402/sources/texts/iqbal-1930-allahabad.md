# Bound excerpt - iqbal-1930-allahabad

Iqbal, M. (1977). Presidential address, annual session of the All-India Muslim League, Allahabad,
29 December 1930. In L. A. Sherwani (Ed.), *Speeches, writings, and statements of Iqbal* (2nd ed.,
pp. 3-26). Iqbal Academy.

Read 2026-10-28 in the open online transcription by F. W. Pritchett, Columbia University:
https://franpritchett.com/00islamlinks/txt_iqbal_1930.html. The transcriber states the text is
lightly edited for classroom use and that paragraph numbers are hers. The printed edition was not
consulted.

## Muslim nationhood (used by Unit 1, U1-04)

Paragraph 9b: "the Muslims of India are the only Indian people who can fitly be described as a
nation in the modern sense of the word."

## A single north-western State (used by Unit 1, U1-04)

Paragraph 3b: "I would like to see the Punjab, North-West Frontier Province, Sind and
Baluchistan amalgamated into a single State."

Topic 1.2 quotes only the short phrases "a nation in the modern sense of the word" and
"amalgamated into a single State", and notes that historians still debate whether Iqbal meant an
independent country or a self-governing unit within India.
