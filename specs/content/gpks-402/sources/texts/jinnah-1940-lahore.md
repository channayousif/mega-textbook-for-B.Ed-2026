# Bound excerpt - jinnah-1940-lahore

Jinnah, M. A. (1983). *Address by Quaid-i-Azam Mohammad Ali Jinnah at Lahore Session of Muslim
League, March, 1940* (pp. 5-23). Directorate of Films and Publishing, Ministry of Information and
Broadcasting, Government of Pakistan.

Read 2026-10-28 in the open online transcription by F. W. Pritchett, Columbia University:
https://franpritchett.com/00islamlinks/txt_jinnah_lahore_1940.html. The transcriber adds
paragraph numbers and some punctuation. The page gives no exact day for the address, so the unit
says only "March 1940".
