## Poverty at the lower-middle-income line (used by Unit 6, U6-05)

The brief reports a lower-middle-income poverty rate of 40.5 percent at US$3.65 per day (2017 PPP)
for FY24, up from 40.2 percent in FY23. The brief attributes the increase to muted growth, high
inflation and fiscal consolidation, and notes that it pushed an additional 2.6 million people below
the line.

## National poverty line (used by Unit 6, U6-05)

The brief also reports the national poverty rate. It is a different measure from the
lower-middle-income threshold, and the two must not be substituted for one another without
explanation.
