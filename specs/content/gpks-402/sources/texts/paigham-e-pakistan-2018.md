## National consensus declaration (used by Unit 6, U6-06)

Paigham-e-Pakistan is a national narrative on extremism and terrorism prepared under the auspices
of the International Islamic University and launched in January 2018; the declaration text is signed
and stamped 5 June 2018. It sets out a unanimous fatwa and joint declaration by scholars of the
major schools of thought. It declares suicide attacks and the killing of innocents to be prohibited
(haram), describes sectarianism and the spreading of sectarian prejudice as against Islam, and
treats rebellion against the state and the use of force to impose Shari'ah as unlawful.
