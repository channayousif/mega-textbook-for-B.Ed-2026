## National Action Plan (used by Unit 6, U6-06)

The National Action Plan is a 20-point plan for countering terrorism and extremism, prepared by
NACTA and the Ministry of Interior with stakeholders and approved by Parliament on 24 December 2014,
after the Army Public School attack in Peshawar on 16 December 2014. Its measures include
implementation of death sentences for terrorism convictions, special trial courts, a dedicated
counter-terrorism force, choking financing for terrorist organisations, preventing the re-emergence
of proscribed organisations, and strict action against literature promoting hatred, extremism,
sectarianism and intolerance.
