# Bound source notes - first-amendment-1962

Government of Pakistan, Ministry of Law and Parliamentary Affairs. (1964). *The Constitution (First Amendment) Act, 1963*, Act I of 1964.
Official Gazette reproduced in *A collection of central acts and ordinances for the year 1964*:
https://molaw.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/Collections/1964.pdf

Retrieved 2026-10-28. The first Act follows the volume's short-title list. The Gazette heading is 16 January 1964; the Act begins at printed page 653. The following notes are paraphrases, not facsimile transcription.

- The publication notice states that presidential assent occurred on **10 January 1964**.
- Section 1 gives the short title as the Constitution (First Amendment) Act, **1963**, and provides immediate commencement. The title year and enactment year differ.
- Section 3 changes Article 1's Republic of Pakistan title to Islamic Republic of Pakistan.
- Section 4 replaces Part II's heading with Fundamental Rights and Principles of Policy and replaces Chapter 1 with Fundamental Rights. Its new Article 6 addresses laws inconsistent with the rights and contains specified exceptions.

This supports the dated qualification in Topic 3.1 and the comparison figure. An undated statement that the 1962 framework had no enforceable rights throughout is inaccurate; describing the Act as taking effect in 1963 merely because of its title is also inaccurate. Unit 3 uses 1964 for the effective change and retains 1963 only in the formal Act title.
