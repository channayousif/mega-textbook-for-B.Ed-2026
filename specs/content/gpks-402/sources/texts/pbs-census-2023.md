# pbs-census-2023: bounded source evidence for Unit 5

Official index: https://www.pbs.gov.pk/census/
Working Table 11: https://www.pbs.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/census_tables/tables/table_11_national.pdf
Index and two-page table opened 2026-10-28.

## Bounded excerpt

Table 11 index label: “Population by mother tongue, sex and rural/urban”.
Table 12 index label: “Literacy rate, enrolments, and out-of-school population by sex and rural/urban”.

## Support and limits

The index links national, province and district results. Table 11 contains several language
categories and rural/urban breakdowns. U5-05, U5-06 and U5-07 use this to establish that such
categories can be compared and that several language communities appear within provinces.
No percentages are copied into the unit. A mother-tongue category is not a full language
repertoire. The table is not used as an account of tribal affiliations or as a basis for
extending estimates to GB/AJK. Regional examples beyond that table are illustrative rather
than census-derived population estimates. Do not confuse census year with website release date.
