# Bound excerpt - jinnah-1947-assembly

Jinnah, M. A. (1969). Presidential address to the Constituent Assembly of Pakistan, 11 August 1947.
In G. Allana (Ed.), *Pakistan Movement historical documents* (pp. 407-411). Department of
International Relations, University of Karachi (no date printed; 1969 is the transcriber's
estimate).

Read 2026-10-28 in the open online transcription by F. W. Pritchett, Columbia University:
https://franpritchett.com/00islamlinks/txt_jinnah_assembly_1947.html. The transcriber notes
slight punctuation changes.

## Freedom of worship and equal citizenship (used by Unit 1, U1-06)

"You are free; you are free to go to your temples, you are free to go to your mosques" (the
sentence continues to any other place of worship in the State of Pakistan).

"we are all citizens, and equal citizens, of one State"

## Law and order, and corruption (used by Unit 1, U1-06)

"the first duty of a government is to maintain law and order"

"bribery and corruption. That really is a poison."

## Context used in the worked source-reading example

The address states that the Assembly "has got two main functions to perform": framing the future
constitution and functioning "as the Federal Legislature of Pakistan".
