## Course-wide items

- **Course Objectives (guide, verbatim)**:
  1. Understand the ideological foundations of Pakistan
  2. Analyze the historical evolution of the Pakistan Movement
  3. Examine constitutional and political developments
  4. Understand Pakistan's geography, economy, and society
  5. Promote national integration and civic responsibility through education
- **Teaching strategies**: none listed in the guide beyond the weekly topic breakdown - Content gate reviewers should not expect a course-overview `teaching_strategies` list distinct from what individual units derive from their own topics.
- **Assessment criteria**: no course-specific weighting given; falls back to the Constitution Art. III.7 default - 60% summative / 40% formative.
- **Practical work**: none listed in the guide.
- **Recommended resources**: see `## Reading list` below (cited by reference only, never reproduced - Constitution Art. III.5).

## Course Description

From the course guide (paraphrased): this course provides a comprehensive study of Pakistan's historical, ideological, political, geographical, economic, and socio-cultural development. It emphasizes the Pakistan Movement, constitutional evolution, governance structure, national issues, and the role of education in nation building. The course aims to develop informed, responsible, and patriotic citizens, particularly future educators.
