# GPKS-402 Unit 4 - Figure manifest

Manifest v2 per `specs/009-figure-rendering/contracts/figure-manifest-v2.md`. Seven of the eight figures are schematics (diagram, concept map, timeline, table, flowchart) authored as SVG by Codex (geometry repaired and measured by Claude), optimised, visually checked and placed; dark twins derived by `npm run figures:variants`. English only; the `.ur.svg` labels belong to the Urdu mirror job. fig-U4-6 is a sourced map (see its row and the history note below).

fig-U4-6 history: on 2026-10-29 a freehand "map-style" outline (invented border, Thar outside the country, Jammu and Kashmir missing) was withdrawn (PR #368). The replacement is built only from Natural Earth data as recorded in its row and checked against the Survey of Pakistan folio map. The regions table that stood in for it is kept as fig-U4-8. Maps of real territory are never drawn freehand.
