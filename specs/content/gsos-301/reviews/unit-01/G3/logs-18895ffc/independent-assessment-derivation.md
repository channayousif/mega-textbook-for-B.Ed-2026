## 1. Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs) Independent Blind Solve

### Item 1
- **Stem**: Which of the following best defines sociology as described in this unit?
  - A) The study of individual personality and motivation
  - B) The systematic, evidence-based study of human social life, groups and societies
  - C) The practical art of helping people with social problems
  - D) Common sense applied to social issues
- **Independent Derivation**: Option A describes psychology (focus on individual personality/motivation). Option C describes social work (applied intervention to help individuals). Option D describes common sense, which sociology is explicitly contrasted against. Option B accurately captures the definition established in Topic 1.1: systematic, evidence-based study of human social life, groups, and societies.
- **Derived Answer**: **B**
- **Author Key**: B
- **Agreement**: Exact Match.

### Item 2
- **Stem**: Who coined the term "sociology"?
  - A) Karl Marx
  - B) Emile Durkheim
  - C) Auguste Comte
  - D) Max Weber
- **Independent Derivation**: Auguste Comte coined the term "sociologie" in 1838. Marx, Durkheim, and Weber were foundational classical theorists, but Comte named the discipline.
- **Derived Answer**: **C**
- **Author Key**: C
- **Agreement**: Exact Match.

### Item 3
- **Stem**: A teacher says, "My pupils fail because their families do not value education." How would a sociologist most likely respond?
  - A) Agree, because families are responsible for children's attitudes
  - B) Ask what social conditions shape families' capacity to support education
  - C) Suggest the teacher try harder in the classroom
  - D) Accept the explanation because it matches common sense
- **Independent Derivation**: A sociological response shifts from individual or moral blame ("families do not value education") to structural and social conditions (poverty, child labour, school distance, language barriers, livelihood pressures) that constrain families' ability to support schooling.
- **Derived Answer**: **B**
- **Author Key**: B
- **Agreement**: Exact Match.

### Item 4
- **Stem**: Which of the following is an example of a "social fact" in Durkheim's sense?
  - A) A pupil's personal dislike of mathematics
  - B) The rule that all government school pupils must wear a uniform
  - C) A teacher's decision to use group work today
  - D) The colour of the classroom walls
- **Independent Derivation**: Durkheim defined social facts as ways of acting, thinking, and feeling external to the individual and endowed with coercive power. A school uniform rule is an established institutional rule that exists prior to and outside the individual pupil and exercises constraint over them. A is personal/psychological, C is an individual pedagogical choice, and D is a physical/material attribute.
- **Derived Answer**: **B**
- **Author Key**: B
- **Agreement**: Exact Match.

### Item 5
- **Stem**: A study finds that in District X, 80 per cent of pupils from landlord families complete secondary school, compared with 30 per cent of tenant families. Which founder's ideas most directly explain this pattern?
  - A) Comte
  - B) Marx
  - C) Durkheim
  - D) Weber
- **Independent Derivation**: The contrast between landlord families (owners of the means of production/land) and tenant families (who work the land under unequal economic relations) exemplifies economic class inequality and structural reproduction of advantage, which is central to Karl Marx's conflict theory.
- **Derived Answer**: **B**
- **Author Key**: B
- **Agreement**: Exact Match.

### Item 6
- **Stem**: A symbolic interactionist studying a school would most likely focus on:
  - A) The economic background of the pupils' families
  - B) The daily interactions and meanings negotiated between teachers and pupils
  - C) The function the school serves for society as a whole
  - D) The state policy that funds the school
- **Independent Derivation**: Symbolic interactionism is a micro-level perspective that investigates face-to-face interaction, interpersonal communication, symbols, labels, and shared meanings created in everyday settings (such as teacher-pupil classroom interactions). Option A is conflict theory; Option C is functionalism; Option D is macro-sociology/policy.
- **Derived Answer**: **B**
- **Author Key**: B
- **Agreement**: Exact Match.

### Item 7
- **Stem**: Which pair correctly matches a founder to their key contribution?
  - A) Marx - social facts
  - B) Durkheim - class conflict
  - C) Weber - verstehen and the role of ideas
  - D) Comte - the Protestant ethic
- **Independent Derivation**: Social facts belong to Durkheim (not Marx). Class conflict belongs to Marx (not Durkheim). The Protestant ethic and verstehen both belong to Weber (not Comte). Pair C correctly attributes verstehen and the analytical role of ideas/culture to Max Weber.
- **Derived Answer**: **C**
- **Author Key**: C
- **Agreement**: Exact Match.

### Item 8
- **Stem**: A functionalist analysing the morning assembly in a government school would most likely say it:
  - A) Reinforces the power of the head teacher over the pupils
  - B) Builds shared identity and social cohesion among pupils
  - C) Excludes pupils who cannot afford the required clothing
  - D) Has no important social function
- **Independent Derivation**: Functionalism analyzes institutions and rituals in terms of how they contribute to social integration, collective solidarity, stability, and consensus. Morning assemblies build shared identity, national/civic belonging, and social cohesion. Options A and C reflect conflict theory; D rejects functional analysis.
- **Derived Answer**: **B**
- **Author Key**: B
- **Agreement**: Exact Match.

### Item 9
- **Stem**: Weber's concept of verstehen refers to:
  - A) Measuring social facts statistically
  - B) Understanding the meanings people attach to their actions
  - C) Exposing exploitation in economic systems
  - D) Building social solidarity through shared rituals
- **Independent Derivation**: Verstehen is German for "understanding" or "interpretation". Weber defined it as empathetic understanding of the subjective meanings, motives, and intentions individuals attach to their social actions. Option A is positivism (Comte/Durkheim); Option C is Marxism; Option D is Durkheimian solidarity.
- **Derived Answer**: **B**
- **Author Key**: B
- **Agreement**: Exact Match.

### Item 10
- **Stem**: "Sociology and common sense both study social life, so there is no important difference between them." Which unit idea best rejects this claim?
  - A) Sociology is broader than common sense because it studies more countries
  - B) Sociology replaces personal judgement with systematic, evidence-based methods
  - C) Sociology is harder than common sense because it uses longer words
  - D) Sociology agrees with common sense most of the time
- **Independent Derivation**: The foundational distinction taught in Topic 1.1 is methodological and epistemological: common sense relies on individual experience, tradition, and unquestioned assumptions, whereas sociology uses systematic empirical evidence, logic, and testable theoretical frameworks.
- **Derived Answer**: **B**
- **Author Key**: B
- **Agreement**: Exact Match.
