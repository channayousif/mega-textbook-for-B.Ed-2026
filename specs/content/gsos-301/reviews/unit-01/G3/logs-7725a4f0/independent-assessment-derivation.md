   - **Supplied key:** C.
   - **Agreement:** Yes.

3. **Stem:** A teacher says, "My pupils fail because their families do not value education." How would a sociologist most likely respond?
   - Options: A) Agree, B) Ask what social conditions shape families' capacity to support education, C) Suggest teacher try harder, D) Accept explanation.
   - **Derived answer:** B (sociological perspective investigates structural conditions).
   - **Supplied key:** B.
   - **Agreement:** Yes.

4. **Stem:** Which of the following is an example of a "social fact" in Durkheim's sense?
   - Options: A) pupil's personal dislike of mathematics, B) rule that all government school pupils must wear a uniform, C) teacher's decision to use group work today, D) colour of classroom walls.
   - **Derived answer:** B (external constraint exercising power over individual).
   - **Supplied key:** B.
   - **Agreement:** Yes.

5. **Stem:** A study finds that in District X, 80 per cent of pupils from landlord families complete secondary school, compared with 30 per cent of tenant families. Which founder's ideas most directly explain this pattern?
   - Options: A) Comte, B) Marx, C) Durkheim, D) Weber.
   - **Derived answer:** B (class conflict and economic inequality sorting educational outcomes).
   - **Supplied key:** B.
   - **Agreement:** Yes.

6. **Stem:** A symbolic interactionist studying a school would most likely focus on:
   - Options: A) economic background of families, B) daily interactions and meanings negotiated between teachers and pupils, C) function school serves for society, D) state policy that funds the school.
   - **Derived answer:** B (micro-level negotiation of meaning).
   - **Supplied key:** B.
   - **Agreement:** Yes.

7. **Stem:** Which pair correctly matches a founder to their key contribution?
   - Options: A) Marx - social facts [Durkheim], B) Durkheim - class conflict [Marx], C) Weber - verstehen and the role of ideas, D) Comte - Protestant ethic [Weber].
   - **Derived answer:** C.
   - **Supplied key:** C.
   - **Agreement:** Yes.

8. **Stem:** A functionalist analysing the morning assembly in a government school would most likely say it:

   - **Derived answer:** B (social cohesion / solidarity).
   - **Supplied key:** B.
   - **Agreement:** Yes.

9. **Stem:** Weber's concept of verstehen refers to:
   - Options: A) measuring social facts statistically, B) understanding the meanings people attach to their actions, C) exposing exploitation, D) building social solidarity.
   - **Derived answer:** B (interpretive understanding of subjective meaning).
   - **Supplied key:** B.
   - **Agreement:** Yes.
