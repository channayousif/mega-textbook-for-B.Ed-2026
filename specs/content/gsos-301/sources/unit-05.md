## Source passages checked for G3 repairs (2026-11-05)

These notes address the two source advisories in `reviews/unit-05/G3/20261102T212207Z-g3-desk-92fc9b44.json`. They supplement the existing prose without changing its scope.

- **Religion, `conerly2021`, section 15.1:** https://openstax.org/books/introduction-sociology-3e/pages/15-1-the-sociological-approach-to-religion . The opening paragraphs describe religion as organised beliefs, practices and norms and give marriage and funeral services as examples. Under **The History of Religion as a Sociological Concept**, the paragraph beginning “Durkheim is generally considered” says: “It binds people together (social cohesion), promotes behavior consistency (social control)”. This supports community and moral guidance. Under **Functionalism**, the first three paragraphs discuss meaning, comfort, shared rituals and social support. These support Topic 5.1's meaning, cohesion/identity and life-course claims. Section 15.1 examines religion's social effects; it does not adjudicate religious truth. The topic's statement about not judging belief describes that methodological stance, rather than quoting the section.
- **Lady Health Worker programme, `u05-who-lhw`:** under **Health knocking at the door**, the opening sentence states that the programme “was established in 1994”. The following paragraph describes community workers and maternal/child health services. The WHO search-index passage was readable; a direct page-open retry returned HTTP 502. Independent corroboration is in WHO's 2014 *Community health workers: a strategy to ensure access to primary health care services*, Annex 1, **Pakistan's lady health worker programme**: https://applications.emro.who.int/dsaf/EMROPUB_2014_EN_1760.pdf . That indexed passage records government initiation in 1994 with WHO support. These passages support the date in Topic 5.2, not every illustrative institutional link in the worked example.
- **Private schooling, `u05-andrabi2015`:** Appendix 1, **The Pakistani Education System**, **Size and Distribution**, printed p. 25 (PDF page 27), records expansion in private-school numbers from 1982 to 2005 and multiple public/private options in rural villages. This supports Topic 5.2's historical growth claim, not an assertion that every village has access or that growth continues at the same rate today. The example's causal links are a teaching application, not a causal estimate from this paper.
- **Council of Islamic Ideology, `u05-cii-functions`:** **Functions**, item 2, reproduces Article 230's role of advising a House, Provincial Assembly, President or Governor on referred questions about proposed laws and Islamic injunctions; items 1 and 3 describe recommendations. This supports Topic 5.2's advisory-role claim without implying legislative power. The official site's search-index passage was readable; a direct page-open retry timed out. This is indexed-source evidence, not a claim to have inspected a downloaded constitutional PDF.
