3. boeve-de-pauw-2015:
   Publisher direct full-text PDF download timed out; publisher indexed text at MDPI (https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/7/11/15693) supplied accessible passages.
4. Input manifest binding omission:
   In scripts/lib/review-evidence.mjs, citedKeysFor() regex extracts keys ending with four digits (and optional letter) or hyphen-four-digits. As a result, lim-allan-2016-futures.md and unesco-2017-esd-objectives.md were not captured by citedKeysFor and remain unbound in the prepared manifest despite existing under specs/content/efmp-407/sources/texts/.
