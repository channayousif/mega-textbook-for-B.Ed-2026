RRQ answers (independent solve)
-------------------------------
1. Negative peace = absence of overt violence; positive peace = presence of trust, justice and
   non-violent resolution; it enables cooperative school sustainability work. Matches key.
2. (a) Primary responsibility for water/fuel collection, longer walks, exhaustion and dropout;
   (b) exclusion from village resource-governance committees. Matches key.
3. Environmental injustice = poor communities bear industrial pollution while others profit;
   Article 37. Matches key.
4. Article 25A = free and compulsory education ages 5-16; barriers: hidden costs (uniforms,
   stationery, fees) and physical/infrastructure barriers (latrines, boundary walls, ramps).
   Matches key.
5. A disability-only definition blinds teachers to poverty, child labour, gender, language and
   displacement barriers, leaving systemic exclusion unaddressed. Matches key.
6. Access = physical/legal/economic entry (ramps, fee-free); participation = active voice and
   collaboration (group roles, home-language respect); achievement = real learning progress
   (differentiated tasks). Matches key.
7. (a) Translanguaging / bilingual peer pairing; (b) visual, tactile and graphic scaffolding.
   Matches key.
8. Indigenous knowledge is empirical: generational observation, trial and adaptation, verified by
   real-world ecological outcomes. Matches key.
9. Tube-wells pump continuously beyond recharge and collapse water tables; the karez gravity flow
   fluctuates with the water table and shabana governance prevents hoarding. Matches key.
10. Two-eyed seeing = indigenous knowledge and modern science used together; e.g. neem/earthen
    granary storage analysed for azadirachtin and compared with synthetic fumigation. Matches key.
