## Points for the independent reviewer

- **Growth.** The spec's misconception names growth and jobs. The ILO evidence read here concerns jobs only, and the unit says it does not settle growth. A reviewer may wish to add a source on growth.
- **Ali Enterprises.** Topic 6.2 uses a real fire with many deaths. It sets out both parties' accounts and does not decide the dispute. Sources disagree on timing: ECCHR says certification came three weeks before the fire, RINA dates the audit to August 2012 and elsewhere speaks of about two months. The unit uses only "an audit in August 2012".
- **CSR law is moving.** The National Assembly passed the bill on 21 January 2026 and APP reported Senate passage on 11 May 2026. No source found confirms assent or a notified Act. The Senate committee recommended amendments, so the bill may have had further steps. The unit and teacher notes tell the reader to check. SECP's 2009 press release says "mandatory disclosure" while FAFEN calls the framework voluntary; the unit reports both.
