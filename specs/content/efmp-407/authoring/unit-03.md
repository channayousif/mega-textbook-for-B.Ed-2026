## Backward design

### Topic 3.1 enduring understandings
- The 17 SDGs are a universal, holistic roadmap across the 5 Ps (People, Planet, Prosperity, Peace, Partnership) where social justice, economic dignity, and ecological health depend on each other.
- Target 4.7 defines the qualitative purpose of education: schooling cannot be judged by physical access and rote recall alone; it requires transformative learning across cognitive, socio-emotional, and behavioural dimensions.

### Topic 3.2 enduring understandings
- Pakistan demonstrated sovereign legislative commitment to the 2030 Agenda by unanimously adopting the SDGs as National Development Goals in 2016 and formulating a 3-tier phased priority framework.
- Under the 18th Constitutional Amendment, education is devolved to the provinces, making provincial localization (such as through the Sindh SDG Support Unit and SELD) and local school-level action essential for national success.

### Topic 3.3 enduring understandings
- Global citizenship education (GCED) is an ethical civic mindset and moral responsibility rooted in local community care, not an abstract cosmopolitan identity that competes with national loyalty.
- Under Target 4.7, Education for Sustainable Development (ecological limits and natural systems) and Global Citizenship Education (human rights, equity, and peace) unite into an integrated pedagogical model for all grade levels.
