## Course-wide items

- **Course Objectives (guide, verbatim, `3rd 2026.txt:328-335`)**:
  1. Define sustainable development and its dimensions (environmental, economic & social).
  2. Explain the philosophy and global frameworks of ESD.
  3. Describe the role of education in achieving the SDGs (particularly SDG 4.7).
  4. Apply pedagogical strategies that integrate sustainability concepts.
  5. Demonstrate values of environmental stewardship and responsible citizenship.
  6. Evaluate ESD-related policies and implementation in Pakistan.
- **Learning Outcomes (guide, verbatim, `:336-339`)**: (a) Integrate sustainability concepts into lesson planning and classroom practice. (b) Analyze environmental and social challenges using systems and critical thinking. (c) Design and implement community-based sustainability projects.
- **Teaching strategy (guide, `:372-373`)**: lectures, class discussions, question-answer sessions and brainstorming; practical work through group work, group assignments, individual assignments and presentations.
- **Assessment (guide, `:375-378`)**: mid-term 30, final term 50, assignment/presentation 10, attendance 10 (total 100). The guide does not label these components formative or summative; units follow the Constitution Art. III.7 default (60% summative / 40% formative) for their own item banks, and the course-level marks above are reported as the guide gives them.
- **Recommended resources**: see `## Reading list` below (cited by reference only, never reproduced - Constitution Art. III.5).

## Course Description

From the course guide (paraphrased, `:326-327`): the course introduces the principles, practices and pedagogies of Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) in line with the UN Sustainable Development Goals and HEC Education Policy 2025. It brings the environmental, social and economic dimensions of sustainability into teaching and learning, and encourages transformative, participatory and community-based approaches.
