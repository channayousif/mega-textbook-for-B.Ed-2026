# Concept graph - EFMP-407 Unit 3 (SDGs and Education)

Per `specs/016-concept-graph-v4/contracts/concept-graph.md`. The fourth per-unit governance table,
beside `coverage/unit-03.md`, `sources/unit-03.md` and `figures/unit-03.md`. Those record what the
unit covers, what grounds it and what it shows; this records **what a learner must understand, and
in what order**.

`Topic` values are the `### Topic list` labels in `specs/content/efmp-407/content-spec.md`
(`## Unit 3`). `Assessment item IDs` are **derived** from `unit-assessment.mdx`'s existing
numbering under its three bank headings - no prose was changed to create them.

**Prerequisites resolve within this unit only**, which the gate requires. Units 1 and 2 (the concept,
evolution and frameworks of sustainable development and ESD) are the unit's entry condition, recorded
in `index.mdx` under `## Prerequisite knowledge` and deliberately not retaught; they are therefore not
concept rows here.

**Urdu labels.** Labels are drawn from `specs/content/terminology.csv`, the approved bank, where the
term is banked. The remaining are authored here and **carry a G5 flag** (see the list at the foot):
a governance file is not published prose, so it never passes the EN/UR parity gate and nothing else
would catch a poor label.
