## Access and mapped-reading notes

The 2017 UNESCO record resolved but exposed no readable text through the browser. The open PDF at https://bne.unesco.de/sites/default/files/2018-08/unesco_education_for_sustainable_development_goals.pdf supplied the content. The 2024 official PDF is linked from Topic 2.3; its title, year, ISBN and DOI were verified through UNESCO's indexed publication and its general-strategies text. The direct PDF fetch timed out in one browser attempt, but a later curl retrieval succeeded; the downloaded text was read for the transformative-learning discussion. Maurer and Bogner's full publisher article was accessible.

The approved Unit 2 mapped list was checked. Kopnina (2012), DOI 10.1080/13504622.2012.658028, returned 403 from its publisher in this session. Palmer (1998), Jones, Selby and Sterling (2010), and Orr (1992) remain bibliography/print references as documented in the approved content-spec; no detailed claim is attributed to unread full text. UNESCO (2014) is a mapped background report; this draft uses the more directly relevant 2017 learning-objectives text instead. The open Maurer and Bogner article substitutes for inaccessible comparative readings; UNESCO (2024) substitutes for inaccessible transformative-learning discussion. No checklist sub-topic lacks a relevant open source.

All school cases, calculations, role cards, prompts and assessment scenarios are original hypothetical examples, not empirical findings from the sources. No quotation is reproduced.
