## Mapped-reading limits

All three mapped readings from the approved content-spec are cited. `zidny-2020-indigenous` and `owuor-2008-indigenous` are read in full and provide the theoretical grounding for indigenous knowledge in formal sustainability education. `unesco-2017-esd-objectives` provides the international learning objectives connecting SDG 4.7 to gender equality, peace and social justice. The additional rows are primary legal frameworks and peer-reviewed or IUCN research consulted to support specific Pakistani cases (Karez irrigation, Constitution articles 25, 25A and 37, UNCRC, and GEMR inclusion frameworks).
