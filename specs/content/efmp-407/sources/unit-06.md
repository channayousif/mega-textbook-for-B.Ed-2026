## Mapped-reading limits

All three mapped readings are cited and were read from the publisher pages. None of them defines a green economy, so `unep-green-economy` carries U6-01. `zulfiqar-2019-csr` covers bank employees only, and `szmigin-2009-conscious` covers nine people in England; the unit states both limits. Each other row is an author-selected primary or open source added for a specific fact or Pakistani example.

Bound excerpts for `rina-statement` and `pakistan-halal-act-2016` come from search excerpts of those pages, because direct reading failed; a G3 reviewer should compare them with the originals. The status of the 2026 CSR bill after the Senate report of 11 May 2026 was not confirmed.
