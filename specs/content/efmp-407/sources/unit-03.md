## Unverifiable sources

- `gop-national-sdg-framework`: the course guide prints the year 2021 and notes "URL may vary by ministry portal" (`3rd 2026.txt:382`). The former portal `sdgpakistan.pk` now hosts an unrelated site and `pc.gov.pk` returns HTTP 404 (checked 2026-10-31; logged in `D-2026-0001` and escalated in `G-2026-168`). Owner ruling `D-2026-0076` confirmed approval of the spec and accepted the escalated item as drafted. The document is authentic and authoritative for Pakistan's national SDG prioritization and localization process, but because no stable digital copy is retrievable on the host, the textbook prose uses it at the policy and framework level only (the three-tier priority categorisation, federal-provincial coordination, and school-level action linkages). No statistical baseline figures or contested policy data are attributed without disclosing this status.
