## Short excerpt

> Systems thinking competency: the abilities to recognize and understand relationships

## Support and limits

Page 10 names eight competencies: systems thinking, anticipatory, normative, strategic, collaboration, critical thinking, self-awareness and integrated problem-solving. Page 11 distinguishes cognitive, socio-emotional and behavioural objectives. These ground U2-01 and the systems-thinking framing in U2-04.

The source does not supply the unit's fictional tank quantities, reporting loop, classroom role cards or worked arithmetic. Those are original applications. Its guidance is adaptable, not a requirement to teach all eight competencies in one primary lesson. The official record had no readable browser text; the linked UNESCO commission-hosted copy supplied the content.

## Unit 4 source support: UNESCO learning objectives

Consulted 2026-11-07. UNESCO (2017), *Education for Sustainable Development Goals: Learning Objectives*. DOI: https://doi.org/10.54675/CGBA9153. Open text: https://www.unesco.at/fileadmin/Redaktion/Publikationen/Publikations-Dokumente/2017_Education_for_SDG.pdf. Crossref metadata checked on the same date. These notes are paraphrases, not quotations.

- Box 1.1, printed p. 10: systems thinking addresses relationships, complexity and uncertainty; anticipatory competence addresses alternative futures and consequences. Supports U4-03 and U4-04.
- Section 2.4, printed pp. 54-55: learner-centred approaches involve active construction and reflection; action-oriented learning joins experience to reflection. Methods include collaborative projects, scenarios and system analysis. Method choice depends on learners, context and available support. Supports U4-01, U4-02 and U4-05.
- SDG 12, printed pp. 34-35: learning connects consumption choices to their effects and responsible action. Supports the U4-05 paper-use example's conceptual connection.

The school cases, numerical records, causal loop, six-stage sequences and lesson timing are original adaptations. The source does not establish their effectiveness or identify the particular Pakistani schools described in the fictional cases.
