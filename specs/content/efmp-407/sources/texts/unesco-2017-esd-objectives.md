## Passages used by Unit 5

"SDG 4.7 requires that all learners acquire the knowledge and skills needed to promote sustainable development, including education for human rights, gender equality, promotion of a culture of peace and non-violence, global citizenship, and appreciation of cultural diversity" (U5-01, U5-02, U5-04).

"SDG 5 aims to achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls, recognising that gender equality is both an essential human right and a foundation for a peaceful, prosperous, and sustainable world" (U5-02).

"SDG 16 focuses on promoting peaceful and inclusive societies for sustainable development, providing access to justice for all, and building effective, accountable, and inclusive institutions at all levels" (U5-01, U5-03, U5-04).

"Education for Sustainable Development emphasises cognitive, socio-emotional, and behavioural learning dimensions that foster peaceful dialogue, empathy, critical reflection on inequality, and cooperative civic action" (U5-01, U5-03).
