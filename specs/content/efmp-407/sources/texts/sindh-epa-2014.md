# Bound excerpt - sindh-epa-2014

Sindh Environmental Protection Act, 2014, section 17. https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/pak175884.pdf. Read 2026-11-07.

(1) "No proponent of a project shall commence construction or operation unless he has filed with the Agency an initial environmental examination or environmental impact assessment, and has obtained from the Agency approval in respect thereof."

(3) "Every review of an environment impact assessment shall be carried out with public participation and, subject to the provisions of this Act, after full disclosure of the particulars of the project."

(5) "The provisions of sub-sections (1), (2), (3) and (4) shall apply to such categories of projects and in such manner as prescribed."
