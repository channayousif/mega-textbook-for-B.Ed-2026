"Rural and pastoral communities across Pakistan possess sophisticated systems of traditional ecological knowledge developed over centuries to manage fragile dryland and mountain ecosystems" (U5-05).

"Traditional rangeland management systems in northern Pakistan and Balochistan incorporate periodic rotational grazing and seasonal closures (such as 'kash' or 'punn' systems), allowing vegetation and water sources to regenerate without modern regulatory enforcement" (U5-05).

"In arid farming systems, traditional practices such as rod-kohi (spate irrigation) divert seasonal monsoon torrents from mountain streams onto terraced plains using earthen bunds, maximizing soil moisture and silt deposition while preventing erosive flooding" (U5-05).

"Traditional crop management includes intercropping legumes with cereals and rotating crops according to lunar and seasonal calendars, sustaining soil nitrogen and micro-nutrients without dependency on synthetic chemical inputs" (U5-05).
