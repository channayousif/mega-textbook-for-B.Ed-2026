## Support and limits

The article reports household survey evidence that glacier grafting sustains irrigation water and food security in Gilgit-Baltistan. It supports the unit's framing of glacier grafting as an indigenous water-conservation technique in water-stressed valleys. It does not describe the specific ice-combination mechanics in its abstract; the unit's paragraph is deliberately framed at the level the source supports.
