# Bound excerpt - jumani-abbasi-2015

Jumani, N. B., & Abbasi, F. (2015). Teacher education for sustainability in Pakistan. *Journal on Innovation and Sustainability RISUS*, 6(1), 13-19. https://doi.org/10.24212/2179-3565.2015v6i1p13-19. Text accessed and read 2026-11-07.

## Excerpt on teacher training and action projects (U8-01, U8-02)

"Teacher education programmes in Pakistan must shift from transmissive lectures to participatory, action-oriented pedagogies. Prospective teachers need practical competencies to design, organize, and guide school-based sustainability projects that address immediate local environmental challenges such as clean water provision, tree plantation, and waste reduction. Practical projects bridge the gap between abstract policy goals and school reality."

The published unit uses this source to support the rationale for structured, student-led sustainability project planning and execution in Pakistani schools.
