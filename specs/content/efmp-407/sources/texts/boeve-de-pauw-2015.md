## Bound passage notes

The abstract and discussion frame the research around holistic content and pluralistic teaching. The discussion distinguishes knowing, attitudes and behaviours across sustainability's environmental, social and economic dimensions. It also cautions against assuming that a school programme's label establishes its effect. These are paraphrases of the accessible publisher passages.

Supports U4-01 and U4-02's attention to teaching design and different perspectives, and U4-03's attention to connected dimensions. It does not directly evaluate the unit's project-versus-inquiry comparison, particular causal loop, fictional counts, Grade 6 lesson or any Pakistani intervention. Those are original teaching designs. No effect size or causal effectiveness claim is inferred here.
