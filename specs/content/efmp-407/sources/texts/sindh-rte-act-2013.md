## Section 16 - School Management Committee (U8-03, U8-04)

"16. (1) Every school shall constitute a School Management Committee consisting of the Principal or Head of the Institution as its head and eight other members having equal representation from the Government, teachers, parents of children admitted to the school, and the management of the school.

(2) The Committee shall meet at least three times in an academic year.

(3) The School Management Committee shall perform the following functions, namely:
(a) monitor the working of the school;
(b) ensure that the prescribed norms and standards are observed;
(c) prepare and recommend the School Improvement Plan;
(d) monitor the utilization of grants received from the Government or any other source;
(e) ensure attendance of every child in the school."

The published unit cites this provision to explain the legal and institutional framework for parent-community involvement in school improvement planning and project monitoring in Sindh.
