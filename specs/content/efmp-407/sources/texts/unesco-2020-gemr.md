## Passages used by Unit 5

"Inclusion in education is a process of addressing and responding to the diversity of needs of all learners through increasing participation in learning, cultures, and communities, and reducing exclusion within and from education" (U5-04).

"Inclusion is not confined to learners with disabilities; it encompasses all learners regardless of gender, poverty, geography, language, displacement, ethnicity, or social background - 'all means all'" (U5-04).

"Inclusive schooling requires systemic alignment across three interconnected dimensions: physical and administrative access to schools, meaningful participation in classroom activities, and equitable learning achievement for every child" (U5-04).
