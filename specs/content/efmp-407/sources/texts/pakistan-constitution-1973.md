# Bound excerpt - pakistan-constitution-1973

Government of Pakistan. (1973). *The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.* National Assembly of Pakistan. https://na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1333523681_951.pdf

Text consulted on 2026-11-07.

## Passages used by Unit 5

Article 25: "All citizens are equal before law and are entitled to equal protection of law. There shall be no discrimination on the basis of sex. Nothing in this Article shall prevent the State from making any special provision for the protection of women and children" (U5-02, U5-04).

Article 25A: "The State shall provide free and compulsory education to all children of the age of five to sixteen years in such manner as may be determined by law" (U5-04).

Article 37: "The State shall promote, with special care, the educational and economic interests of backward classes or areas; remove illiteracy and provide free and compulsory secondary education within minimum possible period; make technical and professional education generally available and higher education equally accessible to all on the basis of merit" (U5-03, U5-04).
