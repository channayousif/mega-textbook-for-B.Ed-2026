## Support and limits

The surrounding discussion connects transformative learning with reflective consideration of beliefs, assumptions and conclusions. It distinguishes natural changes in learners' thinking and action from forced change. This supports U2-03's reflection and agency framing.

The unit's four-step flowchart is an author-created scaffold. The source is not cited as establishing a universal four-stage process or proving that a single classroom activity produces lasting transformation. The stock-and-flow arithmetic is an original teaching model, not a claim extracted from this source.
