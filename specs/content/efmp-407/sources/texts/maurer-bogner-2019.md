## Support and limits

The introduction explains environmental, economic and social scope and records competing views of the EE-ESD relationship. It also describes participation, values and action in EE. This supports U2-02's emphasis on overlap and on avoiding rigid exclusive categories.

The nested figure is explicitly one planning model, not a settled hierarchy. The article examines university entrants; no finding is transferred to Pakistani primary pupils. School cases are original teaching examples, not study observations.
