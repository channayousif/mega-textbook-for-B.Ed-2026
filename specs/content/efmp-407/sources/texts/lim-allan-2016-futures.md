## Bound passage notes

The abstract, printed manuscript p. 1, discusses combining problem-based learning with scenarios to address uncertain futures and interacting environmental, social and economic issues. Its application is a postgraduate Water Law course at Dundee. It suggests potential use in other settings. This is a paraphrase, not a claim of measured effectiveness in Pakistani primary schools.

Supports U4-04's use of conditional scenarios and multiple options. The school waste scenarios, probable/possible/preferable table and backward-planning task are classroom adaptations; the source does not supply their fictional observations or guarantee their outcomes. Project-based learning in Topic 4.1 is not attributed to this paper's use of problem-based learning.
