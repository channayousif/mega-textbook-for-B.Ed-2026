## Passages used by Unit 3

"Global citizenship education aims to be transformative, building the knowledge, skills, values and attitudes that learners need to be able to contribute to a more inclusive, just and peaceful world." (p. 15, U3-04)

"Global citizenship education takes 'a multifaceted approach, employing concepts and methodologies already applied in other areas, including human rights education, peace education, education for sustainable development and education for international understanding' and aims to advance their common objectives." (p. 15, U3-04)

"Global citizenship education is based on the three domains of learning – the cognitive, socio-emotional and behavioural." (p. 21, U3-04)

"The key learning outcomes, key learner attributes, topics and learning objectives suggested in this guidance are based on the three domains of learning mentioned above. They are interlinked and integrated in the learning process and should not be understood as distinct learning processes." (p. 21, U3-04)
