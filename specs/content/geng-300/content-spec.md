## Course-wide items

**Course Learning Outcomes** (verbatim from the guide):
1. Apply enhanced English communication skills through effective use of word choices, grammar
   and sentence structure.
2. Comprehend a variety of literary / non-literary written and spoken texts in English.
3. Effectively express information, ideas and opinions in written and spoken English.
4. Recognize inter-cultural variations in the use of English language and to effectively adapt
   their communication style and content based on diverse cultural and social contexts.

**Teaching strategies** (from the guide): Lecture, Discussions, Question-Answer, Brain storming.

**Practical work** (from the guide): Group work, Group assignments, Individual assignment,
Presentations.

**Assessment criteria** (from the guide): Class test, Mid Term test, Evaluation of assignments,
Class attendance, Class participation/Performance (initiative, responding, curiosity, inquiry).

## Unit 1: Foundations of Functional English

Weeks 1-5 (15 hours). Unit Spec (G1) for `docs/semester-1/geng-300/unit-01/`.

- **CLO refs**: course outcomes 1 and 3.
- **Key terms**: Vocabulary building, Communicative grammar, Word formation, Sentence structure, Pronunciation.
- **Topics**: vocabulary building (contextual usage, synonyms, antonyms, idiomatic expressions);
  communicative grammar (subject-verb agreement, verb tenses, fragments, run-ons, modifiers,
  articles, word classes); word formation (affixation, compounding, clipping, back formation);
  sentence structure (simple, compound, complex, compound-complex); sound production and
  pronunciation.
- **Worked-example / activity concepts**: given a short paragraph with grammatical errors,
  identify and correct them; given a set of root words, build new words using affixes.
- **Assessment blueprint**: formational on accurate identification and correction of grammatical
  errors; summative includes one Apply-or-higher item requiring a short correct paragraph on a
  given topic.

## Unit 2: Comprehension and Analysis

Weeks 6-9 (12 hours). Unit Spec (G1) for `docs/semester-1/geng-300/unit-02/`.

- **CLO refs**: course outcome 2.
- **Key terms**: Purpose, Audience, Context, Reading strategies, Active listening.
- **Topics**: understanding purpose, audience and context; contextual interpretation (tones,
  biases, stereotypes, assumptions, inferences); reading strategies (skimming, scanning, SQ4R,
  critical reading); active listening (overcoming barriers, focused listening).
- **Worked-example / activity concepts**: given two texts on the same topic, identify the
  different tones and the evidence for each; listen to a short audio clip and summarise the
  speaker's purpose and audience.
- **Assessment blueprint**: formative on distinguishing purpose from audience and identifying
  tone in a described text; summative includes one Analyze-or-higher item comparing two texts on
  the same topic for tone, bias and intended audience.

## Unit 3: Effective Communication

Weeks 10-16 (21 hours). Unit Spec (G1) for `docs/semester-1/geng-300/unit-03/`.

- **CLO refs**: course outcomes 3 and 4.
- **Key terms**: Principles of communication, Document structuring, Inclusivity, Public speaking, Professional writing.
- **Topics**: principles of communication (clarity, coherence, conciseness, courteousness,
  correctness); document structuring (introduction, body, conclusion, formatting); inclusivity in
  communication (gender-neutral language, stereotypes, cross-cultural communication); public
  speaking (overcoming stage fright, voice modulation, body language); presentation skills
  (organising content, visual aids, engaging the audience); informal communication (small talk,
  networking, conversational skills); professional writing (business e-mails, memos, reports,
  formal letters).
- **Worked-example / activity concepts**: draft a professional e-mail responding to a described
  workplace scenario; deliver a two-minute impromptu speech on a given topic and receive structured
  peer feedback.
- **Assessment blueprint**: formative on distinguishing formal from informal register and on
  structuring a short document; summative includes one Create-level item producing a complete
  professional document (e-mail, memo or short report) for a described scenario.
