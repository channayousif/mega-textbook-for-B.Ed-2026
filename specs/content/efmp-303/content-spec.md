## Course Description

This course examines the development, implementation, and evaluation of educational policies and
planning in Pakistan. It focuses on historical and contemporary education policies, national
development plans, challenges in educational planning, and the role of government and institutions
in shaping quality education. Emphasis is placed on linking educational planning with the social,
economic, and political contexts of Pakistan.

Two consequences for authoring, both load-bearing:

1. **The course is policy and planning, not classroom practice.** Examples come from government
   offices, provincial education departments, planning commissions and schools as institutions, not
   from lesson-pedagogy methods. A teacher reading this course is preparing to understand the system
   they work within, not to learn a new teaching technique.
2. **The material is factual and institutional.** Names, dates, policy titles, plan periods and
   structures are the substance of this course. Prose must be accurate at the factual level; where a
   source gives a date or a name, the text must match it.

## Course review plan

Seeds an eventual `docs/semester-2/efmp-303/course-review.mdx`.

- **Course summary points**:
  - Pakistan's education policies evolved through distinct phases tied to political and social change.
  - Development plans (five-year plans, Vision documents) are the mechanism through which policy intent becomes budget and action.
  - Planning is a staged process: needs assessment, goal setting, resource allocation, implementation.
  - Monitoring and evaluation are where policy meets reality; without them, plans remain paper documents.
  - Quality assurance (accreditation, standardization, teacher development) bridges policy intent and classroom outcomes.
  - International comparisons and SDGs frame Pakistan's education goals in a global context.
  - The future of educational planning is increasingly data-driven and technology-enabled.
- **Practice-question mix**: `### MCQs` ~15; `### RRQs` ~10; `### ERQs` ~5.
