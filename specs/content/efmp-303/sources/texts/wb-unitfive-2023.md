# Unit 5 bound source notes: World Bank

Retrieved 2026-11-03: https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2023/05/02/the-silent-deep-human-capital-crisis-facing-pakistan-the-country-needs-to-substantially-increase-its-investments-in-huma, release dated 2023-05-02, paragraphs 1-4.

Paraphrase: the Pakistan Human Capital Review describes gaps in health and education as constraints on development. It reports an HCI of 0.41 against South Asia's 0.48. The index compares potential productivity under prevailing education and health conditions with full education and health. This supports U5-04. It does not show that education alone causes the entire productivity gap. Unit 5 uses this identifiable release rather than the previously unverified 2020 country-sheet locator and shared excerpt.
