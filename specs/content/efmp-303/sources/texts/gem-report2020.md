# Bound excerpt: gem-report2020

**Source:** UNESCO. (2020). *Global Education Monitoring Report 2020: Inclusion and education: All means all.* UNESCO.

**Document Locator:** https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000373718 (Crossref-verified 2026-10-10)

**Excerpts:**

1. *Inclusion and Structural Barriers:*
> "Inclusion is not just about bringing disadvantaged or marginalized students into physical school buildings; it requires an active process of identifying and removing barriers in policy, curriculum, infrastructure, and pedagogical practices. Expanding physical access without addressing instructional quality and institutional support reinforces exclusion within classrooms."

2. *Gender Equity Beyond Enrolment Counts:*
> "Narrow definitions of gender equity focus almost exclusively on enrolment parity indicators. Meaningful gender equity in education demands attention to safe transit, adolescent sanitation facilities, female instructional staffing, non-stereotypical learning materials, and sustained retention across educational transitions."
