# Unit 3 bound source passage record: u03-vision2025

Retrieved and inspected 2026-11-03. Passage summaries below are paraphrases, not verbatim excerpts.
Primary document: https://www.pc.gov.pk/uploads/vision2025/Pakistan-Vision-2025.pdf
Retrieval limitations and edition details: ../unit-03.md.

- **Vision 2025, p. 3 (PDF page 7).** Distinguishes the aspiration from programmes implemented through five-year and annual plans. Topic 3.1 definition and worked example; the Vision is not itself a costed five-year plan.

- **Vision 2025, p. 33 (PDF page 37), Education.** Reports education spending as barely 2% of GDP and proposes 4% by 2018; reports literacy of 57%, rural literacy 50%, low enrolment and gender gaps. Figure U3-2 and Topics 3.1/3.3. These are historical statements in the 2014 publication, with no claim that all baseline observations were measured in 2014 or that targets were achieved. GDP is not the plan-budget denominator.

- **Vision 2025, pp. 33–34 (PDF pages 37–38).** Describes education development as province-led with federal partnership/support. Topics 3.1/3.2 and RRQ 7. Does not establish exclusive provincial control of every education function or a school-financing formula.

- **Vision 2025, p. 46 (PDF page 50), Productivity Growth.** Links skills development and labour-force quality to intended productivity growth. Topic 3.1 and RRQ 10 explain a proposed mechanism, not a measured causal return.

- **Vision 2025, p. 101 (PDF page 105), Goals 1 and 3.** Lists literacy, primary participation/completion and gender parity targets. Figure U3-2 and the provided exercise. Targets are not outcomes.
