# Unit 5 bound source notes: UNESCO GEM 2020

Retrieved 2026-11-03: https://www.unesco.org/gem-report/en/publication/inclusion-and-education?hub=70285, introductory paragraphs 1-4, and https://gem-report-2020.unesco.org/thematic/ (teacher support discussion). Paraphrases, not quotations:

The report addresses exclusion by background and ability. Its overview identifies inadequate teacher support, infrastructure, finance, coordination, community support and policy implementation as barriers. Its teacher discussion calls for inclusive instructional techniques, classroom management and assessment, with follow-up support for classroom use. This supports discussing inclusion as more than enrolment and teacher development as more than a workshop. It does not establish a ranking of Pakistani barriers or current national trends.

Supports U5-01, U5-03, U5-04 and U5-06 at general conceptual level.
