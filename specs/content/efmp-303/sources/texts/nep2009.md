# Bound excerpt: nep2009

**Source:** Government of Pakistan. (2009). *National Education Policy 2009.* Ministry of Education.

**Document Locator:** https://media.unesco.org/sites/default/files/webform/r2e002/a9a9a5c08261acdc4d1cff930054a63dd4b44a6d.pdf (retrieved 2026-10-18, 73 pages)

**Excerpts:**

1. *Section 6.1, Policy Action 1, pp. 33-34 (Teacher Qualifications and Phased Upgrading):*
> "Basic qualifications for all teachers shall be upgraded. The minimum qualification for elementary teachers shall be an Associate Degree in Education (ADE) or equivalent. For secondary education, the minimum qualification shall be a Bachelor's degree with a professional education credential. All teachers shall be required to possess at least a B.Ed. degree by 2018, supported by phased transitional mechanisms and provisional exemptions for remote and underserved areas."

2. *Section 6.1, Policy Actions 6 and 11, p. 34 (In-Service Teacher Development):*
> "In-service teacher training shall be institutionalized across all provinces, transitioning away from ad hoc workshops to continuous professional development cycles that incorporate classroom-based mentoring, follow-up, and ongoing pedagogical support."

3. *Chapter 6, p. 47, paragraph 137 (Quality Assurance and Accreditation):*
> "Quality assurance frameworks and accreditation systems shall be strengthened. Accreditation bodies and monitoring agencies must look beyond physical infrastructure inputs to evaluate actual teaching practices, curriculum delivery, and student learning outcomes."

4. *Chapter 2 and Annex (Equity and Disparity Reduction):*
> "National education strategies must eliminate structural disparities across regions, socioeconomic groups, and gender by providing targeted financial incentives, building safe learning environments, ensuring necessary school facilities, and removing barriers to educational retention."
