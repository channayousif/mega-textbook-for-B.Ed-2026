# Unit 5 bound source notes: NEP 2009

Retrieved 2026-11-03 from https://media.unesco.org/sites/default/files/webform/r2e002/a9a9a5c08261acdc4d1cff930054a63dd4b44a6d.pdf (73 PDF pages). The following are paraphrases, not quotations. These replace the earlier inaccurate purported quotations.

- Printed p. 33, section 6.1, action 1: elementary teaching requires a bachelor degree plus B.Ed.; secondary and higher secondary require a master degree plus B.Ed. by 2018. Existing PTC/CT teachers should upgrade. Less developed areas lacking qualified teachers have exceptions; D.Ed. is an interim pathway. ADE is not the qualification named in this action.
- Printed p. 34, actions 6 and 11: professional development opportunities follow a three-year cycle, linked to careers; provinces lacking an institutionalised in-service regime should establish one.
- Printed p. 47, chapter 8, paragraph 137: higher education needs internal/external evaluation and transparent university/programme accreditation. This is not a school accreditation mandate.
- Printed p. 33, paragraph 104: quality involves teachers, curriculum, textbooks, assessment, learning environment and relevance to employment.
- Chapter 2 and Annex I, B1-B3: access and equity priorities include gender, rural/urban and provincial disparities. These are historical policy observations, not present-day trend measurements.

Supports U5-01 through U5-06 within those limits.
