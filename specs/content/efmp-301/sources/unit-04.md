# Sources consulted - EFMP-301 Unit 4

Verified 2026-09-24. Every URL below was fetched and its title, authors and year confirmed
against the page itself; nothing is cited from memory (Art. III.5, `author-unit` Step 1.3).

The guide's Chapter 4 names attention and perception, memory and information processing,
and thinking, problem-solving and reasoning. The Seifert texts carry the
problem-solving material (Chapter 9); the attention, perception and memory model
material is bound to the OpenStax psychology text, whose Sections 5.1 and 8.1 carry
exactly this content, open access.
