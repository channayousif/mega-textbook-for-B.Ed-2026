# Sources consulted - EFMP-301 Unit 3

Verified 2026-09-24; re-verified and extended at G3 repair on 2026-09-25. Every URL below
was fetched and its title, authors and year confirmed against the page itself; nothing is
cited from memory (Art. III.5, `author-unit` Step 1.3).

The unit's teaching prose rests on seifert2009 (guide book 2, bound), the already-verified
IAE/UNESCO-IBE booklet, and two open-access supplements registered below (OpenStax
Psychology 2e for the conditioning quadrant and Bandura's modeling steps; Antonides &
Battista for Bruner's enactive-iconic-symbolic terminology). The second guide book
(seifert-hk) is bound at course level for its development chapters
(sources/texts/seifert-hk.md); its Chapter 2 is the same text as seifert2009's. The
guide's Chapter 3 (Weeks 5-7) supplies the scope and sequence, including the named
theorists (Pavlov, Skinner, Piaget, Bruner, Bandura) and the Week 7 application slot.
