# Sources consulted - EFMP-301 Unit 5

Verified 2026-09-24. Every URL below was fetched and its title, authors and year confirmed
against the page itself; nothing is cited from memory (Art. III.5, `author-unit` Step 1.3).

The guide's Chapter 5 names theories of intelligence, multiple intelligences, measurement
of intelligence, and creativity and giftedness. The intelligence and creativity material
is bound to the OpenStax psychology text (Sections 7.4-7.5); the giftedness material to
the guide's own recommended book (Chapter 5); the multiple-intelligences classroom
framing to the IAE/UNESCO-IBE booklet.
