## Scope of support

The bound excerpt in `sources/texts/seifert2009.md` (Chapter 7) supports the prevention
quotation, room arrangement for learning, rules as responsibility standards few in number,
and natural and logical consequences. It does not support, and the unit does not attribute
to it: the five-part environment framework as a whole, the five-step escalation ladder, any
comparative claim about how often proximity or the private word resolves disruption, or how
much misbehaviour arises from design. Those are the unit's own synthesis and are worded as
such. The Tando Allahyar head teacher, the Sukkur room and the two Class 5 rooms are
illustrative classroom situations written for the unit, not documented cases.
