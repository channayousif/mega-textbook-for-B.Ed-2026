## Mental health: Strengthening our response (verified; used by Unit 11)

On the definition: "Mental health is a state of mental well-being that enables
people to cope with the stresses of life" - realising their abilities, learning and
working well, and contributing to their community. WHO adds that it "has intrinsic
and instrumental value and is a basic human right."

On schools and environments: WHO urges countries to "reshape environments - in
homes, schools, workplaces and communities - to better protect mental health", and
records that "school-based social and emotional learning programmes are especially
effective across all income levels."
