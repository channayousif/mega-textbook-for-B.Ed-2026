## The study (verified; used by Units 1 and 12)

The study examines how Pakistan's National Education Policy 2009 and the National
Professional Standards for Teachers (NPST) shape the development of teacher
professionalism. Its framing of professionalism is public and accountability-based:
professionalism is treated as a quality of the teaching force that policy and standards
are meant to build and measure, not as a private virtue of individual teachers. The
NPST are presented as the state's instrument for defining and assessing what a
competent, professional teacher in Pakistan should know and do, and the study analyses
the alignment (and gaps) between the policy's intentions, the standards' demands, and
the professionalism actually observed in the field.

Used for (Unit 1): the professional context behind the reflective-teacher material
(U1-14). Used for (Unit 12): the public-accountability standing of the teacher's
ethical duties (U12-4) and the reinforcement row (U12-3).
