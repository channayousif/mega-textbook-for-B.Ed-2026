## Backward design

Topic 4.1 enduring understandings: constitutional changes differ by authority and by the fate
of the text; democratic recovery has several institutional milestones. Retrieval checks test
classification, explanation and application; the checklist mirrors these understandings. The
comparison task evaluates the claim that an election alone completes restoration.

Topic 4.2 enduring understandings: constitution-making responds to linked problems through
negotiation and institutions; Islamic provisions have different functions and historical layers.
Retrieval checks distinguish milestones and layers. The summative explanation corrects a
one-day account and a confusion between advice and governing authority.

Topic 4.3 enduring understandings: a framework's survival differs from uninterrupted operation;
implementation connects text, institutions and practice. Retrieval checks test both distinctions.
The summative judgement weighs durability against interruptions and imperfect implementation.
