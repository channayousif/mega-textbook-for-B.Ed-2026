## Course-wide items

- **Course Objectives (guide, verbatim, `3rd 2026.txt:577-582`)**:
  1. Understand the ideological basis of Pakistan
  2. Analyze the historical evolution of constitutional development
  3. Explain the salient features of the Constitution of Pakistan (1973)
  4. Understand fundamental rights and duties of citizens
  5. Promote constitutional values and national integration through education

## Course Description

From the course guide (paraphrased, `:575-576`): the course builds an understanding of the ideological foundations and constitutional development of Pakistan. It covers the growth of Muslim political thought in South Asia, the Pakistan Movement, constitutional history, the salient features of the 1973 Constitution and the rights and duties of citizens, with emphasis on constitutional values, democracy, the rule of law and the role of education in strengthening national ideology and civic responsibility.
