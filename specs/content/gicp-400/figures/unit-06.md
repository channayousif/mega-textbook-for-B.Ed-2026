| fig-U6-3 | 6.2 | flowchart | flowchart, portrait: how a Constitution Amendment Bill passes under Article 239. Bill introduced in either House; decision two-thirds of the total membership of that House, No leads to Bill fails; Sent to the other House; decision two-thirds of the total membership there, No leads to Bill fails; a side branch for a Bill passed there with amendment, which the first House must pass by two-thirds of its total membership; decision alters the limits of a Province, Yes leads to that Provincial Assembly passing it by two-thirds of its total membership; Presented to the President for assent; Constitution amended. Clean flat vector, labelled, high contrast, no colour-only meaning | Flowchart of a constitutional amendment under Article 239. A Bill starts in either House and needs two-thirds of the total membership there, then two-thirds of the total membership of the other House; if either vote falls short, the Bill fails. If the second House changes the Bill, the first House must pass the changed Bill by two-thirds of its total membership. A Bill altering the limits of a Province also needs two-thirds of the total membership of that Provincial Assembly. It then goes to the President for assent, and the Constitution is amended. | /img/figures/gicp-400/unit-06/fig-U6-3.svg | placed |

Clean flat vector, labelled, high contrast, no colour-only meaning | Table comparing the 18th, 19th and 25th Amendments by year, main change and effect on federalism. The 18th, 2010, removed the Concurrent List, limited the President's powers, added rights and created the Judicial Commission; its effect was large, with law-making and resources moving toward the Provinces. The 19th, in force 2011, changed how judges are appointed under Article 175A; its effect was small and indirect. The 25th, 2018, merged the tribal areas into the Provinces and reduced seats in both Houses; its effect was large for the merged areas, where federal rule ended and provincial government began. | /img/figures/gicp-400/unit-06/fig-U6-4.svg | placed |
