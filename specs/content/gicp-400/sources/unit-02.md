## Verification and access limits

- The Aligarh article's publisher supplies the authors, year, volume, pages and DOI `10.46422/jhss.v11i1.102`. The full PDF was retrieved from its `/102/96` download. The paper contains typographical date errors; the school/college distinction is checked against AMU's own history instead of copying those dates.
- The approved spec's Iqbal article DOI is `10.53762/alqamar.06.04.e09`. The current publisher page lists `10.53762/7468rh39` for the same title, author group, year and pages. Use the verified publisher URL above. This records a resolver discrepancy without revising the approved spec. Detailed philosophical propositions use Iqbal's primary texts rather than stretching the abstract.
- Iqbal's 1930 address is catalogued as 23 pages. Its text is also reproduced in the Iqbal Academy archival volume at https://www.iqbalcyberlibrary.net/files/015/NSM-ALLAHABAD.pdf (document 125). The year and month are sufficient here; no disputed day-of-delivery detail is assessed.
- The National Assembly history was retrievable through indexed text while direct requests were intermittent. Its official brochure also describes the sequence: https://www.na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1308928052_477.pdf. The 11 August speech is independently available as the Assembly debate at https://www.na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1434523779_849.pdf.
- The UK National Archives collection is an open substitute for the inaccessible mapped print movement/constitutional histories. Its British participant documents are situated accounts. No claim here relies on treating a later official commemoration or one author's judgement as the unanimous view of all South Asian communities.
