# GICP-400 Unit 5 - Sources consulted

Checked 2026-11-06. Prose is original. Quotations stay under 15 words. Unit 1 used the National Assembly edition amended to 28 February 2012, but that text predates the Twenty-sixth (2024) and Twenty-seventh (2025) Amendments, which change articles this unit teaches. So this unit reads the transcription below, whose notes show amendments up to the Twenty-seventh, and checks the omission of Article 184 against the official Act. The National Assembly 2012 PDF was not re-tried for this unit, since it could not support the later text. No claim here is legal advice.
