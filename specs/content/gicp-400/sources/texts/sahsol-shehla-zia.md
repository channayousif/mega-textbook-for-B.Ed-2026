## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

The commentary says the Court gave life in Article 9 a wide meaning that includes quality of life, read Articles 9 and 14 together, and treated a clean environment as protected. It says Parliament made this an express right nearly thirty years later through Article 9A. It also records the view that the Court went beyond its mandate; the unit does not report that view.
