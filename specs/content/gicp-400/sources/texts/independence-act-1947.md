# Bound source note - independence-act-1947

UK Parliament. (1947). Indian Independence Act 1947, 10 & 11 Geo. 6 c. 30.

Source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Geo6/10-11/30/body/enacted

Consulted: 2026-11-06.

## Locator and access

Original as enacted: assent line and sections 1, 2 and 8.

## Support in original paraphrase

Section 1 establishes India and Pakistan as independent Dominions from the appointed day, 15 August 1947. Section 2 addresses territories including East Bengal and West Punjab. Section 8 addresses transitional constitutional government. The assent date is 18 July 1947. The Act is used for legal arrangements, not for the date chosen for Pakistan's national commemoration.

Short checkable excerpt: "two independent Dominions"

## Limits

This historical law is cited as a primary source for 1947, not as current legal guidance.
