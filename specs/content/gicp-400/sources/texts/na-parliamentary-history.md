## Located chronology

- The 1956 Constitution began on 23 March 1956 and was abrogated with the October 1958
  martial-law declaration. Mirza appointed Ayub as Chief Martial Law Administrator; Ayub
  subsequently took over the presidency. The new 1962 framework was presidential.
- The 1962 Constitution began on 8 June 1962 and was abrogated in March 1969 as authority
  passed to Yahya. The 1970 election preceded the failed transfer of power and separation
  of East Pakistan in 1971. Bhutto took office in December 1971 as President and civilian
  Chief Martial Law Administrator. The temporary constitutional settlement followed in 1972.
- The Assembly met in April 1972; it adopted the interim framework and formed a Constitution
  Committee on 17 April. The committee draft was presented on 31 December 1972. Passage
  followed on 10 April 1973, authentication on 12 April and commencement on 14 August.
- The parliamentary account describes a two-House centre under the 1973 settlement,
  contrasting with the earlier single-chamber arrangements. Provincial voice and
  population-based representation are distinct principles in the design.
- The July 1977 intervention was followed by non-party elections in February 1985,
  revival of the constitutional framework with alterations and the Eighth Amendment.
  Party-based elections in November 1988 preceded Benazir Bhutto's premiership.
- The October 1999 takeover used emergency and provisional constitutional orders and held
  the Constitution in abeyance. Elected institutions returned after the 2002 election under
  Musharraf's presidency; the 2008 election and civilian government were later milestones.

## Use and limitations

Supports U4-01, U4-02, U4-03, U4-04, U4-05 and U4-07. This official institutional chronology
establishes formal milestones; it is not by itself an assessment of electoral fairness,
popular attitudes or successful implementation. The unit's explanation of democratic recovery
is an explicitly reasoned interpretation using institutional criteria. The difference between
survival and uninterrupted operation is tested against the recorded interventions.
