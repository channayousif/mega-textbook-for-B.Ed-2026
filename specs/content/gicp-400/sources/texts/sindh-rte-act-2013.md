Locator: Sindh Act No. XIV of 2013; sections 3, 13(3), 16.

## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Section 3 gives every child aged five to sixteen a right to free and compulsory education in a school. Section 13(3) bars corporal punishment and mental harassment. Section 16 requires school management committees and sets out their functions. The unit uses the Act as a provincial law on school education and as Sindh's means of giving effect to Article 25A.
