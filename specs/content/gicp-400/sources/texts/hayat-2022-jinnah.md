Hayat, S. (2022). Jinnah's personality traits and their relevance and role in the making of Pakistan. Journal of the Punjab University Historical Society, 35(2), 41–56.

Source: https://pu.edu.pk/images/journal/HistoryPStudies/PDF_Files/04-v35_2_2022.pdf

## Locator and access

Full publisher PDF, pp. 42–43 on constitutional initiatives and pp. 45–46 on continuity of interests alongside changing tactics.

## Support in original paraphrase

Hayat discusses the 1916 Lucknow Pact, the 1929 Fourteen Points and provincial autonomy in a federal arrangement. His account supports distinguishing a continuing concern for Muslim interests from an unchanging political programme. The unit does not adopt his evaluative personality claims as uncontested historical fact.

Short checkable excerpt: "a truly federal constitution"

## Limits

The PDF spans printed pages 41–56. Its discussion also contains contestable interpretation; the unit uses the identified constitutional initiatives, not a claim that one leader alone caused state creation.
