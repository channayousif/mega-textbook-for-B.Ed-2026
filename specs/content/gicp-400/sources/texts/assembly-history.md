# Bound source note - assembly-history

National Assembly of Pakistan. (n.d.). Parliamentary history.

Source: https://na.gov.pk/en/content.php?id=75

Consulted: 2026-11-06.

## Locator and access

Official history paragraphs on the first Constituent Assembly, transfer ceremony and Governor-General oath. Parallel brochure: https://www.na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1308928052_477.pdf.

## Support in original paraphrase

The official sequence places the Assembly's first session in Karachi on 10 August, Jinnah's election as its president on 11 August, transfer proceedings on 14 August and his Governor-General oath on 15 August. The unit distinguishes the 14 August commemoration/ceremony from the Act's appointed day.

Short checkable excerpt: "On 14th August 1947, the Transfer of Power took place."

## Limits

Direct host access was intermittent; indexed official text was readable. The unit does not infer that every legal and administrative transition occurred at a single ceremony.
