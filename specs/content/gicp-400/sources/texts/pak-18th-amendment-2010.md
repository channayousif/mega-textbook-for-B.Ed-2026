## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

The preamble refers to the Provinces having an equitable share. Section 2 repeals the Seventeenth Amendment; the Legal Framework Order is declared without lawful authority. Section 3 substitutes the names in Article 1. Section 15 adds time limits to Article 48, including within fifteen days. Section 49 amends Article 142. Sections 54-55 reshape the Council of Common Interests. Section 59 adds Article 160(3A). Section 65 adds Article 172(3). Section 67 inserts Article 175A. Section 86 adds the Provincial Assembly resolution to Article 232. Assent 19 April 2010.
