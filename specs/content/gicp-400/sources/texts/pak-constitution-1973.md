## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

The Preamble joins divine sovereignty, representative authority, rights and minority protection. Article 2 names the State religion; 2A incorporates the Objectives Resolution. Article 8 addresses conflicting laws, with exceptions; 20 protects religion subject to law, public order and morality; 25 states equality; 25A concerns free compulsory education ages five to sixteen. Articles 29-30 distinguish policy duties from a ground for invalidating laws. Articles 31, 33, 36 and 37(b) concern Islamic life, prejudices, minorities and illiteracy. Article 50 defines Parliament; 175 concerns courts. Article 227 concerns Islamic conformity, protecting non-Muslim personal law and status in clause 3. Article 239 requires two-thirds of total membership in each House; provincial boundary changes require further provincial consent. These are study summaries, not a current procedural guide.
