# Constitution of Pakistan - Bound primary-source note for Unit 4

Work: Government of Pakistan, Ministry of Law and Justice. (2024). The Constitution of the
Islamic Republic of Pakistan, as amended through the Twenty-sixth Amendment.
Government of Sindh-hosted copy: https://www.sindhlaws.gov.pk/setup/publications/PUB-24-000027.pdf
Downloaded and read 2026-11-06. Printed page numbers below refer to that edition.
The approved spec's 2012 URL timed out; it has not been represented as retrieved.

## Located provisions, paraphrased

- Printed pp. 5-6: Article 2 declares the state religion. Article 2A incorporates the
  Objectives Resolution substantively; its footnote identifies the 1985 Revival Order.
  Article 6, with 2010 amendment notes, addresses unconstitutional abrogation, subversion,
  suspension and abeyance. These are not simply changes of leader within ordinary rules.
- Printed pp. 12-14: Articles 20 and 22 concern religious freedom and educational safeguards;
  the former carries qualifications concerning law, public order and morality. Article 25
  concerns equality before law and equal legal protection. Article 25A, inserted in 2010,
  concerns state provision of free compulsory education for children aged five to sixteen
  under arrangements determined by law. The text alone does not measure actual access.
- Article 31 is a Principle of Policy concerning enabling Muslims to live according to
  Islamic teachings; Article 30 distinguishes policy principles from directly enforceable
  validity tests. Articles 41 and 91 contain qualifications concerning the President and
  Prime Minister. Amendment notes are necessary when comparing wording across editions.
- Printed pp. 113 onward: Chapter 3A contains the Federal Shariat Court provisions. The
  chapter's source note identifies substitution under the Constitution (Amendment) Order
  1980, effective 26 May 1980. This judicial institution is not original to the 1973 text.
- Printed pp. 135-137: Article 227 concerns conformity of laws with Islamic injunctions
  through the specified constitutional scheme. Clause (3) preserves non-Muslim citizens'
  personal laws and citizen status. Articles 228-230 establish the Council and its advisory
  and recommendatory functions. Article 230(3) distinguishes legislative action from prior
  receipt of advice and provides for reconsideration in the stated situation. Council advice
  is not identical to a judicial decision or the replacement of elected law-making bodies.
- Printed pp. 164-165: Article 265 gives the Constitution its title and identifies 14 August
  1973 as the general commencement date, with exceptions for preparatory constitutional
  actions. The unit therefore distinguishes enactment, authentication and commencement.

## Scope

Supports U4-02, U4-06 and U4-08. This is a dated historical edition, not a current-law claim.
Original-settlement statements and later additions are separated explicitly in the student
text. The full official PDF remains the authority; this note makes the specific checked
content and the edition reproducible for an offline reviewer.
