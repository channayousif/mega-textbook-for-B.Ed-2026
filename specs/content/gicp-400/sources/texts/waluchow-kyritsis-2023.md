## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

In the minimal sense, constitutional norms create and structure government authority and may limit it. In the richer sense, constitutionalism requires real limits, including rights and rules about how power is exercised. This supports the unit's distinction between institutions, power limits and rights, not a claim that every country has one written constitutional document.
