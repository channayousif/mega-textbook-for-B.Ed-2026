## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

The note says the challenge rested on judicial independence; the Court did not strike the provision down and on 30 September 2010 referred the matter to Parliament, suggesting four judges on the Commission and reasons from the Committee. It argues that the Nineteenth Amendment followed from this and weakened the Committee. It says a majority in District Bar Association (2015) held that amendments could be reviewed.
