## Paraphrase of relevant content

Pages 757-759 discuss the delay before the 1956 Constitution. The account identifies disputes
about representation, the distribution of power between centre and provinces, language and
executive design. It treats constitution-making as a problem involving several disagreements.
The conclusion returns to political instability and difficulty reaching agreement.

## Permitted use and limits

Used for U4-07's retrospective account of early constitution-making. The article concentrates
on the period before 1956. It does not substantiate post-1958 transitions or a causal account
of why the 1973 framework survived. Its broad judgements about political or religious groups
are not adopted. Dates and legal distinctions are checked against primary sources. This is
an author-written source summary, not a reproduction of the article or an independent review.
