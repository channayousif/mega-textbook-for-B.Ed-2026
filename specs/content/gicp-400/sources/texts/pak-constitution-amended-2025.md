## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Article 50 makes Parliament the President and two Houses. Article 51 sets 336 National Assembly seats (266 general, 60 women, 10 non-Muslim), voting at 18, population allocation and party-list reserved seats. Article 59 sets 96 senators: for each Province 14, 4 women, 4 technocrats, 1 non-Muslim; 4 for the Federal Capital; six-year terms; no dissolution. Article 52 gives five years. Articles 70, 73, 75 and 77 cover ordinary Bills, joint sittings, Money Bills, assent and tax. Article 41 makes the President Head of State, elected by an electoral college; Article 44 limits consecutive terms; Article 47 requires two-thirds for removal; Article 48 requires action on advice with narrow discretion; Article 58 covers dissolution. Articles 90, 91, 92, 95, 97 and 99 cover the Federal Government, election of the Prime Minister, collective responsibility, the eleven per cent Cabinet cap, no-confidence and action in the President's name. Article 175 lists the courts. Article 175A sets the Commission and nomination route. Articles 175B, 175E, 175F, 175H and 175I set the Federal Constitutional Court's composition, jurisdiction and retirement age; notes show Articles 184 and 186 omitted in 2025. Articles 179, 185, 189, 192, 199, 203, 203D, 209 and 68 cover other courts, removal and limits on discussion. Articles 238 and 239 set the amendment route; the note shows clause (5) substituted in 2025. Article 106 gives Khyber Pakhtunkhwa 145 seats. Article 246(d) records the merger; the note shows Article 247 omitted and its former clauses (1) and (7). Articles 142, 143, 153, 154, 160(3A), 172(3), 101 and 232 cover law-making, federal prevalence, the Council, finance floor, oil and gas, Governors and emergencies. The Fourth Schedule notes show the Concurrent List omitted, including entry 38; Part II entry 12 stays federal. Support limit: a private transcription, not an official gazette.
