# Bound source note - iqbal-1930-allahabad

Iqbal, M. (1930). Presidential address: All India Muslim League, Allahabad session, December 1930.

Source: https://iqbalcyberlibrary.net/en/643.html

Consulted: 2026-11-06.

## Locator and access

Iqbal Academy catalogue: Lahore, Kapur Art Printing Works, 1930, 23 pages. Archival reproduction: https://www.iqbalcyberlibrary.net/files/015/NSM-ALLAHABAD.pdf, document 125.

## Support in original paraphrase

The address proposes combining Punjab, the North-West Frontier Province, Sindh and Baluchistan in a north-western Muslim unit. Its argument concerns self-government amid constitutional debate. It does not provide the full territorial settlement implemented in 1947. The unit distinguishes a proposal from a later boundary award.

Short checkable excerpt: "Punjab, North-West Frontier Province, Sind and Baluchistan"

## Limits

The archival heading and modern catalogues differ about the exact delivery day. The unit uses December 1930, not a disputed day. It does not teach a map derived from the speech.
