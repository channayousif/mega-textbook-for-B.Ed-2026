## Support in original paraphrase

The article studies Aligarh as a sustained social movement. Its discussion links modern education, English and institution-building to Muslim social capacity. This supports the unit's account of educational preparation, not a claim that Sir Syed demanded the eventual Pakistan state.

Short checkable excerpt: "aimed at modernization and uplift of the Muslim community of India"

## Limits

The paper contains obvious date typos. The unit checks school/college dates against AMU and does not repeat the paper's broad characterisations of entire religious communities.
