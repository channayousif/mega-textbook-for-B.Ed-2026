## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Section 3 reduces the National Assembly from 342 to 336 seats. Section 4 reduces the Senate from 104 to 96. Section 6 sets Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's Assembly at 145. Section 8 adds the merger paragraph to Article 246. Section 9 omits Article 247. The Statement refers to bringing the people of the tribal areas into the mainstream. Support limit: this is the Bill as passed by the Senate; the enacted Act number (37 of 2018) and 31 May 2018 date come from the transcription's notes.
