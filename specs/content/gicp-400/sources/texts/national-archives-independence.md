## Support in original paraphrase

The introduction discusses League mobilisation and electoral support, the Cabinet Mission and partition's human costs. The broadcast records Mountbatten's account of unsuccessful attempts to secure a united settlement. The later telegram concerns administrative breakdown and violence. These support the unit's distinctions between demands, negotiations and remaining governing tasks.
