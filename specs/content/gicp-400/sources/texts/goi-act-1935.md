# Source note: Government of India Act 1935

Primary source: UK Parliament. *Government of India Act 1935*, as enacted.
https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Geo5and1Edw8/26/2/contents/enacted

## Author paraphrase for U3-02

The Act supplied a framework for central and provincial institutions and divided legislative subjects into lists. It was colonial legislation, not a constitution written by Pakistan's Constituent Assembly. The Act as enacted must be distinguished from the adapted framework used after independence. Section 8 of the Indian Independence Act 1947 supplied the transitional authority for using that adapted framework while a permanent constitution was prepared.
