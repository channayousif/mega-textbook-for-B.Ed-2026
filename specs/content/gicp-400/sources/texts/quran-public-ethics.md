## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

2:256 rejects compulsion in religion. 3:159 directs consultation in affairs. 4:58 links returning trusts with just judgement. 42:38 describes mutual consultation. 49:13 addresses humanity's common origin, peoples and tribes, and righteousness rather than ancestry as the measure of honour before Allah. The unit paraphrases these themes and does not present them as a complete modern constitutional design.

These notes identify the checked passages and their support limits. They are not verbatim extracts or a substitute for independent source review.
