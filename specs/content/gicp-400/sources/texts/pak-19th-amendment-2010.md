## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Passed by the National Assembly 22 December 2010 and the Senate 30 December 2010; assent 1 January 2011. Sections 2-3 refer to the Islamabad High Court. Section 4 raises Supreme Court judges on the Commission from two to four and requires recorded reasons when the Committee does not confirm a nomination by three-fourths, with another nomination to follow. Section 7 amends Article 246.
