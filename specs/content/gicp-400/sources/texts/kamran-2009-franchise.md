# Bound source note - kamran-2009-franchise

Kamran, T. (2009). Early phase of electoral politics in Pakistan: 1950s. *South Asian Studies, 24*(2), 257–282.

Source: https://pu.edu.pk/images/journal/csas/PDF/7-Tahir%20Kamran.pdf

Consulted: 2026-11-06, publisher PDF, opening historical background on printed pp. 257–258. The article's main subject is later elections; only its explicit background statement about the colonial 1946 franchise is used in this unit.

Checkable excerpt: "The first provincial elections were held in 1946 on a restricted franchise under the British Raj."

This supports the limit on treating the 1946 provincial election results as a universal adult vote. It does not establish precise franchise percentages or prove every voter's intentions. Neither is claimed. The unit's representative-claim discussion uses the National Archives collection separately.
