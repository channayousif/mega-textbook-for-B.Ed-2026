## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Iqbal discusses Islam as a basis of Muslim community and political identity in India. His proposed north-western grouping includes Punjab, the North-West Frontier Province, Sind and Baluchistan. His wording allows self-government within or outside the British Empire. The unit therefore presents a contribution to political thought, not an exact blueprint for the later borders of Pakistan.
