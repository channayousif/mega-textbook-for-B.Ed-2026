Locator: Act No. XXXII of 2025, assent 13 November 2025; section 21 (new Chapter 1A, Article 175E); section 29 (omission of Article 184).

## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Section 21 inserts a chapter on the Federal Constitutional Court. Article 175E(3) gives it power, without prejudice to Article 199, over a question of public importance about enforcing a Fundamental Right, exercised on an application by a person. Section 29 omits Article 184. The transcription's notes for Part VII agree, and show Article 184's former text. Article 175F(1)(c) on appeals from Article 199 orders was read in that transcription.
