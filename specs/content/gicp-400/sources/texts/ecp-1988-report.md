# Election Commission report - Bound primary-source note for Unit 4

Election Commission of Pakistan. Report on the general elections 1988.
https://ecp.gov.pk/storage/files/1/ger-10.pdf
Chapter I, The aftermath of 1985 general elections; official indexed passage checked
2026-11-06. These are author-written paraphrases of numbered paragraphs 1-6.

The report records revival of the Constitution from 10 March 1985 under the Revival Order,
subject to its specified amendments. It describes assemblies elected in February-March,
Junejo taking office and receiving assembly confidence, and the Eighth Amendment in November.
It separately records withdrawal of martial law on 30 December 1985. Thus the election,
revival, executive arrangements and withdrawal were distinct steps. The report also notes
continued restrictions on political party activity after the lifting of martial law.

Supports U4-03. The evidence establishes formal milestones and supports treating restoration
as staged. It does not establish that every democratic condition was met at any one milestone.
