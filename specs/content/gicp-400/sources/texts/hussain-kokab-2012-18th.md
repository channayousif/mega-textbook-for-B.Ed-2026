## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

The paper reports that the amendment added rights of education, fair trial and information, and included for the first time a State duty on education for ages 5 to 16. It describes curtailed presidential powers, including deletion of the discretionary dissolution power in 58(2)(b), and the Prime Minister's role as chief executive and Council of Common Interests chair. On the Concurrent List, it reports abolition and transfer to the provinces, dissatisfaction among autonomist parties, objections from centrist voices, and concern about new provincial costs.
