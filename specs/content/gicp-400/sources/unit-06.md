# GICP-400 Unit 6 - Sources consulted

Checked 2026-11-06. Prose is original. Quotations stay under 15 words. The approved reading note says the 2012 National Assembly PDF predates the 25th Amendment, and it also predates the 26th (2024) and 27th (2025), which change the courts this unit teaches. So the mapped reading `pak-constitution-1973` carries only what its bound note (shared with Unit 1) records, namely Articles 50, 175 and the Article 239 rule. Every other article is recorded under `pak-constitution-amended-2025`: the same primary work, read in the transcription whose notes run to the 27th Amendment. Unit 1's bound note is not edited, because Unit 1's G2 evidence binds it. The National Assembly 2012 PDF was not retried. No claim here is legal advice.

The bound note for `hussain-kokab-2012-18th` is byte-identical to the one Unit 5 commits, so the two branches merge cleanly and Unit 5's evidence stays valid. It does not list section 14 or section 15, which this unit also read; their locators are in the row below.

Dates differ between sources for the 18th and 19th Amendments. Ashfaq gives 15 April 2010 and 4 January 2011; the Acts' transcriptions and amendment notes give assent on 19 April 2010 and 1 January 2011. This unit uses the latter. Hussain and Kokab say the Council of Common Interests meets once in thirty days; Article 154(3) says at least once in ninety days, and this unit uses the Article. The transcription of Article 48(1) prints "after fifteen days", while section 15 of the 18th Amendment Act and Hussain and Kokab say "within fifteen days", and this unit uses "within".
