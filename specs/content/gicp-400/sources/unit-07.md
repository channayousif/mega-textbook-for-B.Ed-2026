## Verification and access limits

- Kennedy (2012) is an open-access article in *Education Sciences*. It supports the international comparative perspective on civic curriculum in nation states and the continuing duty of the state to foster active citizenship. It does not analyze Pakistan-specific schooling or treat the assimilation/integration distinction.
- Dean (2005) is published in the *International Journal of Citizenship and Teacher Education* (AKU eCommons repository). It provides empirical grounding for the critique of rote transmission teaching in Pakistani civics, the emergence of passive citizenship, the need to develop skills and values alongside knowledge, and the essential role of the teacher in democratic pedagogical practice.
- The Constitution text is verified through the accessible transcription carrying amendment annotations. Fundamental Rights (Articles 4, 10A, 25, 25A, 28) are distinguished from Principles of Policy (Articles 33, 36, 37(b)), noting their non-justiciable character under Article 30(2) while highlighting their constitutional guidance for state policy and national integration.
