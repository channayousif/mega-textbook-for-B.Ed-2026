## Kennedy (2012), full text read at www.mdpi.com/2227-7102/2/3/121

Supported: component of the curriculum in all nation states; form and purpose differ; globalisation pressures; key responsibility stays with nation states; active and engaged citizenship as the aim of state strategies. Not supported: a lesson for Pakistan that schools must act and not leave it to chance. Zero hits for "assimilat", so it does not support the U7-02 integration teaching.
