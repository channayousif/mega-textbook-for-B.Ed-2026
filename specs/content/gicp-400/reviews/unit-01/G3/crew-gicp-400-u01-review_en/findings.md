## Readability and pedagogy

Definitions precede use: ideology, constitution, nationhood, sovereignty, trust, theocracy
and the value-principle-article chain are each explained in plain words before they are
applied. Both topics open with a real Sindhi classroom situation (Khairpur and Mithi,
Tharparkar), correct the approved misconceptions, and close by returning to the pupil.
Worked examples are concrete: the ideology test table, the equality chain, the Article 25A
analysis, the three-step reply to Deepak, and the claim-against-the-text test.

Activities are feasible in the stated context: groups of three or four, 25 or 30 minutes,
roles assigned, local materials named, and a pair-and-share check. The practicum tasks are
bounded and ask for three lines or a short note. The summative tasks carry analytic
mini-rubrics that distinguish performance and state that praise or criticism alone does not
meet the analysis criterion. A teacher who has not studied law can follow the explanations
without prior specialist knowledge. Readability and pedagogy pass from source inspection.
