2. External affairs: Parliament, list entry 3. Schooling: Sindh Assembly, residual field. Evidence: both levels, Article 142(b). Guidance agrees, one reasoned point each.
3. Article 95 removal by majority of total National Assembly membership illustrates continuing dependence on the assembly. Guidance distinguishes process, support and the parliamentary link.
4. The premise fails because ministers sit in Parliament while courts remain institutionally separate; no-confidence and dissolution are reciprocal controls. Guidance agrees. Because this repeats the taught correction, its Analyze tag is generous.
5. A minority pupil's religious-instruction protection and a girl's equal protection illustrate security against public power and equal citizenship. Guidance accepts different fitting examples and marks both explanation and application.
6. Article 19 permits lawful reasonable restrictions on listed grounds, so neither unlimited speech nor arbitrary official censorship follows. Guidance demands wording, a ground and limits on restrictions. The exact answer is already demonstrated in Topic 5.2, weakening the Analyze classification.
7. Articles 22(3)(b),25 and 25A fit the stated publicly funded school and age. Begin with school/department, then Article 199 if no other adequate remedy exists. Guidance accepts two fitting articles and ordered steps, without demanding a direct constitutional-court filing.
8. Article 29(1) imposes responsibility and 29(3) parliamentary reporting; non-justiciability does not erase public accountability. Guidance awards the two provisions and inference.
9. Different legal treatment by sex can engage Article 25; economic opportunity and distribution engage Article 38(a), with a different judicial route. Guidance distinguishes examples and legal effect. This closely follows the taught comparison.
10. Article 25A covers the eleven-year-old and has a judicial route; Article 37(b) alone cannot found a challenge and may be resource-dependent. Guidance rewards two explained reasons. Again, a reproduced worked comparison does not automatically become analysis merely from the verb.
