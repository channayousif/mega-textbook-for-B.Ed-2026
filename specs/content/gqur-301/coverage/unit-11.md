## Backward design and Bloom alignment

Enduring understanding for 11.1: a model simplifies a situation; probability
describes uncertainty rather than promising a result. For 11.2: predictions are
useful only with checked assumptions, observed-data comparison and a suitable
decision rule.

- U11-01 (Analyze): taught and assessed through Topic 11.1 Explanation, retrieval 1–4, checklist 1–4, the model-choice summative task, MCQ 1–5, RRQ 1–6 and ERQ 1.
- U11-02 (Evaluate): taught and assessed through Topic 11.2 Explanation, retrieval 1–4, checklist 1–4, the policy summative task, MCQ 6–10, RRQ 7–10 and ERQ 2–5.

The bank samples both topics with five MCQs each and six/four RRQs respectively.
ERQ 1 emphasizes model choice (11.1); ERQs 2–3 apply the models (11.2);
ERQs 4–5 integrate model meaning, assumptions and practical consequences.
