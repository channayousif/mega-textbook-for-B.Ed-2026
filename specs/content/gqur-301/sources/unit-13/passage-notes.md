## lane2023: correlation

[Values of the Pearson Correlation](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/describing_bivariate_data/pearson.html),
definition and sample/population notation paragraphs.

> Pearson's r can range from -1 to 1.

Paraphrase: r describes sample linear association, whereas rho denotes the population
coefficient. Curved relationships need inspection beyond this coefficient. This
supports Topic 13.1's range, sign and sample-only interpretation. This page does
not establish the unit's .30/.70 classroom convention.

## lane2023: regression

[Introduction to Linear Regression](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/regression/intro.html),
Computing the Regression Line and Standardized Variables.

> b = r sY/sX

Paraphrase: the intercept uses both means and the slope. Standardising both variables
gives zero means, unit standard deviations, slope r and intercept zero. This
supports Topic 13.2's scale qualification and y-bar explanation. Raw HTML was also
read because the web text extraction omitted the final equation paragraphs.

## openstax-stats

[12.3 The Regression Equation](https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/12-3-the-regression-equation),
opening, least-squares explanation, correlation formula and prediction discussion.

> Usually, you must be satisfied with rough predictions.

Paraphrase: observations generally differ from fitted responses. Residuals compare
observed and predicted values; Pearson r and least-squares coefficients can be
computed from paired sums. Predictions require appropriate fit rather than mere
membership in the observed x range. Supports the computations and qualified
prediction discussion, not a guarantee for a new pupil.

## nist-sematech

[4.1.4.1 Linear Least Squares Regression](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section1/pmd141.htm),
Why Least Squares paragraph.

> minimizing the sum of the squared deviations between the data and the model

Paraphrase: least squares estimates model parameters through this minimisation.
It supports the fitting criterion; it does not establish causal effects or
confidence in a population link from a large sample r alone.
