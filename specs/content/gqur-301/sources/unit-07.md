# Unit 7 Sources Consulted

Per `specs/007-content-depth-standard/contracts/sources-consulted.md`. Every key cited in
`specs/content/gqur-301/coverage/unit-07.md` appears here.

Course guide: `Scheme-and-Course-guides/extracted-text/2nd 2026.txt`, GQUR-301 Chapter 7. Section
locators used in this unit: Lane Chapter 5 ("Permutations and Combinations"); OpenStax
*Introductory Statistics 2e* Section 3.5; OpenStax *College Algebra 2e* Section 9.5. All three
section pages were fetched on 2026-10-21 and their content matched the claims they support (Lane:
counting orders by listing and multiplying staged menu choices, 3 x 6 x 4 = 72; Illowsky and Dean:
a tree diagram as a graph for finding the outcomes of an experiment; Abramson: the Addition
Principle and the Multiplication Principle, with menu and outfit examples).

Guide-required print texts named in this unit's Further reading (Chaudhry and Kamal (2008)) are
cited at bibliographic level only and carry no content claims, so they are not listed as consulted
sources (Constitution Art. III.5).

`openstax-algebra` is a new open-access substitute for this unit, added because neither course
reading states the addition rule. No scholarly-index record was found for it, so it is not counted
towards the course's open-access floor; `lane2023` and `openstax-stats` carry the floor.
