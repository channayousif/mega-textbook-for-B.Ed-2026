## Consulted section locators

- Lane, Chapter 5, [Binomial Distribution](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/probability/binomial.html): fixed independent trials, probability formula and expected count.
- Lane, Chapter 7, [Introduction to Normal Distributions](https://onlinestatbook.com/2/normal_distribution/intro.html): symmetry, mean and standard deviation, normal-curve areas.
- OpenStax, [Section 4.3: Binomial Distribution](https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/4-3-binomial-distribution): conditions, mean and variance.
- OpenStax, [Section 6.1: The Standard Normal Distribution](https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/6-1-the-standard-normal-distribution): standardized distances and empirical-rule areas.

No print text was consulted for content claims. No source supports a clinical
classification from a score threshold; the unit explicitly distinguishes
planning estimates from diagnosis.
