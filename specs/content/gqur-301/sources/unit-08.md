# Unit 8 Sources Consulted

Per `specs/007-content-depth-standard/contracts/sources-consulted.md`. Every key cited in
`specs/content/gqur-301/coverage/unit-08.md` appears here.

Course guide: `Scheme-and-Course-guides/extracted-text/2nd 2026.txt`, GQUR-301 Chapter 8 (8.1
Permutations, 8.2 Combinations, 8.3 Applications in Problem Solving). Section locators used in
this unit: Lane Chapter 5 ("Permutations and Combinations"); OpenStax *Precalculus 2e* Section
11.5 ("Counting Principles"). Both section pages were fetched on 2026-10-21 and their content
matched the claims they support (Lane: "order counts in permutations", "unlike permutations, order
does not count" in combinations, n! as the number of orders of n items; Abramson: P(n, r) =
n! / (n - r)!, C(n, r) = P(n, r) / r!, 0! = 1, choosing r objects is not choosing n - r, and
dividing by the factorials of identical items).

Guide-required print texts named in this unit's Further reading (Chaudhry and Kamal (2008)) are
cited at bibliographic level only and carry no content claims, so they are not listed as consulted
sources (Constitution Art. III.5).

`openstax-stats` (mapped to this unit by the content-spec) was not used: *Introductory Statistics
2e* does not treat permutations or combinations, so it could not ground any claim here.
`openstax-precalc` is a new open-access substitute for this unit, used because Lane's page prints
its formulas only as images. No scholarly-index record was found for it, so it is not counted
towards the course's open-access floor; `lane2023` carries the floor.
