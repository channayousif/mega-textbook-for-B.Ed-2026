# Unit 9 bound reading notes: NIST/SEMATECH

NIST/SEMATECH. (n.d.). *e-Handbook of Statistical Methods*, Section 1.3.6, Probability Distributions. https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda36.htm

Read 2026-10-21. The section explains that statistical procedures based on distributional assumptions require checking the suitability of those assumptions for the data. Supports U9-02's limited methodological point: do not use a model-based shortcut before checking its assumptions. It does not provide the school rain/transport numbers or establish independence for them. The unit's conditional-probability calculation is grounded in OpenStax Section 3.3, not in this NIST overview. Distribution families are reserved for later units.
