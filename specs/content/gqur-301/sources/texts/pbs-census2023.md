These are the five rounded shares reported in that paragraph, not classroom
marks or school-age population shares. This excerpt supports Unit 6 assessment
ERQ 5 only. Mean, range and SD in its marking guidance are author calculations
on these five observations. The report does not support inferring school-age
populations, enrolment or school resource needs from these shares alone.
