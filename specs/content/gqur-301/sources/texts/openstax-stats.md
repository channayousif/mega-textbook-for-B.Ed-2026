# Bound excerpt - openstax-stats

Illowsky, B., et al. (2020). *Introductory Statistics 2e.* OpenStax, Rice University.
https://openstax.org/details/books/introductory-statistics-2e

Open access (CC BY 4.0). OpenAlex-verified 2026-10-09.

## Section 1.1: Definitions of Statistics, Probability, and Key Terms (verified)

Section 1.1 defines data, distinguishes qualitative data from quantitative
data, and further divides quantitative data into discrete and continuous. It
defines statistics as "the science of collecting, analyzing, organizing and
presenting data" and introduces the terms population, sample, parameter and
statistic.

## Section 1.2: Data, Sampling, and Variation (verified)

Section 1.2 covers data classification, sampling techniques and common
biases in data collection. It distinguishes primary data (collected
directly by the researcher) from secondary data (collected by someone
else), and discusses the trade-offs of each.

## Sections 1.3-1.4: Frequency, Frequency Tables, and Levels of Measurement (verified)

Sections 1.3 and 1.4 cover frequency, relative frequency, cumulative
frequency, and the construction of frequency tables. Section 2 covers
graphical representations including bar charts, histograms and pie charts.

## What this supports in GQUR-301 Unit 1

- Topic 1.1: data-type classification (qualitative vs quantitative,
  discrete vs continuous).
- Topic 1.2: primary and secondary sources of data.
- Topic 1.3: frequency distributions and cumulative frequency.
- Topic 1.4: bar charts, histograms, pie charts and when each is
  appropriate.
