# Unit 15 bound reading notes: NIST/SEMATECH

NIST/SEMATECH. (n.d.). *e-Handbook of Statistical Methods*. Read 2026-11-05.

## Section 1.3.5: Quantitative Techniques

https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda35.htm

The section introduces hypothesis tests: a hypothesis test attempts to refute a specific claim
about a population, stated as a null hypothesis and an alternative hypothesis, with a test
statistic and a significance level. Supports Topic 15.1 Step 4 and Step 5.

## Section 7.1.3: What are statistical tests?

https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section1/prc13.htm

A statistical test provides a mechanism for making quantitative decisions about a process. The
conjecture is the null hypothesis; a test statistic is compared with pre-determined critical
values. Supports Topic 15.2's general framework.

## Section 1.3.6.6: Gallery of Distributions

https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda366.htm

The gallery includes the Student's t-distribution, showing its shape and its relationship to the
normal distribution. Supports Topic 15.3 "Why the thicker tails matter".
