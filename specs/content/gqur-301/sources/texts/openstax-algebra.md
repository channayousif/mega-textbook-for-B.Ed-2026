## Section 9.5: Counting Principles (verified 2026-10-21)

Section 9.5 states two counting principles. The Addition Principle: if one
event can occur in m ways and a second event with no common outcomes can
occur in n ways, then the first or second event can occur in m + n ways. Its
worked example counts dinner entrees: 2 vegetarian and 5 meat entrees give 7
options. The Multiplication Principle (also called the Fundamental Counting
Principle): if one event can occur in m ways and a second event in n ways
after the first has occurred, then the two events can occur in m x n ways.
Its worked example counts outfits: 2 skirts, 4 blouses and 2 sweaters give
2 x 4 x 2 = 16 outfits.
