Lane, D. M. (2023). *Online Statistics Education: A Multimedia Course of Study.* Rice University.
https://onlinestatbook.com/

Open access (CC BY-NC-SA). Semantic Scholar-verified 2026-10-09.

## Chapter 1: Introduction (verified)

The Online Statistics Education project describes statistics as "a
mathematical science pertaining to the collection, analysis, interpretation
and presentation of data." It defines data as "information obtained from a
sample or population" and distinguishes qualitative (categorical) data from
quantitative data (discrete and continuous). Chapter 1 introduces data types,
sources of data and basic terminology.

## Chapter 2: Graphing Distributions (verified)

Chapter 2 covers frequency distributions, histograms, bar charts, pie charts
and other graphical representations. It explains that frequency distributions
organize data into class intervals and count the number in each interval.
It distinguishes bar charts (for categorical data) from histograms (for
quantitative data grouped into intervals), noting that histogram bars touch
because the variable is continuous.
