## Section 3.1: Terminology

https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/3-1-terminology

An outcome is a result, a sample space collects all possible results, and an event collects results meeting a condition. Equally likely elementary outcomes allow event probability to be calculated by counting. Observed relative frequency need not match a theoretical value in a short run. Inclusive OR contains the overlap; AND selects the shared outcomes. Supports U9-01 and event language in U9-02.

## Section 3.2: Independent and Mutually Exclusive Events

https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/3-2-independent-and-mutually-exclusive-events

Independence concerns unchanged conditional chances. Mutual exclusivity concerns an empty overlap. The section contrasts sampling with replacement and without replacement. Supports U9-02's counter experiments and its distinction between the concepts.

## Section 3.3: Two Basic Rules of Probability

https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/3-3-two-basic-rules-of-probability

The general addition rule removes one copy of the overlap. The general multiplication rule uses a conditional second chance; the independent product is a special case. Supports U9-02. The unit's school counts are original examples, not the source's exercises.
