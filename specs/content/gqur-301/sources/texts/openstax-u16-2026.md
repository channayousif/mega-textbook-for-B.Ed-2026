## Section 12.3, paragraphs around Example 12.6 and the regression equation

https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/12-3-the-regression-equation
The section distinguishes observed y from fitted y and describes observed minus fitted as the residual. It connects the fitted line to minimising squared differences. A negative difference places an observation below the prediction. The line represents an approximate pattern, not an exact observation for every case.
