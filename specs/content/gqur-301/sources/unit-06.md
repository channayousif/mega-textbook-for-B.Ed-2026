# Unit 6 Sources Consulted

Per `specs/007-content-depth-standard/contracts/sources-consulted.md`. Every key cited in
`specs/content/gqur-301/coverage/unit-06.md` appears here.

Course guide: `Scheme-and-Course-guides/extracted-text/2nd 2026.txt`, GQUR-301 Chapter 6 (6.1 Range;
6.2 Variance and Standard Deviation; 6.3 Data Interpretation using Dispersion). Section locators used
in this unit: Lane Chapter 3 ("Measures of Variability"); OpenStax Section 2.7 ("Measures of the
Spread of the Data"); NIST/SEMATECH Section 1.3.5.6 ("Measures of Scale"). All three section pages
were re-checked on 2026-10-21 (titles matching; definitions of range, variance, standard deviation
and the n - 1 sample divisor confirmed).

The guide-required print text named in this unit's Further reading (Chatfield (1983)) is cited at
bibliographic level only and carries no content claims, so it is not listed as a consulted source
(Constitution Art. III.5).
