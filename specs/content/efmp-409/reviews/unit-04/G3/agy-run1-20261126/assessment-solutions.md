## 1. Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs)

### Independent Solutions (Derived Before Consulting Key)

- **MCQ 1**: The first word of the first revelation, Surah Al-Alaq 96:1, is:
  - *Analysis*: The first revealed word of Surah Al-Alaq is "Iqra" meaning "Read" or "Recite".
  - *Derived answer*: **B** (Read)
  - *Key*: B (matches)

- **MCQ 2**: Which instrument does Surah Al-Alaq 96:4 name as a means of teaching?
  - *Analysis*: Verse 96:4 states "Who taught by the pen" (allama bil-qalam). The pen is named.
  - *Derived answer*: **A** (The pen)
  - *Key*: A (matches)

- **MCQ 3**: Which verse asks whether those who know and those who do not know are equal?
  - *Analysis*: Surah Az-Zumar 39:9 poses the rhetorical question: "Are those who know and those who do not know equal?"
  - *Derived answer*: **B** (Surah Az-Zumar 39:9)
  - *Key*: B (matches)

- **MCQ 4**: "Seeking knowledge is obligatory upon every Muslim" is reported in:
  - *Analysis*: The hadith "Talab al-ilmi faridatun ala kulli muslim" is recorded in Sunan Ibn Majah (224).
  - *Derived answer*: **C** (Sunan Ibn Majah)
  - *Key*: C (matches)

- **MCQ 5**: The Charter of Medina was drawn up after which event?
  - *Analysis*: The Charter of Medina (Meesaq-e-Madina) was contracted following the Hijrah (migration) to Medina in 622 CE.
  - *Derived answer*: **B** (The Hijrah)
  - *Key*: B (matches)

- **MCQ 6**: A school lets two communities of pupils keep their own festivals while sharing one school council. Which principle of the Charter does this follow most directly?
  - *Analysis*: Allowing distinct faith communities to practice their religion freely while participating in shared civic governance embodies freedom of conscience.
  - *Derived answer*: **A** (Freedom of conscience)
  - *Key*: A (matches)

- **MCQ 7**: The Last Sermon was delivered at:
  - *Analysis*: Khutbat-ul-Wida (the Farewell Sermon) was delivered at the plain of Arafat in 10 AH (632 CE) during the Farewell Pilgrimage.
  - *Derived answer*: **C** (Arafat)
  - *Key*: C (matches)

- **MCQ 8**: Who were the Ashab al-Suffa?
  - *Analysis*: The Ashab al-Suffa were the companions who resided on the shaded platform beside the Prophet's Mosque in Medina, dedicated to study and worship.
  - *Derived answer*: **B** (The learners of the platform beside the Prophet's Mosque)
  - *Key*: B (matches)

- **MCQ 9**: A Class 5 teacher splits forty pupils into circles of five, gives each circle one task, and asks faster pupils to help slower ones. Which feature of As-Suffa does this follow most directly?
  - *Analysis*: Splitting pupils into small groups with peer tutoring mirrors the halaqah (small study circle) structure of As-Suffa.
  - *Derived answer*: **B** (A small study circle)
  - *Key*: B (matches)

- **MCQ 10**: A pupil says, "Islam only values religious knowledge." Which response best answers that claim from this unit?
  - *Analysis*: The first revelation commands reading without subject restriction, and the Qur'an repeatedly commands observation and reflection on the natural world and creation.
  - *Derived answer*: **A** (The first revelation commands reading, and the Qur'an asks people to reflect on creation)
  - *Key*: A (matches)
