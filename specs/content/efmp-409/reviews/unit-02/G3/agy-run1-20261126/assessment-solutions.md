## 1. Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs)

### Independent Solutions (Derived Before Consulting Key)

- **MCQ 1**: Which foundation of education asks how education has developed over time and why the past still shapes the present?
  - *Analysis*: The historical foundation asks how education developed and why past decisions affect present structures (e.g., medium of instruction, dual streams). Philosophical asks what is true/good; sociological asks how society/schooling shape each other; psychological asks how children grow and learn.
  - *Derived answer*: **D** (The historical foundation)
  - *Key*: D (matches)

- **MCQ 2**: Which sociological perspective holds that schools pass on shared culture and sort pupils into the roles society needs?
  - *Analysis*: Functionalism views society as an organism whose parts serve functions like socialization, cultural transmission, and role allocation. Conflict theory focuses on reproduction of inequality; interactionism on micro-level talk/labels; humanism is psychological.
  - *Derived answer*: **B** (The functionalist perspective)
  - *Key*: B (matches)

- **MCQ 3**: Which psychological perspective treats learning as a change in observable behaviour shaped by reinforcement?
  - *Analysis*: Behaviourism (Skinner, Pavlov) defines learning strictly as observable behavioural change conditioned by reinforcement or punishment. Constructivism deals with mental schema construction; humanism with self-actualization; social cognitivism with observational modeling.
  - *Derived answer*: **C** (The behaviourist perspective)
  - *Key*: C (matches)

- **MCQ 4**: A teacher ticks each correct sum and writes "Well done!" beside it. Which psychological perspective most directly explains why the pupils repeat the method?
  - *Analysis*: Praise and ticks act as positive operant reinforcement reinforcing the correct response, which is the foundational mechanism of behaviourism.
  - *Derived answer*: **A** (The behaviourist perspective)
  - *Key*: A (matches)

- **MCQ 5**: A government primary school in Tharparkar has one teacher for four grades and no science equipment, while a private school in Karachi has laboratories and trained teachers. Which sociological perspective best explains this difference?
  - *Analysis*: Unequal resource allocation reproducing regional and social class disparities is the hallmark of the conflict perspective (schools reproduce social inequality).
  - *Derived answer*: **C** (The conflict perspective)
  - *Key*: C (matches)

- **MCQ 6**: Which document, adopted in 1949 and now part of the Constitution as Article 2A, sets out the principles on which Pakistan's Constitution is framed?
  - *Analysis*: The Objectives Resolution was adopted on 12 March 1949 by the Constituent Assembly and inserted substantively into the 1973 Constitution via Article 2A.
  - *Derived answer*: **B** (The Objectives Resolution)
  - *Key*: B (matches)

- **MCQ 7**: Which statement best describes the difference between an ideology and a religion?
  - *Analysis*: An ideology is a shared system of ideas/commitments of a political or social group regarding identity, power, and the good life. Religion is a comprehensive tradition of sacred belief, revelation, worship, and spiritual relation to God and the cosmos.
  - *Derived answer*: **B** (An ideology is a system of shared ideas about the life a group wants, while a religion is a tradition of belief and practice oriented toward the sacred)
  - *Key*: B (matches)

- **MCQ 8**: A school's mission says it will "raise loyal citizens who serve the nation and respect all faiths". Which foundation does this serve most directly?
  - *Analysis*: Stating the kind of citizens the nation seeks to cultivate is the defining task of the ideological foundation of education.
  - *Derived answer*: **C** (The ideological foundation)
  - *Key*: C (matches)

- **MCQ 9**: In the Islamic view used in this unit, which Arabic term for education stresses knowledge that shows itself in right conduct?
  - *Analysis*: The triadic formulation of Islamic educational terms distinguishes *tarbiyah* (nurturing holistic growth), *ta'lim* (instruction/transmission of knowledge), and *ta'dib* (discipline in *adab*, meaning knowledge manifested in ethical, virtuous conduct).
  - *Derived answer*: **C** (*Ta'dib*)
  - *Key*: C (matches)

- **MCQ 10**: A school begins the day with a recitation from the Qur'an and then the national anthem. Which answer best distinguishes the two?
  - *Analysis*: Recitation of the Qur'an is an act of sacred worship grounding the Islamic foundation; the national anthem is a civic expression of national identity and sovereignty grounding the ideological foundation.
  - *Derived answer*: **C** (The recitation belongs to the Islamic foundation, and the anthem to the ideological foundation)
  - *Key*: C (matches)
