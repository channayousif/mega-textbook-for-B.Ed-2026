## Design and assessment trace

- **U1-01**: A teacher needs the foundations to see the reasons behind everyday school choices. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 1.1 activity framing; index outcome 1; ERQ 5 (own philosophy draws on the course frame).
- **U1-02**: Education is wider than schooling, and definitions differ by what they stress; the Muslim view joins knowledge to conduct. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 1.1 retrieval 1, 3, 4 and summative; MCQs 1-3; RRQs 1-3, 5; ERQ 1.
- **U1-03**: Philosophy is reasoned inquiry; its three branches each raise a classroom question. Target: Remember, Apply. Evidence: Topic 1.1 retrieval 2; MCQs 4-5; RRQ 4; ERQ 2.
- **U1-04**: A philosophy reaches the classroom through aims, curriculum, method and roles, and classroom results test it. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 1.2 retrieval 3 and summative; MCQs 6-7; RRQs 6, 10; ERQs 2, 3, 5.
- **U1-05**: Education spans formal, non-formal and informal settings across a life; philosophy matters to teachers in practical ways. Target: Understand. Evidence: MCQ 8; RRQs 7-8.
- **U1-06**: Education has five competing aims; examinations are a tool, not an aim. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 1.2 retrieval 1, 2, 4, activity and summative; MCQs 9-10; RRQ 9; ERQs 3, 4, 5.

MCQs 1-5, RRQs 1-5 and ERQs 1-2 sample Topic 1.1; MCQs 6-10, RRQs 6-10 and ERQs 3-4 sample Topic 1.2; ERQ 5 integrates both. ERQ 4 is the blueprint's Analyze item on three statements by Pakistani policy-makers.
