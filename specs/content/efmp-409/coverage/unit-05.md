## Design and assessment trace

- **U5-01**: A report advises and has no force of law; a policy is adopted and meant to be applied. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 5.1 retrieval 1, 2 and activity; index outcome 1; MCQ 1, 2, 3; RRQ 1; ERQ 4.
- **U5-02**: Terms of reference, evidence, consultation and approval frame a document. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 5.1 retrieval 5 and summative; MCQ 5; RRQ 2; ERQ 1.
- **U5-03**: Launch is a notification, funds and offices; implementation runs to provinces, districts and schools. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 5.1 retrieval 3, 4 and summative; MCQ 4; RRQ 3; ERQ 1.
- **U5-04**: Finance, governance, capacity, continuity, data and politics hinder application. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 5.2 retrieval 1, 2, 4 and activity; MCQ 6, 7; RRQ 4; ERQ 1.
- **U5-05**: The Sharif Commission recommended a twelve-year structure, compulsory primary education, a national-language medium, examination reform, better teachers and expanded technical and women's education. Target: Remember, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 5.2 retrieval 3 and summative; MCQ 5; RRQ 5, 6; ERQ 2.
- **U5-06**: The 1979 policy set Islamic and national aims with an implementation programme; its record is judged by the machinery it built. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 5.2 retrieval 5 and summative; MCQ 9; RRQ 6; ERQ 2.
- **U5-07**: The 1998-2010 policy set universal primary education by 2010 as its cornerstone, with school-building, literacy and non-formal programmes. Target: Remember, Understand. Evidence: Topic 5.3 retrieval 1, 2 and activity; MCQ 8; RRQ 7; ERQ 3.
- **U5-08**: Later evidence shows the main literacy and enrolment targets were not met; the causes are the six implementation factors. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 5.3 retrieval 3, 4 and summative; MCQ 10; RRQ 8, 9; ERQ 3.
- **U5-09**: The case for a new policy is changing circumstances; the case against is that implementation is the constraint. Target: Evaluate. Evidence: Topic 5.3 retrieval 5 and summative; RRQ 10; ERQ 3, 5.
