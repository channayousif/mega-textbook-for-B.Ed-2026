## Design and assessment trace

- **U2-01**: Philosophy asks for reasons; an ideology is a shared commitment about who a nation wants to become. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 2.1 retrieval 2 and activity; index outcomes 1-2; RRQ 1; ERQ 5.
- **U2-02**: Functionalist, conflict and interactionist views explain different features of schooling; education also acts back on society. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 2.1 retrieval 3 and summative; MCQs 2, 5; RRQs 2, 3; ERQ 1.
- **U2-03**: Behaviourist, constructivist, social cognitivist and humanist perspectives describe how every pupil learns. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 2.1 retrieval 4 and summative; MCQs 3, 4; RRQ 4; ERQ 2.
- **U2-04**: The historical foundation explains present arrangements as the results of past decisions. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 2.1 retrieval 5 and summative; MCQ 1; RRQ 5.
- **U2-05**: Ideology and religion differ in source, scope and role, though they overlap in Pakistan. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 2.2 retrieval 1, 2 and activity; MCQs 7, 10; RRQs 6, 10; ERQ 3.
- **U2-06**: The Ideology of Pakistan runs from the Two-Nation Theory and the Lahore Resolution to the Objectives Resolution and Article 25A. Target: Remember, Apply. Evidence: Topic 2.2 retrieval 3, 4 and summative; MCQs 6, 8; RRQs 7, 9; ERQ 4.
- **U2-07**: The Islamic foundation asks that knowledge be sought as a duty and that it shape conduct. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 2.2 retrieval 5 and summative; MCQ 9; RRQ 8; ERQ 4.

MCQs 1-5 and RRQs 1-5 sample Topic 2.1; MCQs 6-10 and RRQs 6-10 sample Topic 2.2. ERQs 1-2 sample Topic 2.1, ERQs 3-4 sample Topic 2.2, and ERQ 5 is the blueprint's integrative Analyze item that sorts ten features of a Pakistani school by the foundation each reflects.
