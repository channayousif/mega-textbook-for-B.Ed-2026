## Design and assessment trace

- **U3-01**: Idealism views ideas and spirit as ultimate reality; knowledge comes from intellectual reflection and values are eternal. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 3.1 retrieval 1; MCQ-01; RRQ-01.
- **U3-02**: Plato formulated idealism in *The Republic*, using Socratic dialectic and progressive educational stages for guardians. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 3.1 retrieval 2; MCQ-02; RRQ-02; ERQ-01.
- **U3-03**: Idealism shapes education toward character, intellectual discipline, liberal arts, and moral exemplars. Target: Apply. Evidence: Topic 3.1 retrieval 3, activity, and summative task; RRQ-02; ERQ-01, ERQ-05.
- **U3-04**: Aristotle held that human flourishing (*eudaimonia*) requires habituating virtue before reasoning, within state-regulated schools. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 3.2 retrieval 1; MCQ-03; RRQ-03.
- **U3-05**: Realism holds that the physical world exists independent of our minds; knowledge begins with sensory observation and inductive logic. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 3.2 retrieval 2; MCQ-04; RRQ-04.
- **U3-06**: Realist education focuses on sciences, factual evidence, demonstration, and mastering natural laws. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 3.2 retrieval 3 and summative task; RRQ-04; ERQ-01, ERQ-05.
- **U3-07**: Naturalism posits nature as the whole of reality; senses are the gateways of truth; artificial societal pressure harms growth. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 3.3 retrieval 1; MCQ-05; RRQ-05.
- **U3-08**: Rousseau proposed negative education and developmental stages in *Emile* (infancy, childhood, pre-adolescence, adolescence). Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 3.3 retrieval 2; RRQ-05; ERQ-02.
- **U3-09**: Naturalistic education prioritises child-centred discovery, sensory experience, and discipline by natural consequences. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 3.3 retrieval 3, activity, and summative task; ERQ-02, ERQ-05.
- **U3-10**: Pragmatism defines truth by workable consequences; ideas are hypotheses tested by solving real human problems. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 3.4 retrieval 1; MCQ-06; RRQ-06.
- **U3-11**: Dewey developed active learning and the reflective inquiry cycle, viewing school as a democratic community. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 3.4 retrieval 2, activity; MCQ-07; RRQ-06; ERQ-03.
- **U3-12**: Ibn Khaldun advocated gradual teaching (*at-tadrij*), mastery of habits (*malaka*), and prohibited harsh corporal punishment. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 3.4 retrieval 3; MCQ-07; RRQ-07; ERQ-03.
- **U3-13**: Pragmatic education applies project-based learning, integrated curriculum, and reflective problem-solving. Target: Apply. Evidence: Topic 3.4 summative task; RRQ-07; ERQ-03, ERQ-05.
- **U3-14**: Iqbal formulated *Khudi* (selfhood) as active, creative moral individuality developed through purposeful struggle. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 3.5 retrieval 1; MCQ-08; RRQ-08.
- **U3-15**: Iqbal's poetry uses symbols like the *Shaheen* and demands a balance between intellect (*aql*) and love/intuition (*ishq*). Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 3.5 retrieval 2, activity; MCQ-09; RRQ-09; ERQ-04.
- **U3-16**: Iqbal envisions the teacher as a creative guide (*murshid*) and the curriculum as balancing empirical science with spiritual values. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 3.5 retrieval 3 and summative task; RRQ-09; ERQ-04, ERQ-05.
- **U3-17**: Western and Muslim scholars share deep commitment to moral development, inquiry, and the vital role of the teacher. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 3.6 retrieval 1; MCQ-10; RRQ-10.
- **U3-18**: Muslim scholars integrate revelation with reason and empirical study, framing education within eternal spiritual accountability. Target: Analyze. Evidence: Topic 3.6 retrieval 2; RRQ-10; ERQ-04, ERQ-05.
- **U3-19**: A teaching philosophy statement articulates educational aims, view of learner, method, and explicit philosophical roots. Target: Create. Evidence: Topic 3.6 retrieval 3, activity, and summative task; ERQ-05 (licence CRQ 8 format).
