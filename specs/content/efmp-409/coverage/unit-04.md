## Design and assessment trace

- **U4-01**: The first revelation joins reading, creation and the pen, so knowledge is central to the Islamic mission. Target: Remember, Understand. Evidence: Topic 4.1 retrieval 1 and 3; MCQs 1-2; RRQ 1; ERQ 1.
- **U4-02**: The Qur'an calls a learner to seek knowledge and to reflect on creation, so the study of the world is invited. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 4.1 retrieval 3-4 and activity; MCQ 3; RRQs 2, 4; ERQ 1.
- **U4-03**: The Hadith honour the seeker of knowledge without naming a subject, and join learning to teaching. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 4.1 retrieval 2-3 and summative; MCQ 4; RRQ 3; ERQ 1.
- **U4-04**: The Charter of Medina organised a plural community around shared rules, religious freedom and justice, which are the values a school also needs. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 4.2 retrieval 1, 3, 5 and activity; MCQs 5-6; RRQ 5; ERQs 2, 4.
- **U4-05**: The Last Sermon is a public act of teaching that sums up the moral and social teaching of Islam. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 4.2 retrieval 2, 4 and 6; MCQ 7; RRQ 6; ERQ 4.
- **U4-06**: The Sermon's principles can be turned into classroom rules and habits without copying the historical document. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 4.2 retrieval 6 and summative; RRQ 7; ERQs 2, 4.
- **U4-07**: As-Suffa joined boarding, structured study and preparation to teach, so it is called the first institute of learning. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 4.3 retrieval 1, 3 and 5; MCQ 8; RRQ 8; ERQ 3.
- **U4-08**: Its method was a small circle of listening, memorising, questioning, example and practice. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 4.3 retrieval 3, 4 and activity; MCQ 9; RRQ 9; ERQ 3.
- **U4-09**: Its curriculum joined the sources, literacy and the duties of daily life, and some of its principles transfer to a large present-day class. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 4.3 retrieval 5 and summative; RRQ 10; ERQs 3, 5.

MCQs 1-4 and RRQs 1-4 sample Topic 4.1; MCQs 5-7 and RRQs 5-7 sample Topic 4.2; MCQs 8-10 and RRQs 8-10 sample Topic 4.3. ERQ 1 samples Topic 4.1, ERQ 2 Topic 4.2, ERQ 3 Topic 4.3 (the blueprint's Analyze item comparing As-Suffa with a present-day primary classroom), and ERQs 4-5 integrate across the unit.
