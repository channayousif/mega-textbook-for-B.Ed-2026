# Bound source evidence: pak-constitution-1973

Source: Government of Pakistan, National Assembly. (2012). *The Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan* (as amended to 28 February 2012; original work adopted 1973).
URL: https://na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1333523681_951.pdf

Consulted: 2026-11-25. PDF retrieved from the National Assembly (the host required certificate verification to be disabled) and its text extracted; the passages below are copied from that extraction. Quotations are under 15 words. The same official copy is bound by `gpks-402`.

## Evidence notes

1. Article 2: "Islam shall be the State religion of Pakistan." Context for the ideological and Islamic foundations (U2-06).
2. Article 2A, heading: "The Objectives Resolution to form part of substantive provisions"; the article makes the principles and provisions of the Objectives Resolution "substantive part of the Constitution". Supports the Objectives Resolution as constitutional text (U2-05, U2-06).
3. Annex (Article 2A), the Objectives Resolution: "sovereignty over the entire universe belongs to Allah Almighty alone"; authority is "a sacred trust"; "the principles of democracy, freedom, equality, tolerance and social justice as enunciated by Islam shall be fully observed"; "adequate provision shall be made for the minorities to [freely] profess and practise their religions and develop their cultures". Supports the Objectives Resolution summary (U2-05, U2-06).
4. Article 25A: "The State shall provide free and compulsory education to all children of the age of five to sixteen years in such manner as may be determined by law." Supports the right to education and the worked example (U2-02, U2-06).
5. Article 31(1): "Steps shall be taken to enable the Muslims of Pakistan, individually and collectively, to order their lives in accordance with the fundamental principles and basic concepts of Islam". Supports the Islamic foundation in national aims (U2-06, U2-07).
6. Article 227(1): "All existing laws shall be brought in conformity with the Injunctions of Islam as laid down in the Holy Quran and Sunnah". Supports the constitutional reference to the Qur'an and the Sunnah (U2-07).
