## Evidence notes

1. Preface, paragraph 1: the Commission on National Education was appointed by a government resolution adopted on 30 December 1958. Supports the date and legal form of the commission (U5-02).
2. Preface, paragraph 2: the Commission was inaugurated by President Mohammad Ayub Khan on 5 January 1959, who asked for a reorganisation and reorientation of the system so that it reflected spiritual, moral and cultural values and met the needs of agriculture, science and technology. Supports the terms of reference (U5-02).
3. Preface, paragraphs 4-5: an exhaustive questionnaire was distributed to institutions and individuals; the Commission met educationists and public figures in East and West Pakistan and held discussions in educational institutions. Supports the consultation stage of framing (U5-02).
4. Chapter 5 (Primary Education), paragraph 8: the first goal should be a five-year universal and compulsory course achieved in ten years, and the second goal an eight-year compulsory course within fifteen years. Supports the compulsory-education recommendation (U5-05).
5. Chapter 5, paragraph 19: the age of admission should be five. Supports the primary-school entry age (U5-05).
6. Chapter 5, paragraph 21: the objectives of primary education include developing all aspects of the child's personality, equipping the child with basic knowledge and skills, awakening citizenship and love of country, and laying the foundation of desirable attitudes. Supports the aims of primary education (U5-05).
7. Higher Education summary, item III.3: the courses of study for the Bachelor's degree in Arts and Science should be extended from two to three years. Supports the degree-length recommendation (U5-05).
8. Higher Education summary, item I: intermediate classes should be transferred from the universities to the boards of secondary education. Supports the restructuring recommendation (U5-05).
9. Higher Education summary, item II.1: admission to universities should follow completion of the present intermediate stage covering twelve years of study. Supports the twelve-year pre-university structure (U5-05).
10. Higher Education summary, item V.2: the award of degrees should be based on a final comprehensive examination (75 percent of marks) and a record of periodic tests and class work (25 percent). Supports the examination recommendation (U5-05).
11. Medium of Instruction, paragraph 14: national languages should gradually and progressively replace English, and Urdu is already the medium of instruction at the primary stage. Supports the medium-of-instruction recommendation (U5-05).
