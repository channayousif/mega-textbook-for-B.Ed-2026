## Evidence notes

1. Course rationale and Unit 1 overview (lines 928-955): the course relates education to philosophy and widens student teachers' understanding of education; the unit introduces the meaning and basic concepts of education. Supports the course introduction (U1-01) and the list of foundations (philosophical, sociological, psychological, ideological, historical) that the 2026 guide's course description also names.
2. Week 1 lecture notes, "Definition of education" (lines 1355-1385): quotes Kneller (1971, pp. 20-21): in its broad sense education is any act or experience with a formative effect on mind, character or physical ability; in its technical sense it is the process by which society, through schools, colleges and universities, deliberately transmits its cultural heritage. Quotes Pestalozzi: education is "a natural, harmonious and progressive development of man's innate powers". Supports the narrow and broad meanings and the Pestalozzi definition (U1-02).
3. "Education in Islam" (lines 1386-1400): seeking knowledge is a duty for every Muslim; the first revealed word was "Iqra"; cites Az-Zumar 39:9. Supports the Muslim view (U1-02).
4. "The nature of education" (lines 1480-1505): education is lifelong, systematic, the development of individual and society; Dewey's view of education as a continuous reconstruction of experience in a socially desirable way. Supports the Dewey summary (U1-02).
5. Week 2 Session 2, "Aims of education" (lines 1576-1700): good citizens, transferring values, education as social change agent, education for vocational preparation, education for individual development, Islamic aims of education (balance of religious and social life, brotherhood and tolerance), modern aims. Supports grouping aims into individual, social, vocational, moral and spiritual (U1-06).
