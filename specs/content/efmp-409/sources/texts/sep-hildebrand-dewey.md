## Evidence notes

1. Pragmatism and instrumentalism: Reality is an evolving process of interaction between organism and environment. Ideas are hypotheses or instruments for action; their truth is validated by their practical consequences in resolving problematic situations. Supports U3-10 (pragmatism).
2. Education as reconstruction of experience: In *Democracy and Education* (1916), Dewey defines education as the continuous reconstruction or reorganization of experience, which adds to the meaning of experience and increases ability to direct future experience. Education is a present living process, not merely preparation for future living. Supports U3-11 (Dewey and active learning).
3. The reflective inquiry cycle: Learning occurs through an active five-phase inquiry cycle: encountering an authentic problem or doubt, intellectualizing the problem, gathering data and forming hypotheses, acting or experimenting, and reflecting on the consequences to reach revised understanding. Supports U3-11 and U3-13.
4. Pragmatic curriculum and democratic classroom: Subject matter is organized around student problems and social occupations rather than rigid academic compartments. The classroom is a collaborative, democratic community where pupils learn cooperation, critical thinking, and social responsibility. Supports U3-13.
