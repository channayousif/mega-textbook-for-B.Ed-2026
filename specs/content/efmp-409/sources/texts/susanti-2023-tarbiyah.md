## Evidence notes

1. Abstract: the term tarbiyah is "focused on guidance and fostering morals". Supports the meaning of tarbiyah as nurturing the whole growth of a person, including character (U1-02).
2. Abstract: for ta'lim "the emphasis is on conveying complete and correct knowledge". Supports the meaning of ta'lim (U1-02).
3. Abstract and body: ta'dib, from the same root as adab, emphasises mastery of knowledge that produces right action; the article attributes the ta'dib concept of education to Syed Muhammad Naquib al-Attas. Supports the meaning of ta'dib and the al-Attas attribution (U1-02).
4. Abstract: the three concepts "form a unified whole" in the educational process. Supports the statement that the Muslim view does not stop at knowledge (U1-02).
