Source: World Bank. *World Development Indicators*. Pakistan, adult literacy rate, total (ages 15 and above), indicator SE.ADT.LITR.ZS; and school enrollment, primary, gross (% of primary school age population), indicator SE.PRM.ENRR. Retrieved 2026-11-25.
URL: https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/PAK/indicator/SE.ADT.LITR.ZS?format=json ; https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/PAK/indicator/SE.PRM.ENRR?format=json

Consulted: 2026-11-25. The two indicator series were queried through the World Bank's public API and the annual values for 2000-2012 were read. The API returns national estimates compiled by the UNESCO Institute for Statistics.

## Evidence notes

1. Adult literacy rate, ages 15 and above: 2005 = 49.87 percent, 2008 = 55.53 percent, 2010 = 55.38 percent, 2011 = 54.74 percent, 2012 = 56.76 percent. Supports the comparison with the 1998-2010 policy's 70 percent literacy target for 2010 and its 55 percent first-five-year target (U5-08).
2. Gross primary enrolment ratio: 2005 = 71.95 percent, 2008 = 72.64 percent, 2009 = 72.93 percent, 2010 = 73.64 percent, 2011 = 70.44 percent. Supports the comparison with the 1998-2010 policy's universal primary education target for 2010 (U5-08).
3. The two series use different measures (adult literacy over 15, and gross primary enrolment), so they are not directly interchangeable with the policy's own "participation rate". The unit therefore reports them as later evidence on the policy's targets, not as the policy's own measure (U5-08).
