## Evidence notes

1. Section 1.1, "Historical Origins of Philosophy": a philosopher is a "lover or pursuer (philia) of wisdom (sophia)". Supports the word origin of philosophy (U1-03).
2. Chapter 6 introduction (Metaphysics): metaphysics is concerned with "identifying that which is real". Supports the metaphysics definition (U1-03).
3. Chapter 7 introduction (Epistemology): epistemology studies "the nature and extent of human knowledge". Supports the epistemology definition (U1-03).
4. Chapter 8 introduction (Value Theory): value theory is the "philosophical investigation of values", including ethical, social, political, religious and aesthetic values. The page does not use the word "axiology"; the unit gives axiology as the older name for value theory, which is how the HEC Foundations guide uses it ("the axiology of education"). Supports the axiology branch and its ethics and aesthetics parts (U1-03).
