# Bound source evidence: pie-1979-policy

Source: Government of Pakistan. (1979). *National Education Policy and Implementation Programme, 1979*. Ministry of Education, Islamabad. Official copy listed by the Pakistan Institute of Education.
URL: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1w0BY3bjsDABvZYF_mqzQ_czCzqu_Xr5I/view

Consulted: 2026-11-25. PDF downloaded from the PIE listing (120 pages, OCR text layer) and the foreword, the aims of education and the implementation strategy read. Quotations are under 15 words.

## Evidence notes

1. Foreword, paragraph 1: educational policies reflect the aspirations of a nation and embody principles of action derived from philosophic choices. Supports the definition of a policy as a statement of intent (U5-01).
2. Foreword, paragraph 2: President General Mohammad Zia-ul-Haq convened a National Education Conference soon after assuming office as Chief Martial Law Administrator; the salient features of the National Education Policy were announced by the Minister for Education in October 1978. Supports the framing of the policy through a conference (U5-02).
3. Foreword, paragraph 4: the Draft Work Plan was presented to the cabinet in December 1978 and examined by a cabinet-appointed Standing Committee; the document contains the approved implementation programme. Supports the approval stage (U5-02).
4. Foreword, signature block: the foreword is by Dr. M. A. Kazi, Education Secretary, dated 14 February 1979. Supports the date and office of the policy (U5-02).
5. National Aims of Education, aim (a): to foster in the hearts and minds of the people a deep consciousness of Islam and of the ideology of Pakistan. Supports the Islamic and ideological aim (U5-06).
6. National Aims of Education, aim (e): to provide and ensure equal educational opportunities to all citizens and to provide minorities with adequate facilities for their cultural and religious development. Supports the equity aim (U5-06).
7. National Aims of Education, aim (g): to provide a minimum acceptable level of functional literacy and fundamental education to all citizens, particularly the young. Supports the literacy aim (U5-06).
8. National Aims of Education, aim (i): to promote and strengthen scientific, vocational and technological education, training and research. Supports the science and vocational aim (U5-06).
9. Implementation Strategy, item 1: the highest priority will be assigned to the revision of curricula with a view to reorganising the entire content around Islamic thought. Supports the curriculum strategy (U5-06).
10. Implementation Strategy, item 3: the national language will be used as the medium of instruction to strengthen the ideological foundation of the nation. Supports the medium-of-instruction strategy (U5-06).
11. Table of Contents: the policy has chapters on primary education, mosque schools, mohalla schools, village workshop schools, female education, adult and non-formal education, secondary education, technical and vocational education, madrasah education, teacher education, evaluation and examinations, and financing of the policy. Supports the breadth of the policy and its institution types (U5-06).
