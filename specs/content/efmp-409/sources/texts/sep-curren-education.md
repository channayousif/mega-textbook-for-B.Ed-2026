## Evidence notes

1. Section 1, "The Object of Inquiry and Most Basic Questions": characterises education as "the promotion of desirable forms of learning and personal development". Supports the Curren definition (U1-02).
2. Section 1: philosophy of education is concerned with philosophical questions about education and with "larger questions of education policy" and the roles of educational institutions. Supports the scope of philosophy of education (U1-05).
3. Table of contents: sections on the nature and aims of education, the content of education, manner and methods (instructional, disciplinary, administrative practices and policy), authority to educate, and educational responsibilities including adequacy, equality and justice. Supports the list of questions in the scope of philosophy of education (U1-05).
4. Section 2.3 (civic, social and economic aims): philosophical justifications of educational aims have clear significance for the content, manner and methods of education. Supports the relationship between philosophy and educational practice (U1-04).
5. Section 2 subsections: 2.1 formative aims (character and virtue), 2.2 student-centered aims (well-being, autonomy), 2.3 social, civic and economic aims. Supports the note that thinkers group aims differently (U1-06).
6. Section 1: the entry asks which knowledge is worth teaching and how a tradition judges it, which frames the unit's question about what the Qur'an and Hadith value in knowledge. Supports the analysis of which kind of knowledge a school rule values (U4-02).
