# Bound source evidence: etymonline-education

Source: Online Etymology Dictionary. (n.d.). Education. https://www.etymonline.com/word/education

Consulted: 2026-11-25. Quotations are under 15 words.

## Evidence notes

1. The entry derives "education" from Latin *educationem* (nominative *educatio*) "a rearing, training". Supports the word origin (U1-02).
2. It gives *educare* "bring up, rear, educate", and relates it to *educere* "bring out, lead forth", from *ex-* "out" + *ducere* "to lead". Supports the two Latin roots and the two pictures of education (U1-02).
3. Earliest English sense, 1530s: "child-rearing". Supports the link between education and bringing up a child (U1-02).
