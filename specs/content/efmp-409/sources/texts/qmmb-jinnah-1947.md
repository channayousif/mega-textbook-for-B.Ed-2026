# Bound source evidence: qmmb-jinnah-1947

Source: Quaid-i-Azam Mazar Management Board (Government of Pakistan). (n.d.). Quaid's quotes, "Education". https://qmmb.gov.pk/quotes-full.html

Consulted: 2026-11-25. The page attributes the quotation to the Quaid-e-Azam's message to the All Pakistan Educational Conference at Karachi, 27 November 1947. Quotations are under 15 words each.

## Evidence notes

1. The quoted message says there is an "immediate and urgent need" for training people in scientific and technical education "in order to build up our economic life", and that people should undertake science, commerce, trade and well-planned industries. Supports the worked example and ERQ 4 Statement A (U1-06).
2. The quoted message adds that "greater attention should be paid to technical and vocational education". Supports reading the statement as stressing the vocational aim (U1-06).
3. Limitation: the PIE scan of the conference proceedings (https://www.pie.gov.pk/SiteImage/Publication/1947.pdf) has no text layer and was not checked line by line; the unit therefore quotes only the lines the official QMMB page gives, and notes that the quoted lines do not show the whole message.
