## Evidence notes

1. Occasion: Muhammad Ali Jinnah, presidential address to the All-India Muslim League, Lahore session, March 1940. Supports the citation line (U2-06).
2. On Muslims as a nation: "The Mussalmans are not a minority. The Mussalmans are a nation by any definition." Supports the claim that Jinnah declared the Muslims a nation (U2-06).
3. On homelands: "Mussalmans are a nation according to any definition of a nation, and they must have their homelands". Supports the demand for homelands (U2-06).
4. On two nations: "The Hindus and Muslims belong to two different religious philosophies, social customs, and literature[s]." Supports the two-nation framing (U2-06).
5. The transcription retains the source's occasional typographical errors; quotations above are reproduced as printed (U2-06).
