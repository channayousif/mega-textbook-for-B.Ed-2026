# Bound source evidence: quran-translation

Source: *The Holy Qur'an*, Arabic text with English and Urdu translations, Quran.com.
URLs: https://quran.com/96 (Surah Al-Alaq, verses 1-5) ; https://quran.com/39/9 (Surah Az-Zumar, verse 9)

Consulted: 2026-11-25. Verse meanings are paraphrased; no single translator's wording is quoted.

## Evidence notes

1. Surah Al-Alaq 96:1-5: the passage opens with the command to read (*Iqra*) in the name of the Lord who created, and says that He taught by the pen and taught people what they did not know. Widely accepted as the first revelation. Supports the Muslim view of education (U1-02). The HEC Foundations guide (lines 1386-1390) makes the same point.
2. Surah Az-Zumar 39:9: asks whether those who know and those who do not know are equal. Supports the value placed on knowledge (U1-02).
