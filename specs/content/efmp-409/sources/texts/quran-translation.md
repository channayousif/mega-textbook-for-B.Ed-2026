# Bound source evidence: quran-translation

Source: *The Holy Qur'an*, Arabic text with English and Urdu translations, Quran.com.
URLs: https://quran.com/96 (Surah Al-Alaq, verses 1-5) ; https://quran.com/39/9 (Surah Az-Zumar, verse 9)

Consulted: 2026-11-25. Verse meanings are paraphrased; no single translator's wording is quoted.

## Evidence notes

1. Surah Al-Alaq 96:1-5: the passage opens with the command to read (*Iqra*) in the name of the Lord who created, and says that He taught by the pen and taught people what they did not know. Widely accepted as the first revelation. Supports the Muslim view of education (U1-02). The HEC Foundations guide (lines 1386-1390) makes the same point.
2. Surah Az-Zumar 39:9: asks whether those who know and those who do not know are equal. Supports the value placed on knowledge (U1-02).
3. Surah Al-Alaq 96:1-5, read again for Unit 4: the command to read (*iqra*) in the name of the Lord who created; man created from a clinging clot; the Lord most Generous; God taught by the pen; God taught humanity what it did not know. Supports the explanation of the first revelation and its educational ideas (U4-01).
4. Surah Al-Mujadilah 58:11: God will raise those who believe and those who have been given knowledge by degrees. Supports the link between knowledge and rank (U4-02).
5. Surah Taha 20:114: the prayer to be increased in knowledge. Supports the call to keep seeking (U4-02).
6. Surah Al-Baqarah 2:269: wisdom is given to whom God wills, and the recipient receives an overflowing benefit. Supports the distinction between stored knowledge and used wisdom (U4-02).
7. Surah Al-Ghashiyah 88:17-20: the questions about looking at the camels, the sky and the mountains. Supports the Qur'anic call to observe and reflect (U4-02).
