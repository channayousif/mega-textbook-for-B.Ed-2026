## Evidence notes

1. Occasion: Muhammad Ali Jinnah, presidential address to the Constituent Assembly of Pakistan, 11 August 1947. Supports the citation line (U2-05).
2. Freedom of worship: "You are free; you are free to go to your temples" and "you are free to go to your mosques or to any other place or worship in this State of Pakistan." Supports the freedom-of-worship point (U2-05).
3. Equal citizenship: "we are all citizens, and equal citizens, of one State". Supports the equal-citizenship point (U2-05).
4. Religion and the business of the state: "you may belong to any religion or caste or creed - that has nothing to do with the business of the State." Supports the distinction between religious identity and citizenship (U2-05).
5. The address also states that "the first duty of a government is to maintain law and order". Not used in the unit's prose, recorded here for scope (U2-05).
