## Notes

- `hec-2013-foundations` is read from the repository's bundled extraction (`Scheme-and-Course-guides/extracted-text/course-guides-2025/FoundationsEd_Sept13.txt`), which the content spec records as identical to the public PDF. Kneller (1971) and Pestalozzi are quoted from its Week 1 lecture notes, not from their original works.
- The PIE scan of the 1947 conference proceedings (https://www.pie.gov.pk/SiteImage/Publication/1947.pdf) was downloaded but has no text layer, so the Jinnah wording is taken from the official QMMB page.
- Qur'an verses are paraphrased; no single translator's wording is quoted.
