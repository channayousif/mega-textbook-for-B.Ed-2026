## Notes

- The HEC guide's Unit 4 outline (lines 1036-1055) names the same topics this unit teaches, and its "Education in Islam" notes (lines 1384-1400) supply the three short sayings on seeking knowledge. The guide gives the outline, not the detail; the detail is drawn from the bound sources above.
- The Qur'an verses are paraphrased; no single translator's wording is quoted. Verse meanings follow the reading recorded in `sources/texts/quran-translation.md`.
- The hadith reportings in Topic 4.1 are cited by collection and number and paraphrased. The HEC guide's wording is used for the three short sayings, which the guide attributes to the Prophet (peace be upon him).
- The Farooq article's full text was not retrievable at the date above; its Crossref abstract was read, and the note records only what the abstract supports.
- The Sani and Shaid article is in Arabic with an English title and abstract; the abstract was read, the body was not.
- `besari-2023-knowledge-ethics` is bound for the single point that seeking knowledge is joined to ethics and conduct; the full text was not read.
