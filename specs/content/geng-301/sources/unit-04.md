## Open-access binding note

The content-spec maps `UNESCO2022` and `openstax2021` section 11.3 (Robinson & Jerskey, *Writing Guide
with Handbook*, OpenStax, 2021) as Unit 4 open-access readings. `openstax2021` section 11.3 URL resolved
on 2026-10-19; however, the OpenStax reuse terms bar ingesting the book into large language models or
generative AI offerings without prior written permission, and `UNESCO2022` is a statistical report on
gender parity rather than an exposition of rhetorical modes. The unit's open-access floor is met by
`writing2015` instead (chapter 10, "Rhetorical Modes", accessible mirror resolved HTTP 200), bound to
U4-03, U4-04, U4-05 and U4-06 above. `openstax2021` and `UNESCO2022` remain mapped readings in the
content-spec; aligning the spec's mapping is an owner decision, since the spec is approved and frozen.
