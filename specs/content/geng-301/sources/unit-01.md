## Unverifiable sources

The following sources are print monographs recommended by the course guide. No open-access
excerpt could be bound; they are cited by reference only per Constitution Art. III.5.

- `graff2011`: Graff & Birkenstein (2011), *They Say / I Say*. Covers expository writing modes and purpose.
- `williams2017`: Williams & Bizup (2017), *Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace*. Covers clarity and coherence.
- `strunk2000`: Strunk & White (2000), *The Elements of Style*. Covers organization and paragraph construction.
- `axelrod2016`: Axelrod & Cooper (2016), *The St. Martin's Guide to Writing*. Covers paragraph structure, unity and completeness.

## Open-access binding note

The content-spec maps `openstax2021` sections 6.7 and 10.6 (Robinson & Jerskey, *Writing Guide
with Handbook*, OpenStax, 2021) as Unit 1's open-access reading. Both section URLs resolved on
2026-10-19. Section 6.7 is an evaluation rubric for a proposal assignment, not an exposition of
clarity or coherence, and the OpenStax reuse terms bar ingesting the book into language models
without permission, so no agent-written excerpt of it is bound. The unit's open-access floor is
met by `writing2015` instead, bound to U1-06 and U1-07 above. `openstax2021` remains a mapped
further reading in the content-spec; aligning the spec's mapping is an owner decision, since the
spec is approved and frozen.
