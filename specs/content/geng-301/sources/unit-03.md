### Bound Unit 3 excerpts

- Unit 3, verbatim excerpts recorded in the earlier 2026-11-03 repair and rechecked 2026-11-05 from the same chapter page (CC BY-NC-SA):
  - Section 9.1 (thesis, U3-03): "A sentence that presents the controlling idea of an essay." and "A thesis statement must concentrate on a specific area of a general topic." (anchor `#fresh-ch09_s01`)
  - Section 9.2 (body paragraphs, U3-05): "If your thesis gives the reader a roadmap to your essay, then body paragraphs should closely follow that map." (anchor `#fresh-ch09_s02`)
  - Section 9.4 (introduction, U3-02): "As you move deeper into your introduction, you gradually narrow the focus, moving closer to your thesis." (anchor `#fresh-ch09_s04`)
  - Section 9.4 (conclusion, U3-08): "restate your thesis at the beginning of your conclusion" and "Introducing new material in your conclusion has an unsettling effect on your reader." (anchor `#fresh-ch09_s04`)
  - Scope note: section 9.1 also lists "Ability to be argued" as a quality of a strong thesis. Unit 3 follows the approved spec (U3-03, U3-04: clear, focused, specific in scope) and does not adopt arguability as a universal requirement.
