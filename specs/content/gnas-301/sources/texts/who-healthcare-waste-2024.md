## Key facts (verified, excerpt)

- About 85% of waste from health-care activities is general, non-hazardous waste comparable
  to household waste; the remaining 15% is hazardous material that may be infectious,
  toxic, carcinogen, flammable, corrosive, reactive, explosive or radioactive.
- Roughly 16 billion injections are given worldwide each year, and not all needles and
  syringes are safely discarded afterward.
- Waste categories: infectious, pathological, sharps, chemical, pharmaceutical/cytotoxic,
  radioactive, and non-hazardous/general waste.
- WHO recommends waste minimisation and segregation, and alternatives to incineration where
  feasible (autoclaving, microwaving, steam treatment, chemical treatment).
- In 2021, only 61% of hospitals had basic health-care waste services.
