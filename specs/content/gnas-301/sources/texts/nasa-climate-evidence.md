# Bound excerpt - nasa-climate-evidence

NASA Science. (2024). *Evidence* (climate change page, updated 23 October 2024).
https://science.nasa.gov/climate-change/evidence

Open access (NASA publisher page). Verified by direct fetch on 2026-09-23; the page's key
statements were read and are excerpted below.

## Key statements (verified, excerpt)

- There is unequivocal evidence that Earth is warming at an unprecedented rate; human
  activity is the principal cause.
- Global average surface temperature has risen about 2 degrees Fahrenheit (about 1 degree
  Celsius) since the late 19th century, with most warming in the past 40 years.
- Human-caused carbon dioxide is increasing about 250 times faster than natural increases
  after the last ice age.
- Greenland and Antarctica are losing about 279 and 148 billion tons of ice per year
  respectively; global sea level rose about 8 inches (about 20 centimetres) in the last
  century.
