## Abstract (verified, excerpt)

Background: Exposure to dust in textile mills adversely affects workers' health. We
collected epidemiological data on textile workers suffering from respiratory diseases and
assessed work absence associated with illnesses in Faisalabad, Pakistan. Methods: We
recruited 206 workers using multistage sampling from 11 spinning mills in Faisalabad,
Pakistan. The data was collected using 2-week health diaries and face-to-face interviews.
