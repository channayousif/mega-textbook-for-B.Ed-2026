## Overview (verified, excerpt)

- Title: "WHO global air quality guidelines", covering particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10),
  ozone, nitrogen dioxide, sulfur dioxide and carbon monoxide.
- Published 22 September 2021; updates guidelines issued roughly 15 years earlier.
- The overview notes the evidence now shows air pollution affects different aspects of
  health at even lower concentrations than previously understood, so the recommended levels
  were tightened.

## What this supports in GNAS-301 Unit 3

Topic 3.1 (U3-01) cites this source as the yardstick the control strategies aim for: the
WHO's 2021 guideline levels for PM2.5, PM10, ozone, nitrogen dioxide, sulfur dioxide and
carbon monoxide, and the fact that they were tightened because harm occurs at lower
concentrations than previously understood. The unit does not rely on this source for any
specific numeric guideline level, which it does not state in prose.
