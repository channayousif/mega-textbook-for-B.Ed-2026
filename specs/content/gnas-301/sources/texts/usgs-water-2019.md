## Key figures (verified, excerpt)

- The oceans hold about 96.5 percent of all Earth's water, and over 96 percent of the
  world's total water supply is saline.
- Only about 2.5 percent of Earth's water is fresh.
- Of that freshwater, more than 68 percent is locked up in ice caps, glaciers and permanent
  snow; roughly 30 percent is groundwater.
- Rivers make up only about 1/10,000th of one percent of total water.
