# Bound excerpt - who-sanitation-2024

World Health Organization. (2024). *Sanitation* (fact sheet, 22 March 2024).
https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/sanitation

Open access (WHO publisher page). Verified by direct fetch from who.int on 2026-09-23; the
page's "Key facts" section was read and is excerpted below.

## Key facts (verified, excerpt)

- In 2022, 57% of the global population (4.6 billion people) used a safely managed
  sanitation service.
- Over 1.5 billion people still lack basic sanitation services.
- In 2020, 44% of household wastewater globally was discharged without safe treatment.
- 1.4 million deaths per year are attributable to inadequate drinking-water, sanitation and
  hygiene.
