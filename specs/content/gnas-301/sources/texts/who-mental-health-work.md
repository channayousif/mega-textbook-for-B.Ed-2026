## Key facts (verified, excerpt)

- Decent work is good for mental health; poor working environments, discrimination,
  excessive workloads, low job control and job insecurity pose mental health risks.
- 16% of working-age adults were estimated to have a mental disorder in 2023.
- An estimated 12 billion working days are lost annually to depression and anxiety,
  costing US$ 1 trillion per year in lost productivity.
