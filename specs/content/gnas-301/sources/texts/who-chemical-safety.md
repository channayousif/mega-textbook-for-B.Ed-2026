# Bound excerpt - who-chemical-safety

World Health Organization. *Chemical safety* (WHO health topic page).
https://www.who.int/health-topics/chemical-safety

Open access (WHO publisher page). Verified by direct fetch from who.int on 2026-09-23; the
page's overview was read and is excerpted below.

## Overview (verified, excerpt)

- Chemical safety means conducting all chemical-related activities so that human health
  and the environment are protected; it spans all chemicals, natural and manufactured,
  and every exposure situation, from natural presence through extraction, production,
  transport, use and disposal.
- WHO works on this via the International Programme on Chemical Safety (IPCS).
- Chemical production and use keep growing, especially in developing countries, raising
  risks without sound management.
- WHO highlights 10 chemicals/groups of major public health concern; fact sheets include
  dioxins, mercury, lead poisoning, asbestos and e-waste.
