## Overview (verified, excerpt)

- Occupational health is defined as "an area of work in public health to promote and
  maintain highest degree of physical, mental and social well-being of workers" across all
  occupations.
- Three objectives: maintaining workers' health and working capacity, improving working
  conditions and environments, and developing work organisation and cultures that support
  safety and health.
- The field draws on occupational medicine, nursing, ergonomics, psychology, hygiene and
  safety.
- Key concerns referenced include asbestos, falls, mental health at work, workplace heat
  stress and extreme heat, airborne particulates, occupational injuries, violence and
  harassment.
