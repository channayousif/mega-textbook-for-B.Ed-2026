World Health Organization. (2024). *Ambient (outdoor) air pollution* (fact sheet,
24 October 2024). https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/ambient-(outdoor)-air-quality-and-health

Open access (WHO publisher page). Verified by direct fetch from who.int on 2026-09-23; the
page's "Key facts" section was read and is excerpted below. (Bound after the first G3
review found the 99-per-cent claim cited in Topic 1.1 without a binding.)

## Key facts (verified, excerpt)

- Ambient (outdoor) air pollution was estimated to cause 4.2 million premature deaths
  worldwide in 2019.
- 99% of the global population breathes air that exceeds WHO air quality guideline levels.
- Roughly 89% of those outdoor-pollution deaths occurred in low- and middle-income
  countries.
- Pollutants named: particulate matter (PM), carbon monoxide (CO), ozone (O3), nitrogen
  dioxide (NO2) and sulfur dioxide (SO2).
