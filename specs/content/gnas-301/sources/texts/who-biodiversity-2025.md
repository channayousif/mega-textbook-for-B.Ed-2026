## Key facts (verified, excerpt)

- Biodiversity - diversity within species, between species, and across ecosystems -
  underpins all life on Earth.
- Well-functioning ecosystems supply clean air, fresh water, natural medicines, and food
  security, and help regulate disease and climate.
- Roughly 1 million species face extinction risk; extinctions are running 10-100 times above
  the natural baseline.
- Over 75% of food crops depend on pollinators (worth US$ 235-577 billion annually).
- 35% of wetlands have been lost since 1970.
