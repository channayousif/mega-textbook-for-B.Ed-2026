# Bound excerpt - un-restoration

United Nations. *UN Decade on Ecosystem Restoration (2021-2030)*.
https://www.decadeonrestoration.org/

Open access (UN publisher page). Verified by direct fetch on 2026-09-23; the page's headline
statements were read and are excerpted below.

## Headline statements (verified, excerpt)

- "There has never been a more urgent need to revive damaged ecosystems than now."
- Ecosystems underpin all life, and healthier ecosystems mean a healthier planet and people.
- The Decade's goal is to "prevent, halt and reverse the degradation of ecosystems on every
  continent and in the ocean."
- Restoration is presented as a way to end poverty, combat climate change, and prevent a
  mass extinction - but success "will only succeed if everyone plays a part."
