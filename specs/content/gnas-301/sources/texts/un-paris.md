## Key statements (verified, excerpt)

- The Paris Agreement, adopted at COP21 on 12 December 2015, sets long-term goals to cut
  greenhouse gas emissions so that global temperature rise is held to "well below 2C
  above pre-industrial levels" while pursuing efforts to "limit it to 1.5C above
  pre-industrial levels."
- It is a legally binding treaty that entered into force on 4 November 2016, with 195
  Parties (194 States plus the European Union).
- It operates on a five-year cycle of increasingly ambitious climate action through
  Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs).
