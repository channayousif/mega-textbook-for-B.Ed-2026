# Bound excerpt - who-water-2023

World Health Organization. (2023). *Drinking-water* (fact sheet, 13 September 2023).
https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/drinking-water

Open access (WHO publisher page). Verified by direct fetch from who.int on 2026-09-23; the
page's "Key facts" section was read and is excerpted below.

## Key facts (verified, excerpt)

- In 2022, about 2.2 billion people lacked safely managed drinking-water services, while 73%
  of the global population (6 billion people) had them.
- At least 1.7 billion people used a drinking-water source contaminated with faeces.
- Over 2 billion people lived in water-stressed countries as of 2021.
- Microbiologically contaminated water can transmit diarrhoea, cholera, dysentery, typhoid
  and polio, and is estimated to cause about 505,000 diarrhoeal deaths each year.

## What this supports in GNAS-301 Unit 2

Topic 2.1 (U2-01) cites this source for the 2-billion-in-water-stressed-countries figure and
the global access picture. Topic 2.2 (U2-02) cites it for the 505,000 annual diarrhoeal
deaths from microbiologically contaminated drinking water, grounding the claim that
pathogens are the heaviest water-pollution burden. The unit does not rely on this source for
any figure it does not state here.
