# Bound excerpt - who-climate-2023

World Health Organization. (2023). *Climate change* (fact sheet, 12 October 2023).
https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-and-health

Open access (WHO publisher page). Verified by direct fetch from who.int on 2026-09-23; the
page's "Key facts" section was read and is excerpted below.

## Key facts (verified, excerpt)

- Climate change is driving humanitarian emergencies from heatwaves, wildfires, floods, and
  tropical storms, which are growing in scale, frequency and intensity.
- 3.6 billion people already live in areas highly susceptible to climate change.
- Approximately 250 000 additional deaths per year, from undernutrition, malaria, diarrhoea
  and heat stress alone, are projected between 2030 and 2050.
- Areas with weak health infrastructure - mostly in developing countries - will be least able
  to cope without assistance.
