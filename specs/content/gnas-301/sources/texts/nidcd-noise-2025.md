Verified directly against the publisher page on 2026-10-19. The notes below are
paraphrases of the named sections, not quotations.

## Verified supporting content

Long or repeated exposure at or above 85 dBA can harm hearing; louder sounds require less exposure time. Typical conversation is 60-70 dBA; distance also changes exposure.

## What this supports in GNAS-301 Unit 3

Topic 3.2 (U3-02): the hearing-risk threshold and the role of exposure duration. The threshold is
A-weighted (dBA); the lesson uses dB as shorthand. The ladder is a conventional
illustration, not a measurement of a Karachi road or a particular horn.
