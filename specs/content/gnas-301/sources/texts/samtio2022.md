## Abstract (verified, excerpt)

Groundwater quality of taluka Chachro was investigated by analyzing thirty-two groundwater
samples for drinking purpose. Multivariate statistical approaches with GIS interpolations
(IDW) were applied for the identification of significant geochemical processes governing the
groundwater quality. Results showed the concentrations of physiochemical parameter in the
study area exceeded the prescribed level of WHO guidelines. The EC and TDS varied from
2593-18950 uS/cm and 1659-12128 mg/l, respectively. The Ca, Mg, Na, and K range from 36-288,
17-272, 420-3280, and 9.5-101 mg/l, respectively. While, Cl, HCO3, SO4, and NO3 varied from
539-5738, 210-1150, 79-870, and 6.66-17.13 mg/l respectively.
