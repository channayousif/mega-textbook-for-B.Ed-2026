## Unverifiable sources

Bound excerpts could not be committed for these keys. Recorded here so `check:depth-gate`
and any G3 reviewer see the limitation explicitly rather than inferring it from silence.

- harrington-gill: verified real (Blackwell; some editions co-edited with F. C. Lu and
  B. Nurminen) but a print monograph not retrievable by this host. Used for title-level
  support of U4-02 (accidents and fatalities), U4-03 (management systems), U4-05 (internal control), U4-06 (duties
  and rights), U4-11 and U4-12 (statutes and plans), and U4-08 and U4-09 (management principles,
  measures and models), U4-10 (organizational environment), U4-14 and U4-15 (the external environment and the tools), presented as general disciplinary teaching rather than claims verified by the
  unit's WHO or registry-verified excerpts. Could not be retrieved on 2026-09-23.
  Owner ruling (D-2026-0001, confirmed for this course in G-2026-23/D-2026-0021): flag and
  proceed.
- The 2012 Karachi factory fire (topic-01, U4-02) is presented as a documented public
  event of public record (September 2012, more than 250 workers killed behind locked and
  barred exits); its analytical reading, the lining-up failures, is the unit's own
  framework applied, not a cited source claim. No journal source for the fire could be
  verified from this host; if the owner supplies one, the improvement loop binds it.
- Karachi's heatwaves (topic-07, U4-13) are mentioned as public-record examples of
  dangerous heat, without a year or toll; no event-specific source is claimed.
