## RRQs

1. Define honest lawful income, contrast fraud/bribery/unjust gain, and cite 4:29 or 2:188. The supplied three marking points fit; underlying scriptural translation is not frozen.
2. Explain divine trusteeship and limits on use of wealth. A valid contrast need not assume every secular property theory allows unrestricted destruction or hoarding. The first marking point improperly requires that caricature.
3. Consent does not excuse adulteration or hidden defects. Require honest disclosure and lawful goods. Supplied reasoning is coherent; hadith text/reference remains unverified.
4. A levy can transfer purchasing power and discourage idle holdings; distinguish intended mechanism from a demonstrated guarantee of ending poverty. The model treats the mechanism as inevitable and needs qualification.
5. Contrast returns based on loan conditions with agreed shares of realised partnership profit. Distinguish the topic's Mudarabah rule (capital provider bears ordinary financial loss; manager loses effort, barring misconduct) from Musharakah loss allocation by contributed capital. The model groups both together without this distinction and repeats categorical zero-risk language. Repair it to assess the two structures separately.
6. Define Hakimiyyah and explain the ideal moral constraint on rulers; distinguish a normative standard from proof that tyranny cannot occur. Model broadly follows the taught ideal but needs this distinction.
7. Explain 42:38 and 3:159 as support for consultation; universal legal obligation and institutional form require qualified scholarly attribution. Model currently accepts only a categorical formulation.
8. Consult affected science teachers, identify rooms and time constraints, compare options and agree a workable timetable. The model's three marking points fit the practical problem.
9. Explain the Charter's claimed common civic obligations, religious autonomy and mutual protection, with a specific source/version rather than unsupported universal civic equality. The supplied model repeats the unsourced historical assertions.
10. Article 20 states general religious freedom subject to qualifications; Article 22(1) specifically addresses instruction, ceremony and worship of another religion. Inclusion in scheduling is a policy application requiring explanation, not a quoted rule in Article 22. Model should separate these levels.

## Topic formative and summative tasks

Topic 5.1 checks 1-5: trusteeship; three prohibited practices and harms; pricing case; Zakat versus Sadaqah; loan return versus partnership return. Check 3 does not establish withholding or artificial scarcity, so high price alone cannot demonstrate the given definition of ihtikar. Check 1 requires explanation despite Remember label; check 5 primarily repeats explanation despite Apply label. Welfare activity: reject the proportional monthly loan charge, separate relief from lending, verify eligibility and ownership rather than assuming every needy pupil qualifies for Zakat. Summative policy: transparent procurement, dignified relief, affordable non-interest advances, accountability and a feasible funding ceiling. Its three 0-3 criteria genuinely require analysis but lack enough detail for consistent partial marks.

Topic 5.2 checks 1-5: divine sovereignty as ideal constraint; 42:38 and 3:159; fair notice/hearing and accountability; source-qualified Charter account; distinguish general freedom from specific educational no-coercion. Check 1 is explanation rather than mere Remember. Scheduling activity: consult families and teachers, identify holidays, adjust options and communicate a fair calendar; Article 22 does not itself explicitly prescribe festival scheduling. Summative policy: council mandate, fair grievance process, confidentiality and minority inclusion with explicit resource responsibility. The 0-3 rubric requires analysis but should permit evidence-based qualified conclusions.
