## Independent assessment solutions

All 25 items in `unit-assessment.mdx` were independently solved before inspecting the answer key:

1. **MCQ 1:** B (Wisdom [hikmah] concerns responsible judgement about how knowledge guides action in context, while recall, speed or confidence alone do not establish it). Matches key.
2. **MCQ 2:** D (Checking the working and acknowledging any correction protects accuracy and shows that authority can acknowledge reasoned correction). Matches key.
3. **MCQ 3:** A (Asking the pupil to explain a new example checks transfer of understanding rather than surface recall or manipulation). Matches key.
4. **MCQ 4:** C (Surah Al-'Alaq 96:1-5 connects reading, teaching and the pen with the Creator). Matches key.
5. **MCQ 5:** B (The Samarkand heritage case study supports the existence of observation and calculation in an institutional learning centre; it does not establish universal education or the end of scholarship). Matches key.
6. **MCQ 6:** C (Calling an improvement a further development of an existing method matches the evidence, acknowledging earlier work without diminishing the later contribution). Matches key.
7. **MCQ 7:** A (The final date of a timeline defines the display's selected period of coverage, not the end of scholarship). Matches key.
8. **MCQ 8:** D (Coexistence includes shared work with continuing belief differences; it does not depend on religious agreement). Matches key.
9. **MCQ 9:** A (The teacher should immediately protect participation by stopping exclusion, while referring the allegation for a fair check by responsible adults). Matches key.
10. **MCQ 10:** C (A thematic comparison between the Farewell Pilgrimage report and UDHR shows selected related concerns without making distinct sources legally identical or authenticating popular composite sermons). Matches key.
