# GISS-401 Unit 7 - Figure manifest

Three figures planned in the content-spec Figure plan (one concept-map, one table, one flowchart); all are schematics, none is an illustration. Authored as hand-written SVG by the author job (Claude, the generate-figures fallback author), optimised with `optimize:figure --svg`, with dark variants derived by `figures:variants`, and placed. Urdu `.ur.svg` localisation belongs to the separate Urdu job. Contract: `specs/009-figure-rendering/contracts/figure-manifest-v2.md`.
