## Unverifiable sources

- Hadith references: *Sahih Muslim* 1015, *Sahih al-Bukhari* 2989, *Sahih Muslim* 1009, *Sunan Abi Dawud* 3052, and *Sahih Muslim* 101 are named in the prose, but no bound excerpt of these hadith is in the bundle. Their wording is paraphrased or quoted only in short phrases; the qualified Islamic Studies reviewer must verify the passages and their translations before doctrinal claims rest on them.
- Historical accounts: the trial of Caliph Ali ibn Abi Talib before Qadi Shurayh, the audit of Umar ibn al-Khattab, and the detailed terms of the Charter of Madinah are narrated from classical historical reports. No passage-level bound historical source is in the bundle. The prose labels them as reported traditional accounts, not established facts, and the assessment tests the principles they illustrate rather than the reports themselves.
- Article 38(f): the wording "eliminate riba as early as possible" is quoted from the 2012 consolidation of the Constitution (as modified up to 28 February 2012). It is a Principle of Policy, not an enforceable fundamental right. Its present text and scope must be verified against the current consolidated Constitution before it is taught as a present binding provision.
- Source-binding status: the three cited source-text files (quran-translation, rahman-2020-zakat, abdel-salam-2015-democracy) are tracked in git but were absent from the G3 review manifest prepared at commit 929158ac, because the manifest's citation-key extraction did not capture keys with a suffix after the year or keys without a year. A fresh review bundle must bind them; citation metadata is not support.
