## Mapped-reading limits

Both mapped readings for Unit 7 are cited: `quran-translation` and `tambak-2020-teachers`. Tambak and Sukenti (2020) is a correlational survey in one Indonesian province; the unit uses it for context and says so in the text, and takes no doctrinal point from it. Its regression tables report degrees of freedom (65) that do not match the stated sample of 397, so the unit cites no statistic from it. `sahih-bukhari-33-893` is not a mapped reading for Unit 7 in the content spec; it is drawn from the guide-required *Sahih al-Bukhari* because the hadith of the guardian is the classic statement of responsibility in leadership (U7-02) and the hadith of the hypocrite makes the link between faith and work (U7-01) that the unit's first misconception needs.
