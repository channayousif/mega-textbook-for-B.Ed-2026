## Mapped reading availability

The guide's *Islami Nazriya-e-Hayat* is title-level only in the approved specification: its author,
edition and accessible text were not established there. It was not read, quoted or used to support
specific claims here. The mapped, accessible Qur'an and democracy article support the teaching
instead, under the owner-approved reading policy in D-2026-0079 and D-2026-0001. No invented
bibliographic attribution or unread-book paraphrase is supplied. The existing intake escalation
G-2026-197 records the reading issue; no new unsourced checklist leaf is introduced.

School cases, procedures and worked evaluations are authored teaching applications, not reports of
actual schools or claims that a verse prescribes a particular modern institution. Detailed family
law and judgements about present governments are outside the unit. Source interpretation and the
religious-content accuracy remain for independent G3 review, including the scholar review flagged
in the approved course specification.
