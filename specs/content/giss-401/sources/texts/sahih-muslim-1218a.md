## Bound excerpt and locator

Collection: Sahih Muslim. Book of Pilgrimage. Hadith 1218a, Jabir's extended pilgrimage account. Locator: speech after arrival at Arafat, before the account of the combined prayers.

Short excerpt: "your blood, your property are as sacred and inviolable".

Paraphrase: the speech rejects inherited blood revenge and addresses responsibilities towards women, including maintenance. These are the selected themes compared in the unit. The unit does not attribute a modern equality slogan or the wording of UDHR articles to this report. Popular composite sermon texts must not be substituted for the named narration.
