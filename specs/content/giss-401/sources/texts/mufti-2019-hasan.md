## Verified fragment

Under "The Definition of Hadith Hasan": "continuous sanad with just narrators but their memorization is lesser".

The surrounding definition also requires freedom from the named contradiction and defect
conditions. Later sections distinguish hasan in itself from a report strengthened by other
routes. The evidential-use discussion states general acceptance while noting conditions.
This is the author's paraphrase of those sections.

Supports U2-08: precision matters, hasan is an accepted category, and supporting routes can
matter to a scholarly judgment. It does not support a trainee assigning grades from a
quotation card, nor a claim that every weak report becomes acceptable through repetition.
The unit deliberately omits this article's further technical subdivisions from its basic
three-grade teaching outline.
