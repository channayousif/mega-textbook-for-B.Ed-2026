## Verified fragments and locators

Chapter 4, "Sura and Aya": "The Qur'an has 114 suras of unequal length".
Chapter 5, "Makkan and Madinan Revelations": "The period in Makka, before the hijra";
"The period in Madina, after the hijra".

The chapter 5 discussion explains broad differences of emphasis between the periods while
retaining the basic themes. It also distinguishes a chapter's classification from the
possibility that it contains verses associated with the other period. The text's introduction
to the revelation and its transmission distinguishes the received arrangement from a simple
chronological list. These are author paraphrases of the checked sections.

Supports U2-04 only. The unit uses this work to introduce structure and period labels, not
as a specialist resolution of every debated verse chronology. The student prose names the
chapter 5 section and does not repeat unrelated historical claims from the transcription.
