# Bound excerpt - tambak-2020-teachers

Tambak, S., & Sukenti, D. (2020). Strengthening Islamic behavior and Islamic psychosocial in developing professional madrasah teachers. *Jurnal Cakrawala Pendidikan, 39*(1), 65-78.
https://doi.org/10.21831/cp.v39i1.26001

Open access (OpenAlex W3003291306, gold OA, CC BY-SA, checked 2026-11-03). The full text was read from the publisher PDF (https://journal.uny.ac.id/index.php/cp/article/download/26001/pdf) on 2026-11-03. Short extracts only.

## U7-01

Page 65 (abstract): the study used an ex post facto design "involving 397 Madrasah Aliyah teachers in 12 regencies/cities of Riau Province, Indonesia", and reports that "mastery of Islamic behavior and Islamic psychosocial contributes to developing professional madrasah teachers"; "Islamic behavior has a higher contribution than Islamic psychosocial."

Page 68 (method): the Islamic behaviour scale covered *hikmah* (wisdom), *syaja'ah* (courage), *'iffah* (self-restraint, rendered "simple"), *'adalah* (fairness) and related qualities. Pages 67-68: professional madrasah teachers are described by five competencies, pedagogic, personality, social, professional and leadership.

Page 69 (discussion): with Islamic behaviour, "Teaching students is no longer just merely completing routine tasks", and "Teaching is no longer aimed at earning money", but at reward from Allah.

## U7-03

Page 71: citing Yusoff and Hamzah (2015), "the Islamic behavior possessed by teachers such as exemplary will be able to develop teacher performance in the learning process."

Page 71: "Teaching is no longer just the task of conveying knowledge, but more than that it is trying to change bad habits to the more civilized and dignified good."

## Limits

Correlational survey in one Indonesian province; Table 1 reports 65 total degrees of freedom against a stated sample of 397, so the unit cites no statistic. Used for context only, not doctrine.
