## Verified fragments and supporting paraphrase

Among the five conditions listed: "The chain is continuous" and "It does not have a hidden defect".

The other conditions concern narrator integrity, precision and absence of contradiction
with stronger transmission. The page gives references to Sharhun Nukhbah and Tadrib and
includes Arabic passages. This is a paraphrase of the list, not an independent assessment
by the unit author of any particular chain.

Supports U2-08: the introductory definition of sahih and the distinction between recognising
isnad/matn and carrying out specialist assessment. It does not establish the grade of an
unknown forwarded saying or authorise a student to replace scholarly grading with a checklist.
