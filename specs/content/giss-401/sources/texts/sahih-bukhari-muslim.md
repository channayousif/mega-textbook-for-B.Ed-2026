## U1-06

The questioner, later identified as Jibril, asks about faith (Iman). The reply as given in the Sunnah.com English text:

"That you affirm your faith in Allah, in His angels, in His Books, in His Apostles, in the Day of Judgment, and you affirm your faith in the Divine Decree about good and evil."

At the end of the hadith the Prophet (peace be upon him) says of the questioner: "He was Gabriel (the angel). He came to you in order to instruct you in matters of religion."

The same hadith describes Islam as testifying to the oneness of Allah and the messengership of Muhammad, establishing prayer, paying Zakat, fasting in Ramadan and performing the pilgrimage; the unit's MCQ 8 relies on this distinction between the pillars of Islam and the articles of faith.
