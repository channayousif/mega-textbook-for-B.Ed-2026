## Bound excerpt and locators

Educator resource Unit 4, Science and the Art of the Islamic World, printed pp. 91-106.

Short excerpt, p. 93: "religiously, ethnically, and geographically diverse".

Paraphrase: pp. 91 and 93 describe translation into Arabic and a varied scholarly community. Pages 93-94 connect observation, instruments and practical purposes; observatories are described as settings of learning and research. The unit uses these points for cultural exchange and educational conditions. It does not infer universal educational access or treat historical astrology as validated modern science.
