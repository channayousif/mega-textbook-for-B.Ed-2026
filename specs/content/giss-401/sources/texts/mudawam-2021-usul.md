of legal method to the purposes of Islamic law. In the body, the discussion following the
historical account of the jurists identifies Ijma and Qiyas as dependent sources and explains
that they do not stand apart from the Qur'an and Sunnah. The conclusion restates the distinction.
These sentences are a paraphrase of the checked discussion, not additional quotation.
