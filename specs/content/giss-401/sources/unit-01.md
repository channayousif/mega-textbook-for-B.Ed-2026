## Mapped-reading limits

Three of the four mapped readings are cited: `quran-translation`, `bhat-2019-education` and `maududi-towards-understanding`. The fourth, `islami-nazriya-e-hayat`, is not cited for any claim: the guide gives no author or publisher, no catalogue record was found (content spec, Reading list), and its text could not be consulted. `sahih-bukhari-muslim` is mapped to Unit 2 in the content spec; Unit 1 uses one hadith from it because the six articles of faith (U1-06) are stated together in the hadith of Jibril and not in any single verse.

## Unverifiable sources

- maududi-towards-understanding: the book's text could not be consulted from this host (no open full text; Open Library holds the catalogue record OL12324456W only). The unit's U1-01 summary of chapter 1 (all creation follows Allah's laws; human beings choose freely in their moral choices) and its listing as further reading for U1-05 and U1-06 rest on the author's general knowledge of the book and are unchecked against its pages (`D-2026-0001`, title-level support).
