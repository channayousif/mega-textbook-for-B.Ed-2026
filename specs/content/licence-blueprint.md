**Status:** draft, first pass 2026-09-12. **Sourcing discipline:** Constitution Art. II.3. Every
row below is traceable to a primary source listed under Provenance, or is explicitly marked
`uncovered` / `unverified`. **No invented mappings.**

Purpose: make "one curriculum, two outcomes - your degree and your licence" a checkable claim by
mapping every licence-test objective to a `course_code` / `unit_no` in the B.Ed 4-year scheme, or
recording that no course covers it.

## Test specification (S2, verbatim)

> **Summary of Test Division**

| S# | Type | No of Questions | Marks | Weightage | Time (Mins) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Extended Response Question (ERQ) Case Study Analysis | 1 | 100 | 25 | 50 |
| 2 | Constructed Response Question (CRQ) | 10 | 150 | 50 | 50 |
| 3 | Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) | 50 | 50 | 25 | 50 |

Totals: 61 items, 300 marks, weightage 100, 150 minutes.

### The finding that matters most

**75% of the licence test is constructed response** (CRQ 50 + ERQ 25). Only 25% is MCQ, and every
sampled MCQ is tagged `Content knowledge` at level `Content knowledge` - school-subject recall,
not pedagogy. Every sampled CRQ and the ERQ is tagged `Comprehension and analysis` or
`Analysis & Evaluation`, each with a published points-based rubric.

This has a direct product consequence. An MCQ bank addresses a quarter of the paper. The
pipeline's existing **10 RRQ + 5 ERQ with worked answers and rubrics** per unit is the format that
addresses the other three quarters, and rubric-marked constructed response is materially harder
for a printed guide to serve than an MCQ list.

## Correction to a prior assumption

The 2026-09-11 repositioning plan states the specification as *"Pedagogical Content 50% · Content
Knowledge 30% · Psychometric & Analytical Reasoning 20%"*. **No source found supports this.**
S1 and S2 describe two parts and three item types, with no psychometric or analytical-reasoning
section at any weight. A secondary web summary cites a now-404 STEDA FAQ for a "40% MCQ / 60% CRQ"
split, which S2 also does not support.

Treat 25 / 50 / 25 from S2 as authoritative until a better primary source appears, and correct
the plan and any pricing or marketing copy derived from it. The 50/30/20 figures may belong to a
different instrument - the PST/JEST recruitment tests are a separate examination from the
teaching licence and were not examined here.

| Social Studies, Class 1-8 | - | **uncovered** |
| English, Class 1-8 | - | **uncovered** |
| Urdu or Sindhi, Class 1-8 | - | **uncovered** |

**Part I is out of scope for the B.Ed corpus.** It is assessed from the school curriculum for
Classes 1-8, not from the B.Ed scheme. `GQUR-300`/`GQUR-301` and `GENG-300`/`GENG-301` are
university-level courses and are **not** substitutes; mapping them here would be an invented
mapping. Covering Part I requires a separate content line built on the DCAR school curriculum.

The dual-outcome claim is therefore precise only for Part II. Stated honestly: **the B.Ed corpus
can serve 75% of the licence paper; the remaining 25% is a distinct product decision.**

## The 2025 scheme is the test's actual basis *(owner clarification, 2026-09-12)*

S1's five Part II headings are not topic groupings. They are **course titles**, and S1 says so:
Part II is assessed *"from the curriculum developed by the Higher Education Commission for
different courses offered in the B.Ed (2.5 years) and B.Ed Hons (4 years) degree programs"*.

The pre-2026 Sindh scheme (`B.Ed (4-Year) 2025-2.docx`, `EED-` codes) implements those HEC
courses almost one to one:

| STEDA Part II heading (S1) | 2025 scheme course | 2026 scheme successor |
|---|---|---|
| Methods of Teaching | `EED-312` General Methods of Teaching | `EFPG-*` pedagogy courses (dispersed) |
| Foundations of Education | `EED-511` Foundations of Education | `EFMP-409` Foundations of Education |
| Child Development | `EED-311` Child Development | `EFID-501` Human Growth & Development |
| Education Psychology | `EED-513` Educational Psychology | `EFMP-301` Educational Psychology |
| **Classroom Management** | **`EED-313` Classroom Management** | **none** |
| Classroom Assessment | `EED-411` Classroom Assessment | `EFMP-408` Educational Assessment and Evaluation |
| Test Development and Evaluation | `EED-624` Test Development and Evaluation | `EFMP-408` (merged) |
| School Community and Teacher | no `EED-` equivalent; HEC ADE/B.Ed(Hons) course title | `GCCE-400` + `GSOS-301` (partial) |

**This closes the Classroom Management gap as a mystery.** It was never missing from the
curriculum; it was a standalone Foundation course, `EED-313`, that the 2026 revision restructured
away. The licence still tests it at the weight of two sampled CRQ items. The owner holds detailed
course guides for the 2025 courses, which are the authority the test was built against.

### What this changes

1. **The gap is real but now sourceable.** Classroom Management needs authored content and has no
   2026 course to host it. Author it from the `EED-313` guide as a licence-track module that sits
   outside the degree corpus.
2. **`EFMP-408` absorbs two 2025 courses** (`EED-411` Classroom Assessment and `EED-624` Test
   Development and Evaluation), which is why it carries 16 objectives - the highest count in the
   mapping. Its content-spec should be scoped against both guides, not one.
3. **The dual-outcome claim survives, at topic level rather than course level.** The 2026 courses
   still cover most 2025 material; only the packaging changed. A unit authored for `EFMP-301`
   serves `EED-513` topics. The exception is Classroom Management, which needs its own module.
4. ~~The 2025 course guides are not in the repository.~~ **Resolved 2026-09-13**: supplied at
   `Scheme-and-Course-guides/course-guides-2025/` (25 files, 108 MB). All eight Part II guides are
   present and text-extractable. See S5 and the guide-anchored mapping below.

### Consequences

2. **Authoring has a direct source.** Licence-track content is written from the guide unit the
   syllabus points at, under the usual Art. II.3 sourcing discipline - these guides are the
   authority the examiner used.
3. **The eight Part II guides are the whole Part II corpus.** 1,142 pages across Methods of
   Teaching (95), Foundations of Education (106), Child Development (156), Educational Psychology
   (133), Classroom Management (56), Classroom Assessment (308), Test Development and Evaluation
   (120) and School, Community and Teacher (168).
