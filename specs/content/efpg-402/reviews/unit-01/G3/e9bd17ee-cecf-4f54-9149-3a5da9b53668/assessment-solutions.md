2. **Tentative and reliable nature of science (Understand, 3 marks)**:
   - Evaluated model answer: Explains that science is tentative because it remains open to revision upon new empirical evidence, and reliable because existing knowledge has withstood repeated empirical verification. Self-correction is a fundamental strength.
   - Verdict: Sound pedagogical reasoning addressing a prevalent misconception.

   - Evaluated model answer: Fact (water boils at 100 °C at sea level), concept (evaporation), law (law of reflection), theory (particle theory of matter).

   - Verdict: Directly targets common misconception with clear rubric breakdown.

5. **Three process skills in NCP Scientific Enquiry (Understand, 3 marks)**:
