## 2. Restricted-Response Questions (RRQs 1-10)

All RRQ stems were solved independently and compared against the mark schemes in `unit-assessment.mdx`:

- **RRQ-01 (Understand)**: Explain two limits of unbroken 35-min lecture and how chunking helps. Solved: Attention drift and hidden misconceptions; chunking introduces checks/pair discussions every 8-10 minutes. Matches model answer (4 marks).
- **RRQ-02 (Apply)**: Open discussion question on water pollution + 2 ground rules + openness justification. Solved: Formulates open multi-perspective question (e.g. canal water protection responsibility); rules include respectful listening and evidence-backed claims; justifies openness. Matches model answer (4 marks).
- **RRQ-03 (Analyze)**: "No lab room means no laboratory method" critique + practical example. Solved: Rebuts misconception that method requires specialized room; laboratory method is hands-on empirical investigation possible on ordinary desks (e.g. turmeric indicator test); identifies true resource limits. Matches model answer (4 marks).
- **RRQ-04 (Apply)**: Planning and demonstrating candle burning under glass. Solved: Aim & safety, trial run for visibility, predict before covering, observe and conclude with class. Matches model answer (4 marks).
- **RRQ-05 (Understand)**: Scientific method vs problem-solving method by aims and suitable questions. Solved: Scientific method explains natural phenomena ("Why does iron rust?"); problem-solving tackles practical human difficulties ("How can we stop the school gate rusting?"). Matches model answer (4 marks).
- **RRQ-06 (Analyze)**: Fertilizer plant growth test fair test analysis and redesign. Solved: Uncontrolled confounding variables (pot size, light position, fertilizer); redesigned fair test controls pot size, position, and water, changing only fertilizer. Matches model answer (4 marks).
- **RRQ-07 (Understand)**: Three pre-visit field trip actions with reasons. Solved: Site reconnaissance for safety/planning, permissions from head/parents, pre-trip pupil orientation on goals and task sheets. Matches model answer (3 marks).
- **RRQ-08 (Analyze)**: Light straight lines deductive lesson critique + adding inductive direction. Solved: Deductive rule-copying without empirical grounding leads to rote memorization; adds inductive card-hole torch beam observation. Matches model answer (4 marks).
- **RRQ-09 (Analyze)**: Classify two inquiry activities with justifications. Solved: (a) Confirmation inquiry (question, procedure, result given); (b) Open inquiry (question and procedure chosen by pupils). Matches model answer (4 marks).
- **RRQ-10 (Understand)**: Refuting "activity-based learning means no teacher input" + two teacher roles. Solved: Without teacher facilitation, pupils remain busy without cognitive reflection; teacher must question/guide reflection and introduce formal scientific concepts. Matches model answer (3 marks).

## 3. Extended-Response Questions (ERQs 1-5)

All 5 ERQs provide detailed prompts requiring synthesis, evaluation, and application:
- **ERQ-01 (Evaluate)**: Low-cost plan for three separation techniques in Grade 6 (50 pupils, no lab); laboratory method for sand/salt dissolving/filtration and magnetic separation; teacher demonstration for boiling salt water to dryness due to heating safety; analytic rubric (10 marks).
- **ERQ-02 (Create)**: Mosquito breeding problem-solving project across Kilpatrick's stages, incorporating systematic empirical observation and individual learning checks; analytic rubric (10 marks).
- **ERQ-03 (Analyze)**: Parallel inductive and deductive lesson designs on light traveling in straight lines, evaluating suitability for novice Grade 6 learners and synthesizing both reasoning directions; analytic rubric (10 marks).
- **ERQ-04 (Evaluate)**: Unguided magnetic discovery lesson evaluation using Bybee / Prince & Felder research on discovery failure, with 5E guided inquiry redesign; analytic rubric (10 marks).
- **ERQ-05 (Evaluate)**: Integrative method selection and defense for three topics in a 65-pupil class without a laboratory (seeds need water -> group practical fair test; burning needs air -> demonstration POE; plastic bags ban -> guided discussion/survey); risk identification and remediation; analytic rubric (10 marks).
