## RRQs (independent answers vs model answers)

1. Curriculum vs textbook. Independent: a curriculum is the authority's plan for what is learned,
   in what order and how; a textbook is one resource that turns it into chapters and exercises.
   Model answer agrees, but its science example attributes "classify materials as conductors or
   insulators" to the NCP as an SLO. That wording is not in the published NCP (see
   `source-checks/ncp-science-2022-unit-02-search.txt`), so the example is unsupported.
2. Five principles with an NCP example each. Independent answer matches the model. The
   child-centredness, activity-basis, relevance (Science in Context / Benchmark VII) and
   flexibility examples are supported by the bound excerpt; the integration example
   ("data-handling links to mathematics") is loosely supported by the NCP Models and
   Representations text (graphs, charts, percentages and ratios).
3. Aims vs objectives. Independent answer matches the model.
4. "I teach whatever is in the textbook." Independent: violates relevance (ignores the pupils'
   community) and child-centredness (ignores the class's level and needs); the curriculum, not
   the textbook, is the plan. Model answer agrees.
5. Topical vs spiral. Independent: topical = each topic once (complete but forgotten); spiral =
   same concepts return with new depth (needs planning, reduces forgetting). Model agrees.
6. Why spiral reduces forgetting, with an NCP example. Model uses "properties of matter" at
   Grades 4, 6 and 8. Domain B does revisit matter at every grade (Grade 4 states of matter,
   Grade 6 particles, Grade 8 particle model and changes of state), so the example is defensible,
   though "properties of matter" is the unit's label rather than the NCP's topic wording.
7. Two weakest criteria for a chapter with no activities and only city-based examples.
   Independent: Activities (no practical work) and Inclusivity (city-only examples exclude rural
   pupils). The model answer names "Activities" and then "Relevance (or inclusivity via
   'illustrations')". Relevance is not one of the six checklist criteria (accuracy, curriculum
   alignment, language, activities, illustrations, inclusivity), so the model answer names a
   non-criterion and would teach the wrong second answer. Recorded as a defect.
8. Good exam results do not prove a textbook is good. Independent: curriculum alignment (a
   narrow exam can miss SLOs) and activities (recall-only exams can succeed without practical
   work). Model agrees.
9. Science and mathematics correlation with a Grade 6-8 example. Model agrees (measurement,
   data, graphing; plant-growth line graph).
10. Science and Islamiat connection for an environment topic. Model agrees (stewardship / khalifah
    and deforestation).
