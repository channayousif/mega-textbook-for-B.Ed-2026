## RRQs (independent solution, compared with the model answers)

1. Definition plus two reasons: a lesson plan is a single-period roadmap of objectives, activities,
   apparatus, safety and assessment checks; written planning gives cognitive coherence and aligns
   daily work with curriculum outcomes. Matches the model and the 3-mark scheme.
2. Unplanned teacher is reactive (hunts equipment, lectures hurriedly, rushes, no formative check);
   planned teacher is a proactive facilitator (materials ready, time blocks, active inquiry, formative
   checks). Matches the 4-mark scheme.
3. Preparation, Presentation, Comparison and Association, Generalization, Application, in that order.
   Matches the 5-mark scheme.
4. 5E is constructivist because pupils explore and build ideas before the teacher introduces formal
   terms; Herbartian is transmission. Matches the 3-mark scheme.
5. Audience, Behaviour, Condition, Degree/Criterion. Matches the 4-mark scheme.
6. The objective is teacher-centred ("the teacher will explain") and has no condition or criterion;
   a measurable rewrite states the pupil performance, condition and degree. Matches the 4-mark scheme.
7. Wait-time 1 is the pause after the question; Wait-time 2 is the pause after the pupil's response.
   Matches the 4-mark scheme.
8. Orient and link, analogy, logical steps, formative check, synthesize; analogies give a familiar
   mental schema for invisible entities. Matches the 4-mark scheme (2.5 + 1.5).
9. Materials Manager, Principal Investigator, Data Recorder, Safety and Clean-up Officer, with the
   duty of each. Matches the 4-mark scheme.
10. Silence signals passive transmission; order is purposeful engagement, safety compliance and prompt
    response to signals. Matches the 3-mark scheme.
