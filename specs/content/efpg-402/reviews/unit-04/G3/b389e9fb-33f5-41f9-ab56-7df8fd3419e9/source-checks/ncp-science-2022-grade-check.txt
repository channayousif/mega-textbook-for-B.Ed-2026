Source check: ncp-science-2022 grade placement of the unit's example topics

Retrieved by this review on 2026-11-28.
- URL: https://ncc.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/8_%20NCP%20Science%20PG%204-8.pdf
- HTTP 200, 74 pages, 19.6 MB, PDF modified 2023-11-22. Read through pdftotext -layout.
- SLO codes encode the grade: S-04 = Grade 4, S-05 = Grade 5, S-06 = Grade 6, S-07 = Grade 7,
  S-08 = Grade 8. Page numbers are PDF page numbers.

Checked claims:

1. Human respiratory system / diaphragm mechanics.
   - Grade 5, SLO S-05-A-02 (PDF p. 17): "Recognize the integration of the different systems
     (Respiratory, and Circulatory) in carrying out life processes." The same table carries the
     note: "Detailed knowledge of the respiratory system (e.g., alveoli) and circulatory system
     (e.g., heart chambers and valves) is not required."
   - Grade 7, SLO S-07-A-11 (PDF p. 17): "Trace the path of air in and out of the body and how
     the oxygen it contains is used during the process of respiration."
   - Unit claim under review: topic-03.mdx:28 "When teaching Grade 5 respiration, the curriculum
     expects pupils to understand how the diaphragm muscle moves downward ...".
   - Finding: Grade 5 expressly excludes detailed respiratory-system knowledge; the air-pathway
     detail is a Grade 7 SLO.

2. Plant water conduction / transpiration and xylem.
   - Grade 8, Benchmark II (PDF p. 13 and 16): "Explain the root and shoot system of plants
     emphasizing the process of photosynthesis, respiration, and transpiration."
   - Grade 7, SLO S-07-A-02 (PDF p. 14): "Predict the role of xylem and phloem in transport of
     water and food in plants ...".
   - Grade 4, SLO S-04-A-07 (PDF p. 13): parts of the plant transport system, with the note that
     the terms "xylem" and "phloem" are not required.
   - Unit claim under review: topic-03.mdx:94 "A Grade 5 teacher wants to teach plant water
     conduction (transpiration and xylem flow).".
   - Finding: transpiration is Grade 8 and xylem/phloem role is Grade 7, not Grade 5.

3. Electric circuits / closed circuits / conductivity.
   - Grade 5, SLO S-05-B-16 (PDF p. 56): "Describe flow of electric current in an electric
     circuit."; S-05-B-17 (PDF p. 56): "Draw circuit diagram with symbols."
   - Grade 6, SLO S-06-B-37 (PDF p. 56): "Differentiate between open and closed circuits."
   - "Electrical conductivity" is not an NCP SLO; the grid names thermal conductivity
     (Grade 7, S-07-B-18 and S-07-B-32).
   - Unit claim under review: unit-assessment.mdx:129 "the Grade 5 General Science concept of
     'Closed Electric Circuits and Electrical Conductivity'".
   - Finding: closed circuits is a Grade 6 SLO; the content-spec blueprint (content-spec.md:268)
     requires a named Grade 5 concept.

4. Atomic structure / chemical bonding.
   - Grade 7, SLO S-07-B-01 (PDF p. 36): "Describe and draw the structure of an atom in terms of
     electrons, protons and neutrons."
   - Grade 8 topic header (PDF p. 34-35): "Chemical Bonds".
   - Unit claim under review: topic-04.mdx:28 "Grade 8 ... atomic structure and chemical bonding".
   - Finding: atomic structure is Grade 7; only chemical bonding is Grade 8.

5. Changes of state.
   - Grade 5, Benchmark VII (PDF p. 35): "Compare the properties of different states of matter and
     identify the conditions that cause matter to change states."
   - Grade 4, SLO S-04-B-02 (PDF p. 35): "Identify and describe the three states of matter ...".
   - Unit claim under review: topic-04.mdx:94 "the Grade 6 topic: 'Changes of state: melting and
     boiling of water'".
   - Finding: changes of state is Grade 5 content (states are introduced in Grade 4), not Grade 6.

6. Light and sound.
   - Grade 5, SLO S-05-B-11 (PDF p. 48): "Demonstrate that sound can travel through different
     states of matter with different speed."; S-05-B-12: "Describe the structure and discuss the
     mechanism of the conduction of sound waves."
   - Grade 5 (PDF p. 48): "Demonstrate the characteristics of light and sound with the physical
     phenomena."
   - Grade 8, Benchmark VIII (PDF p. 48): "Compare types and properties of waves and explain how
     they interact with matter."
   - Unit claim under review: unit-assessment.mdx:125 "the Grade 8 topic 'Sound Transmission and
     Waves'".
   - Finding: sound characteristics and sound transmission are Grade 5 SLOs; waves are a Grade 8
     benchmark. The combined label is not a Grade 8 topic.
