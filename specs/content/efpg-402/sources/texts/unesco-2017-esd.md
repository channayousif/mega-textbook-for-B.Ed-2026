## Excerpts

### 1. Introduction and Definition of Sustainable Development (p. 7)

"Sustainable development begins with education. To build a world that is more just, peaceful and sustainable, all individuals and societies must be equipped and empowered by knowledge, skills and values as well as be instilled with heightened awareness to drive such change. This is the vision of Education for Sustainable Development (ESD)."

"Development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs."

### 2. The Three Domains of Learning in ESD (p. 11)

"ESD aims at developing cross-cutting sustainability competencies in learners. The learning objectives are categorized into three domains:
- Cognitive domain comprises knowledge and thinking skills necessary to better understand the SDG and the challenges in achieving it.
- Socio-emotional domain includes social skills that enable learners to collaborate, negotiate and communicate to promote the SDGs as well as self-reflection skills, values, attitudes and motivations that enable learners to develop themselves.
- Behavioural domain describes action competencies."

### 3. Key Sustainable Development Goals in Science (pp. 16-40)

- **SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being (p. 16):** "The learner understands the importance of physical and mental health... understands transmission of infectious diseases and prevention strategies."
- **SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation (p. 22):** "The learner understands the water cycle, water scarcity, and water management... understands technologies for water purification, sanitation, and recycling."
- **SDG 7: Affordable and Clean Energy (p. 24):** "The learner understands different energy sources, renewable and non-renewable... understands how energy efficiency and renewable energy technologies function."
- **SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production (p. 34):** "The learner understands the connection between individual lifestyle choices, production systems, and resource depletion... understands the 3Rs (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle)."
- **SDG 13: Climate Action (p. 36):** "The learner understands climate change as a result of human activities... understands mitigation and adaptation strategies at local and global levels."
- **SDG 15: Life on Land (p. 40):** "The learner understands basic ecology, ecosystems, biodiversity, and ecosystem services... understands threats from deforestation, habitat destruction, and climate impacts."
