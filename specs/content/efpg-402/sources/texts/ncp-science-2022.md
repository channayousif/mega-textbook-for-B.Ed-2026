## Excerpts

1. Foreword (p. 4): the National Curriculum "aims to shift classroom instruction from rote learning to concept-based learning". The foreword also names "the core, nation-building values of inclusion, harmony, and peace".
2. Progression grid introduction (p. 5): science for Grades 4-8 "is organized in the following domains: A. Life Sciences, B. Physical Sciences, C. Earth and Space Sciences".
3. Scientific Enquiry, by the end of Grade 5 (p. 5): "Ask questions"; "Know the five main types of scientific enquiry (observe over time, identify and classify, compare and contrast, fair test, research-by finding information)"; "Use equipment to carry out scientific investigations"; "Take measurements and record them"; "Make a conclusion from results informed by reasoning".
4. Scientific Enquiry, by the end of Grade 8 (p. 5): "Identify whether a given hypothesis is testable"; "Make predictions of likely outcomes for a scientific enquiry"; "Collect and record observations and/or measurements"; "Describe trends and patterns in results"; "Make conclusions by interpreting results informed by reasoning"; "Suggest improvements while doing experiments".
5. Models and Representations (p. 5-6): by Grade 5 "Use models to show scientific ideas"; by Grade 8 "Describe the strengths and limitations of a model".
6. Science in Context (p. 6): by Grade 5 "Describe how science is used in their local area", "Identify people who use science, including professionally, in their area", and "Discuss how the use of science and technology can have positive and negative environmental effects locally and globally". By Grade 8 "Describe how science is applied across societies and industries, and in research", "Discuss issues which involve and/or require scientific understanding" and "Describe how people develop and use scientific understanding".
7. Domain A topics (p. 7): Grade 6 includes "Human digestive system" and "Balanced diet"; Grade 7 includes "Immunity and Diseases".
8. Benchmark IV, Human health and disease (p. 23-25): SLO S-05-A-13 "Explain some methods of preventing the transmission of contagious diseases COVID-19 & Polio"; SLO S-07-A-24 "Illustrate how adaptive immunity develops over time"; SLO S-07-A-28 "Suggest ways in which communities of people can safeguard against the spread of infectious diseases".
9. Biotechnology (p. 32): SLO S-08-A-37 "Discuss the applications of biotechnology in the Pakistani context and their effects on the people and the environment of Pakistan over time", with illustrative examples including "vaccines for immunization"; SLO S-08-A-38 relates biotechnology to foods "with higher nutritional value", for example raising "iron content in rice".
10. Benchmark VII (p. 35): by Grade 5 and Grade 8, "Recognise the importance of science and technology to solve everyday problems" and "Integrate scientific concepts/ STEAM in daily life to improve the quality of their own life and lives of others".
11. Energy (p. 49-50 and p. 66): SLO S-06-B-30 asks pupils to "Compare the Renewable Energy Sources (wind, water, Sun and plants) and Non-Renewable Sources of energy (coal, natural gas, crude oil)"; SLO S-06-B-32 "Assemble and demonstrate a solar panel to operate a small fan. (STEAM)"; SLO S-06-B-54 "Design a solar oven to convert solar energy into heat energy"; SLO S-08-B-55 "Assemble a concave mirror type solar cooker".
