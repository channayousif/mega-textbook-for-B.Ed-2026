# Bound source evidence: behrendt-franklin-2014

Source: Behrendt, M., & Franklin, T. (2014). A review of research on school field trips and their value in education. *International Journal of Environmental and Science Education, 9*(3), 235-245. https://doi.org/10.12973/ijese.2014.213a. ERIC EJ1031445.
URL: https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1031445.pdf

Consulted: 2026-11-27. ERIC full text downloaded (HTTP 200, 11 pages) and read in full through `pdftotext`.

## Excerpts

1. Abstract (p. 235): "Experiential learning at formal and informal field trip venues increases student interest, knowledge, and motivation. The teacher's role in preplanning, implementation, and reflection often dictates the impact that the field trip will have on students."
2. Kinds of field trips (p. 236): "Formal field trips consist of planned, well-orchestrated experiences"; "Informal field trips are less structured and offer students some control and choice".
3. Before the field trip (p. 240): "The teacher should visit the venue prior to the field trip, to learn the layout of the venue and determine whether the venue is suitable for all the students." "The students should understand the focus or purpose of the experience". Preparing for the "novelty space" enhances learning; "The field trip should not be a stand-alone experience".
4. During the field trip (p. 240-241): "Worksheets are quite effective when one worksheet is given to a small group"; "Simple fill-in-the-blank task completion worksheets are not effective, when every student is responsible for his or her own data".
5. After the field trip (p. 241): "The teacher's actions after the field trip are very important." In Tal and Steiner's (2006) study "neither elementary nor secondary grade level teachers carried out quality post-visit activities."
6. Conclusion (p. 242): "The teacher should previsit the venue"; "Student groupings should be set up prior to arrival"; "Chaperones need to be trained. The trip needs to connect to the curriculum"; "Teachers need to consider safety issues". Back in class "it is imperative that the teacher spend sufficient and quality time to reflect on the experiences".

## Unit 3 use

All six excerpts support Topic 3.4 (U3-08): the before, during and after stages, the shared group task sheet, and the neglected follow-up.
