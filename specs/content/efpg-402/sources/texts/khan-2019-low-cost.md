# Bound source evidence: khan-2019-low-cost

Source: Khan, S., Farooq, R. A., & Fleury, N. (2019). Effectiveness of low cost materials on diverse achievers in the teaching of physics at secondary level. *Sir Syed Journal of Education & Social Research, 2*(2), 33-47.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.36902/sjesr-vol2-iss2-2019(33-47)
URL: https://www.sjesr.org.pk/ojs/index.php/ojs/article/download/14/5 (diamond open access, PDF 15 pages)
Authors: Saifullah Khan (Northern University Nowshera), Rehmat Ali Farooq, Nilsa Fleury (York College)

Consulted: 2026-11-28, read in full from the open-access PDF.

## Excerpts

1. Abstract (PDF p. 33): "The equipment's of science teaching in the most of secondary school is not available in Pakistan. The main purpose of this research is to find the effectiveness of low cost materials on diverse achievers in the physics instructing at secondary level. The nature of the study was experimental. ... The group which has been instructed with low cost teaching aids showed successful result scores in the posttest and their achievement level further gets improved."
2. Method (PDF p. 40): Pre-test/post-test equivalent-group design with 40 Grade X pupils of Government High School No. 2 Nowshera Cantt over six/seven weeks, with a retention test administered after two months.
3. Results for low achievers (PDF p. 43, Table 3): Experimental post-test mean 52.63 (SD 12.06) vs control mean 36.72 (SD 2.14), t = 4.31 > table value 1.725: "students in the experimental group learned better than the students of control group."
4. Results for high achievers (PDF p. 43, Table 4): Experimental post-test mean 76.77 (SD 4.02) vs control mean 53.00 (SD 5.70), t = 10.22 > table value 1.746.
5. Conclusions (PDF p. 44): "Using of low cost materials is found to be more fruitful for teaching science especially physics contrary to conventional/traditional teaching." Furthermore, "low achiever students of the experimental group showed brilliant performance over the control group as they were instructed with the help of low cost materials and took keen in interest in the learning process."
6. Pedagogical value (PDF p. 35): Improvisation with low-cost everyday materials connects classroom science to familiar surroundings, demystifies abstract apparatus, and stimulates curiosity and participation across diverse learners.
