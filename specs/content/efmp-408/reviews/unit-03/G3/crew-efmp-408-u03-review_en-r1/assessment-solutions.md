## MCQ 10 - key rationale defect (A-1)

Item: "An example of an ancient Indus Valley settlement in Sindh is an: A) Harappa,
B) Taxila, C) Mohenjo-daro, D) Lahore."

The supplied key (unit-assessment.mdx:156) says the article "an" "provides a
grammatical clue that penalises consonant-beginning words and points toward
vowel-beginning options." Every option begins with a consonant sound
(Harappa, Taxila, Mohenjo-daro, Lahore), so the article points at no option. The
real defect is that the stem's article is ungrammatical against all four options
(the keyed answer C is consonant-initial), so the item is a non-functional
example of Rule 3 rather than a working article clue. The letter A is still the
best answer, but the printed rationale is inaccurate and would confuse a teacher
who checks it.

## RRQ Bloom labels (A-2)

The style-guide default RRQ band is Understand to Analyze
(specs/content/style-guide.md:248). The content-spec Unit 3 section does not
declare a different band. RRQ 1 ("Define ..."), RRQ 7 ("Describe the anatomical
structure ...") and RRQ 9 ("State three essential structural rules ...") are tagged
*(Remember)*, below that floor. The labels match the thinking actually required,
so the repair is to raise the items to Understand/Apply demand or to record an
owner ruling that this unit's RRQ band extends to Remember.
