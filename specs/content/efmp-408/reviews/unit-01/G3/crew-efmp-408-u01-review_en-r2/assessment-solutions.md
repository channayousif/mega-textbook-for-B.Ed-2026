(purpose, reference frame, timing) and its rubric keys formative, criterion-referenced and

satisfying Constitution Art. III.3 at the per-topic level. ERQ 1(b) and ERQ 2(a) are tagged

Apply sub-parts appear in the accepted Unit 2 bank, so this is a corpus-consistent reading of the
band rather than a Unit 1 defect, and it is not raised as a finding.
