Semester 4, Major: Professional, 3 (3-0) credit hours. Source:
`Scheme-and-Course-guides/extracted-text/4TH SEMESTER.txt` (the approved 2026 course guide, which

## Course-wide items

- **Course Learning Outcomes (guide, paraphrased for the spec record)**: after studying this
  course, prospective teachers will be able to:
  1. Understand the concept and nature of assessment
  2. Differentiate between standardized and classroom tests
  3. Integrate objectives with learning assessment
  4. Develop and analyse test items of different levels
  5. Understand different alternative classroom assessment techniques
  6. Interpret test scores and the results of different assessment techniques
- **Teaching strategies (guide)**: lectures, interactive discussions, question-answer sessions,
  demonstration, case-study analysis.
- **Assessment criteria**: no course-specific weighting in the guide; falls back to the
  Constitution Art. III.7 default - 60% summative / 40% formative.
- **Guide shape**: a 16-week outline. Weeks 8 and 16 are review and examination, not content, so
  the six units below partition the 14 content weeks.

## Unit 1: Understanding Assessment and Evaluation

Weeks 1-2. Unit Spec (G1) for `docs/semester-4/efmp-408/unit-01/`.

- **CLO/SLO refs**: `SLO:EFMP-408-1-1` (explain how measurement, test, assessment and evaluation differ and why assessment matters in teaching), `SLO:EFMP-408-1-2` (contrast formative and summative assessment by purpose, timing and use), `SLO:EFMP-408-1-3` (distinguish diagnostic and placement assessment and choose a norm- or criterion-referenced interpretation) - traces to guide outcomes 1 and 6. **Licence objectives**: 1, 2, 3, 12.
- **Key terms**: Assessment, Evaluation, Measurement, Test, Formative, Summative,
  Norm-referenced, Criterion-referenced.
- **Worked-example concept**: one class's end-of-term marks read three ways - as measurement, as
  assessment and as evaluation - so the distinction is shown rather than defined.
- **Common misconceptions**: "assessment and evaluation are the same word"; "formative means
  ungraded"; "norm-referenced means harder".
- **Assessment blueprint**: formative 9 items, 3 per topic (`D-2026-0072`) (Remember/Understand/Apply); summative includes one Analyze item asking the learner to classify a described assessment practice by purpose (formative or summative) and reference frame (norm or criterion) and justify it. Weighting: 60/40 default.
- **Prerequisite knowledge**: `EFMP-301` Educational Psychology and `EFMP-302` Teaching Profession (Semester 1). This is the opening unit of the course.
- **Mapped readings**: hec-2013-classroom-assessment, hec-2013-testdev, seifert-sutton-2009, scheerens-2003.
