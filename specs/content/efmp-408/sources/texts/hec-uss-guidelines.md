# Bound source evidence: hec-uss-guidelines

Source: Higher Education Commission. (n.d.). *Policy guidelines for the implementation of uniform semester system in higher education institutions of Pakistan*. HEC, Islamabad. The foreword describes it as the second document of the National Qualifications Framework, revised to be consistent with the 2015 NQF; no publication date is printed (the PDF was created in July 2018).
URL: https://bnu.edu.pk/portals/Final%20Policy%20Guidelines%202019_compressed%20%281%29.pdf (copy published by Beaconhouse National University; HEC lists the document on https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/universities/Pages/NCES.aspx)

Consulted: 2026-11-27, text extracted from the PDF with pdftotext.

## Evidence notes

1. Section 13.1, Fractionalized Grading System (p. 19): grade, grade points and "Percentage obtained in a Semester System": A 3.67-4.00, 85 and above; A- 3.34-3.66, 80-84; B+ 3.01-3.33, 75-79; B 2.67-3.00, 71-74; B- 2.34-2.66, 68-70; C+ 2.01-2.33, 64-67; C 1.67-2.00, 61-63; C- 1.31-1.66, 58-60; D+ 1.01-1.30, 54-57; D 0.10-1.00, 50-53; F 0.00, below 50.
2. Note to 13.1 (p. 19): a student with any CGPA "will be given the minimum of the corresponding percentage"; "if a student gets a CGPA of 3.00, the percentage associated will be 71%."
3. Section 12, Grading Policy (p. 14): HEIs may adopt relative grading (recommended above 20 students) or absolute grading (below 20).

## Unit 5 use

Note 1 supports F earning 0.00 grade points and the example bands (A 85 and above, B 71-74, F below 50) in Topic 5.1 (U5-02). Note 2 supports the CGPA-to-percentage rule and the 3.00 to 71 percent example (U5-02).
