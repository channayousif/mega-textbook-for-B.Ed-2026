## Evidence notes

1. Paragraph 1: the National Quality Assurance Committee, in its 22nd meeting on January 09, 2017, "decided the following relationship between letter grades and grade point averages for different scales". The table gives the grade points for letters A, B, C and D on scales of 4, 4.5, 5, 8 and 10:

| Letter grade | 4 | 4.5 | 5 | 8 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 4 | 4.5 | 5 | 8 | 10 |
| B | 3 | 3.375 | 3.75 | 6 | 7.5 |
| C | 2 | 2.25 | 2.5 | 4 | 5 |
| D | 1 | 1.125 | 1.25 | 2 | 2.5 |

2. Paragraph 2: "national universities following the semester system should preferably use four (04) grade point system", with A equivalent to 4.0, B to 3.0, C to 2.0 and D to 1.0.

## Unit 5 use

Note 2 supports the four-point scale table in Topic 5.1 (U5-02). Note 1 supports the statement that other scales are in use and the B equivalents on the 5 and 10-point scales (U5-02).
