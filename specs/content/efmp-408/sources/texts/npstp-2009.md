## Evidence notes

1. Standard 5 (Assessment): Standard statement establishes that teachers understand and use multiple assessment strategies to evaluate student learning, inform instruction, and guide student self-assessment.
2. Standard 5 Knowledge and Understanding (pp. 14-16): Teachers know different types of assessment (formative, summative, diagnostic, authentic, performance-based), measurement theory (validity, reliability, bias, item design), and understand the ethical, legal, and privacy constraints surrounding assessment data.
3. Standard 5 Dispositions (pp. 14-16): Teachers value assessment as an integral part of learning, believe all students can achieve high standards, value diverse learner backgrounds, and promote student self-evaluation.
4. Standard 5 Performance and Skills (pp. 15-17): Teachers align assessment tasks with National Curriculum SLOs, design rubrics, provide descriptive actionable feedback, adapt daily pedagogy based on student evidence, maintain secure confidential records, and communicate results constructively to parents.

## Unit 6 use

Notes 1-4 support Topic 6.2 (U6-06) and Topic 6.4 (U6-11, U6-12).
