## Notes

- The HEC course guides (`hec-2013-classroom-assessment` and `hec-2013-testdev`) are the official curriculum guides from which the Sindh Teaching Licence Part II examination syllabus was developed. The shared bound excerpts in `sources/texts/` carry the Unit 1 evidence notes; the Unit 2 evidence notes below were recorded here so that authoring Unit 2 did not invalidate Unit 1's accepted G2 evidence by editing a shared excerpt.
- **hec-2013-testdev, Unit 2 evidence:** Unit 3, "Taxonomy in testing: Using Bloom's Taxonomy in test development" (pp. 63-65) on choosing Bloom levels and command words; Unit 3, "Determining the behaviours to be assessed" (pp. 66-68) on learning outcomes, command words and the cognitive, affective and psychomotor outcomes; Unit 4, "Test reliability" (pp. 68-69) on interrater, split-half and test-retest reliability; Unit 4, "Test validity" (pp. 69-70) and "Types of reliability" (pp. 71-72) on content, construct, concurrent and predictive validity and on rubrics for scorer reliability; Unit 4, "Utility of test" (p. 73) on applying validity, reliability, objectivity, adequacy and differentiability.
- **hec-2013-classroom-assessment, Unit 2 evidence:** Unit 1, Week 1 (p. 16) on validity and reliability as interpretations of test scores rather than properties of tests; Unit 1, Week 6 (p. 60) on interpretations that should be valid and reliable; Unit 4 (p. 22) on effective and fair tests representing the taught material and on assessment as a non-neutral enterprise; Unit 4, Week 12 (pp. 87-88) on a fair test covering the topics taught and on the Table of Specifications as the blueprint; Unit 4 (p. 252) on the clear alignment of learning outcomes, instructional activities and assessment strategies.
- Seifert & Sutton (2009) is an open-access textbook licensed under CC BY 3.0, verified through OpenAlex on 2026-11-24 and excerpted in `sources/texts/seifert-sutton-2009.md`. Chapters 10 and 11 support the treatment of alignment as the integration of assessment with every phase of teaching.
