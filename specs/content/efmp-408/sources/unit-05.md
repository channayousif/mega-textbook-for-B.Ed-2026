## Notes

- `hec-2013-testdev` is one of the two HEC course guides the Sindh Teaching Licence Part II syllabus was written from, and the guide this unit's weeks come from. Its Unit 3 (Grading and reporting) and its teaching notes on measures of central tendency, measures of variability, difficulty level and discrimination power ground Topics 5.1 to 5.3. Bound notes at `sources/texts/hec-2013-testdev.md`.
- `openstax-statistics-2e` supplies measures of centre and spread for Topic 5.2. Bound notes at `sources/texts/openstax-statistics-2e.md`.
- `seifert-sutton-2009` grounds the reporting-as-communication idea in Topic 5.1. Bound notes at `sources/texts/seifert-sutton-2009.md`.
