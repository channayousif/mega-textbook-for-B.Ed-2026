## Finding F2 - "seven characteristics that appear in both sources"

`topic-01.mdx:91`: "The table below sets out seven characteristics that appear in
both sources."

The bound lists do not intersect on all seven:

| Characteristic (fig-U1-2) | OpenStax 1.3 | Butler p. 23 |
|---|---|---|
| Curiosity and alertness | curious | alertness to opportunities (not curiosity) |
| Creativity | creativity | creativity |
| Persistence | persistent | commitment (not persistence) |
| Willingness to take calculated risks | calculated risk / mitigate risks (1.1, 1.3) | willingness to take risks |
| Problem-solving | problem-solving skills | not in the trait list |
| Confidence | not in the list | confidence |
| Openness to learning | open-mindedness | not in the list |

At most three rows (creativity; willingness to take calculated risks; arguably
curiosity/alertness as a combined row) are supported by both lists. Confidence is
not in the OpenStax list and problem-solving / openness to learning are not in
Butler's list. The sentence overstates the sources. Repair: reword to say the
seven are drawn from the two sources (or "common to the two accounts"), not that
each appears in both.
