## Notes

- `openstax-entrepreneurship` is the course's core open text, mapped to Units 1-7 by the content spec. Unit 3 reads chapters 5, 8 and 11; the spec's Unit 3 row names chapters 5 and 11, and chapter 8 is added here because section 8.2 is the book's market-research chapter and is the closest match to the Week 5 leaves.
- `spriggs-2016-bmc` is an author-selected open-access source, beyond the spec's reading list, added under the author-unit skill's rule for a fact that needs a source: the spec maps no source to the canvas-as-a-teaching-tool point in U3-04. It is diamond-free to read and its teaching case matches the unit's classroom frame.
- The `scott-2017-efmd` edition is the 2nd edition, 2017, confirmed from the PDF's own cataloguing page (ISBN 978-0-9818-260-1-1). The PDF's embedded metadata carries the title of the author's earlier book, so the cataloguing page was used instead.
