# Bound source evidence: secp-registration

Source: https://www.secp.gov.pk/company-formation/

Consulted: 2026-11-24, official regulatory procedures confirmed.

Locator: Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. (n.d.). *Registration of company* (incorporation through eZfile under the Companies Act, 2017).

## Evidence notes

1. Under the Companies Act, 2017, company registration and incorporation are administered digitally by the SECP through its eZfile portal.
2. The incorporation process consists of five primary stages:
   - Name availability search and name reservation.
   - Drafting constitutive legal documents: Memorandum of Association (defining business scope and objects) and Articles of Association (defining internal governance rules).
   - Online submission of incorporation application, subscriber CNIC details, registered office address, and payment of official statutory filing fees.
   - Statutory compliance scrutiny by the Company Registration Office (CRO).
   - Issuance of a Digital Certificate of Incorporation containing a unique Corporate Universal Identification Number (CUIN).
3. Incorporation creates a separate legal entity distinct from its shareholders, conferring limited liability protection and perpetual succession.
4. Distinguishes incorporated companies (regulated by SECP under Companies Act 2017) from sole proprietorships (registered with FBR for NTN) and partnerships (registered with District Registrar of Firms under Partnership Act 1932).
