## Evidence notes (paraphrased from the abstract)

1. The review uses a systematic literature review method and analyses 29 peer-reviewed articles published between 2006 and 2024 on risk management in SMEs.
2. Effective risk management improves SME operations by letting a business identify, assess and mitigate potential risks; structured frameworks increase resilience and help a business handle uncertainty.
3. Limited resources and a lack of awareness hinder wider adoption of risk management practices in SMEs, which is why the topic is taught rather than assumed.

Use in GENT-401 Unit 7: note 2 supports the definition of risk management and the three-stage habit of identifying, assessing and reducing risk in Topic 7.1; note 3 supports the teaching premise that these are learned practices. The risk matrix itself is taken from the OpenStax textbook, not from this article.
