Locator: Wati, C. R., & Winarno, A. (2018). The performance of crowdfunding model as an alternative funding source for micro, small, and medium-scale businesses in various countries. *KnE Social Sciences, 3*(3), 16-33. First International Research Conference on Economics and Business.

## Evidence notes (paraphrased; short phrases quoted)

1. The authors describe crowdfunding as an alternative funding source for micro, small and medium-scale businesses, used to solve the problem of funding through an online campaign.
2. The research compares the success of different crowdfunding models and the factors that influence their success. It found that the highest funding was achieved by the equity-based crowdfunding model.
3. The funding target, the total number of backers and the minimum sum of investment affected success positively, while the time range of funding did not affect success significantly.
4. The paper notes that small and medium enterprises often face major constraints on capital and funding, and that the funding cycle and sources differ as a company grows.

Use in GENT-401 Unit 5: notes 1, 3 and 4 support the crowdfunding definition and the success factors in Topic 5.2. The study's setting (Indonesia and other countries) is not presented as Pakistani evidence.
