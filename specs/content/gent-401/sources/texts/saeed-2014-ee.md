## Evidence notes (from the abstract)

1. The study examines how a university's support affects students' entrepreneurial intentions.
2. Entrepreneurship education, concept-development support and business-development support increase such intentions; students see education and concept-development support as highly influential.
3. The sample was 805 undergraduate students at universities in Pakistan.

Use in GENT-401 Unit 1: Topic 1.1 cites notes 2 and 3 as Pakistani evidence that entrepreneurial capacity can be developed through education, against the misconception that entrepreneurs are born, not made. The unit claims only what the abstract states.
