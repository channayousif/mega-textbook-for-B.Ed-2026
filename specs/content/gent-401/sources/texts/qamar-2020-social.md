## Evidence notes (from the abstract)

1. The paper examines social entrepreneurship (SE) in Pakistan. SE is gaining global scholarly attention, but its development in Pakistan remains moderate, and research has largely centred on ideological and definitional debates.
2. The study draws on 14 in-depth semi-structured interviews with leading social entrepreneurs, practitioners and academicians.
3. It identifies the challenges and prospects these entrepreneurs encounter, offers theoretical insights, and argues that local and national government could use the findings to raise awareness of the "fourth sector", strengthening the country's social capital.
