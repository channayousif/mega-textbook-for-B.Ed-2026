# Bound source evidence: ipo-pakistan

Source: https://ipo.gov.pk/

Consulted: 2026-11-24, statutory authorities and procedures confirmed.

Locator: Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan. (n.d.). *IPO-Pakistan* (patents, designs, copyright, trademarks, geographical indications).

## Evidence notes

1. Established under the Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan Act, 2012 as the focal autonomous organization for administering and managing intellectual property rights in Pakistan.
2. Oversees four primary branches of intellectual property:
   - Trademarks under Trade Marks Ordinance, 2001: protects distinctive words, logos, slogans, and symbols distinguishing goods and services; initial term 10 years, renewable indefinitely.
   - Copyright under Copyright Ordinance, 1962: protects original literary, artistic, musical, dramatic works, computer software, and instructional textbooks; protects tangible expression rather than abstract ideas; term author's life plus 50 years.
   - Patents under Patents Ordinance, 2000: protects new, inventive, industrially applicable technical solutions and processes; term 20 years from filing date.
   - Industrial Designs under Registered Designs Ordinance, 2000: protects novel, aesthetic shapes, surface patterns, and configurations of manufactured articles; initial term 10 years, extendable up to 20 years.
3. IP protection principle: ideas existing solely in human thought carry zero legal protection; rights attach only upon reduction to a tangible medium (copyright) or formal examination and registration with IPO-Pakistan (trademark, patent, industrial design).
