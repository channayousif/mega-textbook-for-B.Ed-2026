## Evidence notes (paraphrased from the abstract)

1. The study reports a weekly assignment in which students work in small teams as entrepreneurs, pitching marketing recommendations to real small business owners, in an upper-level marketing elective.
2. An end-of-semester survey of 25 students found the assignment well rated for being enjoyable and helpful, and students said they would take another course offering similar assignments.
3. The authors present the assignment as experiential learning that works in face-to-face, hybrid and online formats.

Use in GENT-401 Unit 7: notes 1-2 support the practical work in Topic 7.2, where trainees pitch in teams to a panel and receive feedback on the delivery rather than the idea. The seven-part pitch structure itself is taken from the OpenStax textbook, not from this article.
