## Evidence notes (from the abstract)

1. The research identifies startup characteristics and the relationship between the development phase of a startup and its sources of financing, using data from the Startup Poland Foundation and the European Startup Monitor 2015.
2. The startup life cycle includes phases of development, and these influence the choice of funding sources, which divide into classical and alternative sources.
3. The classical sources are the founding capital, bootstrapping and the so-called FFF (friends, family and fools). The alternative sources are crowdfunding, business angels and venture capital.
4. In Poland, startups are financed mainly by the founders' capital and make little use of alternative sources of financing.

Use in GENT-401 Unit 5: note 3 supports the classification of funding sources in Topic 5.2, and note 4 supports the point that most small ventures start with the founder's own money. The Polish setting is not presented as Pakistani evidence.
