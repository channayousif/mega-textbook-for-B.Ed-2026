## Evidence notes (paraphrased; short phrases quoted)

1. Opening: "Students and teachers can use the Business Model Canvas to describe, organize and design a business model for an entrepreneurial venture or a business development opportunity in an existing business." The article is built as a teaching case that can be used alone or with Osterwalder's *Business Model Generation*.
2. The case: Brian wants his son to learn to drive a manual transmission without using his own sports car. He researches whether driving schools nearby teach it, who might want it (newly licensed drivers, valets, commercial truck drivers, buyers of manual sports cars), how long learning takes, what certifications an instructor needs, what the market size is, and what competitors, barriers to entry and operating costs look like.
3. Stated teaching purposes: explain the nine building blocks of the Business Model Canvas; use the canvas "as a tool to communicate and coordinate the business development process"; and understand how hypothesis testing refines or pivots the idea.
4. Value propositions offered for the case, each naming a job and a gain: "Helping you learn to drive a manual transmission safely"; "Training you to be a complete driver; able to drive any car or truck"; "Teaching you to drive the car you always dreamed of owning"; "Expanding your driving possibilities"; "Learn to drive a manual transmission without harming the car or your friendships".
5. Closing: the canvas is used before a full plan, as "an organizing framework, a communication tool, and a roadmap that increases the likelihood of success for the business". The article contrasts this with "a more traditional business plan development process that focuses on writing the complete plan before getting feedback from customers to test the components of the business plan".

Use in GENT-401 Unit 3: notes 1-3 support U3-04 (the canvas as a tool, the nine blocks, and testing an idea rather than writing the whole plan first); note 4 supports U3-05 (candidate value propositions for one venture). The case's own research questions also mirror the U3-01 and U3-03 material, but the source is not cited for those sub-topics in the coverage matrix.
