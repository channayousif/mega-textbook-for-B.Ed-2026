## Notes

- The break-even example in Topic 5.1 (fixed costs Rs 20,000, fee Rs 2,000, variable cost Rs 400, break-even 13 pupils) is the author's teaching device, built on the formula in OpenStax section 9.4 and Scott chapter 13. The numbers are chosen for a small tuition centre in Larkana and are not a case study of a real business.
- The three open-access articles are used only where they directly support a sub-topic: break-even analysis (Sintha, 2020), crowdfunding success factors (Wati & Winarno, 2018), and the classical-versus-alternative funding source split (Cegielska & Zawadzka, 2018). The Polish and Indonesian settings are not presented as Pakistani evidence.
- Scott (2017) is a free PDF from the publisher's own site. It is cited by chapter and section; the page numbers in the printed text are those of the PDF.
