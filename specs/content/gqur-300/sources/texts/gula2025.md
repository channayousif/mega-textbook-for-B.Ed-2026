## Record summary

The authors ask whether math educators should care about numeracy education research and
whether math departments ought to add a numeracy course. They observe that numeracy "is
often conflated with math-lite and dismissed as not useful to mathematicians and math
educators", and that the term gets "used interchangeably with quantitative reasoning (QR),
quantitative literacy (QL), and mathematics literacy (ML)". They adopt "numeracy" as an
umbrella term, and argue that numeracy tasks constitute their own category of mathematics
task - distinct both from word problems and from mathematical modelling tasks. They
advocate quality numeracy tasks, "those that inspire transfer between concrete and abstract
thinking spaces", and present a case study of their course "Numbers for Life".
