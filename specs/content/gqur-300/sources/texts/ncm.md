## Record summary

The National Curriculum Council, under the Ministry of Federal Education and Professional
Training, publishes the national mathematics curriculum for Pakistan covering Grades 1-12,
organised as a progression grid across the grades plus suggested guidelines for Grades 1-8
and 9-12, with a separate Functional Mathematics stream for Grades 9-10.
