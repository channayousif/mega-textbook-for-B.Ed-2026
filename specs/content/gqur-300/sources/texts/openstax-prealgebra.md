## Record summary

*Prealgebra 2e* is an open-access prealgebra textbook with 11 chapters: Whole Numbers; The
Language of Algebra; Integers; Fractions; Decimals; Percents; The Properties of Real
Numbers; Solving Linear Equations; Math Models and Geometry; Polynomials; Graphs. Chapter 5
Section 5.7 covers simplifying and using square roots, including estimating and
approximating square roots and applying them to areas. Chapter 9 covers math models and
geometry, including linear, square and cubic measures and the properties of rectangles,
triangles and trapezoids, and volume and surface area applications.
