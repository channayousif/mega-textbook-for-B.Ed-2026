## Record summary

*Prealgebra 2e* is an open-access prealgebra textbook with 11 chapters: Whole Numbers; The
Language of Algebra; Integers; Fractions; Decimals; Percents; The Properties of Real
Numbers; Solving Linear Equations; Math Models and Geometry; Polynomials; Graphs. Chapter 5
Section 5.7 covers simplifying and using square roots, including estimating and
approximating square roots and applying them to areas. Section 5.5, Averages and
Probability, lists four learning objectives - calculate the mean of a set of numbers,
find the median of a set of numbers, find (identify) the mode of a set of numbers, and
use the basic definition of probability - and its mode subsection defines frequency and
builds frequency tables in worked Examples 5.54 (student ages) and 5.55 (student
heights). The section contains no bar graphs, pie charts or histograms. Chapter 9 covers
math models and geometry, including linear, square and cubic measures and the properties
of rectangles, triangles and trapezoids, and volume and surface area applications.
