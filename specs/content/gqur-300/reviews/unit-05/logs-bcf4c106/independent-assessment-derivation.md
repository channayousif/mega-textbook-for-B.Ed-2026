### Item 1

- **Independent Solution**: A data inquiry begins with formulating a question, then collecting raw data, organizing it (e.g. tally, frequency table), summarizing it into key indicators/displays, and finally interpreting what the numbers say in context. Order: question, collect, organize, summarize, interpret.

- **Author Key**: (b)
- **Verdict**: Agreement.

### Item 2
- **Prompt**: To learn how many pupils arrive after the bell each day this week, the best method is:
- **Independent Solution**: Arriving after the bell is an event occurring in real time. Survey relies on pupil self-report and recall bias; census is national demographic data; pie chart is a display, not a collection method. Watching and counting arrivals directly at the school gate yields exact factual counts.
- **Derived Option**: (b) observation at the gate
- **Author Key**: (b)
- **Verdict**: Agreement.

### Item 3
- **Prompt**: A survey of 40 returns frequencies 12, 14 and 9. The table tells you:
- **Independent Solution**: Sum of reported frequencies = 12 + 14 + 9 = 35. Sample size = 40. 40 - 35 = 5 responses are unrecorded or missing.
- **Derived Option**: (b) 5 responses are unrecorded
- **Author Key**: (b)
- **Verdict**: Agreement.

### Item 4
- **Prompt**: The display that best shows each travel mode's share of the class is:
- **Independent Solution**: When representing parts/shares of a single defined whole (100% of the class), a pie chart is the archetypal display. Bar charts compare category counts; line graphs show trends over time.
- **Derived Option**: (c) a pie chart
- **Author Key**: (c)
- **Verdict**: Agreement.

### Item 5
- **Prompt**: The display that best tracks attendance across a week is:
- **Independent Solution**: Tracking a continuous or ordered variable across time (Monday to Friday) is the explicit function of a line graph, where slopes between points indicate daily rise and fall.
- **Derived Option**: (b) a line graph
- **Author Key**: (b)
- **Verdict**: Agreement.

### Item 6
- **Prompt**: A category that is 30 percent of the whole takes a pie-slice angle of:
- **Independent Solution**: A full circle has 360 degrees. Angle = 30% * 360° = 0.30 * 360° = 108°.
- **Derived Option**: (c) 108 degrees
- **Author Key**: (c)
- **Verdict**: Agreement.

### Item 7
- **Prompt**: A bar chart whose vertical axis starts at 10 instead of 0:
- **Independent Solution**: When the vertical baseline is cut (truncated axis), the visual heights of bars represent differences from 10 rather than proportions of the total value. A bar of 12 (height 2) looks half the size of a bar of 14 (height 4), exaggerating small differences.
- **Derived Option**: (b) exaggerates small differences between bars
- **Author Key**: (b)
- **Verdict**: Agreement.

### Item 8
- **Prompt**: For the takings 4,000, 4,200, 4,100, 4,300, 39,000 rupees, the honest "typical day" is the:
- **Independent Solution**: Sum = 4,000 + 4,200 + 4,100 + 4,300 + 39,000 = 55,600. Mean = 55,600 / 5 = 11,120 rupees. The 39,000 outlier drags the mean upwards to a figure higher than any ordinary day. Sorted values: 4,000, 4,100, 4,200, 4,300, 39,000. Median = 4,200 rupees, which accurately reflects ordinary daily earnings.
- **Derived Option**: (b) median, 4,200 rupees
- **Author Key**: (b)
- **Verdict**: Agreement.

### Item 9
- **Prompt**: The only average that works for categories (like travel modes) is the:
- **Independent Solution**: Categorical (nominal) data such as "walk", "van", "rickshaw" cannot be summed or sorted numerically, ruling out mean and median. The mode identifies the category with the highest frequency.
- **Derived Option**: (c) mode
- **Author Key**: (c)
- **Verdict**: Agreement.

### Item 10
- **Prompt**: Sindh's 2023 literacy rate of 57.54 percent is measured on the base:
- **Independent Solution**: Standard Pakistan Bureau of Statistics census literacy definitions measure literacy among the population aged 10 years and above.
- **Derived Option**: (b) 10 years and above
- **Author Key**: (b)
- **Verdict**: Agreement.

- **Author Model**: Matches. 2 marks (1 for gap identification, 1 for correct remedial action).

### RRQ 3
- **Derived Solution**:
  Dataset: 20, 45, 50, 55, 60, 62, 62, 70, 70, 70, 96 (n = 11).
  Sum = 20 + 45 + 50 + 55 + 60 + 62 + 62 + 70 + 70 + 70 + 96 = 660.
  Mean = 660 / 11 = 60.
  Median: 11 values, position (11 + 1)/2 = 6th value = 62.
  Mode: 70 appears three times (all others appear at most twice). Mode = 70.
- **Author Model**: Matches (mean 60, median 62, mode 70). 3 marks (1 each).

### RRQ 5
