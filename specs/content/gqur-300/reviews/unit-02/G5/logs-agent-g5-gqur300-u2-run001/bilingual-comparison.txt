F3 (blocking) topic-02.mdx:55-61 EN vs UR:53-56 - MISCONCEPTION PARAGRAPH
COMPRESSED AND ITS INVARIANT CHANGED. EN: trainees add instead of multiplying:

Dropped: the batch-feeds-20 explanation, the 50-person worked counterexample with

check-item 5 (UR:95-97) and RRQ-06 (UR:108-109) quote the full batch-addition
rule ("ایک اور بخش 6 چمچ چینی اور 4 چمچ پتی") which the UR explanation never
establishes; the UR unit is internally inconsistent and the English repair (G3

F8 (uncertain, terminology dispute) "Whole number" -> "تمام اعداد" throughout
(index UR:2,3,21; topic-01 UR:2,3,21,37,39; topic-03 UR:94; unit-assessment UR:36,
145; teacher-notes UR:41; fig-U2-1.ur.svg labels; concepts/unit-02.md CON-2-1) vs
English "whole number(s)". "تمام اعداد" literally means "all numbers"; the
standard Urdu mathematics term is "مکمل اعداد". The choice is consistent across
the unit and is embedded in glossary.json (the Integer entry's Urdu definition
says "کوئی تمام عدد یا اس کا منفی"), but it is not a banked term
(specs/content/terminology.csv has no mathematics entries) and MCQ-1's stem "کس
فہرست میں صرف تمام اعداد ہیں؟" reads as "which list contains only all numbers?",
which can confuse an examinee. Owner ruling requested: bank "Whole number" (proposed
مکمل اعداد) or bless تمام اعداد, and align glossary.json.

F9 (uncertain, terminology dispute) "Exponent" -> "وسیع" (topic-03 UR:40,52) vs
English "exponent". "وسیع" means "wide/spacious"; the standard term is "قوت نما"
(also اسّی). Again consistent with glossary.json (whose Exponent entry uses
"وسیع (exponent)"), not banked, and misleading as a literal calque. Owner ruling
requested alongside F8.
