## Unverifiable sources

Bound excerpts could not be committed for these keys. Recorded here so `check:depth-gate`
and any G3 reviewer see the limitation explicitly rather than inferring it from silence.

- digcomp2022: the DOI resolves to the official Publications Office record, but
  op.europa.eu serves HTTP 429/no content to this host, so the report text could not be
  bound. The specific claim cited in prose (DigComp 2.2 describes digital competence in
  five areas and twenty-one competences) is corroborated by the European Commission's Joint
  Research Centre DigComp page (joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/digcomp, read
  2026-09-23, confirming the framework and its 2022 edition) and by multiple secondary
  descriptions of the publication. Could not be retrieved on 2026-09-23. Owner ruling
  (D-2026-0001): flag and proceed.
- khanacademyCIT: the course pages are JavaScript-rendered and could not be fetched as text
  by this host on 2026-09-23; the course's existence, provider and topic coverage (how
  computers work, bits and binary, data storage, the internet) were corroborated through a
  web search of Khan Academy's catalogue on 2026-09-23. Used as a beginner-level
  corroborating account of the standard history and hardware story that the bound
  bourgeois2019 excerpt independently supports. Owner ruling (D-2026-0001): flag and
  proceed.
- shellyVermaat: print-only monograph (Cengage), no open-access text retrievable by this
  host. The unit's use is at the level of the standard generations and classification
  account, which the open-access sources above corroborate independently. Could not be
  retrieved on 2026-09-23. Owner ruling (D-2026-0001): flag and proceed.
- norton: print-only monograph (McGraw-Hill), no open-access text retrievable by this host.
  Could not be retrieved on 2026-09-23. Owner ruling (D-2026-0001): flag and proceed.
- hecICT: official publication; the HEC policy-guidelines portal page was verified to exist
  on 2026-09-23 (the evaluator's D-2026-0030 basis records the same check), but the full
  text was not retrievable by this host for excerpt binding. Used at policy-priority level
  only. Owner ruling (D-2026-0001): flag and proceed.
- unescoictcft2018: unesdoc.unesco.org serves HTTP 403 to this host, so the PDF text could
  not be bound; the document's existence, title and framework structure (three levels, six
  aspects) are corroborated by the ISBN record (978-92-3-100277-8) and multiple secondary
  sources. Could not be retrieved on 2026-09-23. Owner ruling (D-2026-0001): flag and
  proceed.
- ptaIndicators2025: the PTA indicators page is JavaScript-rendered and could not be fetched
  as text by this host on 2026-09-23; the figures used in prose (more than 190 million
  cellular subscriptions, roughly 140 million broadband connections, mobile-dominant
  access) are corroborated by multiple 2025 reports of PTA's published indicators and are
  stated in prose with "reported" and "roughly" hedges. Owner ruling (D-2026-0001): flag
  and proceed.
