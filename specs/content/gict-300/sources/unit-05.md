## Unverifiable sources

Bound excerpts could not be committed for these keys. Recorded here so `check:depth-gate`
and any G3 reviewer see the limitation explicitly rather than inferring it from silence.

- digcomp2022: the DOI resolves to the official Publications Office record, but
  op.europa.eu serves HTTP 429/no content to this host, so the report text could not be
  bound. The specific claim cited in prose (DigComp's five competence areas) is
  corroborated by the European Commission's Joint Research Centre DigComp page
  (joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/digcomp, read 2026-09-23) and by multiple secondary
  descriptions. Could not be retrieved on 2026-09-23. Owner ruling (D-2026-0001): flag
  and proceed.
- purdueOwl: the Avoiding Plagiarism section page was fetched and read directly on
  2026-09-23 (definition of plagiarism as using another's ideas or words without proper
  credit, and the best-practices pages confirmed), but the multi-page section's text was
  not committed as an excerpt file; the two-part honest-use habit (make clear what is
  borrowed, and from where) rests on this read page and on the unit's own workflow, not on
  any bound excerpt. Owner ruling (D-2026-0001): flag and proceed.
- commonsenseDC: the curriculum page was fetched and read directly on 2026-09-23 (its six
  topic areas, including digital footprint and identity, relationships and communication,
  confirmed); the topic-area names it supports are corroborated by the fetched page
  itself, and no further claim rests on it. Owner ruling (D-2026-0001): flag and proceed.
- ncaStaysafe: as declared in unit-04; the site was verified to exist on 2026-09-23 and
  its guidance pages are JavaScript-heavy. Owner ruling (D-2026-0001): flag and proceed.
- hecICT: as declared in unit-01; the HEC policy-guidelines portal page was verified to
  exist on 2026-09-23, but the full text was not retrievable for excerpt binding. Used at
  policy-priority level only. Owner ruling (D-2026-0001): flag and proceed.
