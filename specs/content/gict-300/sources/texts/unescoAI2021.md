## The guidance's framing (corroborated)

The guidance is intended to guide and orient the effective and equitable use of AI in
education, addressing both policy-makers and learning providers. Its key themes,
consistent across the corroborating records: human-centred AI in education; ethics,
gender equality and equity in AI's deployment for learning; and the positioning of AI as
a tool to enhance human capacity in teaching and learning rather than to replace
teachers, with human accountability for outcomes.

Used for: UNESCO's framing rule for AI in education - enhance rather than replace, with
human accountability (U6-05).
