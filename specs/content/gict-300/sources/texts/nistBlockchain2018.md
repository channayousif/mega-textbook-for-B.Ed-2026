## The overview's key content (verified)

The publication is a high-level technical overview explaining how blockchain technology
works, covering consensus models, cryptographic hash functions, smart contracts, and
distributed ledgers. Its framing of the technology: blockchains are tamper-evident,
distributed ledgers - records replicated across many holders, with each new block
cryptographically linked to its predecessor so that altering an old entry breaks the
chain of seals and every copy-holder can see the break. The report describes how
consensus among distrusting parties allows the shared record to be extended without a
central editor, and surveys the technology's use beyond its first application
(cryptocurrencies).

Used for: the blockchain definition, its tamper-evident distributed-ledger property, and
the ledger-design-versus-application distinction (U6-06).
