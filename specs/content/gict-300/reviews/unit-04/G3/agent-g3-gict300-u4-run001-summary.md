## Blocking findings (repair required)

1. **RRQ per-topic distribution violates the approved blueprint.** `unit-assessment.mdx` RRQ items 1-10 map 5 to topic 4.1, 1 to 4.2, 4 to 4.3 and **0 to 4.4**. The spec (`content-spec.md` lines 327-331) and the file's own `blooms_summary` require at least 2 RRQs per topic. No RRQ covers personal data, the four data questions, consent or collect-the-minimum. The depth gate counts items per band, not per topic, so G2 could not catch this.
2. **Citation beyond the bound source.** `topic-01.mdx` lines 46-47 attribute "hit tens of thousands of computers" to Bourgeois et al. (2019); the bound excerpt records only that the I Love You virus "spread through an email attachment".
3. **Sources-file mapping not carried by the bound excerpt.** `sources/unit-04.md` line 19 maps bourgeois2019 to "footprint framing (U4-03)" and "data protection framing (U4-06)", neither of which appears anywhere in the bound excerpt. U4-03 and U4-06 therefore rest entirely on declared-unretrievable primaries, and the ncaStaysafe declaration mitigates only its habits role while the table makes it U4-03's primary.
4. **Unused declared source and false preamble.** `sources/unit-04.md` claims every key is "cited both in prose and in a per-topic ## Further reading section", but erendorYildirim2022 (a registry-verified open-access source whose excerpt claims U4-01/U4-02 framing use) appears only in topic-01's Further reading; no prose cites it. laudonLaudon and stairReynolds are also Further-reading-only (the sanctioned title-level pattern for guide-required print monographs, so the preamble sentence is what is wrong).
