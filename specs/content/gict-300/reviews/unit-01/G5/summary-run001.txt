DISPOSITION: ESCALATE

WHY ESCALATE, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
The Urdu mirror itself is close to excellent: complete, structurally aligned, semantically
faithful, with an equivalent assessment bank and clean RTL rendering. But (1) the English
dependency required by the G5 rubric does not exist for these bytes - the only G3 report
(run001) is disposition "revise" and binds the PRE-repair English, while the English under
review changed on 8 files after that report (the 140dd8a repairs and the 6361ad6 course-overview
rewrite) with no fresh G3 recheck; and (2) one terminology label is disputed and needs an owner
ruling. Neither can be repaired by editing Urdu prose, so the correct disposition is escalate,
with the four blocking content findings below listed precisely so a repair cycle can clear
everything at once.
