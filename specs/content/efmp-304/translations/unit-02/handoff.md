answers and rubrics, and the final bounded Urdu answers section. Options use الف، ب، ج، د in
original order. Translator self-solve sequence: B, C, B, B, B, A, B, C, B, C. This is a
translator's check, not an independent review result.

with mirrored reading order and Nastaliq labels. `.ur.dark.svg` twins are generated by the
repository variant builder. All original SVGs and their English dark twins are untouched.
