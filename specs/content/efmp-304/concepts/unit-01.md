# Concept graph - EFMP-304 Unit 1 (Understanding Critical Thinking)

Per `specs/016-concept-graph-v4/contracts/concept-graph.md`. The fourth per-unit governance table,
beside `coverage/unit-01.md`, `sources/unit-01.md` and `figures/unit-01.md`. Those record what the
unit covers, what grounds it and what it shows; this records **what a learner must understand, and
in what order**.

`Topic` values are the `### Topic list` labels in `specs/content/efmp-304/content-spec.md`
(`## Unit 1`). `Assessment item IDs` are **derived** from `unit-assessment.mdx`'s existing
numbering under its three bank headings - no prose was changed to create them.

**Urdu labels.** One is drawn from `specs/content/terminology.csv`, the approved bank: `Critical
Thinking`. The remaining are authored here and **carry a G5 flag** (see below): a governance file
is not published prose, so it never passes the EN/UR parity gate and nothing else would catch a
poor label.
