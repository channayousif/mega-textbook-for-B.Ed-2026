## Limits

These locators support underlying distinctions, not the claim that every school argument falls
into exactly four types. The unit's broad evidence category and feasibility/value/alternatives
policy checklist are explicitly labelled teaching tools demonstrated on its school examples.
They overlap: a practical conclusion may use analogy, evidence or relevant expertise.

The rhetorical-triangle audience examples and the direct-check versus weighing distinction are
worked analyses, not claims that this source prescribes fixed roles for heads, colleagues or parents.
The unit's classification-first exercise method is not attributed to Yu and Zenker on this basis.
