## Summary (verified from abstract and metadata)

This article examines reflective practices in teacher education contexts. Kuswandono argues that
reflective practice is a professional capability that teacher-education programmes should develop
systematically, and that trainee teachers who engage in structured reflection develop stronger
professional identities and more adaptive teaching practices. The study was conducted with
pre-service teachers in an Indonesian teacher-education programme.

## What this supports in EFMP-304 Unit 4

- U4-01: supports the claim that reflective practice is a professional capability that can be
  trained, not a personality trait - consistent with the unit's framing of reflective thinking as
  a skill that can be developed.
- U4-04: supports the claim that reflection produces professional growth (Topic 4.2), though the
  evidence base is from Indonesian teacher education rather than Pakistani schools in particular.

## Scope note

The study is from Indonesian teacher education. It supports the *mechanism* (reflection develops
professional capability) but not claims about Pakistani classrooms specifically.
