## Unverifiable sources

- `bassham2010`: bibliographic-only, as required by D-2026-0013. Retrieval attempt on
  **2026-11-03**: searched for the 2010 fourth edition and opened the publisher product page
  https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/Critical-Thinking-A-Students-Introduction-Bassham.html.
  It supplies a newer release's catalogue, purchase and access options, not an accessible verified
  fourth-edition full text. Search results include third-party copies, which were not used to
  establish an authorised edition. No quotation, taxonomy or particular distinction is attributed
  to the unopened book. Each topic's reading entry states this limit at the point of use.
- `malone2003`: the stored file is a scope summary, not passage-level evidence for every previous
  attribution. Unit 2 no longer attributes the real-discourse teaching recommendation or its
  support test to that note. Use the verified Van Cleave locators for those analytical distinctions;
  retain Malone as qualified further reading, not proof of specific recommendations.
- `yuzenker2022`: title/abstract only. The classification-before-evaluation claim and purported
  phrase are withdrawn; no full-text finding is inferred from that absence. Removed from Unit 2
  reading and coverage bindings; the shared note is left unchanged for the course owner to audit
  against Unit 3 separately.
- `qin2016`: supports the qualified sixteen-student instructional finding in Topic 2.2, not a
  universal taxonomy of arguments.
- The four overlapping teaching labels and three-part policy checklist are this unit's school
  analysis tools. Van Cleave verifies the underlying distinctions, not this exact four-part list.
