# Figure manifest - EFMP-302 Unit 6 (Sustaining Professionalism through Continuous Learning)

Per `specs/009-figure-rendering/contracts/figure-manifest-v2.md` (v3 `Kind` vocabulary, Spec 012).
One row per figure carrier in the unit's `topic-*.mdx` files; `Topic` is the `topic_label` of the
file the carrier sits in. **Two figures per topic** across four topics, and the unit carries one
`concept-map` (`fig-U6-2`), one `timeline` (`fig-U6-3`) and two `flowchart`s (`fig-U6-6`,
`fig-U6-8`) as its schematics.

Every row is `Status: placed` with a committed `Src`, and every `Kind` is a deterministic
schematic. Under ADR-0024 these are Claude's to author as self-contained SVG; no row here is a
raster `illustration`, so nothing in this unit is a Codex handoff.

The unit is `translation_status: draft` with no Urdu mirror on disk, so the bilingual figure rule
does not apply yet.
