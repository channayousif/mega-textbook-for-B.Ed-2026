# Sources consulted - EFMP-302 Unit 2 (Models and Frameworks of Professional Ethics and Behaviors)

Per `specs/007-content-depth-standard/contracts/sources-consulted.md`. Every key cited in
`specs/content/efmp-302/coverage/unit-02.md` appears here, and every key here is cited both in
prose and in a per-topic `## Further reading` section.

Verified 2026-09-14. `bebeau1999` and `ehrich2011` were confirmed against the **Crossref
registry** (title, authors, journal, year, volume, issue and pages all match). Direct fetch of
publisher landing pages is blocked from this host: SAGE, Taylor & Francis and UNESCO all return
HTTP 403 to it, while other DOIs resolve normally from the same machine, so the 403s are a
network condition rather than evidence about the URLs. Verification therefore went through the
DOI registry, which is the authoritative record.

## Source gaps, after the G3 review (2026-09-15)

Three gaps, revised after review. The first two were declared at drafting; the second was
declared **wrongly**, and the third was missed entirely.

- **Rest's four-component model** (U2-10) is named in the guide with no reference and is grounded
  in `bebeau1999`, in which Rest is a co-author. The citation is Crossref-verified, but **the full
  text could not be retrieved** from this host (SAGE 403, the author-hosted copy 403, no open
  location reported). The prose is written to attribute the model to Rest and only the exposition
  to the 1999 paper. The claim that the paper sets out the failure mode of each component is
  **unverified against the source text** and should be checked before this unit is certified.
- **NACTE guidelines** (U2-09). The drafting note claimed these resolve to `npst-pakistan-2009`
  because NACTE "publishes no separate code of ethics" and accredits against the national
  standards. **That was wrong**, and it was an argument from a failed search rather than from a
  document. Review evidence indicates NACTE does publish its own accreditation standards for
  teacher education programmes, in which the national standards appear as one indicator. Neither
  the reviewer's finding nor the original claim could be re-verified from this host, which cannot
  reach `nacte.org.pk` at all. The unsupported sentence has been removed from `topic-02.mdx` and
  from the assessment bank, and the reference is now recorded as an **unresolved gap** in
  `specs/gaps.md` rather than filled with a substitute.
- **The four-step Empathy, Context, Reflect, Action framework** (U2-11) has **no source**. It was
  mapped to `ehrich2011` at drafting; that paper contains a different, five-part critical-incident
  model. The framework is named in the course guide and is taught here as guide-given, with a
  `no-external-source` row below and an entry in `specs/gaps.md`.

Two further citation defects found at G3 and corrected in prose: `ehrich2011` was described as
having *found* that teachers weigh institutional against pupil interests, when the paper is
conceptual and illustrated by constructed scenarios; and its model's competing forces were
misstated, including placing the pupil at a centre occupied by the decision maker.
