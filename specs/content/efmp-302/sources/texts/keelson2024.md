## Record summary

Quantitative questionnaire study of **614 participants** from **a Technical University**, across
education programmes, collected by Google Form on five-point Likert scales. Finds significant
associations between specific non-verbal behaviours (facial expressions, gestures) and positive
perceptions of teaching quality, with gender and age moderating, though students placed limited
weight on those demographic factors.
