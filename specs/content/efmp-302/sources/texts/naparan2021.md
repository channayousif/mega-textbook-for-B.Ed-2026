## Record summary

Qualitative study of **ten multigrade teachers** in the East district of Tukuran, Zamboanga del
Sur, **Philippines**, across six remote elementary schools, each teacher handling two grade
levels at once. Data from semi-structured interviews.

Strategies identified: classroom management; collaborative learning; differentiated instruction;
connecting instruction to real-world contexts; integrating technology; and teacher flexibility.

The study concludes that multigrade instruction can benefit learners where teachers use
deliberately designed strategies, while acknowledging significant obstacles for learners in that
setting.

## Scope note

Ten teachers, one district, one country, interview-based. This supports a claim about *what
multigrade teachers report doing*, not a claim about outcomes or about multigrade teaching in
Pakistan. Any transfer to the Pakistani context in the unit is the authors' inference and should
be marked as such.

## What this supports
