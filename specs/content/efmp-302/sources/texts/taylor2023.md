# taylor2023 - verification excerpt

Taylor, S., & Thion, S. (2023). How has teaching effectiveness been conceptualized? Questioning
the consistency between definition and measure. *Frontiers in Education, 8*.
DOI 10.3389/feduc.2023.1253622. Retrieved 2026-09-18 from frontiersin.org (open access).

**Provenance:** verbatim publisher text, quoted from the open-access full text.

## Abstract

> "there is a lack of consistency in how TE has been conceptualized: many papers used inaccurate
> or implied definitions of TE"

## Introduction (section 1)

> "The lack of defining TE and the inconsistency in measuring may have been detrimental to TE
> research"

Quoting Campbell et al. (2004) in the same section:

> "an agreed-upon conceptualization (i.e., definition and measurement) of TE does not exist"

## Discussion (section 4)

> "The literature often does not define TE, even when measuring it. When defining TE, there is
> neither an agreed-upon definition"

## What this supports

EFMP-302 Unit 3 topic-01 and fig-U3-1: that competing definitions of teacher effectiveness are
in live use and that the field has not settled one. Supported directly, including the specific
"inaccurate or implied definitions" wording the unit relies on.
