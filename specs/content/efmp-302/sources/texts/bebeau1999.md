## Record summary

The paper argues that competing approaches to moral education address different dimensions of
development rather than contradicting one another, and should be synthesised into evidence-based
theory. Its concrete proposal is to replace conventional tripartite models with the
**Four Component Model** for defining researchable variables in moral development:

1. sensitivity
2. judgment
3. motivation
4. character
