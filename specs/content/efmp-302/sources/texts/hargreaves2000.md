## Record summary

The paper frames the development of teacher professionalism as four historical phases seen in
many countries:

1. the **pre-professional** age
2. the **autonomous professional** age
3. the **collegial professional** age
4. the **postmodern** or **post-professional** age

It argues that teachers' current experience of professionalisation draws on all four at once,
and closes by proposing directions that connect professional projects to broader social
movements supporting public education.
