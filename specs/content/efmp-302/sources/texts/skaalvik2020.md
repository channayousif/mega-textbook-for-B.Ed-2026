## Record summary

Longitudinal study of Norwegian high school teachers, **n = 262**, surveyed in September and
again in April. Confirmatory factor analysis and structural equation modelling.

Findings as the record states them:
- **time pressure** most strongly predicted **emotional exhaustion**
- **dissonant value context** most strongly predicted **cynicism**
- **autonomy** and **supervisory support** were positively associated with self-perceived
  accomplishment and negatively associated with intention to leave teaching

## What this supports

EFMP-302 Unit 5 topic-01 and fig-U5-2: specifically the branches where an added resource
interrupts the path. Autonomy and supervisory support as the named interrupting resources are
supported at record level, as is the demand-side split between time pressure and value
dissonance.
