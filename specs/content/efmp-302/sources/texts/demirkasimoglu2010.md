## Record summary

Reviews how teacher professionalism is defined across perspectives, opening from the long debate
over whether teaching is a profession or a semi-profession given limits on teacher autonomy and
decision-making authority. It sets out three interpretive approaches:

1. professionalism as an **ideological control mechanism** for occupational oversight
2. professionalism as a **positive attitude** toward teaching as work meeting established
   standards
3. an **integrative framework** combining sociological, political and educational contexts

It concludes that professionalism encompasses adherence to specified educational standards and
competency requirements.
