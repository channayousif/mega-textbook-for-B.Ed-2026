## Record summary

Scans OECD international data sets to propose a **Teacher Professional Identity (TPI)
development and outcome model**, and considers implications for practice, policy and research.
Frames stronger professional identity as connected to student learning success, long-term
empowerment at work, and effective policy development.

## Scope note, and why it matters

teacher as **change agent** or **moral agent**.
