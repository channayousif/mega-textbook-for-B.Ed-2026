Per `specs/007-content-depth-standard/contracts/sources-consulted.md`. Every key cited in
`specs/content/efmp-302/coverage/unit-04.md` appears here, and every key here is cited in prose

## Verification limitation, carried into G3

**The primary document could not be retrieved from the authoring host.** `npst-pakistan-2009` is
the core text of this unit, and every route to it returned an error to this machine:
`itacec.org` HTTP 403, `nacte.org.pk` HTTP 404, `teachertaskforce.org` HTTP 403. Other sources
resolved normally from the same machine during the same session, including the ERIC full-text
service and the Crossref registry, so this is a per-host access condition rather than evidence
that the document is unavailable.

Consequence, stated plainly so a reviewer can act on it:

- The **names of the ten standards** listed in `topic-02.mdx` were corroborated across several
  independent secondary sources that agreed with one another. They were **not** read from the
  primary document. A reviewer or the curriculum owner should check them against a physical or
  downloaded copy, and `unit-teacher-notes.mdx` instructs the teaching tutor to do the same.
- The **three-part internal structure** (knowledge and understanding; dispositions; performance
  and skills) and the **2009 Ministry of Education origin with UNESCO and USAID support** were
  corroborated the same way.
- The unit teaches the standards' architecture and use and **cites the document by reference
  without reproducing it**, per Constitution Art. III.5. This follows the legacy Unit 4 draft,
  which took the same conservative posture.

No `no-external-source` row was needed: every sub-topic is grounded in a real, identified source.
The limitation is about verification depth on one key, not about the absence of a source.

`isore2009` was verified against the **ERIC registry** (record ED530786, Isoré, OECD Education
Working Papers No. 23, 2009), and `goe2008` against the same registry (ED521228). The remaining
two keys were already on the course reading list.
