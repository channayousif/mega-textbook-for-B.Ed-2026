## Verification method

Continuing the method adopted in Unit 5 after the Unit 2 and 3 G3 reviews established that
registry metadata verifies a citation's existence and accuracy but not that the source supports
the claim.

Four keys (`day1999`, `guskey2000`, `villegas2003`, `brookfield2017`) were already on the course
reading list, are guide-required or previously verified, and are print books or a UNESCO report.
`hennessy2022` was verified by reading its abstract for Unit 5 and is retagged here.

`kwakman2003` is new and carries a stated limit: its bibliographic record was verified through
OpenAlex (Teaching and Teacher Education, 19(2), 149-170, single author Kitty Kwakman) and **no
abstract was available**. It is therefore cited only for the **question** its title supports, that
teacher participation in professional learning is not automatic and its determinants are worth
studying. `topic-01.mdx` tells readers to use the title to frame a question, not as evidence of an answer.
A reviewer with access to the full text should either extend or remove the citation.

The guide's Unit 6 content is the international professional-development literature, which is well
represented on the course reading list, but listing a source does not establish support.
Seven sub-topics now carry `no-external-source` mappings because their prose does not cite or
verify the previously named reading. The existing G-2026-64 escalation remains open.
Two additional passages rest on no source: the Pakistani-provision description in `topic-01.mdx` and
the doctor/teacher supervision comparison in `topic-02.mdx`. These passage limits remain
recorded alongside the seven sub-topic gaps. The second is
load-bearing, since MCQ 5 keys on it.
