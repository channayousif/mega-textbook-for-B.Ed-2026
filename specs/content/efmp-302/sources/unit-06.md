# Sources consulted - EFMP-302 Unit 6 (Sustaining Professionalism through Continuous Learning)

Per `specs/007-content-depth-standard/contracts/sources-consulted.md`. Every key cited in
`specs/content/efmp-302/coverage/unit-06.md` appears here, and every key here is cited in prose
and in a per-topic `## Further reading` section.

## Verification method

Continuing the method adopted in Unit 5 after the Unit 2 and 3 G3 reviews established that
registry metadata verifies a citation's existence and accuracy but not that the source supports
the claim.

Four keys (`day1999`, `guskey2000`, `villegas2003`, `brookfield2017`) were already on the course
reading list, are guide-required or previously verified, and are print books or a UNESCO report.
`hennessy2022` was verified by reading its abstract for Unit 5 and is retagged here.

`kwakman2003` is new and carries a stated limit: its bibliographic record was verified through
OpenAlex (Teaching and Teacher Education, 19(2), 149-170, single author Kitty Kwakman) and **no
abstract was available**. It is therefore cited only for the **question** its title supports, that
teacher participation in professional learning is not automatic and its determinants are worth
studying. `topic-01.mdx` states this to the reader directly: "this unit cites that work only for
the question it asks ... no finding from it is claimed here." A reviewer with access to the full
text should either extend or remove the citation.

No `no-external-source` row was needed for this unit, unlike Unit 5: the guide's Unit 6 content is
the international professional-development literature, which is well served by the sources already
on the course list.
