## RRQ - derived expected content (abridged)

1. Achievement gain (captures learning gain / misses what the test does not test); observed
   practice (captures process a test cannot / can be satisfied by a performance); breadth of
   learner outcomes (captures attitudes, persistence, retention / hard to measure reliably).
2. A sum "feels objective and hides every judgement that went into it"; components are chosen
   for a purpose (Goe: development instrument != high-stakes appraisal); components interact,
   so a sum conceals that a weak climate cancels strong subject knowledge.
3. Layers outward-in: appearance (thin outer band, brief attention/presumption of seriousness),
   then conduct, reliability, competence (the core that carries a career). Ayesha's evening was
   misallocated because the inner bands cannot be assembled the night before and she had not
   prepared the content or anticipated pupils' prior ideas about fractions.
4. Any three of: empathy (working: pupil admits confusion / excess: excuses everything, lowers
   expectations / balanced by holding expectations); patience (working: pupil reaches the idea /
   excess: passivity, a term of drift / balanced by intervention); self-control (working:
   regulated reaction / excess: flatness, unreadable / balanced by expressiveness); optimism
   (working: belief pupil can improve / excess: denial / balanced by naming the problem);
   humour (working: lowers cost of being wrong / excess: entertainment without learning; plus
   the distinct inverted failure of humour aimed at a pupil); resilience (working: recovery /
   excess: stubbornness / balanced by adaptability); adaptability (working: change the plan /
   excess: incoherence / balanced by planning and structure).
5. Pupils - so confusion is admitted and risk taken. Parents - so action happens at home and the
   teacher learns what is invisible in class. Colleagues - so practice improves and a pupil is
   handled consistently. Head/institution - so things can be authorised and difficult matters
   heard. Community - so the school can ask things of it (rural Sindh: girls staying past Class 5).
6. Beyond the wrong fact: the parent now cannot rely on any future answer; it converts a factual
   dispute into a credibility one; "I will find out and tell you by Thursday" would have survived
   checking; it damages the reliability on which the whole relationship is built, and can
   re-open the original suspicion (here, penalising for poverty).
7. Explaining, instructing, questioning, responding. The sentence after a pupil speaks "teaches
   the rest of the class what happens to people who answer" - "no, who else?" closes the room,
   "what made you think that?" keeps it open and surfaces the shared misconception.
8. Any five of: proximity (availability, makes off-task behaviour visible); body orientation
   (where the explanation is sent; signals answering is worth attention); gaze/eye contact
   (distribution; neglected zones disengage); facial expression incl. smiling (read fastest,
   least self-monitored); gesture (carries structure words cannot); vocal variety (pace, pitch,
   volume, pause - a pause before a key sentence).
9. Trying new methods is adopting or abandoning on how the lesson felt. The researcher role
   forms a question, changes one thing, decides in advance what evidence would show whether it
   worked, collects it, and is willing to find it did not work. Mr Imran keeping last year's
   marks is the differentiator.
10. Change requires standing and standing is earned by being visibly good at the ordinary work
   first; blanket criticism "is a way of ensuring nobody listens to you for two years". First:
   competence in the ordinary work, and a specific, shareable improvement rather than a verdict.
