## Expected RRQ content, derived before reading the model answers

1. Three definitions with a capture and a miss each: achievement gain (captures learning rather
   than manner; misses what the test does not test); observed practice (captures process a test
   cannot; can be satisfied by performance); breadth of learner outcomes (captures attitude,
   attendance, retention; hard to measure reliably).
2. Summing hides the judgements in the weighting, produces a falsely objective number, and
   ignores that components interact and are selected for a purpose (topic-01, two cautions).
3. Layered image: appearance thin outer band buying brief attention; then conduct, reliability,
   competence as the core. Ayesha's evening is misallocated because the bands that carry a
   career cannot be assembled the night before, and she had not prepared the content.
4. Any three of empathy/patience/self-control/optimism/humour/resilience/adaptability with
   working form, excess failure, and the balancing trait or component.
5. Pupils - so confusion is admitted; parents - action at home and information the teacher
   cannot see; colleagues - practice improves and handling is consistent; head/institution -
   authorisation and being heard; community - the school can ask things of it.
6. Beyond the wrong fact: the teacher's future answers are no longer trustworthy, the
   commitment mechanism ("I will find out and tell you by Thursday") is spent, and the parent's
   suspicion of institutional defensiveness is confirmed.
7. Explaining, instructing, questioning, responding. The sentence after a pupil answers teaches
   the rest of the class what happens to people who answer.
8. Five of proximity, body orientation, gaze/eye contact, facial expression, gesture, vocal
   variety, with the classroom use given in topic-04.
9. Trying new methods vs. deciding in advance what evidence would show whether it worked and
   being willing to find it did not - the comparison, not the innovation.
10. Criticism without standing; standing is earned by being visibly good at the ordinary work
    first, so competence and credibility come before advocacy.
