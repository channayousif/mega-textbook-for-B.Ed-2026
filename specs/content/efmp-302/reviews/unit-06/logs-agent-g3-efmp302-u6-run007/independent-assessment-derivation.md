## RRQ - independently derived expected content (abridged)

1. CPD: ongoing, practitioner-driven, career-long, measured by changed practice. Training course:
   an event, start/end/certificate, provider-driven. Qualification: certifies a threshold met at a
   point in time (past tense), awarding-body driven. Appraisal requirement: employer-imposed
   obligation whose natural product is compliance.
2. Knowledge base changes; pupils change; initial preparation cannot be complete by construction;
   the alternative is drift, not stability. Drift = unexamined practice adapts toward convenience
   (lighter marking, shorter explanations, the same three pupils answering) with nobody deciding.
3. Sustained; embedded in own classroom; focused on specific content/pupils; collaborative;
   feedback on real practice; chosen rather than assigned. Rules out (any three): the one-day event
   as a complete intervention; development that never touches your room; the generic workshop; the
   lone attender; being told things with no observation; the assigned course.
4. Preservice -> new teacher: responsibility transfers (accountability moves to the teacher).
   New teacher -> experienced: mechanics become automatic, attention is freed.
5. Sana (preservice): attention wholly consumed by mechanics, "no room in which to try it".
   Imran (new teacher): knows the technique; blocked by a class he cannot yet hold - the advice
   assumes a room that listens. Different causes: attentional capacity vs an unmet precondition.
6. Mechanics automatic -> attention free -> highest capacity; nothing external requires change
   (classes run, results hold, nobody complains) -> lowest pressure. Follows: development must be
   self-driven and served by genuine challenge/honest observation, not a repeat generic session or
   an administrative promotion.
7. Decide before leaving which single thing to try; agree with one colleague who attended to try it
   and compare in a fortnight; decide what evidence would show it worked. Prose assigns these three
   collectively to sustained, collaborative and feedback-supported.
8. Hennessy et al. (2022), LMICs: opportunities limited, often unsustained, frequently not informed
   by research evidence; technology has real potential to extend formal programmes and informal
   peer learning; outcomes so far mixed. Two Pakistani constraints from data cost, device access,
   electricity/connectivity.
9. Gap stated specifically (prevents an unobservable goal); evidence the gap is real (prevents a

   method / a route that cannot deliver it); evidence of change decided now (prevents "cannot tell

10. Written for an appraiser -> written to look good, not an honest gap; Unit 4: honesty requires
