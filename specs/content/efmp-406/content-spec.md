## Course-wide items

- **Learning Outcomes (guide, verbatim, `3rd 2026.txt:240-246`)**:
  1. Define and explain the concept and scope of contemporary literacy.
  2. Differentiate between traditional and modern perspectives of literacy.
  3. Analyze the role of literacy in social, cultural, and digital contexts.
  4. Apply contemporary literacy teaching strategies in classroom settings.
  5. Design literacy activities that promote critical thinking and learner engagement.
- **Teaching-learning strategies (guide, `:279-284`, `:296-299`)**: group discussions and seminars; role-plays and simulations; case study analysis; visits to inclusive schools; reflection journals and portfolios; lecture; discussions; question-answer; brainstorming.
- **Practical work (guide, `:301-304`)**: group work; group assignments; individual assignment; presentations.
- **Assessment (guide, `:285-289`)**: class participation and reflective journals 10%; mid-term written test 20%; project "Developing an inclusive lesson plan" 30%; final exam (written + presentation) 40%. ## Course Description

From the course guide (paraphrased, `:238-239`): the course builds an understanding of contemporary perspectives on literacy in a time of rapid social, cultural and technological change. It treats literacy as a socially situated practice that reaches beyond reading and writing to critical, digital, media, visual and information literacies, and prepares prospective teachers to design, carry out and evaluate literacy practices for diverse learners and the demands of 21st-century education.
