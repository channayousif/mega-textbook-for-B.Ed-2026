## Topic cycle responses

Topic 3.1 retrieval 1: autonomous = context-independent skill, ideological = socially situated practices; 2: the visible ledger calculation is the event, trading norms/trust/authority are the practice; 3: informal family letters and shop ledgers; 4: language devaluation can diminish belonging, participation and opportunities to practise reading.

Topic 3.2 retrieval 1: civic access/comprehension, evaluation, expression, action; 2: replacing the first language versus maintaining it while adding languages; 3: discuss evaporation in Sindhi, demonstrate it, map key Urdu words, then scaffold an Urdu explanation; 4: analyze access advantages of English/Urdu and barriers facing home-language speakers, retaining Sindh's instructional exception.

Both topics' fourth retrieval questions require Analyze, outside the spec's stated formative Remember/Understand/Apply range. Move them to the summative discussion or revise the demand.

Topic 3.1 summative: Aslam treats one sanctioned register as the sole measure; evaluate punishment's impact; replace it with Saraiki discussion linked to Urdu vocabulary and paired reading of familiar community texts. Topic 3.2 summative: examine mismatch between unfamiliar instruction and conceptual access; propose staged mother-tongue support with explicit bridges to Urdu and English; assess comprehension, not only recitation. Both demand higher-order analysis. Their mini-rubrics also need performance anchors. Pupils' home language does not by itself determine intelligence or every educational outcome.
