This is author planning and self-check, not independent G3 review or certification.

## Topic 2.1 enduring understandings

- Critical reading uses textual evidence to examine voice, representation and interests; it permits supported agreement.
- Digital literacy joins practical operations with judgement about purpose, audience, access and responsibility.
- A justified conclusion distinguishes what a text shows from what a reader infers or still needs to check.

## Topic 2.2 enduring understandings

- An information need determines which details and sources fit a decision; copying a claim does not independently confirm it.
- Media production and circulation are relevant to reading, without proving that a sponsored message is false.
- Words, images and other modes work together; accessible redesign preserves essential meaning and conditions.
