## Mapped-reading limits

All three mapped readings (`minott-2011-critical`, `gasque-2016-inquiry`, `hafifah-2020-ict`) are cited. Minott is cited only for what its abstract states. Hafifah and Sulistyo studied Indonesian university English lecturers, not Pakistani school teachers; the unit uses their findings as indicative of teacher ICT literacy and barriers, and says so. U5-04 had no mapped reading, so the open EGRA toolkit was added (`rti-2016-egra`); it is a reading-assessment guide used in many low- and middle-income countries, and the unit cites it only for the assessment continuum, its subtasks and its stated limits. The ASER Pakistan report was considered as a Pakistani assessment source, but its host returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-02 and no statistic from it is used. The guide-listed inclusion readings are not used for any literacy claim (`G-2026-73`).
