## Scope

U4-04. The overview distinguishes attribution, ShareAlike, NonCommercial and NoDerivatives conditions. The lesson asks readers to check the actual licence for a planned use. It does not assert that attribution alone grants permission, and it makes no claim about a particular national exception. Classroom privacy and access routines are original planning guidance, separate from the licence terms.
