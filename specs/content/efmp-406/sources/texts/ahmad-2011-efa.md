# Unit 6 bound source note: ahmad-2011-efa

Ahmad, M. (2011). EFA in Pakistan: Struggle for upgrading literacy. Seminar.net, 7(2). https://doi.org/10.7577/seminar.2404 .

Publisher full-text page retrieved 2026-11-02: https://journals.oslomet.no/seminar/article/download/2404/2274?inline=1 .

## Evidence used

The abstract identifies political, social, economic and cultural obstacles to Pakistan's literacy effort. The introduction describes the earlier Jomtien commitment and its renewal at Dakar in 2000, with 2015 as the new target year. These are the limited historical points used in U6-01 and U6-04. The unit does not carry forward the paper's historical rates or programme targets as current conditions.

A short passage from the introduction states that governments:

> reaffirmed their commitment to ‘Education for All’ and agreed on a new target year

The paragraph immediately names 2015. This is a commitment and horizon, not a report of achieved literacy.
