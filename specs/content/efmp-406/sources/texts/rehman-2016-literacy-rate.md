# Unit 6 bound source note: rehman-2016-literacy-rate

Rehman, A., Jingdong, L., & Hussain, I. (2016). The province-wise literacy rate in Pakistan and its impact on the economy. Pacific Science Review B: Humanities and Social Sciences, 1(3), 140–144. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psrb.2016.09.001 . Publisher volume label: November 2015.

Retrieved 2026-11-02 from the publisher abstract: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2405883116300247 .

## Evidence used

> The basic objective of this paper is to determine and analyse the province-wise literacy rate in Pakistan

This identifies the geographical comparison, the only research point attributed to the paper in U6-01. The unit uses it to motivate looking beyond a national summary. No rates, national rankings, economic causal claims or present-day comparisons are imported from the paper. All classroom numerical examples are invented and explicitly labelled.
