## Article 25A - Right to education (used by Unit 1, U1-03)

"The State shall provide free and compulsory education to all children of the age of five to sixteen years in such manner as may be determined by law."

Unit 1 cites only the content of this Article, not its amendment history. The National Assembly PDF did not answer from this host on 2026-11-02, so the passage was not re-extracted.
