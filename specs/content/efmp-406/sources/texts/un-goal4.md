# Unit 6 bound source note: un-goal4

United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs. (n.d.). Goal 4. https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal4 . Targets page retrieved 2026-11-02.

## Evidence used

Target 4.1 links primary and secondary completion with relevant learning outcomes. Target 4.5 addresses disparities and access for vulnerable learners. Target 4.6 specifies a 2030 literacy and numeracy horizon for youth and adults. The relevant passage includes:

> all youth and a substantial proportion of adults, both men and women, achieve literacy and numeracy

These are targets, not evidence that outcomes have been achieved. Unit 6 uses the SDG period from 2015 to 2030 for its timeline. No current progress figures from this changing webpage are incorporated. The teacher's classroom connection is an original application, not a UN claim of local impact.
