# Unit 6 bound source note: unesco-ai-education

UNESCO. (n.d.). Artificial intelligence in education. https://www.unesco.org/en/digital-education/artificial-intelligence . Retrieved 2026-11-02.

## Evidence used

The opening overview discusses AI's potential and risks, places inclusion and equity at the centre of its educational application, and calls for a human-centred approach. It states that AI should not:

> widen the technological divides within and between countries

U6-04 uses this principle to frame a teacher's resource decision. The app proposal, verification exercise, paper route and classroom comparison are original hypothetical applications. They are not reported UNESCO trials or evidence of an app's effectiveness. No product, adoption rate or predicted universal outcome is endorsed.
