## Definition evidence used

UNESCO IBE's resource pack reproduces the 2017 guide's definitions on page 24: https://www.ibe.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2023/10/unesco_bie_2021_web_inclusive_education_resrouce_pack.pdf .

The reproduced inclusion definition concerns barriers to:

> the presence, participation and achievement of learners

The equity definition concerns fairness and the equal importance of every learner's education. Unit 6 paraphrases these definitions and applies them through original invented classroom cases. No intervention effect or statistical outcome is attributed to the guide.
