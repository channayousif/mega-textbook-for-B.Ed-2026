Bound paraphrase: MIL combines information, media and digital capabilities. It includes evaluating and using information and producing content. Its scope covers paper, audiovisual and digital settings, including contexts with limited advanced technology. This supports the unit's use of both printed posters and digital messages. The classroom analysis of words, pictures and layout is an original teaching application, not an empirical result from UNESCO.
