Every other Urdu label (CON:EFMP-406-5-1 to CON:EFMP-406-5-8, CON:EFMP-406-5-11, CON:EFMP-406-5-12, and "evaluation" rendered as جائزہ in CON:EFMP-406-5-9) is authored, not bank-derived, and is flagged for the separate Urdu job and G5 review. Note the clash to resolve there: the bank gives جائزہ inside "Summative Assessment", while CON:EFMP-406-5-9 uses it for "evaluation". The spec's key terms Learner-centred instruction, Inquiry-based learning, Project-based learning and Literacy assessment have no bank entry.
