## 1. Independent Blind Solve of Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs)

Each MCQ was solved independently prior to consulting the answer key in `docs/semester-2/efmp-305/unit-02/unit-assessment.mdx:183-192`.

- **MCQ 1**: Which of the following is a sensory disability?
  - Options: A) Dyslexia, B) Visual impairment, C) ADHD, D) Poverty
  - Derivation: Visual impairment affects vision, a primary sensory modality. Dyslexia and ADHD are specific learning difficulties / neurodevelopmental conditions, while poverty is a socio-economic difference.
  - Derived Answer: **B**

- **MCQ 2**: What does the term "handicap" describe?
  - Options: A) The medical condition itself, B) The social disadvantage created by an unaccommodating environment, C) The pupil's general intelligence, D) The teacher's attitude
  - Derivation: In the WHO conceptual framework (impairment, disability, handicap), impairment is the anatomical or physiological condition, disability is the functional limitation, and handicap is the social disadvantage resulting when the environment fails to accommodate the person.
  - Derived Answer: **B**

- **MCQ 3**: A pupil who speaks Pashto at home struggles in an Urdu-medium school. Which category of diversity does this most show?
  - Options: A) Sensory disability, B) Physical disability, C) Social difference, D) Learning difficulty
  - Derivation: A mismatch between home language and the language of instruction is a linguistic/social difference, not a sensory or intellectual impairment or learning difficulty.
  - Derived Answer: **C**

- **MCQ 4**: What is the first step in the labelling cycle described in this unit?
  - Options: A) The pupil internalises the label, B) The teacher attaches a label to the pupil, C) The pupil's performance drops, D) The peers tease the pupil
  - Derivation: Topic 2.2 delineates the cycle: 1. Teacher notices a pattern; 2. Teacher attaches a label ("slow", "weak"); 3. Teacher's expectations drop; 4. Pupil internalises the label; 5. Pupil's effort and performance drop, confirming the label. Attaching the label initiates the self-fulfilling cycle.
  - Derived Answer: **B**

- **MCQ 5**: A teacher notices that a pupil reads well when seated near the board but struggles at the back. What is the most likely explanation?
  - Options: A) The pupil is lazy, B) The pupil may have a visual impairment, C) The pupil has an intellectual disability, D) The pupil is not intelligent
  - Derivation: Performance varying specifically with distance from the board is a classic classroom indicator of visual impairment (myopia or acuity limitation).
  - Derived Answer: **B**

- **MCQ 6**: A teacher wants to identify whether a pupil's reading difficulty is dyslexia or simply a lack of practice. Which action is most appropriate for the teacher to take first?
  - Options: A) Refer the pupil for clinical diagnosis, B) Observe the pupil's reading behaviour and try targeted support, C) Label the pupil as "dyslexic" and move on, D) Tell the parent the pupil is "slow"
  - Derivation: The unit emphasizes that classroom teachers should systematically observe reading behaviours, offer informal assessment, and implement targeted adaptations before escalating to external clinical referral.
  - Derived Answer: **B**

- **MCQ 7**: A report card says "Saima is a weak pupil who cannot learn science." Analyse this statement using the concepts from this unit. What is the main problem with it?
  - Options: A) It is too short, B) It uses a fixed label and gives no specific information about what Saima can do, C) It is too kind, D) It mentions science
  - Derivation: The comment attaches a fixed, deficit-oriented label ("weak pupil who cannot learn") without describing specific strengths, specific difficulties, or actionable instructional steps.
  - Derived Answer: **B**

- **MCQ 8**: A school places all pupils with learning difficulties in one section and uses a simplified curriculum. Which principle of inclusion does this most violate?
  - Options: A) All learners participate, B) Diversity is not valued, C) The child must adapt, D) Equality is unfair
  - Derivation: Segregating pupils into a separate class or section directly violates the foundational principle of inclusive education that all learners participate in the common classroom environment.
  - Derived Answer: **A**

- **MCQ 9**: Which of the following teacher actions best promotes respect for diversity?
  - Options: A) Saying "This class has too many weak pupils this year", B) Using a pupil's medical condition as the main way to describe them, C) Pairing a strong reader with a struggling reader for a shared task, D) Avoiding calling on pupils who might struggle
  - Derivation: Mixed-ability partnerships for cooperative tasks normalize diversity, allow mutual contributions without deficit labelling, and build peer respect.
  - Derived Answer: **C**

- **MCQ 10**: A head teacher says, "We cannot include because we have no special educator." Evaluate this claim using the misconceptions discussed in Unit 1 and the identification tools from this unit. Which response is most accurate?
  - Options: A) Agree; without a special educator, nothing can be done, B) Disagree; the unit showed that observation and simple adaptations are within every teacher's reach, C) Agree; only a specialist can identify learner diversity, D) Disagree; diversity does not exist in this school
  - Derivation: A central premise of the unit is that everyday observation, simple informal assessments, and classroom adaptations (seating, person-first language, mixed-ability pairs) are within the reach of all general education teachers.
  - Derived Answer: **B**
