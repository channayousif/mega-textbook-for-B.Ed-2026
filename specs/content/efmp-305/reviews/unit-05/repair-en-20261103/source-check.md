## Results and limitations

- Printed p. 243: the prose gives both 68% and 72.9% as four-factor totals.
- Printed p. 244, Table 2: rotated percentages are 18.451, 18.286, 18.212
  and 17.951, cumulatively 72.900. Initial eigenvalues total 78.285%;
  extracted loadings total 72.900%. These are distinct table columns.
- Printed pp. 244–245, Results and Table 3: factors concern beliefs about
  inclusion, social/cultural perspectives, professional readiness, and practical
  implementation concerns. Each factor contains five items.
- Printed p. 245: the paragraph after Table 3 instead gives 51% explained variability.
- Printed p. 246, Discussion: factor labels instead concern resources/support,
  professional knowledge/preparedness, attitudes/beliefs, and collaboration/development.
  The prose assigns the reported percentages to different labels from Results.

The source reports similar rotated percentages, but its conflicting totals and labels
preclude presenting a single consistent interpretation. Similar questionnaire variance
shares would not establish equal causal effects on inclusion or inseparability of
attitudes, resources and preparation. Topic 5.3, its summary, reading note and RRQ 8
now teach the limited result and reporting inconsistency. No national prevalence or
pupil outcome is inferred. This is an author source check, not independent certification.
