# Sources consulted - EFMP-305 Unit 6 (Collaboration and Parental Involvement)

Per `specs/007-content-depth-standard/contracts/sources-consulted.md`. Every key cited in
`specs/content/efmp-305/coverage/unit-06.md` appears here, and every key here is cited both in prose
and in a per-topic `## Further reading` section.

The course guide carries **no** "Text Books and References" section, so no source for this unit is
`guide-required`. Under `D-2026-0053` item 2 the author binds verified real works chosen for
relevance to the course outcomes, labelled author-selected. The content-spec front matter declares
`open_access_floor:` / `default: 1`. The content-spec's per-unit binding floor names `ainscow2020` or
`ainscow2020b` for this unit; both are bound below and both are registry-verified open access, and
four further registry-verified open-access works are bound beside them, so no escalation to
`specs/gaps.md` is needed.

**A note on what this unit does not cite.** This is the closing unit and it draws on all five before
it. Where it does, it names the unit (for example "Unit 5's four-level analysis", "Unit 3's jigsaw")
rather than re-citing that unit's sources. Those cross-unit references are pedagogy, not source
bindings, and they are deliberately absent from the coverage matrix.

## Unverifiable sources

The two primary texts (`unicef2014`, `sindh2018`) are **not** in this list. The interrupted draft recorded full retrieval and checksums. UNICEF is now represented by
paraphrased locator notes; the continuation retrieval returned HTTP 403, so G3 must reopen the source.

The six remaining keys are unverifiable at G1 in the sense the depth gate means: the bibliographic
record and the licence have been checked against two registries, but **no full text was retrieved
from this authoring host** and no excerpt is bound. Support is abstract level only, per `D-2026-0001`;
a reviewer must verify each specific claim against the source text at G2.

- `fu2022`: supports U6-1. CC BY 4.0, so G3 should retrieve and bind an excerpt without difficulty.
  **Scope cautions the prose must respect:** 392 typically developing pupils aged 10 to 17, mean age
  12.7, in Chinese primary and middle schools, using the Inclusive Climate Scale, the Peers Attitude
  to Students with Disabilities Scale and the Balanced Emotional Empathy Scale. **Not Pakistani, and
  correlational with a mediation analysis, which is not a causal demonstration.** Topic 6.1 states
  the country, the sample size and the age range in the same paragraph as the findings, cites the
  study for the **shape of the relationship** and the neutral baseline, and reproduces **no number**
  from it except the sample size, the age range and the mean age. The unit explicitly tells the
  reader that the study cannot show that changing a climate causes a change in attitudes. A G2
  reviewer should check that the mediation is reported as **partial** and that the sex difference the
  abstract also reports is not used anywhere in the unit, because it is not.
- `khalid2021`: supports U6-2 and U6-4. CC BY-NC 4.0. **Scope cautions:** a qualitative study, **ten**
  parents of children with and without disabilities, purposively sampled from inclusive primary
  schools **in Punjab**, semi-structured interviews with thematic analysis. This is an account of
  what ten parents said in one province, not a measurement and not Sindh. Topic 6.2 names the number
  and the province in the same sentence as the finding. The unit cites it for the **direction** of
  parental inclination and for the named sources of dissatisfaction, and for no prevalence claim
  whatever. A G3 reviewer should confirm that "more inclined towards inclusive education" is the
  authors' own reported finding rather than this author's gloss.
- `virani2022`: supports U6-2 and U6-4. CC BY 4.0, gold open access. **Scope cautions:** an
  exploratory qualitative study with **fifteen** purposively selected primary school teachers, five
  of whom had pupils with special needs. An account of what a small number of teachers said. Unit 5
  bound the same record for teacher attitudes and explicitly deferred the parent-teacher point to
  this unit, which is the only new claim taken from it here. The prose names the number and treats
  the finding as the authors' characterisation rather than as an established fact about Pakistan.
- `alvarez2026`: supports U6-3. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. **Scope cautions:** a PRISMA review of **twelve**
  qualitative studies published 2020 to 2025, searched in Web of Science and Scopus, **none of them
  Pakistani** and none in a class of sixty. The article body is in **Spanish**; only the English
  abstract and parallel title were read, which a G3 reviewer should note before accepting any claim
  attributed to its body. The unit cites it for **what collaboration consists of** (co-planning,
  co-instruction, co-assessment) and for its three stated conditions, and for **no effect size and no
  outcome claim**. The unit also states in prose that none of the twelve studies is Pakistani. This
  is the row whose use is most at risk of drift, because co-teaching in the reviewed studies means
  two qualified teachers in one room, which is **not** the Pakistani itinerant-visitor arrangement
  Topic 6.2 applies it to; the topic draws the analogy explicitly and limits it to the three
  conditions, and a reviewer should check that limit has held.
- `ainscow2020`: supports U6-1, U6-3 and U6-5. CC BY 4.0, shared record with Unit 1. **Scope
  cautions:** a conceptual and comparative paper, not an empirical study and not about Pakistan. The
  presence-participation-achievement framing is the author's well-known position and is bound here at
  abstract and title level. G3 should pin it to the body text before a reviewer is asked to accept it
  as the paper's argument, and should note that Unit 1 leans on this record more heavily than this
  unit does.
- `ainscow2020b`: supports U6-4 and U6-5. CC BY-NC 4.0, shared record with Unit 5. **Scope cautions:**
  a conceptual and comparative paper drawing on international experience, not empirical and not about
  Pakistan. The phrase about the field remaining confused as to the actions needed is from the
  abstract. The claim that school culture, collaboration and the use of existing resources matter
  alongside new money is the author's well-known argument, bound at abstract level; G3 should pin it
  to the body text.

## Verification still owed at G2

- Retrieve `fu2022` in full and confirm the neutral mean attitude, the three positive relationships
  and the **partial** mediation are reported as stated. Topic 6.1's misconception section and the
  Summary both rest on them, and the mediation being partial rather than full is load-bearing.
- Retrieve `khalid2021` and confirm the more-inclined finding and the three named sources of
  dissatisfaction. Topic 6.2's misconception section rests on it, and it is the only Pakistani
  evidence the unit has for a claim about parents.
- Retrieve `alvarez2026`. This is the most important G2 retrieval in the unit, for two reasons: the
  body is in Spanish, and the analogy from two-teacher co-teaching to a monthly itinerant visit is
  the author's, not the review's. A reviewer should satisfy themselves that Topic 6.2 does not
  attribute the analogy to the source.
- Pin both Ainscow records to body text rather than abstracts.
- **No further verification is owed on the two primary texts for the claims this unit makes.** What a
  reviewer should still do is adversarial rather than bibliographic: check that the unit never
  upgrades the Sindh Act's s. 9 into a requirement to produce an IEP document, never attributes an
  empirical claim about Pakistani parents to the UNICEF booklet, and never drops the booklet's own
  counterweight about families with mixed views, which is the passage a selective reading would omit.
- **The participation ladder's provenance was not established.** The booklet reproduces it with an
  endnote and does not name its originator in the body text; neither the excerpt nor the unit
  attributes it. If the curriculum owner wants the attribution recorded, that is a separate
  bibliographic task and must not be assumed to have been done here.
