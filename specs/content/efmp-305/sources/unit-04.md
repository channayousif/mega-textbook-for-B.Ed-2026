# Sources consulted - EFMP-305 Unit 4 (Creating an Inclusive Classroom Environment)

Per `specs/007-content-depth-standard/contracts/sources-consulted.md`. Every key cited in
`specs/content/efmp-305/coverage/unit-04.md` appears here, and every key here is cited both in
prose and in a per-topic `## Further reading` section.

The course guide carries **no** "Text Books and References" section, so no source for this unit is
`guide-required`. Under `D-2026-0053` item 2 the author binds verified real works chosen for
relevance to the course outcomes, labelled author-selected. The content-spec front matter declares
`open_access_floor:` / `default: 1`. The `## Reading list` entry for this unit records that neither
mapped reading (`who2011`, `cast2024`) has a registry record and that the floor therefore had to be
met by a source bound at G2. **Four** registry-verified open-access rows are bound below, so the
floor is met and the escalation to `specs/gaps.md` the reading list anticipated is **not** needed.

**Registry verification actually performed.** Each DOI-bearing row below was resolved against the
**Crossref REST API** and the **OpenAlex API** on **2026-10-15** from this authoring host. The
returned title, journal, volume, issue, pages or article number, year, author surnames, licence URL
and OpenAlex open-access status were compared field by field with the citation recorded here. That
is a *metadata and licence* verification. **No full text was retrieved**, so no row below supports a
quotation or a page-specific claim; abstract-level statements of a study's own argument are marked as
such in the scope cautions. No other registry (ERIC, DOAJ) was queried and none is claimed.

## Unverifiable sources

Every source bound to this unit is **unverifiable at G1** in the sense the depth gate means: the
bibliographic record and, where a licence exists, the licence have been checked against Crossref and
OpenAlex, but **no full text was retrieved from this authoring host** and no excerpt is bound under
`specs/content/efmp-305/sources/texts/`. Support is title-and-bibliographic level only, per
`D-2026-0001`; a reviewer must verify each specific claim against the source text at G2.

- `batool2023`: supports U4-1, U4-4. CC BY 4.0, so G2 should retrieve and bind an excerpt without
  difficulty. **Scope cautions the prose must respect:** a quantitative teacher-perception survey in
  **one district** (Multan), in government primary schools. It is cited for what teachers *report*
  as hurdles, never for how widespread those hurdles are nationally, never for a figure, and never
  as evidence about pupils or outcomes. Topic 4.1 and Topic 4.2 both cite it in that restricted
  sense, and Topic 4.2 explicitly says the account does not describe the conditions as hopeless.
- `pham2025`: supports U4-5. CC BY 4.0. **Scope cautions:** forty-one second-degree English language
  students at a university in Vietnam, in EFL presentation classes. This is not a Pakistani primary
  classroom and it is not evidence about children. The prose cites it for the **mechanism** - a
  row-and-column room distributes participation unevenly and the back is the cheapest place to
  disappear - and states the setting in the same paragraph. No number from the study is reproduced.
- `iriyani2025`: supports U4-1. CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. **Scope cautions:** a small qualitative
  interview-based study in one Indonesian inclusive school. Cited as an account of what PBS looks
  like when a staff takes it seriously, explicitly not as proof of effect and not as evidence of
  transfer to a class of fifty-eight in Sindh. The unit also states that whole-school PBS needs
  training and leadership the single teacher does not have, which the study does not contradict.
- `cote2021`: supports U4-4. CC BY 4.0, published in a journal supplement. **Scope cautions:** a
  policy and financing analysis at country-system level across low- and middle-income countries, not
  a classroom study and not Pakistan-specific. It is cited for one purpose only, stated in the
  prose: so that a teacher does not read her own frustration as evidence that her school is uniquely
  badly run.
- `kurniawati2026`: supports U4-2. CC BY 4.0, gold open access. **Scope cautions:** Indonesian
  **higher education**, a multiple-case study of UDL implementation after staff training. The claim
  taken from it - that physical access alone does not close the access-to-success gap - is the
  authors' own stated argument in the structured abstract. The prose says the setting explicitly and
  frames the claim as a warning about where attention goes rather than as data about Grade 3. This
  is the row most in need of a full-text read at G2, because only its abstract has been seen.
- `who2011`: supports U4-2, U4-4. A large institutional report, free to download, with no registry
  record. Same record as Unit 2's. The two claims drawn from it are both high-level framing claims
  that the report is well known for; G2 should still pin them to chapters, because this unit's entire
  four-layer structure rests on the first of them.
- `cast2024`: supports U4-3, U4-5. A living web document, which means its content can change under a
  stable URL. Same record as Unit 3's. G2 must record the retrieval date alongside the version.
