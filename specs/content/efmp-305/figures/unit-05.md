Notes for whoever renders these.

- `fig-U5-1` and `fig-U5-4` are the unit's schematics for Art. III.10. `fig-U5-1` is a timeline and
  carries the devolution point, so the 2010 dividing line must be visually unmistakable, not a
  marker among markers.
- Every figure in this unit paraphrases or quotes a statute. The quoted fragments inside the
  prompts (`shall endeavor that`, `admit them without discrimination`, `including denial of
  reasonable accommodation`, and the two statutory definitions) are **verbatim from the retrieved
  text** bound at `specs/content/efmp-305/sources/texts/sindh2018.md` and `ict2020.md`. A renderer
  must not tidy, modernise or shorten them, and must not silently change `endeavor` to `endeavour`
  inside a quotation. Anything outside quotation marks in those prompts is the author's gloss and
  may be re-worded for fit.
- `fig-U5-3` is the owner-sanctioned federal comparison (`D-2026-0053` item 7). It is one figure
  and must stay one figure; its note line placing the Sindh column as the governing law is part of
  the figure, not decoration, and must not be dropped for space.
- `fig-U5-6` is the widest table in the unit at four columns and nine rows. If it will not read on
  a 5-inch screen as a single table, split it by level into four stacked blocks rather than
  dropping rows; the row count is the evidence for the footer claim.
- No colour carries meaning in any of the six. `fig-U5-5`'s two intervention arrows are
  distinguished by label and entry point, and `fig-U5-1`'s two bands by label as well as shade.
