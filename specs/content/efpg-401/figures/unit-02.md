# EFPG-401 Unit 2 - Figure manifest

Figure-manifest-v2 contract (`specs/009-figure-rendering/contracts/figure-manifest-v2.md`). Ten hand-authored schematic SVGs rendered and placed; dark variants are derived via `npm run figures:variants`. Visual density floor met with two schematics per topic and three required schematics (two flowcharts and one concept-map).

The unit banner illustration is tracked separately in `unit-02.banner.md` (ADR-0029). The five
`illustration` rows (`fig-U2-12` to `fig-U2-16`) are classroom scenes planned at `prompt-only` for the
illustrate job; they are extra to the schematic figures per topic.
