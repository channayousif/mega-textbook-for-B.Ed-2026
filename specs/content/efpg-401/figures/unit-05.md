# EFPG-401 Unit 5 - Figure manifest

Figure-manifest-v2 contract (`specs/009-figure-rendering/contracts/figure-manifest-v2.md`). Fourteen hand-authored schematic SVGs rendered and placed; dark variants are derived via `npm run figures:variants`. Visual density floor met with two schematics per topic and five required schematics (four flowcharts, one diagram). Four classroom raster illustrations (fig-U5-16 to fig-U5-19, topics 5.1, 5.3, 5.4 and 5.6) are planned at `prompt-only` for the illustrate job; topics 5.2, 5.5 and 5.7 get none because their situations turn on a written plan, on what a text makes pupils feel, or on a choice of tense, which a text-free image could only decorate. The unit banner is tracked separately in `unit-05.banner.md`.
