## Design and assessment trace

- **U6-01 (Topic 6.1)**: Audio-visual aids hold attention, build meaning, support memory and invite participation; the misconception that aids need money and electricity is corrected. Evidence: Topic 6.1 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 1-2; RRQs 1-2; ERQ 1.
- **U6-02 (Topic 6.2)**: Charts, models, pictures, flash cards, toys and real objects make English concrete in a crowded classroom; the flash-card routine is worked through. Evidence: Topic 6.2 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 3-4; RRQs 3-4; ERQ 2.
- **U6-03 (Topic 6.3)**: Radio, audio recordings and the language laboratory expose pupils to other voices and turn listening into speaking; the misconception that more aids are always better is corrected. Evidence: Topic 6.3 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 5-6; RRQs 5-6; ERQ 3.
- **U6-04 (Topic 6.4)**: Television, video and film teach only when a preview task, a check, a discussion and a follow-up are planned; the misconception that showing a video is teaching is corrected. Evidence: Topic 6.4 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 7-8; RRQs 7-8; ERQ 4.
- **U6-05 (Topic 6.5)**: Slides, filmstrip, the overhead projector and the multimedia projector make one idea visible; the clear-slide rule replaces the crowded slide. Evidence: Topic 6.5 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 9-10; RRQs 9-10; ERQ 5.
