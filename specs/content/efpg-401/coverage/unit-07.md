## Design and assessment trace

- **U7-01 (Topic 7.1)**: An objective-type test has one fixed answer per item; the four common item types are multiple choice, true/false, matching and fill in the blank; a good item is written from the outcome with a clear stem, one fixed key and plausible distractors. Evidence: Topic 7.1 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 1-6; RRQs 1-4; ERQ 1, ERQ 2, ERQ 5.
- **U7-02 (Topic 7.2)**: A subjective-type test asks the pupil to produce a longer answer judged against a rubric; its three parts are the task, the model answer and the marking rubric; an analytic rubric makes marking fair and consistent. Evidence: Topic 7.2 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 7-10; RRQs 5-10; ERQ 3, ERQ 4, ERQ 5.
