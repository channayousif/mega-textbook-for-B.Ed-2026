## Design and assessment trace

- **U1-01 to U1-06 (Topic 1.1)**: Receptive listening is an active cognitive process. Scaffolded through the three-stage framework (pre, while, post), supported by audio and video media, and developing distinct sub-skills (gist, specific detail, inference, instructions). Evidence: Topic 1.1 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 1-3; RRQs 1-3; ERQ 1, ERQ 5.
- **U1-07 to U1-11 (Topic 1.2)**: Productive speaking requires a supportive low-anxiety climate, intelligible pronunciation and intonation contours, communicative dialogues, interactive games, and oral vocabulary building. Evidence: Topic 1.2 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 4-6; RRQs 4-6; ERQ 2, ERQ 5.
- **U1-12 to U1-14 (Topic 1.3)**: Reading comprehension requires distinguishing loud phonological reading from silent internal meaning-making, balanced phonics and sight-word instruction, and deliberate sub-skill deployment (skimming, scanning, intensive reading, inference). Evidence: Topic 1.3 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 7-8; RRQs 7-8; ERQ 3, ERQ 5.
- **U1-15 to U1-19 (Topic 1.4)**: Productive writing integrates motor-perceptual mechanics (handwriting, spelling) with the five-stage process approach (pre-writing, drafting, revising, editing, publishing) across paragraphs, informal letters, invitations, and formal school applications. Evidence: Topic 1.4 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 9-10; RRQs 9-10; ERQ 4, ERQ 5.
