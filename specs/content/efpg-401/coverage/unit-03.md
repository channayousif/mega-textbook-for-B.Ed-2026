## Design and assessment trace

- **U3-01 (Topic 3.1)**: Grammar-Translation Method grounds language learning in explicit deductive rules, bilingual vocabulary lists, and bidirectional translation. Effective for reading and grammatical analysis in resource-constrained classrooms; limited in developing oral fluency and listening. Evidence: Topic 3.1 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 1-2; RRQs 1-2; ERQ 1, ERQ 5.
- **U3-02 (Topic 3.2)**: Direct Method emphasizes immersive target-language use, banning mother tongue translation to forge direct cognitive links between words and meaning via realia, pictures, and demonstration. Evidence: Topic 3.2 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 3-4; RRQ 3; ERQ 1, ERQ 5.
- **U3-03 (Topic 3.3)**: Audio-Lingual Approach treats language learning as habit formation through oral-aural dialogue mimicry, pattern repetition, substitution, and transformation drills. Evidence: Topic 3.3 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 5-6; RRQs 4-5; ERQ 2, ERQ 5.
- **U3-04 (Topic 3.4)**: Structural Approach organizes language instruction around graded syntactic structures and sentence patterns, utilizing substitution tables to generate error-free sentences systematically. Evidence: Topic 3.4 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 7; RRQ 6; ERQ 2, ERQ 5.
- **U3-05 (Topic 3.5)**: Communicative Approach develops communicative competence through authentic interaction, information-gap activities, and collaborative pair work, prioritizing fluency before accuracy. Evidence: Topic 3.5 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQs 8-9; RRQs 7-8; ERQ 3, ERQ 5.
- **U3-06 (Topic 3.6)**: Word Building equips learners with morphological generative awareness, analyzing roots, prefixes, suffixes, and compounding to expand vocabulary independently. Evidence: Topic 3.6 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 10; RRQs 9-10; ERQ 4, ERQ 5.
