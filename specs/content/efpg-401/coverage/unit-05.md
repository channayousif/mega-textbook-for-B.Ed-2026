## Design and assessment trace

- **U5-01 (Topic 5.1)**: Language is a skill, and a skill improves through use. Activities give pupils practice, give the teacher evidence, and hold attention. Teacher talk shrinks while pupil work grows from controlled to free. Evidence: Topic 5.1 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 1-2; RRQ 1; ERQ 1, ERQ 5.
- **U5-02 (Topic 5.2)**: A lesson plan has seven steps: learning outcomes, warm-up, presentation, practice, production, assessment and homework. Each step has a purpose and the plan is a guide the teacher can adjust. Evidence: Topic 5.2 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 3-4; RRQ 2-3; ERQ 2, ERQ 5.
- **U5-03 (Topic 5.3)**: Activity-based learning organises the lesson around tasks. Pair work, group work, games and role play each suit different aims and class sizes. Group work needs four teacher moves. Evidence: Topic 5.3 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 5-6; RRQ 4-5; ERQ 2, ERQ 5.
- **U5-04 (Topic 5.4)**: A structural lesson teaches one sentence pattern through presentation, controlled practice and free practice. Evidence: Topic 5.4 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 7; RRQ 6; ERQ 3, ERQ 5.
- **U5-05 (Topic 5.5)**: A prose lesson moves from pre-reading through reading and comprehension to an extension activity. Evidence: Topic 5.5 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 8; RRQ 7; ERQ 4, ERQ 5.
- **U5-06 (Topic 5.5)**: A poetry lesson moves from listening for enjoyment through recitation and discussion to a personal response. Evidence: Topic 5.5 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 8; RRQ 7; ERQ 4, ERQ 5.
- **U5-07 (Topic 5.6)**: A paragraph lesson uses a model, a frame and a shared plan before pupils write one organised paragraph. Evidence: Topic 5.6 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 9; RRQ 8; ERQ 2, ERQ 5.
- **U5-08 (Topic 5.6)**: A story lesson uses a story map to make the beginning, middle and end visible before pupils write. Evidence: Topic 5.6 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 9; RRQ 8; ERQ 2, ERQ 5.
- **U5-09 (Topic 5.6)**: An essay lesson uses a model essay, a brainstorm and a paragraph plan to organise ideas. Evidence: Topic 5.6 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 9; RRQ 8-9; ERQ 2, ERQ 5.
- **U5-10 (Topic 5.7)**: A functional grammar lesson begins with a context, then notices the form, then uses it in a task. Evidence: Topic 5.7 retrieval 1-3, summative task; MCQ 10; RRQ 10; ERQ 5.
