## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Daulay examines the implementation of audio-visual media for teaching listening skills in junior high school EFL classrooms, analyzing perceived effectiveness and practical pedagogical challenges. The study finds that audio-visual materials significantly enhance learner comprehension by providing visual scaffolding, facial expressions, and situational context that support acoustic processing. It highlights that listening is an active cognitive skill requiring structured pre-listening preparation and focused guided tasks to prevent cognitive overload.
