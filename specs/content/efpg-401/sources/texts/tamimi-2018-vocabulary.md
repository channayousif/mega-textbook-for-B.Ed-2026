## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Tamimi Sa'd and Rajabi studied 145 intermediate Iranian EFL learners and their teachers. The most frequent vocabulary learning strategies were using words in sentences (67.2%), translating words into the mother tongue (76.1%) and mental repetition of words (61.2%). The least frequent were writing down words from films and television (44.8% never), using dictionaries (44.8% never) and using Leitner's boxes (73.1% never). Learners rated reading and repeating words, and using words in a specific context, as the most effective strategies. Teachers rated explanation in the target language with examples, and repetition, as the most effective teaching strategies. Superficial attention to words, inadequate or complicated explanation, and excessive use of the mother tongue were rated ineffective. The study concludes that learners use a limited repertoire of vocabulary acquisition techniques and need strategy training.
