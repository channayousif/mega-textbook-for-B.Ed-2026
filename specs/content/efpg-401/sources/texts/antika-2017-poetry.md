## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Antika argues that literature is a tool for promoting language learning and that poetry in particular can foster learners' motivation to read and write so that they achieve academic proficiency. The paper states that poetry, as part of literature, develops learners' literacy competence. It argues for approaches to teaching poetry in the EFL classroom, elaborates the benefits of using poetry with learners, and proposes assessments that assess literary competence in poetry.
