## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Kiran presents an empirical study comparing the effectiveness of the Grammar-Translation Method (GTM) and the Communicative Language Teaching (CLT) approach on secondary school pupils' achievement in English grammar in Pakistan. Using an experimental pre-test and post-test control group design, the study finds that while both instructional methods yield measurable improvements in grammatical knowledge, pupils taught through communicative approaches demonstrate significantly higher retention, contextual application, and functional grammatical competence than pupils taught exclusively through deductive translation rules. The author emphasizes that combining structural clarity with meaningful communicative practice offers the most robust pathway for English language development in Pakistani schools.
