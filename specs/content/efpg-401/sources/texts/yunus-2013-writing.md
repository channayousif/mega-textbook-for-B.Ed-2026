## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Yunus, Nordin, Salehi, Embi, and Salehi investigate the use of information and communication technology and structured process support in teaching ESL writing skills. The authors demonstrate that writing instruction in second-language classrooms requires explicit support across both mechanical transcription (handwriting, spelling) and cognitive composing processes (brainstorming, drafting, peer revising, editing). When learners receive scaffolded mechanical feedback and collaborative planning support, anxiety decreases and writing quality improves.
