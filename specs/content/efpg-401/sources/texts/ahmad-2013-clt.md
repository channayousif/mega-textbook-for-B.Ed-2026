## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Ahmad investigates the implementation of the Communicative Approach (CLT) in teaching English as a foreign language in Pakistani educational institutions, comparing communicative practices with prevailing Grammar-Translation Method (GTM) traditions. The study identifies practical challenges faced by Pakistani teachers in large classes, including examination pressures emphasizing rote memorization, pupil hesitation, and physical classroom arrangements. It provides evidence that communicative activities such as information gap tasks, role plays, and structured pair work significantly enhance learners' practical fluency and communicative competence when teachers balance fluency development with appropriate linguistic scaffolding.
