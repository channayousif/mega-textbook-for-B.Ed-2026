## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Indrayani and colleagues investigated the quality of teacher-made multiple-choice tests used as summative assessment for English subject. Using a descriptive document study and interviews, they compared the tests against eighteen norms for constructing a good multiple-choice test. They report that 79 items (99 per cent) were qualified as very good and one item (1 per cent) as good, but that some problems remained in relation to some norms. They suggest peer review, rechecking and editing to minimise the remaining issues.
