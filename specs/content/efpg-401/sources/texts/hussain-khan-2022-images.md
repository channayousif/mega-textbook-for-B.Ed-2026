## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Hussain and Khan report a qualitative study of how images are used in teaching English in primary schools in a Pakistani urban setting. Using classroom observation and interviews, they examined the use of textbook pictures in English lessons. The findings indicate that pictures are quite helpful for teaching English to non-native learners, but that the teachers observed often did not use them, and missed the chance to make lectures and classroom activities more lively, interesting and fruitful.
