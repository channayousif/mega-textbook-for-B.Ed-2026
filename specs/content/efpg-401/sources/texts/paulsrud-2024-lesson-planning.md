## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Paulsrud and Gheitasi studied how pre-service English teachers in Sweden plan lessons for Years 4 to 6 (ages 10 to 12) in increasingly linguistically diverse schools. The study analysed a course called Language Learning and Teaching and the students' lesson-planning assignments. It used the language orientations framework (language-as-right, language-as-problem, language-as-resource) and an ecological perspective that looks at the contextual layers, such as the national curriculum and course materials, that shape planning.

The findings indicate that the pre-service teachers generally took a language-as-resource stance in their planning, across the cohorts studied. The study also found that micro-level factors, such as the literature and materials available, had a more immediate impact on planning than macro-level influences such as national policy. The authors argue that knowing why and how teachers plan for multilingual English classrooms is valuable for all teacher educators.
