## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

Hartati, Tohamba, and Faridawati investigate reading strategies used by teachers in teaching reading in EFL classrooms. The study documents that effective reading instruction combines bottom-up decoding strategies (phonics, sight words) with top-down comprehension strategies (skimming, scanning, predicting, and schema activation). Teachers who balance silent reading with targeted guiding questions achieve significantly deeper comprehension than those relying exclusively on oral recitation.
