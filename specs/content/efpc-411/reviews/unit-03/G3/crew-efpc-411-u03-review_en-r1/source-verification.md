### hec-2013-practicum (guide-required)

Bound note `sources/texts/hec-2013-practicum.md` (six evidence notes, pp. 8-45).

- Topic 3.1 "the school observation portfolio serves as the central documentary evidence of a
  student-teacher's practical learning (HEC, 2013)": the note describes observation as purposeful
  systematic inquiry and describes proformas, the observation cycle and reflective debriefing; it
  does not itself state the portfolio's central documentary role. The role is guide-determined
  (guide Weeks 9-12, `4TH SEMESTER.txt:618-691`), so the claim is acceptable as a guide claim but
  the HEC attribution is looser than the note. Advisory.
- Topic 3.1 "eight sequential components": the note does not enumerate eight components. The
  eight-part architecture is the unit's synthesis of guide Weeks 9-12 and is fixed by the approved
  content-spec Figure plan (`content-spec.md:230`). Not a source defect.
- Topic 3.2 "observe and document three to four complete lessons across different subjects and
  grade levels (HEC, 2013)": the bound HEC note never states the 3-4 lesson count. The count is
  guide-determined (`4TH SEMESTER.txt:641`, Week 10 "Document observations of 3-4 lessons").
  Attributing the number to HEC overstates the note. Finding F6.
- Topic 3.3 "corporal punishment and humiliating verbal abuse are strictly prohibited" under
  "Pakistani educational policy and child protection guidelines": no citation is given and no
  bound note carries the policy text. The claim is plausible but uncorroborated in the bundle.
  Advisory.

### richards-lockhart-1994 (guide-required)

Bound note `sources/texts/richards-lockhart-1994.md` (four evidence notes, pp. 1-208).

- Topic 3.1 / 3.2 reflective writing "moving past immediate emotional reactions to examine the
  underlying causes": note 2 distinguishes descriptive from analytical/evaluative reflection;
  supports the describe-vs-analyse contrast. The phrase "immediate emotional reactions" is not in
  the note. Minor.
- Topic 3.2 four-stage cycle "describe, analyse, interpret, plan (Richards & Lockhart, 1994;

  Lockhart, 1994; Scoupe et al., 2024)": note 3 mentions management strategies, climate and rules
  but does not present this three-style typology. Finding F5.

  plans.
