## RRQs - derived answers

1. **Uncurated dossier vs evidence-based portfolio (3 marks).** A dossier is an unstructured
   accumulation of every paper collected; a portfolio is an intentional, sequenced document that
   links selected evidence to reflection and standards. Matches the scheme.
2. **Four profile categories (4 marks).** Administrative/identification; physical infrastructure;
   learning resources; institutional routines and culture. Matches the scheme. Note the question
   says "physical and instructional infrastructure data", which does not describe the administrative
   and routine categories the scheme rewards. Finding F8.
3. **Anonymity and consent (2 marks).** Protects pupil privacy and dignity; preserves professional
   trust and the non-punitive consent relationship with the school. Matches.
4. **Three proforma dimensions (3 marks).** Context/objectives; methods and staging; questioning
   and engagement. Matches.
5. **Descriptive vs analytical (2 marks).** Description answers "what happened"; analysis answers
   "why it happened" and links teaching decisions to learning. Matches.
6. **Proactive vs reactive (2 marks).** Proactive: pre-established routines, scanning, early
   non-verbal intervention. Reactive: delayed intervention, shouting, coercive threats. Matches.
7. **Evaluative vs formative feedback (2 marks).** Marks/ single words judge without guidance;
   descriptive feedback names the error and the next step. Matches.
8. **Chronological sections (4 marks).** The taught architecture is eight components (topic-01,
   topic-04, fig-U3-1). The model answer regroups them into four phases (opening, observational
   records, reflective entries, capstone). The item's premise ("the four core chronological
   sections") is not taught. Finding F2.
9. **Celebratory vs balanced appraisal (2 marks).** Celebratory writing is evidence-free
   self-praise; balanced appraisal cites evidence and diagnoses growth areas. Matches.
10. **SMART target (3 marks).** Target, concrete action, verifiable indicator, timeline. Matches.
