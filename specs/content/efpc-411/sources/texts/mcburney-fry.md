# Bound source evidence: mcburney-fry

Source: McBurney-Fry, G. (2002). *Improving your practicum: A guide to better teaching practice* (2nd ed.). Social Science Press.
ISBN: 9781876633448.

Consulted: 2026-11-27, examined for practicum portfolio development, school profiles, and documentation standards.

## Evidence notes

1. The practicum portfolio (pp. 42-51): Defines the student-teacher practicum portfolio as a structured, evidence-based showcase of professional learning and classroom competence. Details the essential components: title page, table of contents, institutional profile, observation records, teaching materials, reflection logs, and verification documents.
2. Constructing the school profile (pp. 52-60): Outlines the components of a comprehensive school profile report: administrative data, physical plant and infrastructure, available instructional resources, student demographics, daily routines, school codes of conduct, and overall institutional ethos.
3. Systematic lesson recording (pp. 75-84): Presents protocols for completing lesson observation proformas, ensuring balanced documentation of instructional objectives, teaching sequences, resource usage, student engagement, and formative checks.
4. Classroom management documentation (pp. 102-114): Recommends methods for tracking teacher management styles, transition routines, rule enforcement, and positive discipline strategies during placement.
5. Final review and portfolio assembly (pp. 165-174): Details criteria for assembling, checking, and presenting the final practicum portfolio for university supervisor review and viva assessment.
