# Bound source evidence: hec-2013-methods

Source: Higher Education Commission. (2013). *General methods of teaching: Course guide for B.Ed. (Hons.) Elementary*. HEC Pakistan.
URL: https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/universities/RevisedCurricula/Documents/2011-2012/Education/MethodsTeaching_Sept13.pdf

Consulted: 2026-11-26, read from the repository's extracted text copy (`Scheme-and-Course-guides/extracted-text/course-guides-2025/MethodsTeaching_Sept13.txt`).

## Evidence notes

1. Instructional strategies and questioning (pp. 38-52): Examines teacher-centred versus student-centred teaching strategies. Explains questioning techniques, wait-time, and active learning methods (pair work, group discussions, inquiry tasks) that observers look for during classroom visits.
2. Learning resources and materials (pp. 65-74): Discusses effective selection and use of low-cost teaching aids, chalkboard organization, and concrete learning materials in elementary classrooms.
3. Teacher feedback and debriefing (pp. 82-90): Details principles of providing constructive feedback on instructional performance to enhance teaching competence and student engagement.

## Unit 1 use

Notes 1 and 2 support Topic 1.2 (U1-05).
Note 3 supports Topic 1.4 (U1-12).
