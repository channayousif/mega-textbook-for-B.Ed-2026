## Evidence notes

1. Individual differences (Unit 3, Weeks 10-13): the contents list "The concept of individual differences", "How do people differ?", "Why do people differ?", "How to deal with individual differences", "Children with special needs", "Physical disabilities", "Learning disabilities", "Emotional disturbance", "Social and economic disadvantages", and "Gifted learners". The course description states that the course is designed to help student-teachers "understand and respond to the learning problems of children from diverse backgrounds and their behaviours at various stages of development".
2. Motivation (Unit 4, Weeks 14-16): the learning outcomes ask student-teachers to "identify how learning and motivation are shaped by social contexts in homes, school, and communities", to "analyse the implications of Maslow's theory of motivation", and to "identify success seekers and failure avoiders in their own classrooms". The weekly content includes "Techniques of motivation" and "Application of motivation principles in education".
3. Learning difficulties and disabilities (Unit 5): the unit's essential questions ask "Why is it essential for teachers to study learning disabilities and learning difficulties?" and "How does an understanding of learning difficulties help teachers to facilitate learning?". The contents list "Learning difficulties and disabilities" and "Signs and symptoms of learning disabilities and disorders".
4. Assessment and evaluation: the guide also carries a unit on the role of educational psychology in assessment and evaluation, which is the guide's framing for observation and judgement of learning.

## Unit 2 use

Notes 1 and 2 support Topic 2.3 (U2-06) on pupil behaviour, engagement and motivation.
Notes 1 and 3 support Topic 2.4 (U2-09) on inclusive practice and individual differences.
Note 2 supports Topic 2.3 (U2-07) on the classroom assessment of learning.
