## Evidence notes

1. Observer objectivity and low-inference recording (pp. 6-11): The manual establishes rigorous protocols for standardized classroom observation. Observers must record low-inference, observable behaviors and verbatim evidence rather than high-inference subjective interpretations or evaluative labels.
2. Focus areas of primary instruction (pp. 12-25): Details classroom observation dimensions: classroom culture, time on task, instructional clarity, checks for understanding, feedback quality, and active student engagement.
3. Observation tools and rating rubrics (pp. 26-42): Explains the design and calibration of descriptive rating scales and behavioral rubrics. Demonstrates how to avoid observer bias, including the halo effect, central tendency bias, and severity or leniency errors.
4. Analyzing findings and feedback (pp. 43-52): Outlines procedures for aggregating observational tallies, identifying instructional trends, and utilizing observational data to provide actionable, developmental feedback to primary teachers.
