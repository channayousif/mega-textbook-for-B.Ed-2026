## Design and assessment trace

- **U2-01**: High-frequency words cover most of the Qur'an; many are known from Urdu. Target: Remember, Apply. Evidence: Topic 2.1 retrieval 1; MCQs 1-2; RRQ 1; ERQ 1.
- **U2-02**: Every Arabic word is an ism, fi'l or harf, identified by signs. Target: Remember, Apply. Evidence: Topic 2.1 retrieval 3-4; MCQs 2-3, 9; RRQ 2; ERQ 2.
- **U2-03**: Root families, phrase spotting and daily-verse method aid memorisation. Target: Remember, Understand. Evidence: Topic 2.1 retrieval 2; RRQ 1; ERQ 1.
- **U2-04**: Every noun is masculine or feminine, with signs to tell them apart. Target: Remember, Apply. Evidence: Topic 2.2 retrieval 1; MCQ 4; RRQ 3; ERQ 3.
- **U2-05**: Arabic keeps three numbers apart; plurals are sound or broken. Target: Remember, Understand. Evidence: Topic 2.2 retrieval 2-3; MCQ 4; RRQs 3, 10; ERQ 3.
- **U2-06**: Pronouns are detached or attached; they show ownership, subject or object. Target: Apply. Evidence: Topic 2.2 retrieval 4; MCQ 5; RRQ 4; ERQs 2-3.
- **U2-07**: Past, present and imperative are identified by endings and prefixes. Target: Remember, Apply. Evidence: Topic 2.3 retrieval 1-2, 4; MCQs 6, 8; RRQ 5; ERQ 4.
- **U2-08**: The verb agrees with its subject in person, gender and number. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 2.3 retrieval 3; MCQ 6; RRQs 5, 8; ERQs 2, 4.
- **U2-09**: Form I (basic) and Form II (doubled middle, causing) add meaning to a root. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 2.3 summative; MCQ 7; ERQ 4.
- **U2-10**: Prepositions connect nouns to the sentence; changing the particle changes the meaning. Target: Remember, Understand. Evidence: Topic 2.4 retrieval 1; MCQ 9; RRQ 7; ERQ 5.
- **U2-11**: la, lam, lan and ma negate different tenses; inna and qad add emphasis. Target: Remember, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 2.4 retrieval 2-4; MCQs 8, 10; RRQs 6, 9; ERQ 5.
- **U2-12**: Grammar-based translation labels every word's part of speech, gender, number, tense and meaning. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 2.4 summative; ERQs 2, 5.

The first five MCQs and RRQs sample Topics 2.1-2.2, the remaining five sample Topics 2.3-2.4. ERQ 1 samples Topic 2.1, ERQs 2-3 sample Topics 2.1-2.2, ERQ 4 samples Topic 2.3, and ERQ 5 samples Topic 2.4.
