## Design and assessment trace

- **U1-01**: The Qur'an asks to be understood, and memorising it is a different skill from understanding it. Target: Understand, Evaluate. Evidence: Topic 1.1 retrieval 1, 4 and summative; MCQ 1; RRQs 1-2; ERQ 1.
- **U1-02**: Quranic Arabic is built from roots, compact, rhythmic without metre, gives familiar words exact meanings and speaks directly to its listeners. Target: Understand. Evidence: Topic 1.1 retrieval 2; MCQs 2, 4-5; RRQ 3; ERQ 2.
- **U1-03**: Quranic Arabic is part of Classical Arabic, sharing its words and images but giving them a new purpose, and differs from Arabic spoken today. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 1.1 retrieval 3 and summative; MCQ 3; RRQs 4-5; ERQ 2.
- **U1-04**: Urdu merges Arabic sounds that the Qur'an keeps apart, and the difference can change a word. Target: Remember, Apply, Create. Evidence: Topic 1.2 retrieval 1; MCQs 6, 8; RRQ 6; ERQ 4.
- **U1-05**: A root carries a core meaning and a pattern adds its own; the verse decides the exact meaning. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 1.2 retrieval 3-4 and summative; MCQs 7, 9; RRQs 7-8, 10; ERQ 3.
- **U1-06**: Frequent expressions learned as wholes give footholds in any passage and practice with roots. Target: Remember, Apply. Evidence: Topic 1.2 retrieval 2; MCQ 10; RRQ 9; ERQ 5.

The first five MCQs and RRQs sample Topic 1.1, the remaining five sample Topic 1.2.
ERQs 1-2 sample Topic 1.1, ERQs 3-4 sample Topic 1.2, and ERQ 5 integrates both.
