## Design and assessment trace

- **U6-01**: Concept of asbab al-nuzul as specific historical events, questions, or circumstances accompanying the descent of verses; clarification that most verses descended initially (*ibtidā'an*) without external prompts. Target: Remember, Understand. Evidence: Topic 6.1 retrieval 1, 2; MCQs 1, 3; RRQs 1, 4; ERQ 2.
- **U6-02**: Makki and Madani classification based on the chronological benchmark of the Hijrah (622 CE), comparing their themes (Tawhid/ethics vs law/community), style (rhythmic/intense vs expository/prosaic), and forms of address ("O mankind" vs "O you who have believed"). Target: Remember, Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 6.1 retrieval 1, 5; MCQs 2, 4, 5, 6; RRQs 2, 3; ERQ 1, 3.
- **U6-03**: Cultural and social background of 7th-century pre-Islamic Arabia (*Jahiliyyah*), including oral poetic mastery, tribalism (*asabiyyah*), female infanticide, and usury; and how gradual revelation (*tanjim* and *tadrij*) systematically reformed society. Target: Understand, Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 6.1 retrieval 3; MCQs 7, 10; RRQs 5, 6, 10; ERQ 4.
- **U6-04**: Impact of historical and literary context on interpretation, governed by the hermeneutical rule *al-ʿibrah bi ʿumum al-lafz la bi khusus al-sabab*, ensuring context illuminates divine wisdom and rationale without imprisoning verses in the past. Target: Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate. Evidence: Topic 6.1 retrieval 4; MCQs 8, 9; RRQs 7, 8, 9; ERQs 2, 5.

MCQs 1-10, RRQs 1-10, and ERQs 1-5 sample all four sub-topics of Topic 6.1 systematically, with ERQ 5 integrating skills from Units 4, 5, and 6.
