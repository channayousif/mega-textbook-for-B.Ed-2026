- **U3-02**: A verbal sentence begins with a verb; the fi'l comes first, and a fā'il and, where the verb takes one, a maf'ūl bihi follow. Target: Remember, Understand. Evidence: Topic 3.1 retrieval 1-2; MCQ 6; RRQ 1; ERQ 2.
- **U3-03**: Word order can move a part forward for emphasis or restriction. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 3.1 retrieval 3 and summative; MCQ 5; RRQ 3; ERQ 1.
- **U3-04**: A conditional sentence joins a condition (sharṭ) to a result (jawāb sharṭ), introduced by a condition word. Target: Remember, Apply. Evidence: Topic 3.2 retrieval 1, 3 and summative; MCQs 3, 9; RRQs 5, 8; ERQ 4.
- **U3-05**: The passive voice does not name the doer; the nā'ib fā'il takes the doer's place. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 3.2 retrieval 2, 4 and summative; MCQs 4, 7-8; RRQs 6-7; ERQ 3.
- **U3-06**: Sentence structure is a reading tool for long verses. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 3.2 retrieval 3; MCQ 10; RRQ 9; ERQ 5.
