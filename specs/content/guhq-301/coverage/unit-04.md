## Design and assessment trace

- **U4-01**: Reading accurately means giving each letter its makhraj and signs, stopping where the meaning allows, and not adding or dropping a letter. Target: Remember, Apply. Evidence: Topic 4.1 retrieval 1, 4; MCQ 7; RRQ 3.
- **U4-02**: Word-by-word translation is a five-step method (read, split, look up, order, check), and it is the first half of understanding rather than the last. Target: Remember, Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 4.1 retrieval 2; MCQ 2; RRQ 1; ERQ 1.
- **U4-03**: A short verse yields to patient word-by-word work, and its verses form a shape. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 4.1 retrieval 3; RRQ 2.
- **U4-04**: Meaning-based translation carries the message into natural English or Urdu; Al-Hilali (2026) places translations on a literal-to-interpretive spectrum. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 4.2 retrieval 1, 6; MCQs 3-4; RRQ 6; ERQ 2.
- **U4-05**: Key words, especially repeated ones, carry the theme of a passage. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 4.2 retrieval 2; MCQ 5; RRQs 4, 7.
- **U4-06**: Consecutive verses connect through shared phrases, pronouns and movement, so a verse is understood best with its neighbours. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 4.2 retrieval 4; MCQ 6; RRQ 5.
- **U4-07**: A ruku' is a paragraph-like semantic unit of the Qur'an, not only a numbered marker. Target: Remember, Understand. Evidence: Topic 4.3 retrieval 1, 3; MCQs 1, 8; RRQ 8.
- **U4-08**: The theme of a passage is the main idea its key words keep returning to, and the ruku' structure shows how the verses support it. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 4.3 retrieval 2, 4; MCQ 10; RRQ 9; ERQ 3.
- **U4-09**: The short surahs of Juz 30 are practice ground: read one as a unit, find its key words and state its theme. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 4.3 summative; RRQ 10; ERQ 4; ERQ 5.
