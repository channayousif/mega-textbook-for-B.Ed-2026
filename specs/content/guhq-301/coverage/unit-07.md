## Design and assessment trace

- **U7-01**: Integrated practice unifies root analysis, vocabulary recognition, noun declension, verb conjugation and sentence parsing to unlock text meaning. Target: Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 7.1 retrieval 1, 2; MCQs 1, 4; RRQs 1, 4; ERQs 1, 5.
- **U7-02**: Translation and comprehension move along a spectrum from literal word-by-word glossing to fluent interpretive rendering that respects syntax. Target: Understand, Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 7.1 retrieval 2; MCQs 2, 5, 6; RRQs 2, 5, 6; ERQs 2, 5.
- **U7-03**: Guided recitation with tajwid ensures accurate phonetics and meaningful pauses (waqf) that mirror grammatical and semantic boundaries. Target: Remember, Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 7.1 retrieval 3; MCQs 3, 7; RRQs 3, 7; ERQs 3, 5.
- **U7-04**: Practical reflection (tadabbur) translates divine guidance into ethical action across classroom teaching, family relations and school community. Target: Understand, Evaluate, Create. Evidence: Topic 7.1 retrieval 4 and summative; MCQs 8, 9, 10; RRQs 8, 9, 10; ERQs 4, 5.

MCQs 1-10 sample the four sub-topics of Topic 7.1 across Remember, Understand, and Apply. RRQs 1-10 sample the four sub-topics across Understand and Analyze. ERQs 1-4 explore each sub-topic in depth (Analyze and Evaluate), and ERQ 5 is an integrative Capstone task asking the student-teacher to create a comprehensive lesson presentation on one short surah.
