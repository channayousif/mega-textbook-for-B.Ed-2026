## Design and assessment trace

- **U5-01**: Tafsir is the disciplined effort to understand the Qur'an, grounded in the text, the Sunnah, the Companions' usage and language; a teacher may explain a clear verse without being a mufti. Target: Remember, Understand. Evidence: Topic 5.1 retrieval 1; MCQ 1; RRQ 1; ERQ 1.
- **U5-02**: The principles of interpretation include interpreting the Qur'an by the Qur'an, the generality of the wording over the occasion, and the Sunnah and language as bounds. Target: Remember, Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 5.1 retrieval 2, 4; MCQ 3; RRQ 3; ERQ 2.
- **U5-03**: Context (siyaq) and asbab al-nuzul shape how a verse is heard, and grammar sets the bounds of what a sentence allows. Target: Understand, Apply. Evidence: Topic 5.1 retrieval 3, 5; MCQs 4-5; RRQ 4; ERQ 4.
- **U5-04**: The five-step method runs from reading and translation through context and tafsir to a lesson. Target: Remember, Apply, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 5.2 retrieval 1; RRQ 5; ERQ 3.
- **U5-05**: A theme is the message the key words keep returning to, and repetition is the sign. Target: Understand, Analyze. Evidence: Topic 5.2 retrieval 2, 3; MCQs 7-8; RRQs 6-7.
- **U5-06**: A moral and practical lesson is the theme turned into a concrete duty, traced to the wording. Target: Understand, Analyze, Evaluate. Evidence: Topic 5.2 retrieval 4; MCQ 10; RRQ 9; ERQ 5.
