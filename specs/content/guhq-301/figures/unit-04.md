# GUHQ-301 Unit 4 - Figure manifest

Contract: specs/009-figure-rendering/contracts/figure-manifest-v2.md. Six hand-authored schematic SVGs, placed in the English topic files; dark variants derived by `npm run figures:variants`; text geometry checked with `scripts/measure-figure-text.mjs`. Arabic labels are vowelled Qur'anic words, not verses or calligraphy. Urdu `.ur.svg` mirrors belong to the separate translation job. The unit banner is tracked separately in `unit-04.banner.md` (ADR-0029). The three `illustration` rows (`fig-U4-8`..`fig-U4-10`) are classroom scenes planned at `prompt-only` for the illustrate job, which produces them, adds the Urdu `<Figure>` with Urdu alt text and moves each row to `placed`. They are extra and do not count toward the topic's schematic figures.
