## Urdu label provenance for G5

- No label in this table is banked in `specs/content/terminology.csv`; every Urdu label is authored and needs independent G5 review: درست مطالعہ, لفظ بہ لفظ ترجمہ, مختصر آیات کی فہم, معنی محور ترجمہ, اہم الفاظ و عبارات, آیات کے ربط, رکوع, مرکزی موضوع and منتخب سورتوں کی مشق. They do not certify an Urdu translation.
- رکوع is the standard Urdu term for a ruku', and مرکزی موضوع is the plain phrase for the theme of a passage; both are authored here because the terminology bank has no row for them.
