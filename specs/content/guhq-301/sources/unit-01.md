## Mapped-reading limits

Two of the three mapped readings are cited: `quran-translation` and `quranic-arabic-corpus`. The third, `muallim-ul-quran`, is named in the course overview as a guide recommendation but is not cited for any claim: the guide gives no complete edition, no catalogue record was found (content spec, Reading list), and its text could not be consulted (`D-2026-0001`). Two open-access sources were added for claims the mapped readings do not make: `yazar-batur-2025` for the relation between the Qur'an and pre-Islamic poetry (U1-02, U1-03), and `abdelfattah-2025-tajweed` for the description of tajwid (U1-04).

## Claims without a bound source

- No open-access source was found for the specific claim that everyday Urdu pronounces groups of Arabic letters alike (topic 1.2 table). It is stated as common knowledge of Urdu speech and is shown through Qur'anic word pairs whose spelling was checked (2:32 and 2:10; 2:10 and 18:18). It needs the G3 reviewer's confirmation.
