## Mapped-reading limits

Two of the three mapped readings are cited: `quran-translation` and `kadir-2022-asbab`. ## Claims without a bound source

- The standard historical timeline of the Prophet's mission in Makkah (610-622 CE) and Madinah (622-632 CE), the division of revelation into 13 Makki years and 10 Madani years, and landmark events (Cave Hira, public call, boycott, Year of Sorrow, Hijrah, Badr, Hudaybiyyah, Farewell Pilgrimage) represent consensus historical knowledge taught across Islamic studies curricula. They are not quoted from a single proprietary monograph.
- The distinction between Makki and Madani stylistic features (short rhythmic verses with cosmic oaths versus longer legal prose, and "Ya ayyuha al-nas" versus "Ya ayyuha alladhina amanu") is standard textbook content in classical *Ulum al-Quran* (al-Zarkashi's *al-Burhan* and al-Suyuti's *al-Itqan*), summarized here as general foundational knowledge for student-teachers.
- The classroom case studies, dialogue involving trainee Tariq and pupils Bilawal and Zeeshan, and lesson design templates are original instructional models designed for B.Ed trainees. They require review by a qualified Islamic Studies specialist at G3 (content-spec drafting note 7).
