## Mapped-reading limits

Two of the three mapped readings are cited: `quran-translation` and `quranic-arabic-corpus`. The third, `fehm-ul-quran-urdu`, is named in the content spec as a category of Urdu translations and commentaries, not as a single identifiable work, so it is not cited for any claim; the unit tells learners to compare an Urdu translation they trust, without naming one. Two open-access sources were added for claims the mapped readings do not make: `al-hilali-2026-literal-interpretive` for the literal-to-interpretive translation spectrum (U4-02, U4-04), and `rafei-2024-ruqu` for the ruku' as a semantic unit (U4-07, U4-08). `abdelfattah-2025-tajweed` is carried over from Unit 1 for the description of *tajwid* (U4-01).
