## Mapped-reading limits

Two of the three mapped readings are cited: `quran-translation` and `quranic-arabic-corpus`. The third, `muallim-ul-quran`, is named in the course overview as a guide recommendation but is not cited for any claim: the guide gives no complete edition, no catalogue record was found (content spec, Reading list), and its text could not be consulted (`D-2026-0001`).
