The unit uses these counts to show that one root yields a family of words (U1-05), to label patterns with the corpus's own terms (active and passive participle), and to send learners to look up roots and words in al-Fatiha and in frequent expressions (U1-01, U1-06). The unit's pattern names (faʿala, fāʿil, mafʿūl, faʿīl) are standard grammar terms, not taken from these pages.
