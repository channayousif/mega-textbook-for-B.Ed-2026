## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

The authors review the rules of interpretation (qawaʿid al-tafsir) applied by the Indonesian exegete M. Quraish Shihab in his Tafsir Al-Mishbah, with particular attention to asbab al-nuzul, the occasions of revelation. They report the classical definition of tafsir attributed to al-Zarkashi: a body of knowledge used to reach the best understanding of the Qur'an revealed to the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), by clarifying its meaning, extracting its rulings and wisdoms with the aid of linguistics, Arabic grammar, the principles of jurisprudence, the occasions of revelation, and the science of abrogation. They also report the view that the Qur'an was revealed for three purposes: to be recited as worship, to be understood, and to be acted on.

The article sets out the rule that the decisive point is derived from the generality of the wording, not from the particularity of the occasion (al-ʿibrah bi ʿumum al-lafz la bi khusus al-sabab). Where a verse was revealed about a particular event but its wording is general, the ruling is drawn from the general wording and reaches everyone in a comparable situation. The authors illustrate this with 22:4, revealed about al-Nadar ibn al-Harith but general in its warning, and with 5:33, revealed about the al-ʿUrayniyyin but general in its wording. They contrast this with 92:17-18, where al-Suyuti argued from the grammar of the word al-atqa that the wording is specific, and they judge his argument stronger.

A second rule is that an occasion of revelation is not limited to events before the verse but covers every situation the verse's wording includes, before or after, within the period of revelation. The authors conclude that the lesson lies in the general meaning of the words and not simply in the special circumstances in which they were revealed, while knowledge of the events surrounding the revelation puts the general meaning in context and prevents deviation.

The unit uses the article for the definition of tafsir (U5-01), the rules of interpretation including the rule of the general wording (U5-02), and the role of asbab al-nuzul and context in interpretation (U5-03, U5-04). It does not use the article for any claim about Shihab's biography, and it does not quote the article's translations of the verses beyond the short phrases named here. The ordering of the sources of tafsir in the topic is standard teaching content, not taken from this article.
