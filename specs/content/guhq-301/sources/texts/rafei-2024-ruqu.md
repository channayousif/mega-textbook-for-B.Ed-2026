## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

The authors treat the ruku' as an internal division of the Qur'anic text and argue that it is a semantic unit: a unit of meaning, not only a numbered division. They propose an approach to interpreting the Qur'an through ruku' analysis and apply it to the tenth ruku' of Surah al-Nahl (16:71-76). In that passage they identify a topic sentence, supporting statements, a transitional sentence and concluding sentences, and conclude that the six verses form a structurally and thematically coherent passage that can be read as an independent semantic unit.

They also compare their reading with the categorisations of three exegetes and find that neglecting ruku' divisions in existing commentaries has often led to semantic discontinuity, disruption of textual coherence, deviation from the surah's main objective and reader confusion. They conclude that a ruku'-based reading highlights the thematic independence of each semantic unit, ensures its internal coherence and helps identify the overarching purpose of the surah.

The unit uses the article for the claim that a ruku' is a semantic unit (U4-07) and for the link between ruku' reading and thematic coherence (U4-08). It does not use the article for any claim about Surah al-Nahl, and it does not quote the article's analysis of 16:71-76 in detail. The unit's own worked examples on 108 and 103 are original teaching cases.
