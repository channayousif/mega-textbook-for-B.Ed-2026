## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

The verses the unit uses for the importance of understanding (U1-01) say that the Qur'an was sent down in Arabic so that its hearers would understand (12:2), that it is a blessed Book revealed so that people reflect on its verses (38:29), ask whether people do not reflect on it (47:24), and say it has been made easy for remembrance (54:17). 2:2 calls it guidance for the God-conscious; 1:5 is the declaration of worship recited in al-Fatiha.

For the characteristics (U1-02, U1-03): 69:41 says it is not the word of a poet; 2:183 uses kutiba for fasting being decreed; 2:153 and 2:183 open with the address to believers; the Uthmani text of 2:153 spells the word for prayer with a wāw shape; Surah 112's verses end on the same sound.

For letters (U1-04): 2:32 ends with al-ʿalīm (the Knowing); 2:10 ends with ʿadhābun alīm (a painful punishment) and contains qulūb (hearts); 18:18 contains kalbuhum (their dog).

For word formation (U1-05): 2:282 contains kātib (scribe), forms of kataba and ʿallamahu; 15:21 contains maʿlūm, translated as a known, specified measure; 96:4-5 contain ʿallama; 44:14 contains muʿallam; 1:2 contains al-ʿālamīn; 2:2 uses al-kitāb for the revealed Book.

For frequent expressions (U1-06): 1:1, 1:2, 2:20, 2:25, 2:153, 2:156, 4:26, 18:24, 103:3 and 2:286 contain the expressions listed in the topic 1.2 table, with the translations quoted there. Surah 9's metadata gives bismillah_pre as false.
