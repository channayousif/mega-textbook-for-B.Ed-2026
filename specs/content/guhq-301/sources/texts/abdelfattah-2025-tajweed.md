## Evidence notes (original paraphrase)

The authors note that assessing pronunciation is usually hard, but that for the Qur'an it is made possible by the rigorous recitation rules (tajwid) set out by Muslim scholars. Their phonetic script encodes each Arabic letter with its vowels and, at a second level, the articulation characteristics (ṣifāt) of every sound, and their models detect recitation errors against these rules.

The unit uses the paper only to describe tajwid as precise rules covering where and how each letter is produced, precise enough that even automatic assessment can rely on them (U1-04). It does not use the paper for any teaching method or error rate, and it does not claim the paper studies Urdu-speaking learners.
