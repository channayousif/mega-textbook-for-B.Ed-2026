# Weekly maintenance records

One folder per ISO week (`YYYY-Www`, Asia/Karachi), written by the crew's Sunday survey
(`.claude/skills/weekly-maintenance/`, `ops/crew/README.md` "Weekly maintenance"):

- `spec.md`: findings with evidence, and the week's goals.
- `plan.md`: the approach per goal and what it must not break.
- `tasks.md`: at most six tasks; each became one crew job and PR (format:
  `.claude/skills/weekly-maintenance/references/tasks-format.md`).
- `intelligence.md`: which intelligence candidates were promoted, deferred or rejected, and the
  effect of items already adopted (`specs/intelligence/registry.md`).

The PRs themselves record what shipped; these files record why.
