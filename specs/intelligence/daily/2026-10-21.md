## C1: Concurrent ready branches conflict during hourly batch merge
- **Kind:** crew brief or planner fix
- **Cause:** Multiple concurrent jobs editing shared course trackers (`tasks.md`) conflict during `git merge --no-ff` into the hourly batch branch. While a conflict fix job resolves the conflict against `main`, subsequent hourly batches merge, leaving branches in repeated cascading conflict loops (e.g. `gnas-301-u05-repair_en` through conflict5).
- **Recurrence:** 11 board failures and 4 automatic merge log errors in 24 h (14 total, up from 3).
- **Evidence:** job:gnas-301-u03-repair_en, job:gnas-301-u05-repair_en, job:geng-301-u05-review_en, log:geng-301-u00-salvage-conflict1.1.log, log:geng-301-u00-salvage-conflict2.1.log
- **Proposed home:** crew brief or planner fix: serialize jobs targeting the same course tracker, or enhance `scripts/crew/crew.mjs` batch merge to auto-rebase or automatically resolve additive markdown table entries in `tasks.md`.
- **Signature:** `left out of the batch: <path> conflicts with main (it could not join the hourly batch).; merge fix queued`
- **Registry:** matches candidate C7 in 2026-10-20.md (raises its recurrence).
