## C7: SVG Urdu text starting with numerals or ending with punctuation renders out-of-order without `&#x200F;`
- **Kind:** skill / style-guide proposal
- **Cause:** SVG text renderers apply the Unicode Bidirectional Algorithm (BiDi). In `.ur.svg` diagrams, when an Urdu label begins with European numerals (such as "5 تا 3 تا 2 تا 2") or ends with trailing punctuation, BiDi treats ASCII digits and punctuation as weak or neutral characters, reversing numeral order or placing trailing punctuation at the visual start. Enclosing or prefixing with the Right-to-Left Mark (RLM, `&#x200F;` / U+200F) establishes the correct RTL contextual embedding.
- **Recurrence:** 1 handoff note and multiple redrawn schematics in EFMP-303 Unit 1.
- **Evidence:** `job:efmp-303-u01-refresh_figures` handoff ("SVG text in `.ur.svg` files needs `&#x200F;` around labels that start with a number or end with punctuation, or they display in the wrong order; Unit 1 needs fresh G3 run; the G5 reviewer should check the redrawn Urdu SVGs, including numeral order such as '5 تا 3 تا 2 تا 2'").
- **Proposed home:** `.claude/skills/author-unit/references/svg-style.md` and `specs/content/style-guide.md`: document mandatory `&#x200F;` markers around Urdu labels with leading digits or punctuation in SVG text nodes.
- **Signature:** `svg text in <code> files needs <code> around labels that start with a number or end with punctuation, or they display in the wrong order.`
- **Registry:** new.
