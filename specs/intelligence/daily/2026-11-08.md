## C1: Review reports in subdirectories are invisible to latestReport, causing premature repeat reviews that escalate on unchanged inputs
- **Kind:** brief / planner fix (crew fix)
- **Cause:** `latestReport` (`scripts/crew/crew.mjs:88`) filters `readdirSync(dir).filter(f => f.endsWith('.json'))` directly under `G3/`. When a reviewer places its bundle in a subfolder (such as `crew-efmp-407-u05-review_en/crew-efmp-407-u05-review_en.json`), `latestReport` returns null. `stagesFor` treats the unit as unreviewed and plans `review_en-r1` before an author can repair the initial findings. The second reviewer finds the prior report, sees unchanged inputs, and escalates, burning the unit's final ADR-0019 cycle.
- **Recurrence:** 16 review signals in the window (7 `inherited from <path>, not resolved`, 9 `inherited unresolved finding from <path>`). Two units (EFMP-407 Unit 5 and Unit 8) burned cycle 2 and escalated without repairs.
- **Evidence:** `review:specs/content/efmp-407/reviews/unit-05/G3/crew-efmp-407-u05-review_en-r1.json`; `review:specs/content/efmp-407/reviews/unit-08/G3/crew-efmp-407-u08-review_en-r1.json`; `job:efmp-407-u05-review_en-r1`; `job:efmp-407-u08-review_en-r1`.
- **Proposed home:** `scripts/crew/crew.mjs` (`latestReport`): scan nested subdirectories for review reports containing `disposition`, and enforce a flat review bundle layout in the review-unit skill.
- **Signature:** `inherited from <path>, not resolved`
- **Registry:** new.
