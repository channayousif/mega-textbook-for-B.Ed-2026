## C1: Planner and retry logic dispatch translation while an advisory G3 escalation is active with blocking defects
- **Kind:** crew brief or planner fix
- **Cause:** `scripts/crew/planner.mjs:stagesFor` schedules translation (`translate`) once G3 review artifacts exist, without checking whether the latest G3 review report has an `escalate` or `revise` disposition with unresolved blocking defects. In GPKS-402 Unit 6, G3 review (01a18ccb) escalated with blocking findings across sources (purported quotations and invalid citations in `worldbank-pdu-2025-unit-06.md`), assessment (ERQ 2 stem and rubric mismatch; RRQ 8 scoring criteria mismatch), and readability. Translation was dispatched (`gpks-402-u06-translate`), stopped under the mandatory `translate-unit` skill stop rule, and then the crew scheduled a retry (`gpks-402-u06-translate-r1`) before any prerequisite author repair landed, wasting multiple worker runs.
- **Recurrence:** 2 translation stopped handoffs (`gpks-402-u06-translate`, `gpks-402-u06-translate-r1`), 2 PHRs (20261047, 20261048), and 2 G3 review escalation handoffs (`gpks-402-u02`, `gpks-402-u06`) in 24 h.
- **Evidence:** job:gpks-402-u06-translate; job:gpks-402-u06-translate-r1; phr:history/prompts/general/20261047-gpks-402-u06-translation-blocked.general.prompt.md; phr:history/prompts/general/20261048-gpks-402-u06-translation-retry.general.prompt.md; review:specs/content/gpks-402/reviews/unit-06/G3/01a18ccb-4a8c-7c60-8ed2-7c546d08340e.json.
- **Proposed home:** Planner fix in `scripts/crew/planner.mjs` (`stagesFor`): hold translation and translation-retry jobs when the latest G3 review report disposition is `escalate` or `revise` until an author repair job resolves all blocking findings.
