## C1: Review reports in subdirectories are invisible to latestReport, causing premature repeat reviews that escalate on unchanged inputs
- **Kind:** brief / planner fix (crew fix)
- **Cause:** `latestReport` (`scripts/crew/crew.mjs:88`) filters `readdirSync(dir).filter(f => f.endsWith('.json'))` directly under `G3/` or `G5/`. When a reviewer places its bundle in a subfolder (such as `crew-efmp-407-u05-review_en/crew-efmp-407-u05-review_en.json` or `crew-efmp-407-u08-review_en/crew-efmp-407-u08-review_en.json`), `latestReport` returns null. `stagesFor` treats the unit as unreviewed and plans `review_en-r1` before an author can repair initial findings. The second reviewer finds the prior report, sees 100% unchanged input manifests, and escalates under ADR-0019, burning the unit's final cycle.
- **Recurrence:** 16 review signals in the window (9 `inherited unresolved finding from <path>`, 7 `inherited from <path>, not resolved`; 32 total since 2026-11-07). Two units (EFMP-407 Units 5 and 8) burned cycle 2 and escalated without repairs. Repeats 2026-11-09 C1, 2026-11-08 C1.
- **Evidence:** `review:specs/content/efmp-407/reviews/unit-08/G3/crew-efmp-407-u08-review_en-r1.json`; `review:specs/content/efmp-407/reviews/unit-05/G3/crew-efmp-407-u05-review_en-r1.json`; `review:specs/content/efmp-407/reviews/unit-08/G3/20261108T203238Z-g3-desk-04abd597.json`; `job:efmp-407-u08-review_en-r1`; `job:efmp-407-u05-review_en-r1`.
- **Proposed home:** `scripts/crew/crew.mjs` (`latestReport`): scan nested subdirectories for review reports containing `disposition`, and enforce a flat review bundle layout in the review-unit skill.
- **Signature:** `inherited unresolved finding from <path>`
- **Registry:** repeats 2026-11-09 C1, 2026-11-08 C1 (raises recurrence).

## C2: Rubric-only prefixes in review findings collapse distinct pedagogical, assessment, and accessibility issues into single-word clusters
- **Kind:** crew fix
- **Cause:** In `scripts/crew/intel.mjs`, `FINDING_LABEL` only strips numbered prefixes matching `/^(?:(?:[FPR]|A-)\d+(?:,?\s+(?:blocking|uncertain|advisory))?(?:,?\s+[a-z][\w-]*)?|Previous finding \d+|Prior finding \d+ remains|P\d+ carried):\s+/`. Human desk reviews (such as `CMY` in `20261108T203238Z-g3-desk-*.json`) and various agent reviewers frequently start findings directly with bare rubric names (`Authority: `, `Sources/pedagogy: `, `Pedagogy: `, `assessment: `, `accessibility: `) or unspaced labels (`Prior finding4 remains partly unresolved: `, `Carry-forward prior finding 5: `). When `FINDING_LABEL` fails to match, `findingClause` falls back to `split(/[:.]\s/)[0]`, isolating the rubric keyword or label prefix as the entire signature. This collapses dozens of completely distinct content flaws into uninformative clusters like `assessment`, `accessibility`, and `pedagogy`.
- **Recurrence:** 21 recent review signals across 7 clusters (4 assessment, 4 fn <path>, 3 prior findingn partly unresolved, 3 accessibility, 3 pedagogy, 2 prior finding n, 2 carry-forward prior finding n; 79 total signals).
- **Evidence:** `review:specs/content/efmp-301/reviews/unit-09/G3/20261108T203238Z-g3-desk-cb72c312.json`; `review:specs/content/geng-301/reviews/unit-02/G3/20261108T203238Z-g3-desk-573fa197.json`; `review:specs/content/gicp-400/reviews/unit-07/G3/20261108T203238Z-g3-desk-8314fbc3.json`; `scripts/crew/intel.mjs:116-123`.
- **Proposed home:** `scripts/crew/intel.mjs` (`FINDING_LABEL`): expand the regex to strip leading unnumbered rubric labels (e.g. `^(?:(?:sources|pedagogy|assessment|accessibility|authority|readability)(?:\/[a-z]+)?|prior finding\s*\d+(?:\s+partly unresolved|\s+unresolved)?|carry-forward prior finding\s*\d+):\s+` case-insensitively).
- **Signature:** `assessment`
- **Registry:** matches proposed I-0023 (demonstrates that PR #501 / M4 needs broader label stripping for desk reviews).
