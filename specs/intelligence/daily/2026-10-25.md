## C1: Tracked content-status.json causes merge conflicts that stall tick.sh cron and fail fresh gates
- **Kind:** brief / planner fix
- **Cause:** `static/content-status.json` is gitignored but tracked on `origin/main`. `scripts/crew/crew.mjs` regenerates it on every plan in the crew control checkout, dirtying the working tree. When `origin/main` advances, `tick.sh` (running under `set -e`) fails at `git merge origin/main` with merge conflicts on the dirty file, silently stalling the crew cron for ~6 hours (24 consecutive ticks) without alerting until manual intervention. Concurrently, fresh checkouts inherit a stale copy from `origin/main` that fails `check:content-status`.
- **Recurrence:** 2 stale errors (105 total), 1 content-status gate failure (143 total), 1 multi-gate failure (28 total), 1 handoff note, and 1 PHR reporting a ~6h cron stall in this window.
- **Evidence:** log:efmp-302-u02-repair_en-conflict1.1.log; log:efmp-302-u02-repair_en-conflict4.1.log; log:efmp-302-u02-translate.1.log; log:efmp-301-u01-review_ur.2.log; phr:history/prompts/general/0856-unstall-crew-tick-dirty-content-status.general.prompt.md; job:intel-2026-10-24-distill.
- **Proposed home:** Dispatcher script `scripts/crew/tick.sh` (`git checkout -q -- static/content-status.json` before merge) and repository untracking via survey task M1 (`git rm --cached static/content-status.json`).
- **Signature:** `✗ <path> is stale - it no longer describes the working tree.`
- **Registry:** candidate I-0007 (raises recurrence; provides evidence for survey task M1).
