## C3: Review bundles display assessment stems and answer keys together, preventing required independent pre-key solving

- **Evidence:** review:specs/content/gpks-402/reviews/unit-02/G3/01a18cbc-8067-7f42-995a-419ce6765add.json; phr:history/prompts/general/20261039-gpks402-unit-two-g3-advisory.general.prompt.md.

- **Signature:** `assessment stems and supplied answers were displayed together during inspection`
