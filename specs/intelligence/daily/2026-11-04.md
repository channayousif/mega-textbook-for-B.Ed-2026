## C5: Harvester reviewSignals splices rubric dimension labels and carried finding headers into noisy fragmented clusters
- **Kind:** crew brief or planner fix
- **Cause:** `reviewSignals` in `scripts/crew/intel.mjs:116` extracts finding signatures by splitting on colon or period: `String(f.message).split(/[:.]\s/)[0]`. When reviewers prefix findings with rubric labels (`F02 sources:`, `F05 assessment:`, `F06 readability:`, `F07 pedagogy/assessment:`, `F1, blocking:`, `F4, uncertain:`) or carry-forward tracking markers (`Previous finding 1:`, `P06 carried:`, `Prior finding 4 remains:`, `A-06 (carried from run-005...):`), the split extracts only the label prefix. Normalization converts numbers to `n` and slashes to `<path>`, generating 12 uninformative clusters like `fn sources`, `fn assessment`, `fn <path>`, `previous finding n`, `pn carried`, and `prior finding n remains`. The true defect text following the prefix is lost to the intelligence harvest.
- **Recurrence:** 12 separate review clusters accounting for 88 recent signals (132 total) in 24 h.
- **Evidence:** review:specs/content/gpks-402/reviews/unit-06/G3/01a18ccb-4a8c-7c60-8ed2-7c546d08340e.json; review:specs/content/gqur-301/reviews/unit-08/G3/01a1608a-2fc5-7f41-a824-c08320f0383b.json; review:specs/content/efmp-301/reviews/unit-05/G3/codex-crew-cycle2.json; review:specs/content/efmp-302/reviews/unit-03/G3/20261102T140729Z-g3-desk-0893bf77.json; `scripts/crew/intel.mjs:113-118`.
- **Proposed home:** Harvester fix in `scripts/crew/intel.mjs` (`reviewSignals`): strip leading rubric tags (`^F\d+(?:,\s*(?:blocking|uncertain|advisory))?(?:\s+[a-z/]+)?:\s*`) and carry-forward prefixes (`^(?:Previous finding \d+|Prior finding \d+(?: remains)?|P\d+ carried|R\d+ partial|A-?\d+ [^:]+):\s*`) before extracting the finding signature.
