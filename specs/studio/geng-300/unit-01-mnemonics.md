## Topic 1.1 - Words in context: vocabulary and grammar

| Mnemonic | Stands for (source words) | Source anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **"Make, Take, Commit"** (M-T-C) | the collocations Pakistani English gets wrong: **make** a decision, **take** a photo, **commit** a mistake | Explanation, "Collocation" |
| **"Thin Is Not Slim"** (T-I-N-S) | **connotation** goes beyond literal meaning: thin can be negative, slim positive | Explanation, "Connotation" |
| **"Agree, Time, Complete, Place, Small Words"** (A-T-C-P-S) | the five common error areas: subject-verb **agreement**, verb **tenses**, **complete** sentences (fragments and run-ons), **placement** of modifiers, **small words** (articles) | Explanation, "Communicative grammar" |
