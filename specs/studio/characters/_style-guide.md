# Cast style guide (season 1)

**Style token** (paste after every prompt block):

> Flat 2D vector animation style, clean consistent dark-teal outlines of medium weight, soft cel
> shading with one shadow tone, simple rounded shapes, friendly proportions (head about one fifth of
> body height), large expressive eyes, no photorealism, no film grain, warm even classroom lighting
> from the upper left, muted paper-white background.

**Palette** (house colours, `scripts/media/brand.mjs`): deep teal `#123e43` (outlines, dark clothing
accents), amber `#e5a333` (one accent item per character), pale teal `#a6dfdc` (secondary fills),
paper `#f8f5ef` (backgrounds). Skin, hair and remaining clothing use natural mid-saturation tones.

**Sets**: a bright staffroom (wooden table, mugs, bookshelf) and a primary or secondary classroom
(whiteboard, desks in rows or groups, a window). Signs, posters and boards stay blank: all text is
added in post.

**Aspect ratios**: 16:9 for long-form shots, 9:16 generated natively for Shorts; never crop one from
the other for a final shot.

**Sheets** (per character): front, three-quarter, profile, full body, six expressions (neutral,
smiling, thinking, surprised, doubtful, explaining with an open hand), two outfits (everyday, and a
warm-weather variant). Plain paper background, no props unless named.

**Avoid in every prompt**: on-screen text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, extra characters,
photoreal skin, style drift, uniforms or insignia of a real institution, religious or political
symbols.
