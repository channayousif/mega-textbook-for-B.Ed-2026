## Topic 3.1 - Air pollution

| Mnemonic | Stands for (source words) | Source anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **"PM Two Point Five"** (P-M-2-5) | **PM2.5** is the finer particulate, small enough to reach deep into the lungs; it carries most of the health burden | Explanation, "The pollutants that matter" |
| **"Particles, Nitrogen, Sulphur, Carbon, Ozone, Lead"** (P-N-S-C-O-L) | the six pollutants that matter: **particulate matter**, **nitrogen dioxide**, **sulfur dioxide**, **carbon monoxide**, ground-level **ozone**, **lead** | Explanation, same section; fig-U3-2 |
| **"Source, Pathway, Receptor"** (S-P-R) | controls sit at the three links: clean **source**, a **pathway** that cannot be cleaned only shortened, and the **receptor** where a teacher's AQI check lives | Explanation, "Control strategies sit at the three links" |

## Topic 3.2 - Noise pollution

| Mnemonic | Stands for (source words) | Source anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **"85 Decibels"** (8-5) | long or repeated exposure at or above **85 dB** can damage hearing; the damage zone begins there | Explanation, opening; fig-U3-3 |
| **"Ten dB Is Tenfold"** (1-0-1-0) | decibels are **logarithmic**: every 10 dB is a tenfold increase in sound energy, so 72 to 88 dB is about forty times | Explanation, opening; worked example |
| **"Heart, Sleep, Learning, Stress"** (H-S-L-S) | the health impacts beyond the ear: **cardiovascular** effects, disturbed **sleep**, poorer school **performance**, and stress-hormone effects | Explanation, sources and health impacts |

## Topic 3.3 - Hazards in health, safety and environment

| Mnemonic | Stands for (source words) | Source anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **"Physical, Mechanical, Biological, Chemical, Psychological"** (P-M-B-C-P) | the five hazard classes, each controlled differently | Explanation, "Hazards sort into five classes"; fig-U3-5 |
| **"Hazard the Thing, Incident the Event"** (H-I) | a **hazard** is the potential (the wet floor); the **incident** is the event (the slip) | Explanation, opening |
| **"Environment the Source, Health the Arrival"** (E-H) | the **environment** is where exposures come from; **health** is where they arrive; environmental safety manages that connection | Explanation, "Health and environment" |

## Topic 3.4 - Risk assessment and management

| Mnemonic | Stands for (source words) | Source anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **"Likelihood, Severity"** (L-S) | risk is judged on two axes, how **likely** and how **severe**; the grid ranks so the loudest hazard is not fixed first | Explanation, "Step two: assess" |

| **"Remove, Replace, Guard, Procedures, PPE"** (R-R-G-P-P) | the hierarchy of controls best to worst; **PPE** is last because it protects one person, only while worn, only if correct | Explanation, "Step three: control" |
