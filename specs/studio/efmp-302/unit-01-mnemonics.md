## Topic 1.1 - What makes teaching a profession

| Mnemonic | Stands for (source words) | Source anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **"Know, Train, Code, Answer"** (K-T-C-A) | the four marks of a profession: **specialised knowledge**, **training and a qualification**, a **code of conduct**, **accountability** to a professional community | Explanation, "Features that distinguish a profession from an occupation" |
| **"Person or Profession"** (P-P) | **professionalism** is how one teacher conducts themselves day to day; **professionalisation** is how the field organises itself | Explanation, "Professionalism in teaching versus professionalisation of teaching" |
| **"Route, Bodies, Judgement"** (R-B-J) | what teaching is still building toward fuller status: a longer **training route**, stronger **professional bodies**, more room for **judgement** | Explanation, "Comparative perspective: teaching and other professions" |

## Topic 1.2 - From an industrial model to inquiry-based teaching

| Mnemonic | Stands for (source words) | Source anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **"Cover, Control, Comply"** (C-C-C) | the industrial model values **efficiency**, **control** and **compliance**, measured by covering the syllabus on time | Explanation, "The industrial model of teaching" |
| **"Notice, Adjust"** (N-A) | inquiry-based professionalism: **noticing** how pupils are thinking and **adjusting** the next step on evidence | Explanation, "The shift to inquiry-based professionalism" |
| **"Pre, Alone, Together, Pressured"** (P-A-T-P) | Hargreaves' four ages: **pre**-professional, **autonomous** (alone), **collegial** (together), **post**-professional (pressured) | Same section |

## Topic 1.3 - The four dimensions of teacher professionalism

| Mnemonic | Stands for (source words) | Source anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **"Know, Act, Balance, Belong"** (K-A-B-B) | the four dimensions: **knowledge**, ethical **conduct**, the **balance** of accountability, autonomy and collegiality, **belonging** to professional bodies | Summary |
| **"More Than Grammar"** (M-T-G) | **pedagogical content knowledge** goes beyond subject knowledge: knowing how these learners learn this topic | Explanation, "Specialised knowledge and training" |
| **"All Three or None"** (A-T-O) | **accountability**, **autonomy** and **collegiality** must be held in balance, not maximised one at a time | Explanation, "Accountability, autonomy, and collegiality" |

## Topic 1.4 - Becoming a teacher: developing your identity

| Mnemonic | Stands for (source words) | Source anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **"Outside, Inside, Reflect"** (O-I-R) | identity is shaped from **outside** by context and policy, from **inside** by beliefs and values, and kept growing by **reflection** | Summary |
| **"Not a Diary"** (N-D) | reflection is using the record to question an assumption, not merely recording events | Explanation, "Reflection and self-awareness in teacher-identity formation" |
| **"Who Am I Becoming"** (W-A-I-B) | the habit question that turns experience into learning: *who am I becoming as a teacher?* | Explanation, "Who am I becoming as a teacher?" |
