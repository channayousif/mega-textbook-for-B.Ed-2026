# Quantitative Reasoning Applications

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you can:

1. Build and check a simple statistical model for a school question, stating its assumptions (course outcome 1).
2. Organise and interpret school data through a complete problem-solving cycle (course outcome 2).
3. Choose and defend suitable methods, explain uncertainty, and make a limited data-based decision (course outcome 3).

## Prerequisite knowledge

Review Units 1–15. You should be able to identify data types, describe a sample, calculate centre and spread, use counting and probability, read a scatter plot, interpret a regression line, and distinguish estimation from testing. Revisit the relevant unit whenever the reason for a method is unclear.

## In this unit

1. [Statistical modeling exercises](./topic-01): construct, check and revise a useful simplification.
2. [Problem solving using statistical concepts](./topic-02): take one school question from a plan to a decision.
3. [Review and integration of concepts](./topic-03): connect the course methods and defend their use.

## How to use this unit

Read the worked examples before attempting each group task. All school records supplied here are synthetic teaching data, not findings about named schools. Keep a notebook with four columns: question, method, evidence and limitation. Complete the topic cycles, then attempt the unit assessment before consulting its final self-study guidance.
