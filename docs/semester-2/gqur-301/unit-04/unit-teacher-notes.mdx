# Unit 4 teacher notes

These notes are for the instructor delivering GQUR-301 Unit 4, which follows
Chapter 4 of the course guide (Week 4). They complement the student-facing
topics with teaching strategies, likely difficulties and practical work, in line
with the guide's teaching strategy of lectures, discussion, question-answer,
brainstorming and practical group and individual work.

## Sequencing suggestion

| Week | Topic | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 4 (first half) | 4.1 Mean (arithmetic average) | Computing the mean from a list and from a frequency table |
| 4 (second half) | 4.2 Median | Finding the middle value; robustness |

## Teaching strategies

### Topic 4.1 Mean (arithmetic average)

**Opening.** Give the ten quiz marks from the topic and ask students to compute
"the average". Most will get 14.9. Ask whether 14.9 describes how the class did.
Keep the discomfort: Topic 4.2 resolves it.

**Key move.** Model the balance point with a ruler and coins (or students
standing on a number line taped to the floor), then show the frequency-table
route and check it gives the same answer as writing every value out. Watch for
students dividing by the number of rows.

### Topic 4.2 Median

**Opening.** Return to the 14.9 and ask students to line the ten marks up and
take the middle. The answer (16.5) sits with the cluster.

**Key move.** Change one extreme mark several times and recompute both measures
on the board. The mean moves every time; the median does not. Insist on sorting
first, and show an even-n case where the median is not a data value.

## Common misconceptions and how to address them

| Misconception | How to address |
|---|---|
| "The mean is always the best average" | Use skewed income data to show the median describes a typical family better. |
| "The median is just the middle of the list" | Show that the middle of an unsorted list is meaningless; sorting is the first step. |
| "The median must be one of the values" | With an even number of values it is the average of the two middle ones, as with 16.5 for the quiz marks. |

## Practical work

1. **Two routes to one mean (Topic 4.1).** Pairs compute a mean from a list and from a frequency table and reconcile any difference.
2. **Move one mark (Topic 4.2).** Groups change one extreme value repeatedly and record what happens to the mean and the median.

## Assessment guidance

The unit assessment (10 MCQ / 10 RRQ / 5 ERQ) is summative. Use each topic's
"Check your understanding" items for formative assessment during teaching. The
practicum tasks link the college classroom to the placement school.
