# Social Stratification and Gender

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Explain class, caste and social hierarchy, using a respectful community example. (CLO 2, 4; SLO:GSOS-301-4-1)
- Describe types of social mobility and distinguish personal effort from access to opportunity. (CLO 2, 4; SLO:GSOS-301-4-1)
- Distinguish gender roles from biological sex and identify a stereotype in classroom practice. (CLO 2, 4; SLO:GSOS-301-4-2)
- Analyze inequality using conflict and feminist perspectives, then justify a feasible school response. (CLO 2, 4; both unit SLOs)

## Prerequisite knowledge

Recall Unit 3's distinction between status, a social position, and role, the expectations attached to that position. Recall Unit 1's conflict perspective: groups may have unequal power over resources and rules. Explain each idea to a partner before starting. If you cannot give an example, return to those topics first.

## In this unit

1. [How societies rank people](./topic-01) examines class, caste, hierarchy, mobility and unequal opportunities.
2. [How gender structures social life](./topic-02) examines learned expectations, evidence of inequality and feminist explanations.

## How to use this unit

Read each classroom situation before its explanation. Follow the worked analysis, then test it with your partner in the activity. Attempt the retrieval questions without looking back. Use the checklist to decide what to revisit, rather than ticking it after reading once.

Finish with the [unit assessment](./unit-assessment). Write your responses before opening its final self-study guidance. The public practice bank is for learning; your teacher may set a separate graded assessment. Names, school cases and small classroom datasets are fictional unless a published source is explicitly identified. Never use this unit to rank classmates or ask children to disclose caste, income or private experiences. Urdu translation and independent expert review remain pending.
