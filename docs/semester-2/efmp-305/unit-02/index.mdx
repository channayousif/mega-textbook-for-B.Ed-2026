# Understanding Learner Diversity

Every classroom in Pakistan contains a wider range of learners than any single lesson plan
was designed for. This unit gives you the categories to name that diversity, the techniques
to identify what individual pupils need, and the practices that build a classroom where
difference is respected rather than stigmatised.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

1. Name the main categories of learner diversity and give examples of each.
2. Distinguish impairment, disability and handicap, and explain why the distinction matters.
3. Describe common learning difficulties and their classroom signs.
4. Use simple observation and assessment techniques to identify a pupil's individual needs.
5. Evaluate classroom practices that promote respect for diversity and those that reinforce stigma.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 1 is assumed: you should be comfortable with the concept of inclusive education, the
principles of inclusion, and the difference between equality and equity. If you have not yet
worked through Unit 1, do so first.

## In this unit

1. [The many faces of learner diversity](./topic-01) - the categories of disability, learning difficulty and social difference, and what they look like in practice.
2. [Identifying needs and building respect](./topic-02) - how to identify individual needs and how to promote respect while avoiding stigma and labelling.

## How to use this unit

Each topic is one learning cycle. Start with the classroom situation, work through the
explanation, and try the activity before you move on. The "Check your understanding" items
are there to help you retrieve what you have learned - have a go at them without looking back,
then check your answer. The unit ends with a summative assessment that draws the two topics
together.
