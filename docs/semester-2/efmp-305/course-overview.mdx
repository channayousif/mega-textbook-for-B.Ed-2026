# Inclusive Education - Course Overview

This course equips prospective teachers with a grounded understanding of inclusive education:
what it means, why it matters in Pakistan, and how to put it into practice in real classrooms.
It begins with the movement from segregation to inclusion, builds through the diversity of
learners you will meet, and gives you the teaching strategies, classroom-management tools,
legal knowledge and collaborative skills to make every pupil a full participant.

## Assessment

Assessment follows the owner ruling (D-2026-0053): **40% formative / 60% summative**.

| Component | Marks |
|---|---|
| Mid-Term | 30 |
| Final-Term | 50 |
| Assignment/Presentation/Sessional | 10 |
| Attendance | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

This course's guide does not specify a course-wide breakdown of teaching strategies or practical
work beyond what each unit's own topics cover - see each unit's Teacher Notes for the
strategies used to teach it.

## Recommended reading

The course guide does not list a prescribed textbook list. Each unit points you to open-access
sources and key policy documents (the National Education Policy and the Rights of Persons with
Disabilities Act 2018) for scope and further reading only; they are never reproduced as content.
