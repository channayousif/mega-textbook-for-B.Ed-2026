# Foundations of Inclusive Education

Before you can build an inclusive classroom, you need a clear picture of what "inclusive
education" actually means - and, just as importantly, what it does not mean. In everyday
conversation the words *segregation*, *integration* and *inclusion* are often used as if they
the same thing. They are not. This unit gives you the precise meanings, the principles that
education, and the reasons it matters for every teacher in Pakistan.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

1. Explain what inclusive education means and distinguish it from segregation and integration.
2. Name the key principles that define an inclusive education system.
3. Analyse common misconceptions about inclusion and explain why they fall short.
4. Evaluate the importance of inclusive education in the Pakistani context.

## Prerequisite knowledge

None beyond HSC-level general study skills. This is an opening unit. If you have ever
wondered why some children sit in a separate classroom, or whether a child with a disability
can learn alongside others, you already have the experience this unit builds on.

## In this unit

1. [Meaning, concept and the inclusion spectrum](./topic-01) - what inclusive education is, and how it differs from segregation and integration.
2. [Principles and the case for inclusion in Pakistan](./topic-02) - the principles of inclusion and why it matters in Pakistan.

## How to use this unit

Each topic is one learning cycle. Start with the classroom situation, work through the
explanation, and try the activity before you move on. The "Check your understanding" items
are there to help you retrieve what you have learned - have a go at them without looking back,
then check your answer. The unit ends with a summative assessment that draws the two topics
together.
