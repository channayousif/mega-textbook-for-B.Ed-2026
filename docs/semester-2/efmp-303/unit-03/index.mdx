# Development Plans and Educational Planning

Pakistan's education system does not run on policy alone. Every school building, every teacher
appointment, every textbook printed depends on a decision made years earlier in a planning office.
Development plans set the budgets. Educational planning decides how those budgets translate into
classrooms, teachers and learning outcomes. This unit examines both: how national development plans
treat education, and how educational planning as a process works in practice.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

1. Explain the role of development plans in shaping education and how education links to economic growth in Pakistan.
2. Distinguish between micro, macro and strategic planning and describe the stages of the educational planning process.
3. Analyse the planning process using established planning models and evaluate their application in Pakistani education.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 2's coverage of Pakistan's national education policies. You should be familiar with the National
Education Policy 2009 and 2017 and the difference between a policy statement and a plan for carrying it
out.

## In this unit

1. [Development plans and education](./topic-01) - how national and provincial plans shape education and connect it to economic growth.
2. [Concepts and types of educational planning](./topic-02) - what educational planning means, its types and stages.
3. [Planning process and models](./topic-03) - needs assessment, resource allocation and the models that guide planning.
