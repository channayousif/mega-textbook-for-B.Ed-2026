# Introduction and Historical Background

Every teacher in Pakistan works inside a system shaped by decisions made years before they entered a
classroom. National education policies set the goals. Development plans decide the budgets. Historical
choices about language, access and structure determined which children sit in which classrooms. This
unit gives you the background you need to understand where that system came from and what "educational
policy" and "planning" actually mean.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

1. Explain what educational policy and planning mean and distinguish one from the other.
2. Describe the relationship between education, society and development in the Pakistani context.
3. Identify the major developments in education before and after Pakistan's independence.
4. Place the key milestones in Pakistan's education system in chronological order and explain their significance.

## Prerequisite knowledge

None. This is the opening unit of the course. If you have ever wondered why your school follows a
particular curriculum or why certain grades exist the way they do, this unit begins to answer those
questions.

## In this unit

1. [What educational policy and planning mean](./topic-01) - meaning, scope and importance.
2. [Education, society and development](./topic-02) - how education connects to the wider society.
3. [Education before and after independence](./topic-03) - the historical arc from pre-1947 to the early decades.
4. [Milestones in Pakistan's education system](./topic-04) - the landmark events that shaped the system.

## How to use this unit

Each topic is one learning cycle. Start with the classroom situation, work through the explanation,
and try the activity before you move on. The "Check your understanding" items ask you to recall and
apply what you have learned - try them without looking back first. The unit ends with a summative
assessment that draws the four topics together.
