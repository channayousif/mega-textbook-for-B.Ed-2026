# Quality Assurance and Development

Every education system needs ways to check whether it is working and ways to make it better. Quality assurance is the set of processes that do this checking. Development is what happens when education actually improves people's lives. This unit examines both: how Pakistan assures quality in its schools and teacher training, and how education connects to the country's social and economic progress.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

1. Explain the main quality assurance mechanisms in Pakistan's education system, including accreditation, curriculum standardization and teacher training.
2. Describe how education contributes to human capital formation and to reducing inequality and poverty.
3. Analyse the role of gender and inclusive education policies in making education accessible to all groups.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 2 introduced quality assurance as part of the National Education Policy 2009. Unit 4 covered evaluation as a tool for checking policy implementation. This unit builds on both to examine quality assurance in detail and to connect education to development outcomes.
