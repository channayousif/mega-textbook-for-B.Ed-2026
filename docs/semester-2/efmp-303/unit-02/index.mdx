# National Education Policies

Every education system is shaped by the policies that govern it. In Pakistan, national education policies have set the direction for schooling, curriculum, teacher recruitment and quality assurance for decades. Some of these policies brought real change. Others remained documents on a shelf. This unit examines the major education policies of Pakistan, with particular attention to the National Education Policy 2009 and the National Education Policy 2017.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

1. Identify the major education policies of Pakistan from 1947 to 2000 and describe their main objectives.
2. Explain the objectives, guiding principles and implementation strategies of the National Education Policy 2009.
3. Describe the vision, curriculum reforms and policy priorities of the National Education Policy 2017.
4. Compare the 2009 and 2017 policies and analyse why policy outcomes often fall short of policy intentions.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 1 gave you an overview of Pakistan's education system, its history and the meaning of educational policy and planning. That foundation will help you understand why certain policies were introduced and what problems they tried to solve.
