# EFMP-303: Educational Policies and Plans of Pakistan

**Programme:** B.Ed (4-Year)
**Semester:** 2
**Major:** Professional
**Credit hours:** 3 (3-0)

## Course description

This course examines the development, implementation, and evaluation of educational policies and
planning in Pakistan. It focuses on historical and contemporary education policies, national
development plans, challenges in educational planning, and the role of government and institutions
in shaping quality education. Emphasis is placed on linking educational planning with the social,
economic, and political contexts of Pakistan.

## Course objectives

By the end of this course you will be able to:

1. Understand the historical development of educational policies in Pakistan.
2. Analyse the objectives and impact of major educational plans.
3. Examine educational planning processes at national and provincial levels.
4. Identify challenges and issues in educational policy implementation.
5. Propose strategies to improve educational planning and policy outcomes.

## Units in this course

1. [Unit 1 - Introduction and Historical Background](./unit-01/) - what policy and planning mean, and where Pakistan's education system came from.
2. [Unit 2 - National Education Policies](./unit-02/) - the major policies from 1947 to the present, including the 2009 and 2017 National Education Policies.
3. [Unit 3 - Development Plans and Educational Planning](./unit-03/) - five-year plans, and the concepts, types and models of educational planning.
4. [Unit 4 - Monitoring, Evaluation and Challenges](./unit-04/) - how policies are monitored and evaluated, and what gets in the way of implementation.
5. [Unit 5 - Quality Assurance and Development](./unit-05/) - accreditation, standards, teacher development, and education's role in social and economic development.
6. [Unit 6 - International Perspectives and Future Directions](./unit-06/) - learning from other countries, technology in planning, and where Pakistan's education system is heading.

## Recommended readings

- Andrabi, T., & Rizvi, S. *Educational Planning in Pakistan.*
- Khan, A. (2018). *Education Policy and Planning in Pakistan.*

## Assessment

This course carries the standard B.Ed weighting (60% summative / 40% formative per Constitution
Art. III.7). Each of the six units ends with a summative assessment bank: 10 multiple-choice, 10
restricted-response and 5 extended-response questions, with answers and marking guidance for
self-study. There is no separate course-review document at this stage.
