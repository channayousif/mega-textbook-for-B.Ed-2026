# Critical Thinking and Reflective Practices - Course Overview

This course equips prospective teachers with a grounded understanding of critical
thinking and reflective practice as professional capabilities - the nature of
thinking, the barriers to it, and the habits of mind that make reflection a
disciplined professional act rather than an occasional one.

## Assessment

Assessment follows the GECE default: **60% summative / 40% formative** (Constitution Art. III.7).
This course's guide does not specify a course-wide breakdown of teaching strategies or practical
work beyond what each unit's own topics cover - see each unit's Teacher Notes for the
strategies used to teach it.
