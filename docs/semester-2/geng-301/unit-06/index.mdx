# Ethical Considerations in Writing

Writing is not only about skill - it is also about honesty. When you use someone else's ideas, words or
data, you must give them credit. When you present information, you must ensure it is credible. This
unit teaches you the ethical foundations of academic and professional writing: finding good sources,
citing them correctly, integrating them into your own writing and avoiding plagiarism.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

1. Find and evaluate credible sources using the CRAAP test (currency, relevance, authority, accuracy, purpose).
2. Distinguish APA and MLA citation styles and use one correctly.
3. Integrate sources into your writing through quoting, paraphrasing and summarizing.
4. Identify different types of plagiarism and explain how to avoid them.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Units 1-5 of this course (GENG-301). You should understand expository writing, the writing process,
essay structure, expository types and audience-aware writing.

## In this unit

1. [Finding and evaluating sources](./topic-01) - credibility and the CRAAP test.
2. [Citing your sources](./topic-02) - citation basics, APA and MLA.
3. [Using others' words and ideas](./topic-03) - quoting, paraphrasing and summarizing.
4. [Staying honest in your writing](./topic-04) - avoiding plagiarism.

## How to use this unit

This unit is practical. Each topic teaches a skill you will use in every academic piece of writing.
The activities ask you to evaluate real sources, write citations and practise paraphrasing. The unit
assessment tests both knowledge and application.
