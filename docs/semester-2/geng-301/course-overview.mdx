# Expository Writing - Course Overview

This course builds on the foundation of Functional English (GENG-300) to develop your ability to
produce clear, concise and coherent expository texts in English. You will learn the writing process
from first draft to final proof read, organize essays with strong thesis statements and well-structured
body paragraphs, write in different expository modes for different purposes and audiences, and uphold
ethical standards of originality and proper citation.

## Assessment

Assessment follows the GECE default: **60% summative / 40% formative** (Constitution Art. III.7).
This course's guide does not specify a course-wide breakdown of teaching strategies or practical
work beyond what each unit's own topics cover - see each unit's Teacher Notes for the strategies
used to teach it.
