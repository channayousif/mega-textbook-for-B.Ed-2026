# Unit 1 Teacher Notes

These notes support the teaching of GENG-301 Unit 1. They assume you have read the unit content and
the course guide. They offer strategies, common difficulties and practical work ideas.

## Teaching strategies

The course guide suggests lecture, discussions, question-answer and brain storming. For this unit,
combine them in this order:

1. **Start with the classroom situation** in each topic. Read it aloud or ask a pupil to read it.
   Ask: "What is the problem here?" This activates the topic's central tension before you teach.
2. **Use question-answer** to introduce new terms (mode of writing, clarity, topic sentence). Define
   each term, give an example, then ask pupils to produce their own example.
3. **Use brainstorming** for the activities. For the paragraph-building activity in Topic 1.3, give
   pupils two minutes to write as many supporting details as they can before they organize them.
4. **Use small-group discussion** for the "Sort the passages" activity in Topic 1.1. Circulate and
   listen for disagreements - they are where the learning happens.

## Sequencing

The three topics build on each other. Topic 1.1 establishes what expository writing is. Topic 1.2
names what makes it effective. Topic 1.3 applies both to the paragraph unit. Teach them in order.
A reasonable split for a three-week unit:

- Week 1: Topic 1.1 (what expository writing is).
- Week 2: Topic 1.2 (what makes writing effective).
- Week 3: Topic 1.3 (writing a strong paragraph) and the unit assessment.

## Likely misconceptions and how to handle them

| Misconception | Topic | How to handle |
|---|---|---|
| "Expository writing means long, formal writing" | 1.1 | Give a three-sentence expository example. Ask pupils to compare it with a long, formal paragraph that says less. |
| "Coherence means using transition words" | 1.2 | Give two paragraphs: one with no transitions but logical order, one with many transitions but jumbled ideas. Ask which is easier to follow. |
| "A paragraph must have five sentences" | 1.3 | Ask pupils to count the sentences in three good paragraphs from a textbook. The counts will differ. |

## Practical work

The course guide lists group work, group assignments, individual assignments and presentations. For
this unit:

- **Group work**: the "Sort the passages" and "Revise for clarity" activities in Topics 1.1 and 1.2.
- **Group assignment**: as a group, collect three expository texts from the school environment and
  classify each by mode. Present findings in the next session.
- **Individual assignment**: draft a unified, complete paragraph on a self-chosen topic. Submit for
  peer review using the rubric in Topic 1.3.
- **Presentations**: pairs present their revised paragraph from the Topic 1.2 activity, explaining
  what clarity, coherence and organization changes they made.

## Connection to practicum

In Topic 1.3, the "Try this at your practicum school" task asks pupils to evaluate a Grade 4 or 5
pupil's paragraph. This builds the diagnostic skill teachers need: reading a pupil's writing and
identifying what to teach next. Encourage pupils to bring their notes to the next session for
discussion.
