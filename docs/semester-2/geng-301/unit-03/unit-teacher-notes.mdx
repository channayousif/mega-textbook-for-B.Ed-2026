# Unit 3 Teacher Notes

These notes support the teaching of GENG-301 Unit 3.

## Teaching strategies

This unit teaches essay structure. The most effective approach is to build an essay together as a class,
then have pupils build their own.

1. **Demonstrate the funnel structure** on the board. Write a hook, then a sentence of context, then a
   thesis - narrating your thinking as you go.
2. **Use question-answer** for thesis statements. Give weak theses and ask: "Can you argue with this?
   Is it specific?" Pupils quickly learn to evaluate.
3. **Use group work** for body paragraph building. Give each group one point from a shared thesis and
   ask them to build the paragraph. Combine into a full essay.
4. **Use brainstorming** for hooks. Give a topic and ask pupils to suggest four different hook types.

## Sequencing

Teach the four topics in order - each builds on the previous. A reasonable split for a four-week unit:

- Week 1: Topic 3.1 (hook and introduction).
- Week 2: Topic 3.2 (thesis statement).
- Week 3: Topic 3.3 (body paragraphs).
- Week 4: Topic 3.4 (conclusion and cohesion) and the unit assessment.

## Likely misconceptions and how to handle them

| Misconception | Topic | How to handle |
|---|---|---|
| "The introduction should list every point" | 3.1 | Show a funnel introduction next to a "table of contents" introduction. Ask which makes you want to read on. |
| "A thesis is just the topic title" | 3.2 | Give five weak theses and ask pupils to rewrite them using the checklist. |
| "Evidence speaks for itself" | 3.3 | Give a paragraph with evidence but no explanation. Ask: "What does this evidence prove? Do you see the connection?" |
| "The conclusion just repeats the introduction" | 3.4 | Show four different conclusions for the same essay and ask which leaves the strongest impression. |

## Practical work

- **Group work**: the body paragraph building activity (Topic 3.3) works well in groups of three.
- **Group assignment**: as a group, write a full expository essay (introduction, two body paragraphs,
  conclusion) on a given topic. Submit the outline, the draft and the final version.
- **Individual assignment**: each pupil writes a thesis statement and three body paragraph topic
  sentences for a self-chosen topic. Peer review using the thesis checklist from Topic 3.2.
- **Presentations**: pairs present their essay structure - thesis, three topic sentences, planned
  conclusion type - to the class.
