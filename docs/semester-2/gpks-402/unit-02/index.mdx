# Reform Movements and the Pakistan Movement

Pakistan did not appear suddenly in 1947. It grew from decades of reform, thinking and political
organisation. This unit traces that groundwork: the religious and educational reformers who
revived Muslim consciousness in South Asia, and the political movement that turned the idea of
a separate homeland into reality.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

1. Describe the contributions of Shah Waliullah, Sir Syed Ahmad Khan and the Aligarh Movement to Muslim reform in South Asia.
2. Explain the role of Muslim intelligentsia in shaping political consciousness.
3. Trace the formation and early years of the All-India Muslim League.
4. Identify the key milestones of the Pakistan Movement from 1906 to 1947.
5. Explain how the reform movements created the intellectual groundwork for the creation of Pakistan.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 1, specifically the Two-Nation Theory and the ideological context. This unit builds on that
foundation by showing how the ideas of distinct Muslim nationhood were developed by reformers and
then turned into a political movement.
