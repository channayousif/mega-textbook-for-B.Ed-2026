# Pakistan Studies - Course Overview

This course provides a comprehensive study of Pakistan's historical, ideological,
political, geographical, economic and socio-cultural development. It is designed for
future educators in the B.Ed (4-Year) programme and emphasises the Pakistan Movement,
constitutional evolution, governance structure, national issues, and the role of
education in nation building.

## Syllabus

The course guide (University of Sindh, Faculty of Education, Semester 2) organises the
content across sixteen weeks:

| Week | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to Pakistan Studies: meaning, scope and importance; national ideology and identity |
| 2 | Ideological Basis: Two-Nation Theory; Islamic concept of state; Vision of Quaid-e-Azam |
| 3 | Muslim Reform Movements: Shah Waliullah; Sir Syed Ahmad Khan and Aligarh Movement |
| 4 | Pakistan Movement (1906-1947): All-India Muslim League; key events; creation of Pakistan |
| 5 | Constitutional Development: Objectives Resolution; Constitutions of 1956 and 1962 |
| 6 | Constitution of Pakistan (1973): background, salient features, Islamic provisions |
| 7 | Political and Administrative Structure: federal system; legislature, executive, judiciary |
| 8 | Fundamental Rights and Duties of Citizens |
| 9 | Mid-Term Examination |
| 10 | Geography of Pakistan: physical features; climate and natural resources; strategic importance |
| 11 | Economy of Pakistan: agriculture, industry and trade; economic challenges |
| 12 | Society and Culture of Pakistan: social structure; cultural diversity; languages and traditions |
| 13 | Foreign Policy: principles; relations with neighbouring countries; international organisations |
| 14 | Contemporary Issues: population, poverty, terrorism, environment |
| 15 | Role of Education in Nation Building: civic education; responsibilities of teachers |
| 16 | Review and Final Assessment |

## Course objectives

On completion of this course, prospective teachers will be able to:

1. Understand the ideological foundations of Pakistan.
2. Analyse the historical evolution of the Pakistan Movement.
3. Examine constitutional and political developments.
4. Understand Pakistan's geography, economy and society.
5. Promote national integration and civic responsibility through education.

## Assessment

Assessment follows the default split: **60% summative / 40% formative** (Constitution
Art. III.7). Each unit carries a 10/10/5 summative bank (10 multiple-choice, 10
restricted-response, 5 extended-response questions) plus formative checks embedded in
every topic cycle.
