# Unit 1: Foundations of Classroom Observation

<Figure id="fig-U1-9" kind="illustration" src="/img/figures/efpc-411/unit-01/fig-U1-9.webp" alt="A student-teacher sits quietly at the side of a Sindh government classroom, taking structured observation notes while a mentor teacher facilitates an interactive lesson." width={1344} height={756} />

## Unit learning outcomes

After completing this unit, student-teachers will be able to:

- `SLO:EFPC-411-1-1`: Explain the meaning, importance and types of classroom observation and the roles of supervisors, mentors and observers, and apply professional ethics during school observations.
- `SLO:EFPC-411-1-2`: Identify the five core focus areas of classroom observation and select appropriate observation tools, including checklists, rating scales, anecdotal records and reflective journals.
- `SLO:EFPC-411-1-3`: Prepare for, conduct and record classroom observations using low-inference note-taking techniques while recognising and mitigating personal observer bias.
- `SLO:EFPC-411-1-4`: Analyse observation findings, deliver constructive and supportive feedback to peers, and link observational insights to personal professional growth and reflective practice.

## Prerequisite knowledge

This unit is the opening unit of EFPC-411 School Observation. It builds upon foundational concepts established in earlier semesters:

- `EFMP-302` (Teaching Profession, Semester 1): Understanding the professional role, responsibilities, codes of conduct and ethical obligations of Pakistani school teachers.
- `EFPG-401` (Teaching of English, Semester 3): Lesson planning components, instructional staging (opening, presentation, guided practice, independent practice, closure), and classroom interaction patterns.

## How to use this unit

This unit serves as your core study material during Weeks 1 to 4 of the university seminars before you begin your fifteen-day school placement. Read each topic cycle thoroughly before the scheduled seminar session, take part actively in the pair and group activities, and complete the retrieval checks and summative tasks.

Use the observation frameworks, ethical protocols, and note-taking templates presented across these four topics to prepare your observation tools. When you visit your cooperating placement school, apply these procedures consistently to record objective classroom evidence rather than personal judgements. Complete the self-assessment checklists at the end of each topic to confirm your readiness for the field placement.
