# Unit 3: The Observation Portfolio and Reflective Practice

## Unit learning outcomes

After completing this unit, student-teachers will be able to:

- `SLO:EFPC-411-3-1`: Organise a systematic observation portfolio and compile a detailed, objective school profile documenting the physical, administrative, and social learning environment.
- `SLO:EFPC-411-3-2`: Document classroom lesson observations using standardized proformas and analytically evaluate instructional strategies, teaching aids, and student engagement using a reflective cycle.
- `SLO:EFPC-411-3-3`: Compile classroom management, discipline, and assessment records, and critically compare differing management styles and student feedback practices.
- `SLO:EFPC-411-3-4`: Synthesize observation records and weekly reflections into a comprehensive final reflective report, identifying personal strengths, development areas, and a concrete action plan for future teaching practice.

## Prerequisite knowledge

This unit serves as the synthesis and portfolio completion stage of EFPC-411 School Observation. It builds directly upon the concepts, observational skills, and field tasks developed across earlier units and semesters:

- `EFPC-411` Unit 1 (Foundations of Classroom Observation): Understanding observation types, ethical principles, low-inference note-taking, and constructive feedback protocols.
- `EFPC-411` Unit 2 (School Placement Observation Tasks): Practical field observation tasks conducted during the fifteen-day school placement, focusing on classroom climate, lesson structure, time management, and inclusive practice.
- `EFMP-302` (Teaching Profession, Semester 1): Professional codes of conduct, teacher responsibility, and ethical commitments to students and schools.

## How to use this unit

This unit guides your work during Weeks 9 to 12 of your school placement and seminar schedule. During this period, you transition from daily classroom observation to systematic portfolio organisation, critical reflection, and professional self-appraisal.

As you work through each topic cycle:

- Assemble your field records systematically according to the standard portfolio structure.
- Move beyond descriptive reporting by applying the four-stage reflective cycle (describe, analyse, interpret, plan) to your observation notes.
- Use the authentic examples and templates provided in each topic to verify that your school profile, lesson observation sheets, management logs, and weekly reflections meet professional standards.
- Complete the formative checks and summative tasks in each topic before preparing your final reflective report and personal action plan for university evaluation and viva voce presentation.
