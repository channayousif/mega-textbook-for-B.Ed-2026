# Educational Assessment and Evaluation - Course Overview

This Semester 4 professional course explores how classroom teachers connect learning, assessment and evaluation. Prospective teachers will build their understanding of formative and summative learning assessment, and examine how assessment evidence informs daily pedagogical decisions. The course develops practical skills in designing test items, applying alternative classroom assessment techniques, interpreting assessment results, and ensuring fairness, validity and ethical practice in Pakistani schools.

## Course learning outcomes

The University of Sindh B.Ed course guide gives six learning outcomes:

1. Understand the concept and nature of assessment.
2. Differentiate between standardized and classroom tests.
3. Integrate objectives with learning assessment.
4. Develop and analyze test items of different levels.
5. Understand different alternative classroom assessment techniques.
6. Interpret test scores and results of different assessment techniques.

## Begin with Unit 1

[Understanding Assessment and Evaluation](./unit-01/) covers the first two teaching weeks. It has three topics, a unit assessment and notes for teachers. Other units are added through separate authoring jobs.

## Teaching strategies and assessment

The course guide suggests lectures, interactive discussions, question-answer sessions, demonstrations and case-study analysis. Practical work includes hands-on computer exercises, individual and group assignments, internet-research tasks and presentations.

The course guide specifies no separate weighting for course assessment, so the programme default applies: 40% formative and 60% summative. Your teacher will explain which assignments and quizzes are collected. Unit 1 provides self-contained retrieval questions, collaborative activities and summative tasks with self-study guidance.

## Readings

Each topic lists its own readings. The course guide recommends foundational works on educational evaluation, assessment and monitoring. The course also draws upon open-access guides from the Higher Education Commission (HEC) on classroom assessment and test development, as well as Seifert and Sutton's open textbook *Educational Psychology*. Read all three topics of Unit 1 before attempting the unit assessment.
