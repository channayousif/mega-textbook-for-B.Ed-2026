# Unit 6: Standardized Testing, Ethics and Modern Trends

<Figure id="fig-U6-9" kind="illustration" src="/img/figures/efmp-408/unit-06/fig-U6-9.webp" alt="A senior teacher and a trainee teacher examine student assessment portfolios and rubrics together at a wooden desk in a Sindh educational centre." width={1344} height={756} />

Welcome to Unit 6 of Educational Assessment and Evaluation. This concluding unit examines large-scale standardized testing systems, professional ethics in assessment, contemporary digital innovations, and the evolving assessment landscape in Pakistani schools. Prospective teachers explore how standardized instruments differ from classroom-based tools, examine the ethical responsibilities of educators regarding test integrity and data privacy, evaluate digital assessment platforms, and analyze national policies and teacher standards that guide assessment practice across Pakistan.

## Unit learning outcomes

After completing this unit, prospective teachers will be able to:

1. **Analyse standardized testing systems (SLO:EFMP-408-6-1):** Explain the characteristics, advantages, and limitations of standardized tests, examining national testing initiatives in Pakistan and international comparative assessments.
2. **Apply professional ethics in assessment (SLO:EFMP-408-6-2):** Apply ethical principles of fairness, academic integrity, and learner confidentiality to classroom and institutional assessment decisions.
3. **Evaluate modern assessment trends (SLO:EFMP-408-6-3):** Evaluate digital assessment tools, online testing platforms, and continuous competency-based models for diverse learning contexts.
4. **Appraise Pakistani assessment policy and professional standards (SLO:EFMP-408-6-4):** Describe assessment policies across public and private school sectors, deconstruct Standard 5 of the National Professional Standards for Teachers in Pakistan (NPSTP), and contrast test-based with assessment-based evaluation cultures.

## Prerequisite knowledge

This unit synthesises concepts from earlier units in this course and prior degree studies:

- **EFMP-408 Units 1 to 5:** Foundational principles of assessment, validity, reliability, test construction, performance tasks, and assessment statistics.
- **EFMP-302 The Teaching Profession (Semester 1):** Professional ethics, teacher accountability, and teacher codes of conduct.
- **EFMP-303 Educational Policies and Plans of Pakistan (Semester 2):** Institutional frameworks of examination boards, education policies, and curriculum reforms.

## In this unit

Unit 6 is organised into four interconnected topic cycles:

1. [Topic 6.1 - Standardized testing](./topic-01): Explores the nature, standardization process, norming, advantages, limitations, and examples of national and international standardized assessments.
2. [Topic 6.2 - Ethics in assessment](./topic-02): Examines systemic bias, fairness, academic integrity, prevention of examination malpractice, and the confidentiality of learner data.
3. [Topic 6.3 - Where assessment is heading](./topic-03): Analyzes digital assessment tools, computer-based testing platforms, automated scoring, and the shift toward continuous competency-based evaluation.
4. [Topic 6.4 - Assessment policy and practice in Pakistan](./topic-04): Investigates assessment practices across government and private sectors, deconstructs NPSTP Standard 5, and contrasts traditional test-based cultures with formative assessment-based learning cultures (enrichment topic).
