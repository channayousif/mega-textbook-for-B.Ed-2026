# Unit 3: Methods and Approaches of Teaching Science

## Unit learning outcomes

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

- `SLO:EFPG-402-3-1`: Describe teacher-centred and laboratory-based methods of teaching science, such as the lecture, discussion, laboratory and demonstration methods, and their uses.
- `SLO:EFPG-402-3-2`: Plan inquiry-based, discovery, experiential and project learning, and choose a suitable method for a given science topic and class.

## Prerequisite knowledge

This unit builds on Units 1 and 2 of this course: you should know what science is as a process, a product and an attitude, and how the Grade 4 to 8 science curriculum is organised. It also builds on the general teaching methods you met in `EFPG-401` Teaching of English in Semester 3. Here those ideas are applied to science lessons.

## How to use this unit

The unit covers weeks 5 and 6 of the course. Each topic teaches one section of the course guide and pairs two related methods, so that you can compare them. Each is one short learning cycle: a classroom situation, an explanation with a worked example, a group activity, quick retrieval questions, a summary, a self-assessment checklist, a task for your practicum school and a summative task.

Read one topic before each class and do the activity with your classmates. As you read, keep one question in mind: "When would I use this method, and when would I not?" Topic 3.7 ends with four questions for choosing a method, which pull the whole unit together. When you have finished all seven topics, use the self-assessment checklists to decide what to revise, then attempt the unit assessment.
