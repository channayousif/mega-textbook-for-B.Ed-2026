# Teaching of Science - Course Overview

This Semester 4 pedagogy course prepares you to teach General Science well. It draws on physics, chemistry, biology and mathematics, and focuses on how to teach science: methods, resources, lesson planning, practical work in the laboratory and the assessment of pupils' learning. Its aim is a teacher who can make pupils curious, help them think critically and solve problems. The examples come from Grade 4 to 8 General Science as set out in the National Curriculum of Pakistan 2022-23 for Science.

## Course objectives

The University of Sindh B.Ed course guide gives six objectives. The student-teacher will be able to:

1. Understand the nature, scope, and values of science.
2. Develop skills for effective science teaching at secondary level.
3. Apply modern methods and approaches of teaching science.
4. Use teaching-learning resources and ICT.
5. Plan lessons and conduct science teaching effectively.
6. Evaluate students' learning using appropriate tools.

The guide says "secondary level", but this programme prepares teachers for Grades 1 to 8, so the course applies each idea to elementary science.

## Begin with Unit 1

[Nature and Importance of Science](./unit-01/) covers the first two teaching weeks. It has five short topics and a unit assessment:

- Topic 1.1 explains what science means and its five characteristics.
- Topic 1.2 presents science as a process, a product and an attitude.
- Topic 1.3 separates the aims of teaching science from lesson objectives.
- Topic 1.4 explores the intellectual, moral and social values of science education.
- Topic 1.5 traces the role of science in Pakistani society.

The guide's other seven units follow, two weeks each: science curriculum and textbook; methods and approaches; teaching-learning resources; lesson planning and teaching skills; the science laboratory; assessment and evaluation; and professional growth. They are added through separate authoring jobs.

## Practicum and assessment

The course guide lists four practical tasks: preparing lesson plans, micro-teaching and macro-teaching, preparing teaching aids, and classroom observation with a reflective journal. These run alongside the units; each topic also ends with a short task for your practicum school.

The guide gives no assessment weights, so the programme default applies: 40% formative and 60% summative. Your teacher will explain which work is collected. Each unit includes retrieval questions, group activities and extended tasks with self-study guidance.

## Readings

Each topic lists its readings. The guide's books are older Indian methods texts, such as Das (1985) and Yadav (2002), which are in print only. For curriculum content this textbook uses Pakistan's own National Curriculum 2022-23 for Science, together with open-access sources that it links from each topic. Read all five topics of Unit 1 before attempting the unit assessment.
