# Unit 6: Maintenance and Organization of Science Laboratory

## Unit learning outcomes

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

- `SLO:EFPG-402-6-1`: Explain the role of the science laboratory in school education, plan functional laboratory layouts, organize safe storage and disposal of chemicals, and maintain proper container labelling and stock registers.
- `SLO:EFPG-402-6-2`: Apply laboratory safety rules and emergency first-aid measures, and describe the professional duties and responsibilities of the science teacher in managing school science laboratories.

## Prerequisite knowledge

You need the pedagogical foundations from Unit 3 (Methods and Approaches of Teaching Science) and Unit 4 (Teaching-Learning Resources), particularly the safe handling of basic apparatus and the use of low-cost materials. No previous technical laboratory technician certification is required.

## How to use this unit

This unit covers weeks 11 and 12 of the Teaching of Science course. Each topic represents one self-contained, nine-part instructional cycle: a real classroom situation from Sindh, an evidence-based explanation with worked examples, an active pair task, retrieval checks, a summary, a self-assessment checklist, a school practicum transfer task, and a summative task with an analytic mini-rubric.

Study each topic before lecture sessions, practice conducting risk audits and filling stock registers during campus practicals, and adapt the safety protocols to the physical realities of your practicum placement school.
