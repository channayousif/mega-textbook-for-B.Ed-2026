# Unit 4: Teaching-Learning Resources

<Figure id="fig-U4-11" src="/img/figures/efpg-402/unit-04/fig-U4-11.webp" alt="A science teacher in a Sindh classroom holds up a home-made bottle lung model while pupils set up filtration apparatus, use a projected simulation on a laptop, and shelves display low-cost models, seedlings and stones gathered from the community." />

## Unit learning outcomes

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

- `SLO:EFPG-402-4-1`: Explain the importance of teaching aids in science education and demonstrate the safe and correct use of basic laboratory equipment.
- `SLO:EFPG-402-4-2`: Prepare low-cost and improvised teaching aids from local materials; and use ICT tools, science exhibitions, field trips, and community resources effectively in science teaching.

## Prerequisite knowledge

You need the pedagogical foundations from Unit 1 (Nature and Importance of Science), Unit 2 (Science Curriculum and Textbook), and Unit 3 (Methods and Approaches of Teaching Science). You should also recall digital literacy concepts from GICT-300 Applications of ICT (Semester 1). You do not need previous technical laboratory technician training.

## How to use this unit

This unit covers weeks 7 and 8 of the course. Each topic represents one self-contained learning cycle: a concrete classroom situation from Sindh, an explanation grounded in national standards with worked examples, a collaborative pair activity, retrieval questions, a summary, a self-assessment checklist, a transfer task for your practicum school, and a summative task with an analytic rubric.

Read the assigned topic before class, practice handling apparatus or assembling models during workshops, and reflect on how resources can be adapted for schools that have no formal laboratory.
