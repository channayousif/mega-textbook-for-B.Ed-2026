# Unit 8: Professional Growth and Contemporary Issues

## Unit learning outcomes

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

- `SLO:EFPG-402-8-1`: Describe the science teacher's role in society and explain the essential qualities of an effective science teacher across knowledge, skills, and dispositions.
- `SLO:EFPG-402-8-2`: Plan a small classroom action-research cycle to investigate and solve a science learning problem; explain the principles of environmental education, education for sustainable development, and recent innovations in science teaching.

## Prerequisite knowledge

This unit builds upon Units 1 to 7 of this course. You should be familiar with the nature of science (Unit 1), science curriculum standards and textbook analysis (Unit 2), inquiry and active learning methods (Unit 3), laboratory and low-cost learning resources (Unit 4), lesson planning and teaching skills (Unit 5), laboratory safety and management (Unit 6), and diagnostic assessment techniques (Unit 7). You will synthesize these pedagogical foundations to plan ongoing professional growth and address modern societal challenges through school science.

## How to use this unit

This unit covers Weeks 15 and 16 of the course. Each topic represents one self-contained learning cycle: an authentic classroom hook, an evidence-based explanation with a worked example, a collaborative group activity, quick retrieval questions, a summary of enduring understandings, a self-assessment checklist, a practicum school transfer task, and a summative task with an analytic rubric.

Read the assigned topic before each class session and participate actively in the peer learning activities. Connect the theoretical discussions to your observations during school practicum placement. When you have completed all five topics, review your self-assessment checklists and attempt the unit assessment to evaluate your mastery.
