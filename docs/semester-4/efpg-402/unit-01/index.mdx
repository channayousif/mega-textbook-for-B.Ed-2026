# Unit 1: Nature and Importance of Science

<Figure id="fig-U1-11" src="/img/figures/efpg-402/unit-01/fig-U1-11.webp" alt="A science teacher in a Sindh government classroom watches Grade 6 pupils observe, count and record sprouting seeds, one pupil raising her hand with a question." />

## Unit learning outcomes

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

- `SLO:EFPG-402-1-1`: Explain the meaning and characteristics of science, and describe science as a process, a product and an attitude.
- `SLO:EFPG-402-1-2`: State the aims and values of teaching science and explain the role of science in modern society.

## Prerequisite knowledge

This is the opening unit of the course. It assumes the secondary-school science (Matric) that every student-teacher has studied: you should recall basic ideas such as germs and disease, plants and their needs, matter and energy. You do not need any earlier course in science teaching.

## How to use this unit

The unit covers the first two weeks of the course. Each topic is one short learning cycle: a classroom situation, an explanation with a worked example, a group activity, quick retrieval questions, a summary, a self-assessment checklist, a task for your practicum school and a summative task. Read one topic before each class, and do the activity with your classmates in class.

The examples come from Grade 4 to 8 General Science in Pakistan, as set out in the National Curriculum of Pakistan 2022-23 for Science. Keep a copy of the curriculum's progression grid nearby; several tasks ask you to find a standard in it. When you have finished all five topics, use the self-assessment checklists to decide what to revise, then attempt the unit assessment.
