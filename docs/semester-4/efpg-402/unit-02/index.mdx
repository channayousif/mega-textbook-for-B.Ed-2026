# Unit 2: Science Curriculum and Textbook

<Figure id="fig-U2-11" src="/img/figures/efpg-402/unit-02/fig-U2-11.webp" alt="Four student teachers at a Sindh college of education compare open science textbooks with a curriculum binder and simple apparatus, judging how well the books fit what pupils should learn." />

## Unit learning outcomes

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

- `SLO:EFPG-402-2-1`: Explain the principles of science curriculum construction and the aims of the national science curriculum.
- `SLO:EFPG-402-2-2`: Analyse the organisation and sequencing of content in a science curriculum; evaluate a science textbook; and relate science to other subjects.

## Prerequisite knowledge

You need Unit 1 (Nature and Importance of Science), where you learned what science is and why it is taught. You should also recall the idea of a national curriculum from your Semester 2 course EFMP-303 Educational Policies and Plans of Pakistan. You do not need any special background in curriculum design.

## How to use this unit

The unit covers weeks 3 and 4 of the course. Each topic is one short learning cycle: a classroom situation, an explanation with a worked example, a group activity, quick retrieval questions, a summary, a self-assessment checklist, a task for your practicum school and a summative task. Read one topic before each class, and do the activity with your classmates in class.

Several tasks ask you to look at the National Curriculum of Pakistan 2022-23 for Science, Grades 4-8 and at a Sindh science textbook. Keep a copy of the curriculum's progression grid and one textbook chapter nearby.
