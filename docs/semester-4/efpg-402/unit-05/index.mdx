# Unit 5: Lesson Planning and Teaching Skills

<Figure id="fig-U5-11" src="/img/figures/efpg-402/unit-05/fig-U5-11.webp" alt="A student teacher in a Sindh science classroom pauses after a question beside her open lesson plan while girls in groups raise their hands and one pupil from each group calmly collects a tray of apparatus." />

## Unit learning outcomes

After completing this unit, you will be able to:

- `SLO:EFPG-402-5-1`: Explain the importance and types of lesson plans; write instructional objectives.
- `SLO:EFPG-402-5-2`: Use questioning, explanation and classroom-management skills in a science lesson.

## Prerequisite knowledge

You need the foundation built in Unit 3 (Methods and Approaches of Teaching Science) and Unit 4 (Teaching-Learning Resources). You should also draw upon your study in EFMP-408 Educational Assessment and Evaluation regarding Bloom's taxonomy and learning domains. You do not need prior experience in formal curriculum design, but you will build on your daily observations of classroom teaching during school visits.

## How to use this unit

This unit covers weeks 9 and 10 of the course. Each topic represents one complete learning cycle: an authentic classroom situation from a Pakistani school, a thorough explanation with worked examples, a collaborative activity, check-your-understanding retrieval questions, a summary, a self-assessment checklist, a transfer task for your school practicum, and a summative task with an analytic rubric.

Read each topic before class and carry out the practical activities with your peers. Keep your National Curriculum of Pakistan 2022-23 Science progression grid and a secondary science textbook nearby to practice writing objectives and planning 5E instructional sequences.
