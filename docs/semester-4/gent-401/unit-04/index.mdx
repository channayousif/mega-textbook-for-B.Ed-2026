# Developing a Business Plan

<Figure id="fig-U4-5" kind="illustration" src="/img/figures/gent-401/unit-04/fig-U4-5.webp" alt="Three student-teachers in a sunlit classroom collaborate around a table drafting business plan charts with educational learning materials." width={1200} height={675} />

"Sir, we have a wonderful idea for an after-school science club in Khairpur, but the bank manager and the school head both asked to see our business plan. Where do we begin?" A B.Ed trainee asked this during practicum seminar. Having an innovative idea is only the starting point of venture creation. To turn an idea into an enduring reality, an entrepreneur must build a comprehensive business plan that details how the enterprise creates, delivers, and captures value.

This unit guides you step by step through developing a formal business plan. You will learn the nine standard components of a plan, how to craft an executive summary and articulate clear vision and mission statements, how to design realistic marketing and operational plans, and how to structure organizational roles as your enterprise grows.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Identify and describe the nine standard components of a comprehensive business plan. *(Remember; SLO:GENT-401-4-1)*
- Explain the role of the executive summary and why it must be written last. *(Understand; SLO:GENT-401-4-1)*
- Formulate focused, actionable vision and mission statements for a venture. *(Apply; SLO:GENT-401-4-1)*
- Design a marketing plan that aligns target customer segments with product, price, place, and promotion. *(Apply; SLO:GENT-401-4-2)*
- Map an operational workflow from order intake to customer delivery with clear quality checkpoints. *(Apply; SLO:GENT-401-4-2)*
- Analyze organizational structures across growth stages from a solo founder to a functional enterprise. *(Analyze; SLO:GENT-401-4-2)*
- Synthesize venture elements into a coherent vision statement, mission statement, and executive summary. *(Create; SLO:GENT-401-4-1)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

This unit builds directly on Unit 3 (Market Research and Business Models). You should be familiar with identifying customer needs, collecting market information, assessing competitors, and defining a basic value proposition and revenue streams.

## In this unit

1. [The plan's frame: components, summary, vision and mission](./topic-01) - the nine core sections of a business plan, writing an effective executive summary, and establishing enduring vision and mission statements.
2. [Marketing, operations and organisation](./topic-02) - crafting target-focused marketing plans, mapping daily operational workflows with quality control, and designing functional organizational structures as a venture scales.

## How to use this unit

Study the two topics in sequential order. Each topic opens with a concrete classroom situation from Sindh, walks through theoretical explanations grounded in Pakistani small business and educational contexts, and provides worked examples before asking you to complete tasks.

Engage actively with the collaborative group activities and test your recall using the "Check your understanding" prompts before looking at notes. Use the self-assessment checklists to monitor your mastery, and complete the practicum activities at your placement school.

After finishing both topics, work through the [unit assessment](./unit-assessment), which contains 10 multiple-choice questions, 10 restricted-response questions, and 5 extended-response questions. Check your work against the bounded marking guidance at the end of the assessment page. Trainees and instructors will find practical pedagogical guidelines and workshop activities in the [notes for teachers](./unit-teacher-notes).
