# Marketing, Legal and Ethical Issues - Assessment and Review

## Unit summary

Marketing is the comprehensive entrepreneurial process of identifying customer needs, developing valuable solutions, establishing fair pricing, ensuring convenient delivery, and communicating truthfully with target audiences. A brand is not merely a logo, color scheme, or business name; it is the total perception, emotional connection, and promise of dependable value that customers associate with a venture over time. Market positioning deliberately establishes how an offering sits in the minds of prospective buyers relative to competing options, often mapped on two-dimensional grids comparing price, quality, convenience, or service.

Advertising is merely one sub-component of promotion. Early-stage startups with scarce capital practice bootstrap marketing, relying on high-trust, cost-effective channels including word of mouth, personal demonstrations, targeted community notices, and referral programs. In Pakistan's mobile-first environment, accessible digital tools such as WhatsApp Business and organic educational content marketing allow ventures to build direct, respectful relationships with families and institutions without incurring large advertising expenditures.

Operating an enterprise requires choosing an appropriate legal structure under Pakistani law. A sole proprietorship is simple to establish through the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) for a National Tax Number (NTN), but subjects the owner to unlimited personal liability. A partnership under the Partnership Act, 1932 pools capital and skills across multiple founders, but imposes joint and several unlimited liability for all business obligations. A private limited company incorporated through the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan's (SECP) digital eZfile portal under the Companies Act, 2017 establishes a distinct corporate legal person, providing limited liability protection to shareholders, perpetual succession, and the formal standing needed to secure institutional contracts and bank credit.

Intellectual property (IP), administered by the Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan (IPO-Pakistan), protects intangible creations of the human mind. Trademarks protect distinguishing brand names, symbols, and logos; copyright protects original written, artistic, musical, and software expressions; patents protect novel, inventive, industrially applicable technical solutions; and industrial designs protect unique aesthetic appearances. Bare ideas carry zero legal protection until expressed in a tangible form or registered formally.

Legal compliance sets the minimum standard of behavior, while entrepreneurial ethics governs moral responsibility, fairness, and honesty. Entrepreneurs must resist temptations to exaggerate claims, copy competitors' creative works without authorization, exploit trainee workers, or compromise pupil safety. Enduring entrepreneurial success depends on building authentic customer trust through transparent communication, contractual integrity, and lawful practices.

## Answers and marking guidance

### MCQ answer key

1. B - A brand is the total perception, emotional connection, and promise of value that customers associate with a venture, not merely an artistic logo or name.
2. C - Market positioning is the strategic effort to create a distinct place in the mind of the target customer relative to competing options.
3. B - Advertising is only one tactical communication tool within the broader marketing discipline, which encompasses research, product development, pricing, distribution, and promotion.
4. B - Offering a free demonstration to qualified decision-makers directly proves value, reduces buyer risk, and builds personal relationships.
5. B - WhatsApp Business provides a dedicated mobile business profile, digital product catalogs, automated messaging, and organizational tags suited to Pakistani micro-enterprises.
6. B - A sole proprietor has unlimited personal liability, meaning personal assets and household savings can be legally seized to satisfy unpaid business debts.
7. C - The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) regulates and registers companies under the Companies Act, 2017.
8. C - A patent protects new, inventive, and industrially applicable technical mechanisms and functional processes.
9. B - Unauthorized copying, distribution, and sale of an original literary or instructional work directly violates the Copyright Ordinance, 1962.
10. B - Promising guaranteed exam outcomes while concealing refund restrictions is both deceptive trade practice under consumer protection principles and an ethical violation of parental trust.

### RRQ model answers and mark schemes

1. **Model answer:** Market positioning is the deliberate process of establishing a distinctive image and value perception for a product or service in the minds of target customers compared to competitors. Two common axes used on positioning maps are price (low versus high) and quality or service level (basic versus premium).
   **Mark scheme (2 marks):**
   - 1 mark: Accurately defines market positioning as customer perception relative to competing alternatives.
   - 1 mark: Identifies two valid positioning criteria or map dimensions (e.g., price, quality, speed, convenience).
2. **Model answer:** Word-of-mouth marketing is especially powerful in Pakistani educational settings because parents and teachers place immense value on personal trust and verified recommendations from peers when children's futures are at stake. A positive endorsement from a respected headteacher or relative eliminates skepticism far more effectively than an anonymous commercial advertisement. Furthermore, word of mouth requires zero cash expenditure, making it ideal for budget-constrained startups.
   **Mark scheme (3 marks):**
   - 1 mark: Notes high reliance on community trust and peer verification in educational decisions.
   - 1 mark: Explains that personal recommendations overcome buyer skepticism more than paid ads.
   - 1 mark: Identifies the financial benefit of zero advertising cost for a new venture.
3. **Model answer:** First, the graduate can conduct a free, interactive 20-minute science demonstration during a parent-teacher meeting or school assembly, allowing parents and pupils to witness the excitement and learning directly. Second, she can post professional notices on school noticeboards and in local stationery shops where parents buy books, inviting families to join a community WhatsApp group for weekly science tips. Both tactics are cost-effective because they require only minimal paper printing and direct engagement with motivated audiences.
   **Mark scheme (3 marks):**
   - 1 mark: Proposes two distinct bootstrap tactics (e.g., live demonstration, targeted notice, referral incentive).
   - 1 mark: Explains why the first tactic is cost-effective and reaches target buyers.
   - 1 mark: Explains why the second tactic minimizes waste compared to broad advertising.
4. **Model answer:** Paid advertising involves buying promotional space (such as a sponsored post or banner) to push a direct sales message to an audience. Organic content marketing involves creating and sharing valuable, educational, or informative materials (such as short teaching guides or lesson planning tips) that attract prospective clients naturally by demonstrating expertise. For a teacher-training venture, content marketing could be sharing a free weekly downloadable lesson plan template that teachers find genuinely helpful.
   **Mark scheme (3 marks):**
   - 1 mark: Explains paid advertising as purchased promotional space.
   - 1 mark: Explains content marketing as creating valuable, helpful content to attract and build trust with customers.
   - 1 mark: Supplies an appropriate educational example (e.g., free lesson plan, teaching tip video).
5. **Model answer:** First, liability differs fundamentally: partners in a general partnership have joint and several unlimited liability for all business debts, whereas shareholders in a private limited company enjoy limited liability restricted to their invested capital. Second, legal personhood differs: a partnership is not legally separate from its partners, while a private limited company is an independent legal entity registered under the Companies Act, 2017 with perpetual succession.
   **Mark scheme (2 marks):**
   - 1 mark: Clearly distinguishes unlimited joint liability in partnerships from limited liability in companies.
   - 1 mark: Identifies legal personality differences (separate corporate person vs. unincorporated association) or registration under Companies Act 2017.
6. **Model answer:** (1) Online name availability search and name reservation via the SECP eZfile system; (2) Drafting the company's Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association; (3) Online submission of subscriber CNIC details, office address, and statutory forms; (4) Payment of official government filing and registration fees; (5) Examination by the Company Registration Office (CRO) and issuance of the Digital Certificate of Incorporation.
   **Mark scheme (3 marks):**
   - 1 mark: Identifies name reservation as the initial step.
   - 1 mark: Names drafting constitutive documents (Memorandum and Articles) and digital submission.
   - 1 mark: Names statutory fee payment, examination, and issuance of the Certificate of Incorporation.
7. **Model answer:** Intellectual property law in Pakistan explicitly does not protect bare ideas, abstract concepts, or teaching methodologies that exist only in someone's mind. Protection requires that an original creative expression be fixed in a tangible medium (such as a written book or recorded lesson under copyright) or that a novel technical mechanism be formally registered with IPO-Pakistan (such as a patent). A teaching concept using dried beans is an abstract pedagogical idea, not a patentable technical invention.
   **Mark scheme (2 marks):**
   - 1 mark: Explains that bare ideas or abstract thoughts carry no legal IP protection until fixed or registered.
   - 1 mark: Connects the principle to the specific example, noting that general teaching methods are not patentable inventions.
8. **Model answer:** A trademark protects distinctive symbols, words, logos, or slogans that distinguish the commercial identity of a business from its competitors (governed by the Trade Marks Ordinance, 2001). An industrial design protects the visual, aesthetic shape, configuration, or surface ornamentation of an industrial manufactured product (governed by the Registered Designs Ordinance, 2000). For example, the unique brand name and logo "Sindh Scholar Supplies" printed on packaging is a trademark, whereas the specific ergonomic curved shape of a moulded plastic student ruler is an industrial design.
   **Mark scheme (3 marks):**
   - 1 mark: Defines trademark as distinguishing brand symbols/names.
   - 1 mark: Defines industrial design as aesthetic shape/pattern of a manufactured article.
   - 1 mark: Provides clear, accurate examples of both from a classroom supplies context.
9. **Model answer:** Hiding the defect violates the ethical principle of customer transparency and truthfulness in transactions, as the founder conceals a known material defect to secure commercial gain. Second, it violates professional duty of care toward students and teachers who will depend on the software for fair assessment. While concealing the defect may yield short-term revenue, it risks catastrophic reputational collapse and breach-of-contract liability when schools discover the flaw.
   **Mark scheme (3 marks):**
   - 1 mark: Analyzes the breach of transparency, honesty, and truth in commercial transactions.
   - 1 mark: Analyzes the violation of duty of care or educational harm to pupils and school clients.
   - 1 mark: Evaluates the long-term reputational and contractual consequences for the enterprise.
10. **Model answer:** If a sole proprietorship accumulates 500,000 rupees of debt and fails, the sole proprietor is personally responsible for the entire amount. Creditors can legally obtain court orders to seize the owner's personal savings, household furniture, vehicle, or family property until the debt is satisfied. In contrast, if a private limited company with 500,000 rupees of debt enters liquidation, a shareholder is legally liable only up to the unpaid balance of their subscribed share capital. Once their agreed share investment is paid, their personal household property and family savings cannot be touched by corporate creditors.
    **Mark scheme (3 marks):**
    - 1 mark: Explains that the sole proprietor suffers unlimited liability, exposing personal and household assets.
    - 1 mark: Explains that the company shareholder has limited liability, shielding personal property from corporate debts.
    - 1 mark: Explicitly applies the contrast to the 500,000 rupee debt scenario.

### ERQ rubrics

- **Market positioning framework (0-3 marks):** Identifies relevant competitive dimensions (e.g., Price vs. Active Engagement); accurately maps existing competitors and locates the new STEM lab in an underserved niche.
- **Brand promise and value proposition (0-3 marks):** Formulates a distinct, compelling, and realistic brand identity centered on practical inquiry and curriculum alignment.

- **Clarity and coherence (0-1 mark):** Logical structure, professional tone, and clear paragraph progression.

#### ERQ 2: Choosing the right legal structure
- **Legal structure comparison (0-3 marks):** Accurately details the legal characteristics of general partnerships under the Partnership Act, 1932 versus private limited companies under the Companies Act, 2017.
- **Liability and risk analysis (0-3 marks, Analyze):** Analyzes the immense danger of joint and several liability with vehicle financing and employees; contrasts this with corporate limited liability.
- **Institutional contracting and grant eligibility (0-2 marks):** Explains why multinational CSR donors and banks require formal SECP incorporation and corporate bank accounts.
- **Final justified recommendation (0-2 marks):** Delivers a definitive, well-reasoned recommendation balancing setup costs against long-term liability protection.

#### ERQ 3: Intellectual property audit and protection plan
- **Accurate IP categorization (0-3 marks):** Correctly classifies the storybooks under Copyright, the brand name and slogan under Trademark, and the reading stand mechanism under Patent or Industrial Design.
- **Analysis of unprotectable elements (0-3 marks, Analyze):** Explains clearly why the underlying pedagogical technique of ordering vowels cannot be protected under IP law, distinguishing abstract ideas from tangible expressions.
- **Actionable registration steps (0-3 marks):** Outlines clear procedures for filing applications with IPO-Pakistan (trademark search, copyright registration, patent examination).
- **Structure and terminology (0-1 mark):** Uses correct statutory terms (IPO-Pakistan, Copyright Ordinance, Trade Marks Ordinance).

#### ERQ 4: Evaluating ethics and legality in educational advertising
- **Legal and ethical evaluation (0-3 marks, Evaluate):** Critically evaluates the advertisement, demonstrating that claiming leaked exam papers is deceptive, may be unlawful, and grossly exploits parental panic.
- **Systemic harm analysis (0-2 marks, Analyze):** Analyzes the destructive impact of deceptive advertising on pupil moral development, parental finances, and broader societal trust in educational institutions.
- **Rewritten marketing message (0-2 marks):** Composes an alternative promotional message that is engaging, professional, legally compliant, and rooted in authentic educational value and curriculum mastery.
- **Promotion channel choice (0-2 marks, Apply):** Selects a suitable low-cost channel and justifies it on parental trust, cost and reach.
- **Tone and presentation (0-1 mark):** Articulate, ethical, and professionally expressed argument.

#### ERQ 5: Integrative venture launch plan
- **Brand positioning and bootstrap promotion (0-3 marks):** Establishes an inspiring, clear positioning statement for eco-friendly school furniture; selects high-impact, low-cost promotion channels to reach headteachers.
- **Legal structure and SECP incorporation (0-3 marks):** Justifies the chosen legal structure (Pvt. Ltd. recommended for manufacturing liability and contracts) and outlines the SECP eZfile registration steps.
- **Intellectual property strategy (0-2 marks):** Details trademark protection for the venture name and industrial design protection for the unique furniture configuration under IPO-Pakistan.
- **Ethical operating code (0-2 marks, Analyze):** Formulates three rigorous operating policies covering factory worker safety, environmental truthfulness, and non-exploitative pricing for community schools.
