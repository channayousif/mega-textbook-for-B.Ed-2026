# Marketing, Legal and Ethical Issues

<Figure id="fig-U6-5" kind="illustration" src="/img/figures/gent-401/unit-06/fig-U6-5.webp" alt="In a Hyderabad community room, two young entrepreneurs present science kits to interested school teachers beside a whiteboard displaying a market positioning grid." width={1200} height={675} />

"Miss, if my cousin and I start selling our science experiment kits to private schools in Hyderabad, can we just use a cool English name and start selling immediately, or do we have to register with the government first?" A B.Ed student in Sukkur asked this question during a curriculum seminar. Another student answered: "Just sell through WhatsApp and do not worry about paperwork - registration is only for giant factories in Karachi."

Is that advice safe? What happens when a competitor copies your workbook word for word, or when a tax auditor asks for your National Tax Number? What protects you if a partner walks away with company money, or if a customer claims your science kit caused an injury? This unit examines the practical tools an entrepreneur needs to build a recognized market presence and protect a venture under Pakistani law. You will explore how to position a product, communicate its value without spending large sums of money, select the right legal form of enterprise, register with official bodies such as the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) and the Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan (IPO-Pakistan), and uphold uncompromising ethical standards in advertising and operations.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Define branding and market positioning, and construct a two-axis positioning map for local ventures. *(Understand; SLO:GENT-401-6-1)*
- Compare traditional and bootstrap promotion strategies suitable for early-stage community ventures. *(Apply; SLO:GENT-401-6-1)*
- Apply digital marketing basics to reach and engage customers ethically using common digital channels. *(Apply; SLO:GENT-401-6-1)*
- Compare sole proprietorship, partnership, and private limited company structures in Pakistan, and outline SECP registration steps. *(Understand; SLO:GENT-401-6-2)*
- Distinguish the four major forms of intellectual property protection administered by IPO-Pakistan. *(Understand; SLO:GENT-401-6-2)*
- Evaluate whether a described marketing claim or business action is ethical and lawful, and propose honest alternatives. *(Evaluate; SLO:GENT-401-6-2)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

This unit builds on the foundations established in Units 3 and 4. You should be familiar with conducting basic market research, identifying customer problems, defining target customer segments, articulating a unique value proposition, and structuring a preliminary business plan. No prior legal background is assumed; the unit presents regulatory procedures in clear, step-by-step language.

## In this unit

1. [Marketing a new venture](./topic-01) - branding, market positioning, promotion strategies, and digital marketing basics for early-stage ventures.
2. [Registering, protecting and acting ethically](./topic-02) - legal forms of business in Pakistan, SECP incorporation, intellectual property through IPO-Pakistan, and entrepreneurial ethics.

## How to use this unit

Read the two topics in sequential order. Each topic opens with a concrete classroom situation from Sindh, explores core principles through clear explanations and worked examples, and provides a collaborative group activity.

Answer the retrieval questions in "Check your understanding" from memory before reviewing the text. Use the self-assessment checklist to verify your grasp of key skills, and complete the practicum transfer activity during your school placement.

After completing both topics, work through the [unit assessment](./unit-assessment). It contains 10 multiple-choice questions, 10 restricted-response questions, and 5 extended-response tasks designed to consolidate your learning across both topics. Detailed marking criteria and model answers are located in the final bounded section of that page. Instructors will find teaching sequence suggestions and a hands-on regulatory simulation lab in the [notes for teachers](./unit-teacher-notes).
