# Entrepreneurship - Course Overview

This Semester 4 course is about how people turn ideas into working ventures. You will study the entrepreneurial mindset, how to spot and judge business opportunities, how to research a market, and how to plan, fund, market and grow a new venture. As a future teacher, you will also see how entrepreneurial thinking can help schools and pupils solve real problems in their communities.

## Course outcomes

The University of Sindh B.Ed course guide gives eight outcomes. After this course you should be able to:

1. Understand the concept and importance of entrepreneurship in economic growth.
2. Identify and evaluate business opportunities.
3. Develop creative and innovative business ideas.
4. Prepare a comprehensive business plan.
5. Analyze market trends and customer needs.
6. Apply basic financial and marketing strategies.
7. Demonstrate entrepreneurial skills such as leadership, risk-taking and decision-making.
8. Understand ethical and legal issues in starting a business.

## Begin with Unit 1

[Entrepreneurship and the Entrepreneurial Mindset](./unit-01/) covers the first two teaching weeks. It has two topics, a unit assessment and notes for teachers. Other units are added through separate authoring jobs.

## Practical work and assessment

The guide suggests lectures, discussions, demonstrations and case-study analysis, with practical work in the form of computer exercises, individual and group assignments, internet research tasks and presentations. Across the course, you will build one business plan step by step and present it at the end, as the guide's final week asks.

The guide names a mid-term examination and a final project and examination, but gives no marks for them, so the programme default applies: 40% formative and 60% summative. Your teacher will explain which work is collected.

## Readings

Each topic lists its own readings. The guide recommends three books: *Entrepreneurship for everyone* by Robert Mellor and colleagues, *The entrepreneur's guide to building a successful business* by James Scott, and *An introduction to entrepreneurship* by Eamonn Butler. The course also uses the free OpenStax textbook *Entrepreneurship*, which you can read online. Read both topics of a unit before you attempt its assessment.
