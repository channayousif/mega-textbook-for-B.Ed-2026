# Entrepreneurship and the Entrepreneurial Mindset

"Miss, the man who sells pens outside our gate and the lady who makes our worksheets both run a business. Are they both entrepreneurs?" A Class 9 pupil in Sukkur asked this, and the answer is not as simple as it looks. This unit explains what entrepreneurship is, why a country needs it, and how entrepreneurs think. Every later unit in this course, from finding opportunities to writing a business plan and pitching it, builds on the ideas here.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Define entrepreneurship and explain how an entrepreneur differs from a small business owner. *(Understand; SLO:GENT-401-1-1)*
- Explain five ways entrepreneurship supports economic development, with Pakistani examples. *(Understand; SLO:GENT-401-1-1)*
- Describe the characteristics of successful entrepreneurs and find evidence of them in a case. *(Apply; SLO:GENT-401-1-1)*
- Distinguish creativity, innovation and invention, and apply the steps from a problem to an innovation. *(Apply; SLO:GENT-401-1-2)*
- Explain calculated risk-taking and problem-solving, and carry out a simple risk check. *(Apply; SLO:GENT-401-1-2)*
- Compare how an entrepreneur and a manager would respond to the same problem. *(Analyze; SLO:GENT-401-1-2)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

No earlier business course is needed; general schooling is enough. Bring what you know about shops, workshops, tuition centres and other small businesses in your own town or village. You will use these familiar examples throughout the unit.

## In this unit

1. [What entrepreneurship is and why it matters](./topic-01) - the meaning of entrepreneurship, its importance in economic development, and the characteristics of successful entrepreneurs.
2. [Thinking like an entrepreneur](./topic-02) - creativity and innovation, risk-taking and problem-solving, and entrepreneurial versus managerial thinking.

## How to use this unit

Read the two topics in order. Each one begins with a real situation from a school or town in Sindh, explains the ideas, and shows a worked example before it asks you to use them.

Do the group activity in each topic with classmates. Answer the "Check your understanding" questions from memory before you look back. Use the self-assessment checklist honestly, and take the practicum task into a real school.

When you finish both topics, attempt the [unit assessment](./unit-assessment). It has 10 multiple-choice, 10 short-answer and 5 extended questions. The answers and marking guidance are at the end of that page, so try every question first. Teachers will find planning help and a practical activity in the [notes for teachers](./unit-teacher-notes).
