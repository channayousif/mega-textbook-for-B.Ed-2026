# Introduction to Civics and Citizenship

"Sir, why do we study civics? My father says only politicians need it." A Class 9 pupil in Larkana asked this, and many adults think the same. This unit shows why the question has a better answer. Civics is about how a society is run, and citizenship is about the part each of us plays in it. Every later unit in this course, from the state and government to digital life and social justice, builds on the ideas here.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Define civics in your own words and explain how it differs from politics. *(Understand; SLO:GCCE-400-1-1)*
- Describe the main steps in the growth of civic participation, from early assemblies to universal adult franchise in Pakistan. *(Understand; SLO:GCCE-400-1-1)*
- Identify the main forms of civic engagement and give a Pakistani example of each. *(Apply; SLO:GCCE-400-1-1)*
- Explain citizenship as a legal status, a set of rights and duties, and a way of taking part. *(Understand; SLO:GCCE-400-1-2)*
- Explain how the role of citizens has changed, and how civics and citizenship are related. *(Understand; SLO:GCCE-400-1-2)*
- Classify described actions as active, participatory or digital citizenship, and justify each choice. *(Analyze; SLO:GCCE-400-1-3)*
- Explain why citizenship is a foundation of a modern society. *(Understand; SLO:GCCE-400-1-3)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

No earlier civics course is needed. GSOS-301 Social Science and GPKS-402 Pakistan Studies in Semester 2 give useful background on society, the state and Pakistan's political history. Bring what you know about elections, your union council and the Constitution of Pakistan, and be ready to look again at ideas you may have memorised for examinations.

## In this unit

1. [What civics is and how civic participation grew](./topic-01) - the meaning of civics, the history of civic participation, and the forms of civic engagement.
2. [Citizenship and the changing role of citizens](./topic-02) - the meaning of citizenship, how the citizen's role has changed, and how civics and citizenship are related.
3. [Kinds of citizenship in a modern society](./topic-03) - active, participatory and digital citizenship, and citizenship as a foundation of modern society.

## How to use this unit

Read the three topics in order. Each one begins with a real situation from a school or community in Sindh, explains the ideas, and shows a worked example before it asks you to use them.

Do the group activity in each topic with classmates. Answer the "Check your understanding" questions from memory before you look back. Use the self-assessment checklist honestly, and take the practicum task into a real school.

When you finish all three topics, attempt the [unit assessment](./unit-assessment). It has 10 multiple-choice, 10 short-answer and 5 extended questions. The answers and marking guidance are at the end of that page, so try every question first. Teachers will find planning help and a practical activity in the [notes for teachers](./unit-teacher-notes).
