# Fehm-e-Quran - I - Course Overview

This Semester 3 course helps you move from reading the Holy Qur'an to understanding it. You will study the Arabic of the Qur'an: its letters, its words and how they are built, its grammar and its sentences. You will then read, translate and reflect on short passages, and see how their setting helps explain them. It is a practical course, so most of your time goes into exercises with the text itself.

## Course outcomes

The University of Sindh B.Ed course guide gives four outcomes. After this course you should be able to:

1. Understand the basics of Quranic Arabic.
2. Learn Quranic vocabulary and grammar.
3. Develop skills in reading and understanding Quranic text.
4. Interpret Quranic verses and surahs.

## Begin with Unit 1

[Introduction to Quranic Arabic](./unit-01/) covers the first two teaching weeks. It has two topics and a unit assessment. Other units are added through separate authoring jobs.

## Assessment and readings

The course guide names a final assessment or presentation but gives no weights, so the programme default applies: 40% formative and 60% summative. Your teacher will explain which work is collected. Unit 1 includes retrieval questions, group activities and extended tasks with self-study guidance.

Each topic lists its readings. The guide recommends the *Muallim-ul-Quran* course books and Urdu translations and commentaries. Unit 1 works mainly with two free online tools: Quran.com, for the Arabic text with English and Urdu translations, and the Quranic Arabic Corpus, which shows the root and grammar of every word. Keep both open while you study.
