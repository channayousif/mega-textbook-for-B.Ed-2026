# Quranic Vocabulary and Grammar

<Figure id="fig-U2-9" kind="illustration" src="/img/figures/guhq-301/unit-02/fig-U2-9.webp" alt="A trainee teacher in a Sindh middle school sorts blank word cards into three columns on the board while pupils in small groups sort their own cards into three piles and the cooperating teacher watches from the doorway." width={1376} height={768} />

In Unit 1 you learned to find the root and pattern of a Qur'anic word. That skill gave you a key to word families. This unit puts words into sentences. It teaches the vocabulary a reader meets most often, the three kinds of word Arabic uses, how nouns change for gender and number, how verbs mark tense and agreement, and how small particles and prepositions tie a sentence together. By the end you will be able to take a short verse, label every word and translate it with the help of its grammar.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Recognise and translate twenty high-frequency Qur'anic words, and explain what makes them frequent. *(Remember/Apply; SLO:GUHQ-301-2-1)*
- Classify any Qur'anic word as a noun (*ism*), a verb (*fi'l*) or a particle (*harf*), giving the sign that identifies it. *(Apply; SLO:GUHQ-301-2-1)*
- Describe at least three techniques for memorising Qur'anic vocabulary and use one of them. *(Remember/Apply; SLO:GUHQ-301-2-1)*
- Identify the gender and number of a Qur'anic noun and show how pronouns attach to or replace it. *(Apply; SLO:GUHQ-301-2-1)*
- Identify the tense of a Qur'anic verb and check whether it agrees with its subject. *(Apply; SLO:GUHQ-301-2-2)*
- Explain how particles and prepositions change a verb's meaning or connect parts of a sentence. *(Understand; SLO:GUHQ-301-2-2)*
- Translate a short Qur'anic passage word by word, labelling each word's part of speech, gender, number and tense. *(Apply/Analyze; SLO:GUHQ-301-2-2)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

You need the skills from Unit 1: how to find a root and a pattern, how to use the Quranic Arabic Corpus, and a set of frequent expressions. No formal Arabic grammar is assumed.

## In this unit

1. [Core vocabulary and the parts of speech](./topic-01) - high-frequency words, the three parts of speech (noun, verb, particle) and vocabulary memorisation techniques.
2. [Nouns: gender, number and pronouns](./topic-02) - masculine and feminine, singular, dual and plural, and detached and attached pronouns.
3. [Verbs and agreement](./topic-03) - past, present and imperative tenses, subject-verb agreement and common verb patterns.
4. [Particles, negation and emphasis](./topic-04) - prepositions, particles, negation words, emphasis and grammar-based translation practice.

## How to use this unit

Read the four topics in order, because each builds on the one before. Keep [Quran.com](https://quran.com) and the [Quranic Arabic Corpus](https://corpus.quran.com) open as you work. When the text names a verse, look it up and try to find the word yourself before reading on.

Do each group activity with classmates. Answer the "Check your understanding" questions from memory first. Use the self-assessment checklist honestly, and take the practicum task into a real school.

When you finish all four topics, attempt the [unit assessment](./unit-assessment). It has 10 multiple-choice, 10 short-answer and 5 extended questions. The answers and marking guidance are at the end of that page, so try every question first.
