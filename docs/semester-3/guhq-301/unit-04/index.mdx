# Reading and Understanding Quranic Text

By Unit 3 you can read a verse, find the root of its words and tell a nominal sentence from a verbal one. Unit 4 puts those tools to work on the text itself. You will read a verse accurately, translate it word by word, then translate it for meaning, and finally read a short passage or a whole short surah for the theme it develops. The goal is not to replace recitation or tafsir. It is to read the Arabic with understanding, the way a teacher must when a pupil asks, "Miss, what does this verse say?"

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Apply the rules for reading the Qur'anic text accurately, and explain why a small reading error can change a word. *(Apply; SLO:GUHQ-301-4-1)*
- Translate a short verse word by word, giving each word its meaning and its grammar. *(Apply; SLO:GUHQ-301-4-1)*
- Explain the difference between a word-by-word and a meaning-based translation, with an example. *(Understand; SLO:GUHQ-301-4-1)*
- Produce a meaning-based translation of a short verse that reads naturally in English or Urdu. *(Apply; SLO:GUHQ-301-4-2)*
- Identify the key words and phrases of a passage and explain how consecutive verses connect. *(Analyze; SLO:GUHQ-301-4-2)*
- Read a ruku' as one semantic unit and state the theme of a short surah, with the verses that support it. *(Analyze; SLO:GUHQ-301-4-2)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 2 (vocabulary and grammar) and Unit 3 (sentence structure). You should be able to find a word's root and pattern, tell a noun from a verb and a particle, and recognise a nominal and a verbal sentence. You should be able to read the Qur'anic script with its vowel marks. This unit works mostly with short surahs from Juz 30, which many learners already know by heart.

## In this unit

1. [Reading accurately and translating word by word](./topic-01) - the rules for accurate reading, the steps of word-by-word translation, and understanding short verses.
2. [Translating for meaning](./topic-02) - meaning-based translation, key words and phrases, and how verses connect.
3. [Passages and themes](./topic-03) - the ruku' as a semantic unit, the theme of a passage, and practice with selected short surahs.

## How to use this unit

Read the three topics in order. Each begins with a real school situation, explains the ideas and shows a worked example before asking you to try. Keep [Quran.com](https://quran.com) open for the Arabic text and the Saheeh International translation, and the [Quranic Arabic Corpus](https://corpus.quran.com) for word-by-word lookup. Look up every verse the unit cites, in Arabic and in an Urdu translation you trust.

Do the group activity with classmates. Answer the "Check your understanding" questions from memory before you look back. Use the self-assessment checklist honestly, and take the practicum task into a real school.

When you finish all three topics, attempt the [unit assessment](./unit-assessment). It has 10 multiple-choice, 10 short-answer and 5 extended questions. The answers and marking guidance are at the end of that page, so try every question first.
