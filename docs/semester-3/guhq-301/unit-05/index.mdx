# Interpretation of Quranic Verses and Surahs

By Unit 4 you could read a verse accurately, translate it word by word, and then for meaning, and you could find the theme of a short passage. Unit 5 takes the next step: interpreting the text. You will learn what tafsir is, where it draws its authority from, and which principles keep it honest. Then you will use those principles on selected verses and short surahs, and draw a moral and practical lesson that really belongs to the text. The goal is not to become a scholar in one unit. It is to read the Qur'an with understanding, the way a teacher must when a pupil asks, "Miss, what is this verse for?"

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Define tafsir and name its sources and the tools it uses. *(Remember; SLO:GUHQ-301-5-1)*
- State the principle that the ruling is drawn from the generality of the wording, not the particularity of the occasion, and explain it with an example. *(Understand; SLO:GUHQ-301-5-1)*
- Explain how context and language shape the meaning of a verse. *(Understand; SLO:GUHQ-301-5-1)*
- Interpret a selected verse or short surah by moving from reading and translation to context and tafsir. *(Apply; SLO:GUHQ-301-5-2)*
- Identify the main theme and message of a short surah from its key words and repetitions. *(Analyze; SLO:GUHQ-301-5-2)*
- Draw a moral and practical lesson from a passage and trace it to the wording. *(Evaluate; SLO:GUHQ-301-5-2)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 4. You should be able to read the Qur'anic script with its vowel marks, translate a short verse word by word and for meaning, and find the key words of a passage. You should know what a ruku' is and how a short surah holds one theme. This unit works with short surahs from Juz 30, which many learners already know by heart, and with a few longer verses that show how interpretation works.

## In this unit

1. [Principles of tafsir](./topic-01) - what tafsir is, the sources and principles of interpretation, and the role of context and language.
2. [Interpreting selected verses and short surahs](./topic-02) - the five steps from verse to lesson, finding the main theme, and drawing a moral and practical lesson.

## How to use this unit

Read the two topics in order. Each begins with a real school situation, explains the ideas and shows a worked example before asking you to try. Keep [Quran.com](https://quran.com) open for the Arabic text and the Saheeh International translation, and the [Quranic Arabic Corpus](https://corpus.quran.com) for word-by-word lookup. Look up every verse the unit cites, in Arabic and in an Urdu translation you trust.

Do the group activity with classmates. Answer the "Check your understanding" questions from memory before you look back. Use the self-assessment checklist honestly, and take the practicum task into a real school.

When you finish both topics, attempt the [unit assessment](./unit-assessment). It has 10 multiple-choice, 10 short-answer and 5 extended questions. The answers and marking guidance are at the end of that page, so try every question first.
