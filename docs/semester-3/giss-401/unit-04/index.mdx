# The Social System and the State in Islam

How should people care for one another, and how should shared decisions be made? This unit connects family life and social responsibility with the principles used to judge public authority. Its examples concern schools in Sindh. These are learning cases, not rulings on personal disputes or descriptions of particular families.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of the unit, you should be able to:

- Explain the family system, rights and responsibilities, and social justice and equality in Islam. Use a school case to distinguish equal dignity from identical provision. These outcomes serve `SLO:GISS-401-4-1`.
- Explain the state as a public responsibility, including Allah's sovereignty, khilafat, consultation and justice. Distinguish a guiding principle from a way of putting it into practice. This serves `SLO:GISS-401-4-2`.
- Evaluate one argument about Islam and democracy. Define the terms, use relevant sources, consider a counterargument and give a bounded judgement. This also serves `SLO:GISS-401-4-2`.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Complete Unit 3 on worship and moral conduct. Recall the meaning of justice, honesty and responsibility. Bring the source-reading habit from Unit 2: identify the passage, distinguish its wording from an interpretation, and check what a claim actually requires.

## How to use this unit

Read each worked example before attempting its group activity. Attempt the three retrieval questions without looking back, then use the explanation to improve your response. Keep a record of the source and reason behind each judgement rather than memorising a slogan.

Use invented cases during discussion. Nobody needs to disclose household income, a family disagreement or a political preference. At practicum, examine a routine or a public notice, rather than collecting personal information about children.

After both topics, attempt the [unit assessment](./unit-assessment). It includes ten multiple-choice, ten short-response and five extended-response questions. Attempt them before consulting the final self-study guidance. The default course weighting is 60% summative and 40% formative; your institution determines how it uses this practice bank.
