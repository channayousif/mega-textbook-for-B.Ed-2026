# Islamic Studies - Course Overview

This course gives student teachers a comprehensive understanding of Islamic beliefs, teachings, moral values, and social systems. It emphasizes the application of Islamic principles in personal, social, and professional life, particularly in the field of education. The course also highlights the role of Islam in character building, social harmony, and national development in Pakistan.

## Learning outcomes

The course guide states that on completion of this course, prospective teachers will be able to:

1. Understand the basic beliefs and teachings of Islam.
2. Explain the sources of Islamic law and guidance.
3. Apply Islamic ethical values in daily and professional life.
4. Understand the Islamic social system and its relevance today.
5. Promote tolerance, peace, and moral development through education.

## Units

The course content is organized across seven units corresponding to the 16-week scheme:

| Unit | Title | Weeks | Sub-topics |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to Islamic Studies and the Fundamental Beliefs of Islam | Weeks 1-2 | meaning and scope of Islamic Studies; importance of Islamic education; Islam as a complete code of life; concept of Tauheed; Risalat and Akhirat; articles of faith |
| 2 | Sources of Islamic Knowledge: the Qur'an and the Sunnah | Weeks 3-5 | the Holy Qur'an as a source; Hadith and Sunnah as a source; Ijma and Qiyas; introduction to the Qur'an; Qur'an as guidance; selected verses; importance, classification and role of Hadith |
| 3 | Worship and the Moral System of Islam | Weeks 6-7 | Salat, Zakat, Saum and Hajj; objectives and social significance of Ibadat; moral values in Islam; truthfulness, justice, honesty, patience; character building |
| 4 | The Social System and the State in Islam | Weeks 8-9 | family system in Islam; rights and responsibilities; social justice and equality; state system in Islam; concept of democracy in Islam |
| 5 | The Economic and Political Systems of Islam | Weeks 10-11 | concept of halal and haram; zakat and charity; prohibition of riba; concept of Shura; justice and governance; rights of minorities |
| 6 | Islam, Education, Civilization and Contemporary Issues | Weeks 12-14 | education in Islam; roles of teachers and learners; knowledge and wisdom; Muslim contributions; Islamic civilization; science and education; human rights, peace, tolerance and modern challenges |
| 7 | Islamic Ethics in Professional Life | Week 15 | ethical responsibilities of teachers; Islamic values in educational leadership; role modelling in education (Week 16: review and assessment) |

Units are published as they are authored.

## Assessment

Assessment follows the standard default split: **60% summative / 40% formative** (Constitution Art. III.7). Each unit carries an end-of-unit 10/10/5 assessment bank (10 MCQs, 10 RRQs, 5 ERQs) and formative checks embedded in every topic cycle.

## Teaching approach

Teaching integrates interactive lectures, reflective inquiry, classroom case studies, collaborative group tasks, and practical school-based observation.
