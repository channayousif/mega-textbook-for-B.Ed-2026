# ESD in Pakistan - teacher notes

## Teaching strategies and sequencing

The course guide names lectures, discussion, question-answer sessions, brainstorming, group work, assignments and presentations. Use a short lecture to establish the distinction between policy intention and implementation. Follow it with the Topic 7.1 evidence-ladder activity. Keep the policy extract visible so groups can distinguish its wording from their interpretation. Then use Topic 7.2 for a rehearsal and a brief group presentation of revisions.

A useful discussion prompt is: "What would we need to see before saying this lesson develops informed sustainability choices?" A likely response is, "The poster proves pupils were taught sustainability." Ask learners to distinguish the existence of a resource from how pupils used it. Invite a second response: "The leaking tap proves the teacher failed." Ask what the observation establishes about responsibility for repairs. Neither unsupported claim should become the class conclusion.

## Practical work

Use groups of three where possible: trainee teacher, pupil role and observer. Provide a fictional water or waste scenario, a short verified policy extract and a mentor-selected outcome. The observer records questions and the evidence of reasoning they invite. Rotate roles and ask the group to revise one question before presenting its change. Provide oral or written routes for explaining choices. Do not require learners to reveal their household spending or family practices.

Placement work requires mentor permission. It may be replaced by peer rehearsal if access is unavailable. Rehearsal evidence describes performance with peers, not proven effects on children. Collect no pupil names or identifiable images, and use drawings rather than unsafe handling of waste, water points or tools.

## Evidence and source cautions

Use the 2009 policy as a historical text. Check applicable local curriculum materials before asserting a current requirement. The course guide's reference to HEC Education Policy 2025 does not itself supply an inspectable policy text or establish its scope. Do not invent clauses from that reference.

Jumani and Abbasi (2015) use proposed-study language in their abstract. Do not report it as a completed national implementation survey. The guide's National SDG Framework locator is unresolved in the approved content-spec. Revisit the framework concept from Unit 3 without assigning unverified quotations, dates or targets to that inaccessible document. UNESCO's teacher education recommendations inform a design discussion; a local initiative still needs its own evidence.

## Feedback focus

Comment on the relationship between each claim and its evidence. Reward a precise limited judgement over a sweeping verdict. Ask for a specific actor, feasible next step and follow-up check. Keep independent English review, Urdu translation and figure production as separate pending work; this authoring exercise does not certify them.
