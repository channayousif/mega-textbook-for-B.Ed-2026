## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Compare project-based and inquiry-based learning and choose a guided approach for a local sustainability issue. (SLO:EFMP-407-4-1)
- Explain causal relationships and compare alternative futures without treating a prediction as certain. (SLO:EFMP-407-4-1)
- Create a 35-minute Grade 6 lesson whose learning outcome, activities and assessment fit together. (SLO:EFMP-407-4-2)

## Prerequisite knowledge

Recall Unit 2's systems thinking and transformative learning. You should be able to connect environmental, social and economic effects and explain how evidence can change an assumption. If those ideas are unfamiliar, discuss a shared school resource with a partner before starting.

## In this unit

1. [Learning through projects and inquiry](./topic-01): guide pupils from a question to evidence and purposeful action.
2. [Thinking in systems and imagining futures](./topic-02): explain relationships and compare choices under uncertainty.
3. [Planning a sustainability lesson](./topic-03): join these approaches in an achievable, inclusive lesson.

## How to use this unit

Read each worked example before trying its collaborative activity. Attempt the three retrieval questions with the page closed, then revisit the explanation. Use the checklist to identify what you still need to practise. Finish with the [unit assessment](./unit-assessment), using its final guidance only after attempting the questions. Activities take additional time beyond the reading estimate. Classroom cases and numerical data are invented for practice, not reports about named schools.
