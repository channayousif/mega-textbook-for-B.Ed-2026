# Economic Dimensions

Every day, people in your district earn money, spend it and throw things away. Companies decide how to treat their workers and their waste. This unit asks what sustainability means for those ordinary economic decisions, and how a teacher can help pupils test the claims they hear.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Explain what a green economy is, and test a Pakistani example against its two conditions. *(Understand and Apply; SLO:EFMP-407-6-1)*
- Explain sustainable consumption, and work out what a more sustainable household choice costs over time. *(Apply; SLO:EFMP-407-6-1)*
- Distinguish corporate social responsibility from charity and from greenwashing. *(Analyze; SLO:EFMP-407-6-2)*
- Evaluate a company's CSR claim, using evidence you can check. *(Evaluate; SLO:EFMP-407-6-2)*
- Use a short set of questions and the labels on a product to make and defend a buying decision. *(Apply; SLO:EFMP-407-6-2)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

You should already know the economic dimension of sustainability from Unit 1, including the idea that a cost is wider than the first price. You should also recall what a trade-off is. Before you begin, find the last electricity bill at your home or hostel, and note the number of units used and the amount charged. You will use these numbers in Topic 6.1.

## How to use this unit

Read the three topics in order. Each one starts with an illustrative classroom situation, explains the ideas, and shows a worked example before asking you to try the same thing. Do the group activity with classmates. Answer the "Check your understanding" questions without looking back, then check your answers against the explanation.

The [unit assessment](./unit-assessment) has practice questions and, at the end, a public self-study guidance section. Try the questions before you read that guidance. In formal course assessment the default weighting is 40% formative and 60% summative; your teacher will tell you which work is collected. Teachers will find planning help in the [teacher notes](./unit-teacher-notes).
