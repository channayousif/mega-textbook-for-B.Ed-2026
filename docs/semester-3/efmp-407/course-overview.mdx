# Education for Sustainable Development - Course Overview

This course introduces the principles, practices and teaching methods of Education for Sustainable Development (ESD), in line with the UN Sustainable Development Goals and HEC Education Policy 2025. It brings the environmental, social and economic sides of sustainability into teaching and learning. It encourages teaching that changes how learners think and act, that involves them, and that connects with their community.

## Learning outcomes

The course guide states these objectives. By the end of the course you will be able to:

1. Define sustainable development and its dimensions (environmental, economic and social).
2. Explain the philosophy and global frameworks of ESD.
3. Describe the role of education in achieving the SDGs (particularly SDG 4.7).
4. Apply pedagogical strategies that integrate sustainability concepts.
5. Demonstrate values of environmental stewardship and responsible citizenship.
6. Evaluate ESD-related policies and implementation in Pakistan.

The guide also names three learning outcomes: integrate sustainability concepts into lesson planning and classroom practice; analyze environmental and social challenges using systems and critical thinking; and design and implement community-based sustainability projects.

## Units

| Weeks | Unit | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | 1. Concept and Evolution of Sustainable Development | definitions and evolution; major global conferences; dimensions |
| 3-4 | 2. Principles and Frameworks of ESD | UNESCO framework; ESD and environmental education; transformative learning and systems thinking |
| 5-6 | 3. SDGs and Education | overview of the SDGs and SDG 4.7; Pakistan's National SDG Framework; education for global citizenship |
| 7-8 | 4. Pedagogies for Sustainability | project-based and inquiry-based learning; systems and futures thinking; sustainability lesson plans |
| 9-10 | 5. Cultural and Social Dimensions | peace, gender equality and social justice; human rights and inclusion; indigenous knowledge systems |
| 11-12 | 6. Economic Dimensions | green economy and sustainable consumption; corporate social responsibility; ethical consumerism |
| 13-14 | 7. ESD in Pakistan | national education policy and ESD integration; curriculum reforms and teacher education initiatives |
| 15-16 | 8. School and Community Projects | planning and implementing sustainability projects; community participation and reporting |

Units are published as they are written. Unit 1 is available now.

## Assessment

The course guide gives these course-level marks:

| Component | Marks |
|---|---|
| Mid-term | 30 |
| Final term | 50 |
| Assignment or presentation | 10 |
| Attendance | 10 |

The guide does not label these components formative or summative. Each unit's own practice questions follow the default split of 40% formative and 60% summative. Your teacher will explain which work is collected.

## Teaching approach and practical work

The guide names lectures, class discussions, question-answer sessions and brainstorming. Practical work includes group work, group assignments, individual assignments and presentations.

## Reading

Each unit lists its sources in its "Further reading" sections. Sources are cited by reference only and are never reproduced.
