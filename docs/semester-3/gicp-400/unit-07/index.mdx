# Education for Ideology and the Constitution

"Why do we study Pakistan Studies? It is only about elections." "The country is held together by the army, not by schools." Pupils say things like this, and some teachers agree. This unit takes the other view. Education is one of the main ways a diverse country becomes one. It teaches civic knowledge, civic skills and civic values, and it asks teachers to carry that work in the daily life of the classroom.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Define civic education and explain its three dimensions: civic knowledge, civic skills and civic values. *(Understand; SLO:GICP-400-7-1)*
- Explain what national integration means in a diverse federation, and correct the idea that it requires everyone to be the same. *(Understand; SLO:GICP-400-7-1)*
- Use Articles 33, 36 and 251 to show how the Constitution supports unity in diversity. *(Apply; SLO:GICP-400-7-1)*
- Describe the teacher's responsibility for civic education and constitutional values in the classroom and the school. *(Understand; SLO:GICP-400-7-1)*
- Analyse how a described school practice either builds or weakens national integration. *(Analyze; SLO:GICP-400-7-1)*
- Design a short classroom activity that teaches one constitutional value through experience. *(Create; SLO:GICP-400-7-1)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

You should have studied Units 1 to 6. Unit 1 showed how values become principles and principles become articles. Units 5 and 6 showed the Fundamental Rights, the Principles of Policy and the structure of government. This unit draws those threads together and asks what they mean for a teacher in a school.

## How to use this unit

Read the classroom situation first, then the explanation. Keep a copy of the Constitution nearby and check each article yourself. The topic shows a worked example before it asks you to design your own activity, so follow the example first.

Do the group activity with classmates. Answer the "Check your understanding" questions from memory before you look back. Use the self-assessment checklist honestly, and take the practicum task into a real school.

When you finish the topic, attempt the [unit assessment](./unit-assessment). It has 10 multiple-choice, 10 short-answer and 5 extended questions. The answers and marking guidance are at the end of that page, so try every question first.
