# Ideology and Constitution of Pakistan - Course Overview

This Semester 3 course explores Pakistan's founding ideas, constitutional development, government and citizens' rights. It asks future teachers to connect these ideas with fair treatment and civic responsibility in school life.

## Course objectives

The University of Sindh B.Ed course guide gives five objectives:

1. Understand the ideological basis of Pakistan.
2. Analyze the historical evolution of constitutional development.
3. Explain the salient features of the Constitution of Pakistan (1973).
4. Understand fundamental rights and duties of citizens.
5. Promote constitutional values and national integration through education.

## Begin with Unit 1

[Ideology, Constitution and the Ideological Foundations of Pakistan](./unit-01/) covers the first two teaching weeks. It has two topics and a unit assessment. Other units are added through separate authoring jobs.

## Assessment and readings

The course guide gives no assessment weights, so the programme default applies: 40% formative and 60% summative. Your teacher will explain which work is collected. Unit 1 includes retrieval questions, group activities and extended tasks with self-study guidance.

Each topic lists its readings. The guide recommends the Constitution of Pakistan and works by Hamid Khan, Ishtiaq Husain Qureshi and Muhammad Iqbal. Unit 1 names the constitutional study edition it uses and links the sources behind its claims. Read both topics before attempting the unit assessment.
