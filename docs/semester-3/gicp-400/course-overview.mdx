# Ideology and Constitution of Pakistan - Course Overview

This Semester 3 course follows the University of Sindh guide. It examines Muslim political thought, the Pakistan Movement and Pakistan's constitutional development, then connects constitutional values to education and civic responsibility.

## Course objectives

The guide lists these objectives:

1. Understand the ideological basis of Pakistan.
2. Analyze the historical evolution of constitutional development.
3. Explain the salient features of the Constitution of Pakistan (1973).
4. Understand fundamental rights and duties of citizens.
5. Promote constitutional values and national integration through education.

## Course organisation

The approved content spec groups the guide's weekly topics into seven units: ideological foundations; Muslim political thought and the Pakistan Movement; constitution-making from 1947 to 1962; constitutional crises and the making of the 1973 Constitution; the 1973 Constitution's features, rights and principles of policy; government and amendments; and education for ideology and the constitution. Week 16 is for review and final assessment. The unit grouping is an approved organisation of the guide, which itself gives weeks rather than unit headings.

## Assessment

The guide supplies no separate assessment criteria or weights. The programme default is 60% summative and 40% formative. Topic checks and collaborative activities support learning; unit-end banks provide public self-study practice.

## Recommended reading

The guide names the Constitution of Pakistan, Hamid Khan's *Constitutional and Political History of Pakistan*, a work by Ishtiaq Husain Qureshi printed as *The Pakistan Movement*, and Iqbal's *The Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam*. The approved spec provisionally identifies Qureshi's reading as *The Struggle for Pakistan*. Unit reading lists identify the particular open sources actually used. The guide gives no separate teaching-strategy or practical-work section.
