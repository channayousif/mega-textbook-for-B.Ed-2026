# Four Skills of Language Learning

Language teaching in elementary grades builds upon four interconnected skills: listening, speaking, reading and writing. Listening and reading serve as receptive skills through which pupils receive input and make meaning. Speaking and writing serve as productive skills through which pupils express ideas and communicate with others. This opening unit equips prospective teachers with practical pedagogies, classroom activities and assessment techniques to develop all four skills effectively in Pakistani classrooms.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

1. Explain the nature of listening comprehension and design three-stage listening lessons using audio and video resources. *(Understand, Apply; SLO:EFPG-401-1-1)*
2. Establish a favourable classroom climate for speaking and conduct interactive dialogues and language games. *(Apply, Analyze; SLO:EFPG-401-1-1)*
3. Distinguish between silent and loud reading and apply phonic and whole-word methods to foster reading sub-skills. *(Understand, Apply; SLO:EFPG-401-1-2)*
4. Guide pupils through the stages of process writing, from handwriting and spelling mechanics to paragraphs, letters and applications. *(Apply, Analyze; SLO:EFPG-401-1-2)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

This is the introductory unit of EFPG-401. It requires intermediate-level (HSC) English proficiency and an openness to reflective teaching practice. Prior knowledge of basic child development from EFMP-301 is helpful but not mandatory.

## In this unit

1. [Listening comprehension skills](./topic-01) - techniques for developing active listening habits, utilizing audio-visual media, three-stage lesson architecture and core listening sub-skills.
2. [Speaking skills](./topic-02) - creating supportive communicative climates, pronunciation and intonation fundamentals, conversational dialogues, language games and oral vocabulary.
3. [Reading skills](./topic-03) - purposes of silent and loud reading, foundational methods of reading instruction, and core sub-skills including skimming, scanning and inferencing.
4. [Writing skills](./topic-04) - handwriting mechanics, spelling instruction, process writing for paragraphs, and formal and informal correspondence including letters and applications.

## How to use this unit

Study each topic sequentially. Every topic begins with an authentic Pakistani classroom situation, presents core theoretical and practical explanations, illustrates concepts with worked examples, and provides collaborative activities, retrieval checks and practicum transfer tasks. Complete each self-assessment checklist before proceeding to the next topic. After completing all four topics, attempt the comprehensive unit assessment to consolidate your understanding.
