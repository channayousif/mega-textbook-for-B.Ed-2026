# A.V. Aids in Teaching of English

Audio-visual aids are the pictures, objects, sounds, videos and projections that a teacher brings into a lesson so that English becomes something pupils can see and hear, not only something they read on the board. In many Pakistani classrooms the only aid is the textbook and the chalkboard, so new English words reach pupils as marks on a page with no picture, object or sound attached to them. This unit shows prospective teachers how to choose and use simple aids that hold attention, build meaning, support memory and invite participation, even where money and electricity are scarce.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

1. Explain why audio-visual aids help pupils learn English and name the four benefits they bring to a lesson. *(Understand; SLO:EFPG-401-6-1)*
2. Select and make low-cost aids such as charts, models, pictures, flash cards and real objects, and use them in a Grade 4 or 5 English lesson. *(Apply; SLO:EFPG-401-6-1)*
3. Use radio, audio recordings and a language laboratory to develop listening and clear speech. *(Apply; SLO:EFPG-401-6-2)*
4. Plan a short video lesson with a preview task, a checking stage and a follow-up activity. *(Apply, Analyze; SLO:EFPG-401-6-2)*
5. Compare slides, filmstrip, the overhead projector and the multimedia projector, and choose the right one for the lesson and the resources available. *(Analyze, Evaluate; SLO:EFPG-401-6-2)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

This unit builds on Unit 1 (the four language skills, especially listening) and Unit 2 (teaching prose, poems, composition and vocabulary). You should already know how to plan a simple English lesson and how to set a clear learning outcome. No previous experience with audio-visual equipment is assumed.

## In this unit

1. [Why AV aids matter](./topic-01) - what audio-visual aids are, why they help, and the four benefits they bring to an English lesson.
2. [Charts, pictures, flash cards and real objects](./topic-02) - low-cost aids you can make and use in a crowded classroom with no electricity.
3. [Radio, audio players and the language laboratory](./topic-03) - hearing English from other voices and practising speech in a language laboratory.
4. [Television, video and film](./topic-04) - using a short video clip with a task, a check and a follow-up.
5. [Projected aids](./topic-05) - slides, filmstrip, the overhead projector and the multimedia projector, and how to design a clear slide.

## How to use this unit

Study each topic in order. Every topic opens with a real classroom situation from a Pakistani school, explains the ideas in plain language with a worked example, and then asks you to try a collaborative activity, a retrieval check and a practicum task. When you reach a section on an aid you have not used before, handle the real object or the equipment if you can, so the idea becomes physical for you too. After all five topics, complete the unit assessment to bring the ideas together.
