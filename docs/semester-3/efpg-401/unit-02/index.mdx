# Teaching of English

Teaching English in primary and elementary grades draws on five core classroom tasks: teaching prose, teaching poems, teaching composition, teaching vocabulary and teaching pronunciation. Each task develops a different part of the learner's language. Prose and poems build reading for meaning and response. Composition builds writing for expression. Vocabulary and pronunciation underpin every spoken and written message. This unit prepares prospective teachers to plan and teach all five tasks in Pakistani classrooms, using the skills from Unit 1 as a foundation.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

1. Plan and teach a structured prose lesson that moves from warm-up to comprehension and follow-up. *(Understand, Apply; SLO:EFPG-401-2-1)*
2. Plan and teach a poetry lesson that fosters enjoyment, response and language awareness rather than memorisation alone. *(Understand, Apply; SLO:EFPG-401-2-1)*
3. Guide learners from controlled practice to free composition through a graded sequence of writing tasks. *(Apply, Analyze; SLO:EFPG-401-2-1)*
4. Present and practise new vocabulary through context, realia and learner strategies rather than word lists alone. *(Understand, Apply; SLO:EFPG-401-2-2)*
5. Teach pronunciation through sounds, stress and intonation with drills and minimal pairs. *(Apply, Analyze; SLO:EFPG-401-2-2)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 1 of this course. You should already understand the four language skills and how to develop them. This unit applies that understanding to five specific teaching tasks.

## In this unit

1. [Teaching prose](./topic-01) - the stages of a prose lesson, comprehension question types and a worked Grade 6 example.
2. [Teaching poems](./topic-02) - prose and poetry compared, teaching a poem for enjoyment and response, and rhythm, rhyme and imagery.
3. [Teaching composition](./topic-03) - the guided-to-free continuum, composition tasks by grade band and a worked guided-writing example.
4. [Teaching vocabulary](./topic-04) - ways to present a new word, effective and ineffective vocabulary strategies, and strategy training.
5. [Teaching pronunciation](./topic-05) - sounds that Urdu, Sindhi and Pashto speakers find hard, and stress and intonation on everyday sentences.

## How to use this unit

Study each topic in order. Each topic opens with a classroom situation from a Pakistani school, explains the teaching method with a worked example, and then asks you to try an activity, check your understanding, and transfer the idea to your practicum school. Complete the self-assessment checklist before moving on. After all five topics, attempt the unit assessment to consolidate your learning.
