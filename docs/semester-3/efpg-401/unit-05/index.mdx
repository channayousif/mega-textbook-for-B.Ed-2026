# Lesson Planning

A lesson plan is the teacher's route map for a single lesson. It turns a syllabus aim into a sequence of things the teacher and the pupils actually do. This unit shows how to plan English lessons that pupils take part in rather than sit through: lessons built around activities, clear outcomes and a small number of well-chosen steps.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

1. Explain why every kind of English lesson needs activities, and describe what each common lesson stage achieves. *(Understand; SLO:EFPG-401-5-1)*
2. Write a lesson plan that moves through outcomes, warm-up, presentation, practice, production, assessment and homework, and justify each step. *(Apply; SLO:EFPG-401-5-1)*
3. Select and organise activity-based techniques such as pair work, group work, games and role play for a given class and lesson aim. *(Apply; SLO:EFPG-401-5-1)*
4. Plan structural, prose, poetry, paragraph, story, essay and grammar lessons using the right sequence of stages for each. *(Apply, Analyze; SLO:EFPG-401-5-2)*

## Prerequisite knowledge

This unit builds on Units 2, 3 and 4. You should already know the four language skills, the main methods of teaching English, the structural and communicative approaches, and the functional approach to grammar. You should also be comfortable with the idea of a learning outcome from Unit 1.

## In this unit

1. [Why every lesson needs activities](./topic-01) - what an activity is, why it matters, and the balance of teacher talk and pupil work.
2. [The steps of a lesson plan](./topic-02) - outcomes, warm-up, presentation, practice, production, assessment and homework.
3. [Activity-based approaches](./topic-03) - pair work, group work, games and role play, and how to run them.
4. [Planning a structural lesson](./topic-04) - presentation, controlled practice and free practice for a sentence pattern.
5. [Planning prose and poetry lessons](./topic-05) - reading for meaning, and poetry from enjoyment to appreciation.
6. [Planning writing lessons](./topic-06) - paragraph, story and essay lessons, and the guided writing sequence.
7. [Planning a grammar lesson](./topic-07) - a functional, meaning-first grammar lesson from context to use.

## How to use this unit

Study each topic in order, because the later topics reuse the steps and activity types from the earlier ones. Each topic opens with a classroom situation, explains the teaching point with a worked example, and then asks you to try a collaborative activity, a retrieval check and a practicum task. Keep a notebook of the lesson-plan skeletons you build, because the unit assessment asks you to assemble a complete lesson plan for one lesson type.
