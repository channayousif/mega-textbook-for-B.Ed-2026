# Mega Textbook

A public, bilingual (English + Urdu) textbook collection and learning platform. The MVP serves **ADE and B.Ed(H) students and their teachers** and **STEDA teacher-licence aspirants** under the Government of Sindh. Grades 9-12 subject tracks are the planned next expansion.

Use the sidebar to browse **Semester → Course → Unit**, switch language with the اردو /
English toggle in the top bar, and search the whole book.

> Content is authored Semesters 1–4 first; all 8 semesters are scaffolded, and
> un-authored units are marked **coming soon**.
