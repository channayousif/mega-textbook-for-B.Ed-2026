# Water and Waste Management

Unit 1 ended with a rate: use below renewal. This unit follows the two things a city moves
most, water and waste, and shows what happens when that rate is exceeded on both sides: too
little clean water in, too much polluted water and solid waste out. It runs from the global
water balance to a hospital's sharps bin, and every scale in between.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Describe the distribution of water on Earth, and explain why a flooded district can still
  be water-scarce.
- Describe Pakistan's water resources and the challenges to its water security.
- Explain the sources, types, impacts and remedies of water pollution.
- Explain how industrial effluents are treated and recycled.
- Apply the principles of municipal solid waste management, including the waste hierarchy.
- Distinguish hazardous hospital and industrial waste from ordinary waste, and describe how
  each is managed.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 1 (natural resources, the human-activity nexus, the rate question). You should be able
to name a renewable resource and explain what exceeding its renewal rate does.

## How to use this unit

Work the topics in order: the first sets the supply, the second the pollution, the third the
waste stream, and each builds on the one before. Keep your Unit 1 waste log running: Topic
2.3 asks you to re-read it as a waste stream rather than a personal habit. The activities
need a water bill, a map of your neighbourhood and your school's bins, all ordinary things.
