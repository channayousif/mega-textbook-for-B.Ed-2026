# Air, Noise and Environmental Hazards

Unit 2 followed water and waste. This unit turns to what surrounds everyone, all the time:
the air and the sound, and then to the field that grew up to manage their dangers: health,
safety and environment. It ends with the one tool that organises everything after it, the
risk management cycle, which Unit 4 will take into whole workplaces.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Explain the causes, effects and control strategies of air pollution.
- Explain the sources, human health impacts and control measures of noise pollution.
- Describe the HSE field and its idea of a hazard.
- Classify hazards as physical, mechanical, biological, chemical or psychological.
- Explain the link between health and environment, and what environmental safety means.
- Apply the hazard identification and risk management process to a real situation.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Units 1 and 2 (the human-activity nexus; the source-pathway-receptor logic of pollution).
The air-pollution chain in Topic 3.1 reuses Unit 2's chain with air in place of water.

## How to use this unit

Work the topics in order: two pollution topics first, then the hazard framework they feed
into, then the risk cycle that answers them. The activities use a phone (an air quality app
and a sound meter app) and one walk around a building. Keep the notes: Unit 4's workplace
audit builds directly on Topic 3.4's risk scoring.
