# Topic 6.5 - Smog

## A real classroom situation

In November in Lahore, the morning assembly moves indoors and the announcement says the
AQI is "hazardous, again". A Class 7 pupil asks the trainee teacher the question this
topic exists for: "Sir, is this fog? My grandmother says it is fog." The trainee looks
out at the grey and answers carefully. "Your grandmother's fog was water. This is water
with a city in it."

## Explanation

### Smog: types, causes, human health effects and control measures

<Glossary term="Smog">Smog</Glossary> is polluted fog, or, more precisely, a visible
mixture of pollutants held near the ground, and it comes in two types. **Sulfurous
smog**, the winter kind, forms when sulfur dioxide and particles from coal and
waste burning mix with fog under a temperature inversion, the cold-air lid Unit 3 met.
**Photochemical smog**, the summer kind, forms when traffic and industrial emissions
cook in sunlight into ozone and a haze of secondary pollutants, worst on hot, still
afternoons. Lahore's winter grey is mostly the first kind with a base of the second's
leftovers, and the integrated satellite-and-survey study of Unit 3 found it critical
for both environment and public health in the district (Anwar et al., 2026).

<Figure id="fig-U6-9" src="/img/figures/gnas-301/unit-06/fig-U6-9.svg" alt="Diagram of smog formation, two panels, winter sulfurous smog under an inversion with coal and waste smoke, and summer photochemical smog with traffic emissions cooked by sunlight into ozone" width={780} height={470} />

The health effects are Unit 3's air-pollution effects concentrated: the PM2.5 and the
ozone inflame airways, worsen asthma and heart disease, and the WHO attributes 4.2
million premature deaths a year to outdoor air pollution, of which smog episodes are
the visible peaks (WHO, 2024). The grandmother's fog never did this; the water only
made the poison visible.

<Figure id="fig-U6-10" src="/img/figures/gnas-301/unit-06/fig-U6-10.svg" alt="Table comparing sulfurous and photochemical smog with causes, health effects and control measures" width={780} height={470} />

The control measures are Unit 3's chain, applied to an episode. At the **source**:
cleaner fuel and upgraded kilns, enforced engine standards, an end to crop-residue and
waste burning, the measures that shorten the episode for everyone. At the
**pathway**: little can be done while the inversion lid sits; the honest answer is
that the pathway cannot be cleaned. At the **receptor**: the school's AQI watch,
indoor hours, masks that filter PM2.5, and asthma plans ready, the measures that
protect the assembly.

**A worked example.** Read the announcement morning through the chain. The AQI is
hazardous, so the receptor measures fire at once: assembly indoors, windows shut,
sports cancelled. The same reading, published daily, is the evidence for the source
measures, kiln upgrades, traffic alternation, burning bans, that the district's winter
plans now carry. One number, two ends of the chain; the school acts on one end and
votes for the other.

**Common misconception.** "Smog is just fog." Fog is water; smog is water plus
pollutants, or sunlight plus pollutants, and the difference is a health effect. The
test is the index, not the eye: the AQI reading tells the assembly what to do whatever
the grey looks like.

## Activity: The two-panel sort

Work in pairs. Your teacher gives each pair twelve cards, causes, effects and controls
mixed. Sort them onto the two panels, sulfurous and photochemical, then swap one card
with another pair and defend the placement. Allow 20 minutes.

## Check your understanding

1. Define smog and name its two types with their seasons. *(Remember)*
2. What does a temperature inversion do to a city's winter air? *(Understand)*
3. Why can nothing be done at the pathway link during an inversion? *(Analyze)*
4. The AQI reads hazardous on assembly morning. Name the two ends of the chain the
   school acts on. *(Apply)*

## Summary

Smog is polluted fog: sulfurous in winter, sulfur dioxide and particles under an
inversion from coal and waste burning, photochemical in summer, traffic emissions
cooked by sunlight into ozone. Its health effects are air pollution's effects at their
peaks, 4.2 million premature deaths a year worldwide. Controls act at the source, fuel,
kilns, engines, burning bans, and at the receptor, the AQI watch, indoor hours,
filtration, asthma plans, while the pathway honestly cannot be cleaned. The AQI number
serves both ends.

checks, at what threshold, what changes. Do not name the school.
