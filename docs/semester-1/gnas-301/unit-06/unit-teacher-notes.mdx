# Unit 6 teacher notes

## Sequencing note

Six topics, one arc: the microscope half first (6.1 substances, 6.2 effects, 6.3 the
body), then the atmospheric half (6.4 greenhouse, 6.5 smog, 6.6 the capstone). The
two-bottles hook of 6.1 and the two photographs of 6.6 are the unit's bookends; the
ladder activity in 6.3 and the pane model in 6.4 are its two hands-on anchors. Teach
6.6 with the whole course open: it is the integration, not an appendix.

## Teaching strategies (from the course guide)

The guide names interactive lectures, seminars, group work, case studies, presentations,
guided reading, home study and individual tutorials. This unit leans on case study (the
ward, the lake, the photographs) and on the two measurement activities, the pane model
and the AQI routine. The home-study element is the school's climate page in 6.6, the
course's last practicum task and the one most worth collecting.

## Common misconceptions to probe

1. **"Natural means safe."** Probe with arsenic and snake venom against a life-saving
   synthetic: what is the honest axis?
2. **"A small dose of anything is harmless."** Probe with the no-threshold class and
   the sensitizer: which two classes break the rule, and how?
3. **"The greenhouse effect is the problem."** Probe with the frozen-Earth question:
   what would we lose if the glass came out?
4. **"Smog is just fog."** Probe with the index: what does the AQI read on a foggy
   clean morning and on a smog morning?
5. **"Mitigation and adaptation are alternatives."** Probe with the two photographs:
   which single response answers both?

## Managing the activities

**6.1, Two bottles, four questions (20 minutes, pairs).** The "rather have spilled"
choice forces the dose argument; accept any defensible answer.

**6.2, Class the case (25 minutes, groups of three).** The case cards should mix
clocks and classes; the most instructive card read aloud is usually the sensitizer,
which defeats the intuition.

**6.3, Build the ladder (25 minutes, groups of four).** The board arithmetic of the
magnification factor is the moment; keep the numbers simple enough to multiply in
public.

**6.4, The pane model (20 minutes, pairs).** A desk lamp, a thermometer, two clear
sheets: the second pane's curve is the whole mechanism. If kit is short, run it as one
demonstration with a pupil recording.

**6.5, The two-panel sort (20 minutes, pairs).** The one-card swap and defend is the
learning; a wrong placement defended is worth more than a right one unexplained.

**6.6, Sort the responses (25 minutes, groups of three).** The four cards presented to
"the district government" teach prioritisation; require the one-sentence reasoning.

## Practical work and practicum links

The pane model and the climate page are the unit's practicals. The AQI routine draft
(6.5) and the climate page (6.6) both run at the practicum school and both are
handover artefacts, written to be used. Debrief the course's full set of practicum
tasks together in the last session: the waste log, the risk register, the near-miss
book, the plan, the climate page, that stack is the trainee's portfolio and the
course's real assessment.
