# Toxic Substances, Climate Change and Smog

The course closes where it opened, at the human-environment interaction, now seen whole.
Unit 5 followed a toxin inside one body; this unit opens the full toxicology toolkit,
substances, doses, effects, accumulation, and then turns to the largest interaction of
all: a warming climate, the smog it worsens, and the mitigation and adaptation that
answer it. The 2022 floods and the Karachi heatwave return as the capstone cases.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Define toxicology and its core questions.
- Classify toxic substances and distinguish anthropogenic from natural poisons.
- Explain acute, chronic, genotoxic, mutagenic, teratogenic, carcinogenic and
  sensitizing effects.
- Explain the biological properties of organic and inorganic pollutants, essentiality
  and toxicity, routes of absorption, bioaccumulation and bio-magnification.
- Explain the greenhouse effect, its causes and its enhancement, and global warming.
- Explain smog, its types, causes, health effects and control measures.
- Explain climate change, its causes and effects, and evaluate mitigation strategies
  and adaptation.

## How to use this unit

The topics run from the microscope to the atmosphere: substances and doses (6.1-6.3),
then the planetary half (6.4-6.6). The dose-response idea from 6.1 carries through the
whole unit, and the climate topics deliberately reuse every earlier unit's tools, the
chain, the hierarchy, the governance stack, so the course ends integrated rather than
appendicised.
