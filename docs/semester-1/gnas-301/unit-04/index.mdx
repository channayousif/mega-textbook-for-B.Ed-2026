# Occupational Safety and Health

Unit 3 ended with the risk cycle for one building. This unit scales the same thinking up to
whole workplaces and the systems that govern them: what goes wrong in factories, on
construction sites, in fields and in service workplaces; how management systems are built to
prevent it; and what Pakistan's own statutes, duties and professionals contribute.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Describe the OSH issues of the industry, construction, agriculture and service sectors.
- Explain accidents, injuries and workplace fatalities, and what a real case shows.
- Explain an occupational safety and health management system, international and national.
- Describe safety management, HSE regulation, and internal control.
- State the duties and rights of employers, workers and government, and the OHS
  professional's roles.
- Apply key management and HSE principles, measures and models.
- Describe HSE statutes and regulations in Pakistan, and how an HSE plan is established.
- Analyse health challenges within the working environment, the external environment, and
  the tools and instruments that answer them.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 3 (hazard classes, hierarchy of controls, the risk cycle). This unit assumes you can
score a hazard and name a control at each level.

## How to use this unit

The topics run from the concrete to the systemic: sectors first, then the systems that
manage them, then the law and the profession, and finally the principles, plans and tools.
Topic 4.1's sector portraits and 4.2's PDCA cycle are the two things every later topic
reuses, so give them the time they need.
