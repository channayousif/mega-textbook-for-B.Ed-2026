# Unit 5 teacher notes

## Sequencing note

The unit runs inward then outward: 5.1 the agents, 5.2 the body's handling, 5.3 the
honest version of 5.2 that pupils will meet as marketing, and 5.4 the governance that
manages it all at scale. The CO generator threads 5.1 to 5.3; the arsenic case threads
5.1 to 5.4. Topic 5.4 is deliberately the largest, folding risk management and the
frameworks; give it the guide's full week plus the spill-over from 5.3.

## Teaching strategies (from the course guide)

The guide names interactive lectures, seminars, group work, case studies, presentations,
guided reading, home study and individual tutorials. This unit leans on case study (the
generator, the sprayer, the tea) and on the wall timeline, which is a presentation
format every pupil can walk. The home-study element is the staffroom inventory in 5.3,
a gentle first audit of health claims.

## Common misconceptions to probe

1. **"If you cannot smell it, it is not poisoning you."** Probe with CO and the
   instrument question: what did the nose evolve to detect?
2. **"The liver detoxifies, so chemicals become harmless inside."** Probe with the
   sprayer: what is bioactivation, and what does the queue mean?
3. **"Detox means juice cleanses."** Probe with the plumbing: which organ does the tea
   touch? Name the four helps.
4. **"MEAs are toothless talk shops."** Do not dismiss the frustration; answer it with
   the POPs evidence and the reporting-cycle mechanism, then let the honest limit stand.

## Managing the activities

**5.1, Chain the agent (25 minutes, pairs).** The vote on protection-per-rupee teaches
cost-effectiveness without naming it; keep the voting quick and unweighted.

**5.2, Journey map (20 minutes, pairs).** Comparing maps between pairs with the same
card is the point: two correct maps can differ in the harm's stage, and the argument is
the learning.

**5.3, The honest label (25 minutes, groups of three).** Handle the product claims with
respect for the buyer; the activity teaches evaluation, not mockery. The ninety-second
format keeps it kind.

**5.4, The wall timeline (25 minutes, groups of four).** The fourth card, the agreement
they would want, is the bridge from recall to creation; gallery-walk the results.

## Practical work and practicum links

The staffroom inventory (5.3) and the three-line answer, problem, office, instrument
(5.4), are the unit's practicals, and both run at the practicum school. The guide's
practical hour can also carry the chemical-storage walk in 5.2. Debrief together: the
governance stack of 5.4 answers who owns the hazards of Units 2 to 4, which is the
course's whole argument in one session.
