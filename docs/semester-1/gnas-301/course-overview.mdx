# Environmental Science - Course Overview

GNAS-301 Environmental Science is a 3 (2-1) credit General Education course in Semester 1
of the B.Ed (4-Year) programme. It builds a comprehensive understanding of the
interactions between humans and their environment, from ecosystems and natural resources
through pollution, waste and occupational safety to toxicology, environmental law and
climate change, with Pakistan's own environment as the running case.

## Course Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Explain fundamental concepts of environmental science, including ecosystems, natural
   resources, and human-environment interactions.
2. Analyze major environmental issues such as pollution, climate change, and resource
   depletion at local and global levels.
3. Evaluate environmental health risks and the effects of physical, chemical, and
   biological hazards on human health.
4. Apply environmental management tools and techniques for pollution control, waste
   management, and risk assessment.
5. Develop sustainable solutions and demonstrate responsible environmental behavior in
   personal and professional contexts.

## Units

1. **Environmental Science and Ecosystems** - what environmental science studies, the
   four spheres of planet Earth, the human-activity nexus, ecosystems and natural
   resources, and the individual's part.
2. **Water and Waste Management** - global and Pakistani water resources, water
   pollution and effluent treatment, the waste hierarchy, and hazardous hospital and
   industrial waste.
3. **Air, Noise and Environmental Hazards** - air and noise pollution, the five hazard
   classes, and the risk assessment and management cycle.
4. **Occupational Safety and Health** - sector hazards, OSH management systems and the
   PDCA cycle, regulation and internal control, duties and rights, HSE principles,
   statutes, plans and tools.
5. **Toxicology Essentials and Environmental Governance** - atmospheric agents, the fate
   of toxins, natural detoxification, risk management, multilateral environmental
   agreements, the SDGs and Pakistan's environmental law.
6. **Toxic Substances, Climate Change and Smog** - toxicology proper, effect classes,
   bioaccumulation, the greenhouse effect, smog, and climate change mitigation and
   adaptation.

## Teaching Strategies

From the course guide: interactive lectures, seminars, group work, case studies,
presentations, guided reading, home study and individual tutorials. Students are
encouraged to read widely and keep abreast of current trends and issues; discussions and
presentations develop course themes and the sharing of ideas and knowledge.
