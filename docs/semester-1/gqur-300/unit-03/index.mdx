# Algebraic Reasoning

Algebra is not a new subject; it is the number work of Unit 2 made reusable. This unit
walks the classic road into it: patterns first (because a pattern's rule is an expression
waiting to be written), then equations and inequalities (because a rule plus a known total
is a solvable claim), and finally the translation loop that turns fares, budgets and rate
cards into algebra and back into decisions.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Find the growth step of a pattern and write its rule as an expression with a named
  variable.
- Solve two-step linear equations with a substitution check, and read the equals sign as
  a balance.
- Solve linear inequalities, including the sign flip on negative multiplication, and
  round whole-number answers in the direction the situation demands.
- Translate a fare, budget or pricing situation into an expression or equation, solve it,
  and interpret the result back into the situation.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 2 (especially operations, decimals and the operation-choosing flowchart) and Unit 1's
four-step problem-solving strategy.

## How to use this unit

Work the topics in order. Keep a pencil moving: every worked example hides its conclusion
one line below where you should stop and try. The practicum task in each topic collects
real rate cards and patterns; ERQ-5 will ask for yours, so keep the notes dated. Glossary
terms are underlined; hover or tap them for the Urdu definition.
