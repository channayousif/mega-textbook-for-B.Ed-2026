# Data Analysis and Statistics

Data is where quantitative reasoning meets evidence. This unit runs the full cycle on
real material: a question, honest collection, tidy organization, the right display,
the right average, and finally the reading of published statistics - including the
2023 census figures for Sindh, whose literacy and out-of-school numbers give the
unit its anchor in the province where most trainees will teach.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Run the data cycle: pose a question, collect by observation, survey or records,
  organize with tallies and frequency tables, and check the total.
- Choose the display that answers the question (bar, pie, line, pictograph) and read a
  display's construction, not just its impression.
- Compute the mean, median and mode, and choose the honest average for data with
  outliers.
- Interrogate a published statistic with the five interpretation questions: who
  collected it, how, compared to what, on what base, and what is missing.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 2 (percentages, for pie charts and rates) and Unit 1's reasonableness habit.

## How to use this unit

Work the topics in order; each is one turn of the data cycle. The activities collect
real data from your own class, so keep every frequency table - the unit assessment
builds on them. The Sindh census figures in Topic 5.3 are real (Pakistan Bureau of
Statistics, 2023); treat them the way the topic teaches you to treat any statistic.
Glossary terms are underlined; hover or tap them for the Urdu definition.
