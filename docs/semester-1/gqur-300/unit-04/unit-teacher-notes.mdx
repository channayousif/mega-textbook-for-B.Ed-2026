# Unit 4 teacher notes

## Sequencing note

Topic 4.1 gives the ladders and the conversion habit; Topic 4.2 gives the three
quantities and their formulas; Topic 4.3 runs whole tasks. Keep the unit's shape
visible: units before quantities, quantities before tasks. Across the two weeks the
content-spec allows, spend the saved time on Topic 4.3's plan-writing, because that is
the skill the practicum and ERQ-5 assess.

## Teaching strategies from the guide

- Use the guide's real-life case-study mode: the fabric shop, the librarian and the
  paint question are all real contexts - ask trainees for their own bazaar equivalents
  before revealing the worked examples.
- Run the conversion market (Topic 4.1) as a timed group activity; the race is the
  engagement, but mark only the conversions.
- Use question-answer on the formula table (Topic 4.2): cover the examples column and
  rebuild it from the formulas.

## Common misconceptions to probe

1. **"Bigger things need bigger numbers."** Hold up the same water bottle described as
   1.5 litres and 1,500 millilitres. The thing did not grow; the unit shrank.
2. **"A bigger area means a bigger perimeter."** The corridor-versus-room pair (40/44
   against 42/26) settles it; let trainees compute both before you comment.
3. **"The tape measure is the measurement."** Ask what the librarian did before she
   measured anything. Choosing the quantity is the move most failures skip.
4. **"The calculator said so."** Feed the class the 4,800-square-metre classroom and
   ask one question: could that possibly be right? The round-number check is the
   curriculum's answer.

## Running the activities

- **The conversion market (Topic 4.1)**: keep the rate cards realistic (check current
  bazaar prices if you can); the pricing step exists so the conversions have
  consequences.
- **Fence or carpet (Topic 4.2)**: the odd-shaped staff room (two rectangles) is where
  most groups stumble; do not pre-teach the split, let them find it.
- **The courtyard plan (Topic 4.3)**: the swap-and-mark step is the assessment; the
  marking pair must name the weakest move, not just find arithmetic slips.

## Practical work

Group work in the three activities; a group assignment measuring and planning one real
school space end to end; the individual assignment is the Topic 4.3 practicum plan;
presentations come from the courtyard plans.

## Assessment guidance

The bank's traps are deliberate: MCQ-03 punishes dividing before converting, MCQ-08
tests the area-perimeter split, and RRQ-07's 4,800-square-metre classroom is the unit's
central transfer. When marking the ERQs, require the unit on every answer: a correct
number without its unit is half an answer, and the rubrics say so.

## Practicum links

The Topic 4.3 practicum task hands a real plan to a cooperating teacher. Insist
trainees record the teacher's verdict verbatim; ERQ-5 asks for it, and the habit of
asking a practitioner to check one's work is the unit's larger lesson.
