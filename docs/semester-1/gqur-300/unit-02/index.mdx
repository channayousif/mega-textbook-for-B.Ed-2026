# Numbers and Operations

This unit builds the working toolkit. Unit 1 established what quantitative reasoning is;
here you sharpen the number skills it runs on: the number system from whole numbers to
decimals, the proportional reasoning that connects recipes, marksheets and discounts, and
the powers and roots behind areas and tiling. Every skill is met first in a Pakistani
bazaar, kitchen or classroom, because that is where you will use it.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Place whole numbers, integers, fractions and decimals on one number line and compute
  across the system.
- Scale quantities with ratios and proportions, and handle all four percentage situations
  including the reverse case.
- Compute squares and square roots, and bracket a root between neighbouring squares.
- Choose the right operation for a daily-life problem by asking what the total does, and
  check the answer for reasonableness.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Unit 1 (especially the four-step problem-solving strategy and the reasonableness check),
plus comfortable everyday arithmetic.

## How to use this unit

Work through the topics in order; each assumes the previous. Do every worked example with a
pencil before reading its conclusion, and keep your Unit 1 practicum notes at hand: the
reasonableness habit from Unit 1 is used in every topic here. Glossary terms are underlined;
hover or tap them for the Urdu definition.
