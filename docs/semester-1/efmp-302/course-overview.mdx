# Teaching Profession - Course Overview

This course equips prospective teachers with a grounded understanding of the teaching profession
as a dynamic, ethical, and evolving field of practice - professional ethics and codes of conduct,
national and global professional standards, effective and reflective practice, and continuous
professional development.

## Assessment

Assessment follows the GECE default: **60% summative / 40% formative** (Constitution Art. III.7).
This course's guide does not specify a course-wide breakdown of teaching strategies or practical
work beyond what each unit's own topics cover - see each unit's Teacher Notes for the
strategies used to teach it.
