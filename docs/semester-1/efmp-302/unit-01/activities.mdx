# Activities

## Activity 1: Profession or occupation?

In pairs, pick three jobs you know well — for example, teacher, shopkeeper, and doctor. For each
one, decide whether it shows the four features of a profession from this unit (specialized
training, a body of knowledge, a code of conduct, accountability to a professional community).
Which job is clearly a profession? Which is clearly not? Is there one you disagree about — and
why?

## Activity 2: Compliance or inquiry?

Think of one lesson you have seen taught, or imagine one on a topic you know. Describe it two
ways: first as an "industrial" lesson (fixed procedure, efficiency, compliance), then as an
"inquiry-based" lesson on the same topic (the teacher reflecting on how students are actually
learning and adjusting). Name one concrete change that would move the industrial version toward
the inquiry-based one.
