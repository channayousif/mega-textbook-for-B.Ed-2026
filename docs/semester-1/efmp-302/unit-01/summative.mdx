# Summative Assessment

## Task

Your teacher will give you a **one-page description of a teacher's classroom practice** (real
or realistic — for example, a teacher in a Sindh government school). Read it carefully, then
write a structured analysis of about 500–700 words.

Your analysis must do all three of the following:

1. **Professional features.** Judge the described practice against the **four features of a
   profession** (specialised knowledge; extended training and a qualification; a code of
   conduct; accountability to a professional community). For each feature, say whether the
   description shows it clearly, partly, or not at all, and quote or point to the specific
   detail that tells you.
2. **Dimensions of professionalism.** Using the **four dimensions** (specialised knowledge; an
   ethical code of conduct; the balance of accountability, autonomy, and collegiality;
   organised professional bodies), identify **one dimension that is strong** and **one that is
   weak or missing** in this teacher's practice. Justify both with evidence from the text.
3. **Industrial or inquiry-based?** Decide whether the described practice sits closer to the
   **industrial model** or to **inquiry-based professionalism**, or somewhere between. Support
   your decision with at least **two specific details** from the description, not a general
   impression. Then propose **one realistic change** that would move the practice further
   toward inquiry-based professionalism, and explain why it would help.

## Rubric (20 marks)

| Criterion | Excellent (full marks) | Adequate (about half) | Weak (low marks) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Analysis against the four features** (6) | All four features judged; each judgement tied to a specific detail from the description | Most features judged; some judgements not evidenced | Features listed or defined but not applied to the description |
| **Dimensions: one strong, one weak** (5) | Both clearly identified and justified with evidence | Identified but justification thin or one-sided | Dimensions named without linking to the case |
| **Industrial vs inquiry-based judgement** (5) | Clear position, supported by two or more specific details; nuance where the case is mixed | Position stated with one detail, or details are vague | Assertion with no evidence from the text |
| **Proposed change** (2) | Realistic, specific, and explained in terms of inquiry-based professionalism | Named but generic ("care more", "be better") | Missing or unrelated |
| **Structure and plain, accurate language** (2) | Organised, clear, terms used correctly | Understandable but loosely organised | Hard to follow; key terms misused |

**Note on Bloom's level:** this task is set at the **Analyze** level — you are breaking a real
case into parts and relating it to the unit's frameworks, then defending your reading with
evidence. Simply retelling the description, or defining the terms without applying them, does
not meet the task.
