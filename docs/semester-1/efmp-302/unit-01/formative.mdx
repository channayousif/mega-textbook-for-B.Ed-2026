# Formative Assessment

Low-stakes checks. Answer in your own words. Your teacher will use these to see which ideas
need more work before the summative task — they do not count toward your grade.

1. In one sentence, define a **profession**, and name the **four features** that separate it
   from an ordinary occupation. *(Remember)*
2. Explain, with one example each, the difference between **professionalism in teaching** and
   **professionalisation of teaching**. *(Understand)*
3. Describe **one feature of the "industrial" view** of teaching, then give **one concrete
   example** of what inquiry-based professionalism looks like in a real classroom.
   *(Understand / Apply)*
4. A teacher plans every lesson alone, is never observed, and never has their marking checked,
   but is also never asked what their students have learned. Which of **accountability,
   autonomy, and collegiality** are missing here? Justify your answer. *(Apply)*
5. Name the **four dimensions of teacher professionalism**. For the "specialised knowledge"
   dimension, give one example of *pedagogical content knowledge* (not just subject
   knowledge). *(Remember / Apply)*
6. Give **two ways** the social or policy context a teacher works in can shape what
   "professional teaching" means for them. *(Understand)*
7. Explain in two or three sentences why simply keeping a teaching diary is **not the same as**
   reflective practice. *(Understand)*
8. Complete and extend: *"Two trainees can finish the same B.Ed course and still teach
   differently because…"* — give one reason and link it to the idea of **teacher identity**.
   *(Apply)*
