# Formative Assessment

## Quick check

1. In one sentence, what is a profession?
2. Name two features that distinguish a profession from an ordinary occupation.
3. What is the difference between professionalism in teaching and professionalization of
   teaching?
4. Describe one feature of the "industrial" view of teaching.
5. Give one example of what inquiry-based professionalism looks like in a classroom.
6. Why might reflecting on "who am I becoming as a teacher?" matter for a new teacher?
