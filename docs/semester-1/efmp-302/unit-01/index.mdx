# Understanding Teaching

Before you can grow as a teacher, it helps to be clear about what teaching actually asks of
you. This unit works through that question in four short topics. Each topic is a complete
learning cycle: it opens with a real classroom situation, explains the ideas, gives you an
activity, checks your understanding, and ends with a task you can try on your practicum.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Define a **profession** and name the four features that separate it from an ordinary
  occupation, and use them to judge whether a described job is a profession.
- Explain the difference between **professionalism in teaching** and **professionalisation of
  teaching**, and place teaching in a comparative view alongside other professions.
- Describe the shift from an **industrial** model of teaching to **inquiry-based
  professionalism**, and apply that lens to a described lesson.
- Explain the **four dimensions of teacher professionalism** and use them to analyse a
  described teacher's practice.
- Describe how **teacher identity** develops through reflection and is shaped by personal
  beliefs and by the social and policy context, and begin reflecting on your own.

## Prerequisite knowledge

None beyond HSC-level general study skills. This is the opening unit of the course and of the
semester; you are not assumed to know any education theory. You are assumed to have been a
school student yourself — that experience is a starting point for several of the topics.

## How to use this unit

- **Work through the topics in order.** Topic 1.3 uses the ideas from 1.1, and Topic 1.4
  assumes you have met the four dimensions.
- **Do the activity and the "Check your understanding" questions before you move on.** They
  are not graded; they show you which ideas are still shaky.
- **Keep your Topic 1.4 reflection.** You will add to it through the course and return to it in
  Unit 6 when you build a professional development plan.
- After the four topics, the **unit assessment** page has a full question bank with answers and
  marking guidance so you can test yourself.
