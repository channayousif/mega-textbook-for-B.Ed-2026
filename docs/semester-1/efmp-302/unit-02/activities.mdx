# Activities

## Activity 1: A grading fairness dilemma

A teacher is grading a group assignment. One student clearly did most of the work, but the group
wants an equal grade for everyone. In pairs, walk through this dilemma step by step using
Empathy (who is affected, and how), Context (what facts do you need before deciding), Reflect
(what does fairness require here), and Action (what will you actually do and say to the group).

## Activity 2: Spot the dilemma type

In pairs, sort these situations into the four dilemma categories from this unit — grading
fairness, confidentiality, social media conduct, and equity — and name one relationship (student,
parent, colleague, or the profession) each one mainly involves: (a) a parent asks you to share
another student's grades; (b) a student posts something concerning on social media and tags a
classmate; (c) two students from different backgrounds get different amounts of your attention
during class; (d) a colleague asks you to inflate a mark as a favor.
