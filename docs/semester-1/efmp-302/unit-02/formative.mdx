# Formative Assessment

## Quick check

1. In one sentence, what are professional ethics?
2. Name two of the relationships a teacher's code of ethical conduct covers (for example,
   toward students, toward colleagues).
3. List the four abilities in this unit's model of moral behavior, in your own words.
4. What are the four steps of the Empathy–Context–Reflect–Action framework, in order?
5. Give one example of a classroom ethical dilemma from this unit's four categories.
6. Why might "noticing" a fairness problem not be enough on its own?
