# Summative Assessment

## End-of-unit question

Read the classroom ethical-dilemma scenario your teacher provides. Work through it using the
Empathy–Context–Reflect–Action framework:

- **Empathy**: who is affected by this situation, and how?
- **Context**: what specific facts would you need before deciding — and what should you avoid
  assuming?
- **Reflect**: weigh at least two possible responses against your responsibilities as a teacher.
- **Action**: state the response you would take, and justify it using the reflection above rather
  than restating the scenario.
