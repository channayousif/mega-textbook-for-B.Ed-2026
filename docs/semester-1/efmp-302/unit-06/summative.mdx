# Summative Assessment

## End-of-unit question

Read the sample professional development plan your teacher provides. Critique it against this
unit's principles:

- Does the plan name specific activities, or only vague intentions? Give examples from the plan.
- Does the plan draw from more than one category (formal activities, community, reflective
  practice), or is it narrowly focused on just one?
- Is the plan appropriate for the career stage it appears to be written for? Justify your answer,
  and suggest one specific improvement.
