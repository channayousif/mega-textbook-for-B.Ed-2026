# Activities

## Activity 1: Draft a one-term development plan

Individually, draft a short personal professional development plan for your first term of
teaching. Name at least one specific activity from each category in this unit — a formal activity
(conference, workshop, or online platform), a community activity (a Professional Learning
Community or similar), and a reflective-practice activity (journal, portfolio, peer feedback, or
lesson study) — with a realistic timeframe for each.

## Activity 2: Which stage am I in?

In pairs, discuss which of the three development stages (preservice, new teacher, experienced
teacher) best describes where you are right now, and name one thing you expect to need help with
most in the next stage.
