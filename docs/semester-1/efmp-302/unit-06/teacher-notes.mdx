# Teacher Notes

## Teaching strategies

As the course's closing unit, open by briefly connecting back to Unit 1's question ("who am I
becoming as a teacher?") - continuous professional development is the practical, ongoing answer
to that question, not a separate new topic. When students draft Activity 1's development plan,
push them to make each activity genuinely specific (a named workshop or platform, not just
"attend training") - vague plans are the most common weakness this unit warns against.

## Practical work

Activity 1's development plan is itself meaningful practical work: encourage students to keep
their draft and revisit it once they begin actual teaching practice, treating it as a living
document rather than a one-time assignment.
