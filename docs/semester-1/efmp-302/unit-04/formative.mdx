# Formative Assessment

## Quick check

1. In one sentence, what is a professional standard?
2. Why do professional standards exist, rather than leaving quality to each school's own
   judgment?
3. How are Pakistan's National Professional Standards for Teachers structured - what are domains
   and indicators?
4. Describe how a teacher could use one domain for honest self-evaluation.
5. Name two things professional standards typically connect to beyond self-reflection.
6. Why might it matter that licensing and appraisal use the same standards a teacher already
   uses day to day?
