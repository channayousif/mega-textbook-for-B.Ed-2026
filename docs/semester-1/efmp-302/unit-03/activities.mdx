# Activities

## Activity 1: Self-reflection checklist

Working alone, rate yourself honestly (1–5) on each quality from this unit: patience,
self-control, optimism, humor, resilience, adaptability. Pick the quality you rated lowest and
write two sentences on one concrete step that would help you grow it.

## Activity 2: Verbal or non-verbal?

In pairs, act out a short classroom moment twice: once where the teacher's words and body
language match (e.g. praising a student while smiling and making eye contact), and once where
they contradict each other (e.g. praising a student while looking at their phone). Discuss what
each version actually communicates to the student, and why.
