# Teacher Notes

## Teaching strategies

Start with Activity 1's self-rating before presenting the qualities as a formal list - students
engage more with the concept once they've already tried to rate themselves against it, even
loosely. For the verbal/non-verbal communication contrast, Activity 2's short paired role-play
works better performed live than described in the abstract; give students only 30–60 seconds
per version so the contrast stays sharp. When introducing the five evolving roles, ask students
which role they expect to use most in their own first year of teaching, and why - this previews
the professional-standards unit that follows.

## Practical work

Activity 1's self-reflection checklist can be revisited later in the course (e.g. after the
teaching-practice units) as a simple before/after comparison, giving students a concrete way to
see their own growth rather than only discussing growth in the abstract.
