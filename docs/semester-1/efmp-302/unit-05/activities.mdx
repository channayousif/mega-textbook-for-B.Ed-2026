# Activities

## Activity 1: A multi-grade classroom case

A teacher in a small school is responsible for a single classroom that combines three grade
levels, with few teaching materials and no assistant. In pairs, identify which challenges from
this unit apply to this teacher's situation, then propose one concrete, growth-oriented response
they could realistically try — not simply "get more resources."

## Activity 2: Naming your own expected challenges

Individually, write down two challenges from this unit you personally expect to face early in
your teaching career, and one small step you could take now, as a student teacher, to prepare for
each one. Share with a partner and compare.
