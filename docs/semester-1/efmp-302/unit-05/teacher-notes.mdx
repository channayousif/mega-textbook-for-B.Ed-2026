# Teacher Notes

## Teaching strategies

This unit can land as discouraging if presented only as a list of problems — frame it explicitly
as "naming challenges honestly so you can prepare for them," and close on the growth-oriented
response theme, not the challenges themselves. Activity 1's multi-grade case works best when
students are pushed past the first easy answer ("get more resources") toward something they could
actually do without waiting on outside help. Be mindful that some student teachers may already
have direct experience with several of these challenges (family responsibilities, resource-poor
schools they attended) — invite sharing but do not require it.

## Practical work

Activity 2's personal reflection is light practical work that pairs well with journaling: keep
students' written responses (or a copy) for them to revisit later in the programme, since the
"small step to prepare" they name now is something they can act on well before their first
teaching placement.
