# Guidance and Counseling

The course ends where a teacher's day begins: with the pupil who needs more than a
lesson. This closing unit carries the guide's remaining Week 16 material - guidance
and counseling, professional ethics, the application of educational psychology, and
technology and learning - and does what a final unit owes a course: it looks back.

Unit 11 drew the referral line. This unit stands on the school side of it: what
guidance and counseling actually are, what the teacher may do at the first line,
where the ethical lines run, and what the whole course adds up to when a real
classroom episode asks for everything at once.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Distinguish **guidance from counseling** from teaching, and describe the
  counselor's work and the school's guidance programme.
- Describe the **teacher as first line of support**: the listening that helps, the
  referral that carries, and the line between them.
- Explain **professional ethics** through the teacher's actual dilemmas, with
  Pakistan's professional-standards context.
- Apply the **whole course**: analyse one classroom episode through every unit, and
  answer it with a justified plan.
- Evaluate **technology and learning**: what technology changes, and what only a
  teacher can do.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Units 1-11 (the whole course). This is the integrative closing unit: Topic 12.3 is
the course's look back over itself, and the unit assessment's capstone asks for the
whole course in one answer.

## How to use this unit

- **Three topics, the last week.** The guide shares Week 16 between Chapters 11-12
  and the course review; this unit takes the third session, and the review belongs
  to the course's revision material.
- **Keep one episode in mind.** The capstone ERQ asks for one classroom episode
  read through the whole course; choose it now and let the three topics feed it.
- **Notice the course ending honestly.** The last topic closes on agency - what a
  teacher can actually do - not on a summary.
- After the three topics, the **unit assessment** page carries a full question bank
  with answers and marking guidance.
