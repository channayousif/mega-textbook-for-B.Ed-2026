# Teacher Notes

## Teaching strategies

Open with a familiar Pakistani classroom example, then draw out the idea that learning is
active. Use think-pair-share for Activity 1.

## Practical work

Ask students to bring one example of a lesson they remember well and use it to discuss what
made the learning stick.
