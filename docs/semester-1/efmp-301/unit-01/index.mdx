# Introduction to Educational Psychology

<Glossary term="Educational Psychology">Educational psychology</Glossary> helps teachers
understand how students learn so that lessons can be planned to support that learning.

## What is Educational Psychology?

It is the study of how people learn in schools and classrooms, and how teaching can be
organised to help them learn better.

## Why it matters for teachers

A teacher who understands learning and <Glossary term="Cognition">cognition</Glossary> can
choose activities that match how students actually think and remember. For example, knowing
that recalling facts strengthens memory better than only re-reading notes, a teacher might end
a lesson with a short quiz-style recap instead of asking students to simply re-read the
textbook.

## Key ideas in this unit

- Learning is an active process.
- Teaching decisions should be grounded in evidence about how students learn.
