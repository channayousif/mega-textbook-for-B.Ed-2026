# Educational Psychology - Course Overview

This course introduces prospective B.Ed teachers to how learners develop, think, and
learn, and to the psychological principles that inform effective teaching in Pakistani
classrooms. It runs from what educational psychology is and how it gathers evidence,
through development, learning theories, cognitive processes, intelligence, motivation,
individual differences, classroom management and assessment, to well-being, guidance,
counseling and professional ethics: the whole arc of the learner and the teacher in
twelve units.

## Course learning outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Understand major theories of learning and development relevant to education.
2. Explain individual differences among learners and their implications for teaching.
3. Apply psychological principles to classroom instruction and management.
4. Analyze motivation, intelligence, and creativity in learning contexts.
5. Use assessment techniques to support student learning.
6. Promote inclusive, ethical, and supportive learning environments.

## Teaching strategies

Teaching follows the course guide: lectures, interactive discussions, question-answer
sessions, demonstration, and case study analysis, with Pakistani classroom examples
throughout.

## Practical work

The course guide names five practical components: hands-on computer exercises,
individual assignments, group assignments, presentations, and internet-research tasks.
Each unit's teacher notes carry the unit's practical task.

## Assessment

Assessment for the affiliated colleges (GECEs) is weighted **60% summative / 40%
formative**, per the course guide and Constitution III.7. Each unit carries a fixed
bank of 10 multiple-choice, 10 restricted-response and 5 extended-response items with
marking guidance.

The course guide names two recommended books, both open access. They are listed for
scope and further reading only; they are never reproduced as content.
