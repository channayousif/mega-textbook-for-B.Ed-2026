# Educational Psychology - Course Overview

This course introduces prospective B.Ed teachers to how learners develop, think, and
learn, and to the psychological principles that inform effective teaching in Pakistani
classrooms.

## Teaching strategies

Teaching follows the course guide: interactive lectures grounded in local classroom
examples, small-group discussion, and case analysis.

## Assessment

Assessment for the affiliated colleges (GECEs) is weighted **60% summative / 40%
formative**, per the course guide and Constitution III.7.

## Recommended reading

Recommended books from the course guide are listed for scope and further reading only;
they are never reproduced as content.
