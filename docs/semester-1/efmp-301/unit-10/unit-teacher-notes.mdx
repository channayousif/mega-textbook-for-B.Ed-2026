# Teaching-Learning Process - teacher notes

For the teacher educator. The course guide allots **Week 15** to Chapter 10:
teaching methods and strategies, instructional design, and teacher effectiveness.
Two topics, one week - and the meta-opportunity is even richer than Unit 9's: the
whole course has been a demonstration of both topics.

## Teaching strategies

The guide names lectures, interactive discussions, question-answer, demonstration
and case study analysis. This unit should USE all five, visibly, and say so.

**Run the water cycle first.** Three sections, three methods, three purposes - the
case does the choosing frame's work before any vocabulary arrives. Ask which
section you would want your own child in, and why; the room's answers are the
purposes in disguise.

**Teach the way you are teaching.** In the first session, lecture the map, then
demonstrate something small, then run the discussion - and at the end, name what
you just did and why. In the second session, show the trainees this course's own
outcome sentences (they are in every unit's front matter) and run the forward check
on one of your own lessons aloud.

**The two-teachers exercise is the week's heart.** Every trainee has been waiting
fifteen units to talk about their own teachers. Let them - with the inventory as
the discipline, so the talk produces analysis rather than nostalgia.

## Sequencing across the week

| Session | Focus | Leave time for |
|---|---|---|
| First session | Topic 10.1 - methods as tools, the choosing frame | the one-topic-three-methods activity |
| Second session | Topic 10.2 - backward design, Mager, effectiveness | the two-teachers inventory |

If a session is lost, cut the method table's detail before the backward order. The
order is the unit's transfer; the table can be re-derived from the guide.

## Misconceptions to expect, and how to handle them

**"Lecturing is bad teaching."** Usually learned from progressive rhetoric. Point
at the table: the lecture covers ground no other method covers as fast. The defect
is the two-hour lecture, not the method. Ask what the lecture demands (stamina,
preparation) and how to pay for it (pauses, recaps, brevity).

**"One method is best."** The water cycle case is the reply: three sections, three
purposes, and the examination that cannot see two of them.

**"A lesson plan is a script."** Meet it with the promise framing: the plan is a
promise about learning, checked against, not obeyed. The trainee who has abandoned
plans mid-lesson and felt guilty needs to hear that the check IS the design.

**"Effectiveness is charisma."** The inventory is the reply. Run the two-teachers
exercise; the placements always come out as practices, and the room sees it happen.

## Language and access

The unit's key terms are banked where banked (حکمتِ تدریس، سبقی منصوبہ، معلم کی
افادیت، تدریسیات). The choosing frame's three words (purpose, constraints, method)
and the backward order's three (outcome, evidence, activity) belong on the board in
both languages for the whole week.

## Practical work

The guide's practical component for this chapter is an individual assignment:
**each trainee writes one real lesson plan backward - the outcome sentence, the
evidence, the activity with minutes - teaches it in their practicum, and brings the
plan plus a one-paragraph note on whether the evidence arrived.**

Run the reporting in the second session. Three or four volunteers read their
outcome sentence and their note; the room runs the forward check on each. The
plans are the artefact behind ERQ 2 and ERQ 5.

## Assessment notes

The unit's formative work sits in the two topics' `Check your understanding`
blocks (ten items across the unit, above the five-to-eight floor). The
`unit-assessment` page carries the graded bank with worked responses and rubrics in
its final bounded section.

ERQ 5 is the capstone and integrates both topics; schedule it after the practical
work so the design has been taught once. ERQ 3 rewards the inventory analysis the
second session trains, and works well as the week's written homework.
