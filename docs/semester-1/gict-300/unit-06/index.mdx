# Internet Applications and Emerging Technologies

The course ends where its readers live: on the internet, and just ahead of it. This unit
first names the tools you already use every day - the browser, the search engine, the
communication apps - and teaches you to use them deliberately rather than habitually. It
then turns to the technologies arriving in classrooms: artificial intelligence, the
Internet of Things, blockchain, and virtual and augmented reality. For each, the same
three questions from Units 4 and 5 come along: what is it really, what can it do for a
school in Pakistan, and what should a careful teacher watch for?

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Explain what a <Glossary term="Web browser">web browser</Glossary> and a
  <Glossary term="Search engine">search engine</Glossary> each do, and use search
  strategies deliberately.
- Match <Glossary term="Online communication tool">online communication tools</Glossary>
  to their purposes.
- Apply <Glossary term="Safe browsing">safe browsing</Glossary> practices, including
  judging a website before trusting it.
- Explain <Glossary term="Artificial Intelligence">artificial intelligence</Glossary>,
  <Glossary term="Blockchain">blockchain</Glossary>, the
  <Glossary term="Internet of Things">Internet of Things</Glossary>, and
  <Glossary term="Virtual reality">virtual and augmented reality</Glossary>, and analyse
  their societal impact.

## Prerequisite knowledge

Units 1 to 5, especially Unit 4 (threats and verification) and Unit 5 (checking before
sharing).

## How to use this unit

Topics 6.1 and 6.2 sharpen tools you already hold; 6.3 and 6.4 open boxes you have heard
named but not opened. Keep a running "emerging technology briefing" notebook from Topic
6.3 onwards: the course's final practicum idea is a one-page briefing to a head teacher,
and every topic feeds it.
