# Foundations of Functional English

Every piece of writing and every conversation rests on words, grammar and structure. This unit
builds the foundations: how words are formed and chosen, how grammar makes meaning clear, and how
sentences are constructed. It is the base on which Units 2 (comprehension) and 3 (communication)
rest.

## Unit learning outcomes

By the end of this unit you should be able to:

- Expand your vocabulary through contextual usage, synonyms, antonyms and idiomatic expressions.
- Identify and correct common grammatical errors including subject-verb agreement, verb tense
  inconsistency, fragments, run-ons and misplaced modifiers.
- Build new words using affixation, compounding, clipping and back formation.
- Distinguish simple, compound, complex and compound-complex sentences and use each accurately.
- Apply sound production and pronunciation patterns that support clear spoken English.
- Edit a short text for accuracy of grammar, vocabulary and sentence structure.

## Prerequisite knowledge

None. This is the first unit in the course and assumes only a working familiarity with everyday
English.

## How to use this unit

The four topics follow the logic of a single piece of writing. Topic 1.1 asks what words to use;
Topic 1.2 asks how to arrange them grammatically; Topic 1.3 asks how to build larger structures;
Topic 1.4 asks how to check and improve what you have written. Each topic is one complete
learning cycle. The question bank at the end of the unit covers all four topics together. Its
answers and mark schemes are in a clearly marked section at the foot of that page; try each item
before you read them.
