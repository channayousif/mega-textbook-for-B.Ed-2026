# Licence track

This track prepares you for **Part II (Pedagogical Content Knowledge)** of the Sindh Teaching
Licence test. Part II carries 75% of the paper, almost all of it constructed response: ten
short constructed-response questions (CRQs) and one case-study extended-response question (ERQ),
each marked against a rubric.

## How it is organised

The licence syllabus lists its Part II objectives under five headings. This track follows that
list exactly, so there are no courses or units here, only the syllabus itself:

| Heading | Objectives |
|---|---|
| [A. Methods of Teaching and Foundations of Education](/licence/pedagogy/a-methods-and-foundations/) | 11 |
| [B. Child Development and Education Psychology](/licence/pedagogy/b-child-dev-and-ed-psych/) | 12 |
| [C. Classroom Management](/licence/pedagogy/c-classroom-management/) | 10 |
| [D. Classroom Assessment and Test Development and Evaluation](/licence/pedagogy/d-assessment-and-test-dev/) | 16 |
| [E. School Community and Teacher](/licence/pedagogy/e-school-community-teacher/) | 8 |

Each objective has one page. It says what the test asks, gives an exam-focused summary, and ends
with one or two practice CRQs and a marking rubric. Each heading also has a practice page with a
full CRQ set and a case-study ERQ.

## How it relates to your degree

Much of Part II is already taught somewhere in the B.Ed. Where it is, the objective's page does
not teach it again: it summarises what the test needs and links to the degree topic, which stays
the authoritative treatment. Degree pages that the licence test draws on show an "On the Sindh
Teaching Licence test" box linking back here. What the degree does not teach, such as most of
Classroom Management, is taught in full on the objective's page.

Nothing in this track counts toward the B.Ed. Your credit hours, semester plan and transcript
come from the [main textbook](/), which follows the approved scheme exactly.

## What is not here

Part I (Content Knowledge, 25% of the paper) is multiple-choice recall from the Class 1-8
school curriculum in Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, English and Urdu or Sindhi. It is not
B.Ed material and is not covered by this track.
