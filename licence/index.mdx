# Licence track

This track holds material assessed by the **Sindh Teaching Licence** test that the
approved 2026 B.Ed (4-Year) scheme does not teach.

It is kept apart from the degree programme deliberately. Filing a licence course inside
a semester folder would put a false claim in the URL and in the sidebar: that the course
is part of the approved scheme. It is not. Everything here is offered because you may be
examined on it, not because your degree covers it.

## What is here

| Course | Why it is in this track |
|---|---|
| [EED-313 Classroom Management](/licence/eed-313/course-overview) | Assessed by the licence test; restructured out of the 2026 scheme, so it has no degree course to live in |
| [GENG-300 Functional English](/licence/geng-300/course-overview) | General Education course in Semester 1 of the B.Ed (4-Year) scheme; assessed by the licence test |

## How this relates to your degree

Nothing in this track counts toward the B.Ed. Your credit hours, your semester plan and
your transcript come from the [main textbook](/), which follows the approved scheme
exactly. Use this track to prepare for the licence test, and the main textbook for
your programme.

Where a topic appears in both, the degree course is the authoritative treatment for
your coursework. This track exists to cover what the degree leaves out, not to restate
what it already teaches.
