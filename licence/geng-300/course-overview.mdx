# Functional English - Course Overview

This course equips student teachers with essential English language skills for effective
communication in diverse real-world scenarios. It focuses on developing proficiency in
English language usage: word choices, grammar and sentence structure, comprehension and
analytical skills, professional writing, public speaking, and intercultural communication.

## Assessment

Assessment follows the GECE default: **60% summative / 40% formative** (Constitution Art. III.7).
