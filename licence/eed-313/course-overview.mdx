# Classroom Management

**Licence track.** This course is not part of the approved 2026 B.Ed scheme. It was `EED-313`, a
Foundation course in the 2025 scheme, which the 2026 revision restructured away with no successor.
The Sindh Teaching Licence (Elementary) test still assesses it, at the weight of two sampled
constructed-response items, which is why it is authored here rather than inside a degree course.

## Units

Four units are specified and not yet authored:

1. Learning theories and classroom management
2. Curriculum and classroom management
3. Routines, schedules, and time management in diverse classrooms
4. Creating shared values and community

The unit specifications are in
[`specs/content/eed-313/content-spec.md`](https://github.com/channayousif/mega-textbook-for-B.Ed-2026/blob/main/specs/content/eed-313/content-spec.md),
and the licence-syllabus mapping is in `specs/content/licence-blueprint.md`.
