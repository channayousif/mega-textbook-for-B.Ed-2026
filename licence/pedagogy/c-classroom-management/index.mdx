# C. Classroom Management

Classroom management is the one Part II heading the 2026 B.Ed scheme has no course for, so most of it is taught in full here. It covers the environment, routines, shared values and strategies that let learning happen.

## The syllabus objectives

Each line below is a Part II objective, word for word from the licence syllabus. Its page tells
you what the test asks, links to the degree topic that already teaches it where one exists, adds
whatever the degree leaves out, and ends with constructed-response practice.
