# A. Methods of Teaching and Foundations of Education

Part II of the licence paper tests how you plan and deliver teaching, and why schooling in Pakistan looks the way it does. The first six objectives come from the HEC Methods of Teaching course; the last five are the five foundations of education.

## The syllabus objectives

Each line below is a Part II objective, word for word from the licence syllabus. Its page tells
you what the test asks, links to the degree topic that already teaches it where one exists, adds
whatever the degree leaves out, and ends with constructed-response practice.

## Practice for the whole heading

Once you have worked through the objectives, the [practice page](/licence/pedagogy/a-methods-and-foundations/practice) gives a full set of
