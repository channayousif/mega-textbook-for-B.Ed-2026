Course Description 
This course will equip prospective teachers with knowledge and skills to teach English in grades I through VIII. They will become familiar with the English curriculum and expected student learning outcomes. Prospective teachers will learn the use of different language skills to enhance variety of instructional methods that promote active learning of English, including making and using teaching and learning materials. They will plan English lessons and activities.
Course Objectives
At the end of the course, the prospective teachers are expected to be:
Familiar with the four language skills - Listening, Speaking reading and writing
Identify and prepare activities for developing four skills
Apply modern methods and approaches in teaching of English
Prepare lesson plans of Prose, Poetry, Composition and Grammar
Effective use of audio visual aids.
Measure and evaluate the students’ progress during teaching of English
As a foreign I second language

Course Description
The course "Understanding of Quranic Arabic" is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the Arabic language as it is used in the Holy Quran. The course aims to equip students with the skills necessary to read, understand, and interpret the Quranictext.The course covers the basics of Quranic Arabic, including the alphabet, vocabulary, and grammar. Students will learn the rules of Arabic grammar, including the different types of sentences, verb conjugation, and noun declension. The course also focuses on the unique features of Quranic Arabic, such as the use of specific vocabulary and grammatical structures that are characteristic of the Quran.In addition to the linguistic aspects, the course also explores the historical and cultural context of the Quran, including the life of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and the early Muslim community. This provides students with a deeper understanding of the Quran's message and its relevance to contemporary issues.Throughout the course, students will engage with the Quranic text through reading, translation, and interpretation exercises. They will also have the opportunity to practice reciting the Quran and to develop their own understanding of its teachings.
Course Outcomes
After finishing this course, student-teachers will be able to:
Understand the basics of Quranic Arabic
Learn Quranic vocabulary and grammar
Develop skills in reading and understanding Quranic text
Interpret Quranic verses and surahs

Title of Course :
Contemporary Literacy
Semester : 3rd

Course Description 
This course is designed to develop an understanding of contemporary perspectives on literacy in the context of rapid social, cultural, and technological change. It emphasizes literacy as a socially situated practice and extends beyond traditional reading and writing to include critical, digital, media, visual, and information literacies. The course prepares prospective teachers to design, implement, and evaluate literacy practices that address diverse learners and meet the demands of 21st-century education in alignment with national and international standards.
Learning Outcomes:
By the end of this course, students will be able to:
1. Define and explain the concept and scope of contemporary literacy.
2. Differentiate between traditional and modern perspectives of literacy.
3. Analyze the role of literacy in social, cultural, and digital contexts.
4. Apply contemporary literacy teaching strategies in classroom settings.
5. Design literacy activities that promote critical thinking and learner engagement.
