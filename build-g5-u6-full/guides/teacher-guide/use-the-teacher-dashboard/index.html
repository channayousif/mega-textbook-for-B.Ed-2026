Use the teacher dashboard

Your teacher dashboard is reachable from the "Teacher Dashboard" link in the navbar once you're signed in as a teacher.

The left-side menu​

Every teacher page has a menu down the left: Overview, Classes, Assignments, Grading, Analytics, Quiz authoring, Teaching log, and Feedback & suggestions. The area you're on is highlighted; on a phone it opens from a button. You never need to type a web address by hand to reach Analytics or a student's record - use the menu, or the links on the Overview.

Overview​

The first screen you see: an ungraded-submission count for each of your classes shown separately (never combined into one total), the 5 soonest-due assignments across all your classes, and a feed of your students' 10 most recent submissions and quiz attempts. Each class row links straight to its Analytics, Roster, and Grading queue. If nothing is pending, it says so plainly instead of showing a blank area.

Class command centre​

Open the command centre for one class to see, in one place, its number of students, the students flagged at risk (with the reason and a link to each one's record), and its next due dates. It links out to the full Analytics, the Grading queue, and the Roster.

Classes and Grading​

These reuse the same Classes area covered in Manage classes and assignments and Grade submissions - the Overview's links take you straight there.

My Teaching Log​

Log a classroom activity in one quick flow - which unit/activity, which class, the date, how long it took, and a short reflection - and see your own log ordered most-recent-first. Logging is independent of assignments; you can log an activity you never assigned formally.

Feedback & Suggestions​

"My Suggestions" lists every improvement suggestion you've filed against the book, with its current status (submitted, under review, accepted, rejected, or published) and any note an admin left. Alongside it, your own Activity Feedback history shows every book activity you've rated.

Analytics​

Open Analytics for one class to see the score distribution per assignment, each student's trend over time, a unit-by-unit class average, and an advisory flag on any student who appears to be struggling - each flag comes with a plain-language reason (either two or more missed deadlines, or a run of three declining scores). This flag is visible only to you; it never appears on a student's own dashboard.

Student drill-down​

From within a class's Analytics, open one student's record to see their submissions, grades, and unit coverage for that class together, with nothing from any other class mixed in.

Quiz authoring​

If your account is a verified teacher, Quiz authoring lets you write the quiz-item bank and the answer keys for a unit. Choose a course and a unit, then add or edit multiple-choice items (a question, its options, and which option is correct) and the formative and summative marking guidance. Students taking a quiz never see the correct option. If your account is not yet verified, the page explains how to get verified; an unverified teacher and a student cannot author or even read this material.
