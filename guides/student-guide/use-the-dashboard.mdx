# Use the dashboard

Your dashboard is reachable from the "Dashboard" link in the navbar once
you're signed in as a student.

## The left-side menu

Every dashboard page has a menu down the left with all your areas: Home,
Progress, Assignments, Grades, Achievements, Notes, My classes, History, and Review content.
The area you're on is highlighted. On a phone the menu is a button that
opens a panel; press Escape or tap outside it to close.

## My classes

Enter a join code from your teacher here and you're added to the class
straight away, without leaving the dashboard. The same page lists the
classes you're currently in. If a code is wrong, already used, or you were
removed from that class, the page tells you which.

## Notes

Your own private notes, visible only to you. Write a general note here, or
add a note about a specific unit or topic from the button at the bottom of
that page in the textbook; page notes appear here tagged to their course so
you can filter by course. You can edit or delete any note.

## Home

The first screen puts your next action first: open the most urgent assignment,
join a class if you have none, or continue reading if you are caught up. The
cards below show your current semester and classes, due items (within 48 hours
first), five recent grades and recent achievements. If loading fails, use
**Try again**.

## Review content

Students can apply for a B.Ed track, a B.Ed course, or the teaching-licence
track review grant from **Review content**. Describe your qualifications and
examples of review work. An admin decides the application. An approved grant
opens the review workbench for that scope; it does not unlock private answer
keys or let you edit the textbook.

## Assignments

Every published assignment and quiz you haven't yet completed, across all
your active classes, soonest-due first. Overdue items are clearly marked,
and each one states whether a late submission is still accepted or the
window has closed - a closed item is never shown as something you can still
act on.

## Grades

Every returned mark, with no class or cohort average shown anywhere. A
corrected grade always shows the corrected value.

## Progress

For each course, how many of its units you've covered - through graded
work, a quiz, or your own self-marking - as a simple fraction (e.g. "4 of 8
units"), plus how many of your courses show any progress at all. Courses
you have marked any unit studied in are listed here even if you are not in
a class for them. Per-topic self-assessment checklists you have completed
also show against their course. You can mark any unit "studied" directly
from this page, or from that unit's own page in the textbook - either way, a
unit only ever counts once toward your coverage, no matter how many times or
through how many different ways you complete it.

## History

Once a class's term ends and it's archived, it moves here - a frozen,
read-only record of that semester's classes, grades, and coverage exactly
as they stood at the time. Nothing here can be edited or resubmitted.

## Achievements

Four milestones are available to every student: your first submission, a
3-day self-study streak, reaching 100% coverage in a course, and completing
every assignment in a class on time. Earned badges are marked as such; for
ones you haven't earned yet, this page tells you how to reach them. A newly
earned badge also appears as a small preview on Home.
