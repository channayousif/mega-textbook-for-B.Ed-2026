# Use the dashboard

Your dashboard is reachable from the "Dashboard" link in the navbar once
you're signed in as a student, and has six areas:

## Home

The first screen you see: your current semester (the highest semester among
your active classes), the classes you're enrolled in, anything due soon
(items due within 48 hours are listed first), and your 5 most recent
grades. If nothing is due, it says so plainly instead of showing a blank
area.

## Assignments

Every published assignment and quiz you haven't yet completed, across all
your active classes, soonest-due first. Overdue items are clearly marked,
and each one states whether a late submission is still accepted or the
window has closed - a closed item is never shown as something you can still
act on.

## Grades

Every returned mark, with no class or cohort average shown anywhere. A
corrected grade always shows the corrected value.

## Progress

For each course, how many of its units you've covered - through graded
work, a quiz, or your own self-marking - as a simple fraction (e.g. "4 of 8
units"), plus how many of your courses show any progress at all. You can
mark any unit "studied" directly from this page, or from that unit's own
page in the textbook - either way, a unit only ever counts once toward your
coverage, no matter how many times or through how many different ways you
complete it.

## History

Once a class's term ends and it's archived, it moves here - a frozen,
read-only record of that semester's classes, grades, and coverage exactly
as they stood at the time. Nothing here can be edited or resubmitted.

## Achievements

Four milestones are available to every student: your first submission, a
3-day self-study streak, reaching 100% coverage in a course, and completing
every assignment in a class on time. Earned badges are marked as such; for
ones you haven't earned yet, this page tells you how to reach them. A newly
earned badge also appears as a small preview on Home.
