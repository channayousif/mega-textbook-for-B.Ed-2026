# Submit work

Once a teacher publishes an assignment, it appears in your class's
assignments list and on your dashboard's Assignments area.

- Open the assignment and enter text and/or attach a file (up to 10 MB, in
  an allowed format).
- You can edit and resubmit your work any time **before the due date** — a
  resubmission replaces your previous answer, it doesn't keep old versions.
- After the due date, your submission locks. Whether a late submission is
  still accepted depends on that specific assignment — the assignment page
  and your dashboard's Assignments area both state this clearly.
- **Practice quizzes** work differently: you can retake them as many times
  as you like before the due date, and your best score is the one that
  counts. Quizzes never accept attempts after their due date.

Once your work is graded, the result appears in your Grades area and on
your dashboard's Home preview — no separate step needed.
