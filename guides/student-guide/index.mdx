# Student Guide

This guide explains how to use the B.Ed Mega Textbook platform as a student —
from finding your way around the site, to joining a class, submitting work,
reading your grades, and using your personal dashboard.

- [Navigate the platform](./navigate-the-platform)
- [Join a class](./join-a-class)
- [Submit work](./submit-work)
- [Read your grades](./read-grades)
- [Use the dashboard](./use-the-dashboard)

If something in this guide doesn't match what you see on screen, the app
itself is always the source of truth — this guide describes the intended
behaviour, not every detail of the interface.
