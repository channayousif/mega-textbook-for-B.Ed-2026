# Navigate the platform

The site has two kinds of pages:

- **The textbook** - organized by semester, then course, then unit. Use the
  sidebar or search to find any unit's content, activities, and assessments.
- **Your personal pages** - reachable from the navbar once you're signed in:
  "Dashboard" (your classes, assignments, grades, progress, history, and
  achievements) and "Guide" (this guide).

Use the locale dropdown in the top-right corner to switch between English
and Urdu at any time - every page, including your dashboard, renders in
either language with correct right-to-left layout in Urdu.

If you're signed in as a teacher or admin and open the student dashboard by
mistake, you'll see a notice pointing you back to your own tools instead of
a broken page.
