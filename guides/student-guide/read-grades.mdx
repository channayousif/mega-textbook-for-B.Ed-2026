# Read your grades

Your **Grades** area (part of the dashboard) lists every assignment and
quiz you've had marked, across every class, with the mark and the maximum
possible for each. Your dashboard Home also previews your 5 most recent
results.

A few things are true everywhere your grades are shown:

- No class or cohort average is ever displayed alongside your mark — only
  your own result.
- If a teacher corrects a grade after returning it, you always see the
  corrected value, never the original.
- Quiz scores are computed automatically the moment you submit an attempt;
  assignment marks appear once your teacher grades your submission.
