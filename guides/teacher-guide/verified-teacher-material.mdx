# Verified-teacher material

Every teacher can create classes, assign work, and grade — that's the
`teacher` role itself, and it needs no special approval. A separate
capability, **verified teacher**, unlocks access to restricted material:

- The official correct-answer reference or marking rubric for a formative
  or summative unit item, shown directly in the grading queue.
- The correct-answer data behind practice quizzes (the quiz questions
  themselves are visible to everyone; only the correct-answer reference is
  restricted).

Verified-teacher status is granted only by an admin — it isn't something you
can switch on for yourself, and it doesn't change anything else about your
account or your classes. If you believe you should have this capability and
don't, contact your platform administrator.
