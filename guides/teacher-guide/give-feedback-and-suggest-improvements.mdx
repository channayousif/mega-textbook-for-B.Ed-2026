# Give feedback and suggest improvements

Three controls can appear on book content pages while you're signed in as a
teacher - two are teacher-only, and one you share with students, since
teachers are readers too.

## Suggest improvement

Available on every book page, including course-overview pages - click it,
pick a category (typo, clarity, factual, pedagogy, translation, or other),
and write a short note. The page's slug, the nearest section heading, and
the page's current language are all captured automatically; you never type
these in yourself. Find every suggestion you've filed, and its current
status, in "My Suggestions" on your teacher dashboard.

An admin moderates every suggestion through submitted → under review →
accepted or rejected, and - once any resulting content change ships -
published. You'll see the new status and any note the admin left the next
time you check "My Suggestions."

## Give feedback on this activity

Available on a unit's activity, formative, or summative page (not on a
course-overview page, since feedback is about one specific activity). Rate
it 1–5, note what worked and what didn't, and record how long it actually
took. You can reach this same control from a teaching-log entry for that
activity too. Re-rating an activity you've already rated updates your one
record - it doesn't create a second one. An admin can see the average
rating and every recorded issue across all teachers who've rated an
activity, to help prioritise which content to revise.

## Give feedback (as a reader)

Available on a topic page, a per-topic unit's opening page, its assessment
page, its teacher-notes page, or a course-level review page - every reader
sees this: students, teachers, and a signed-out visitor alike, not only
teachers. Use it for a comment tied to something specific on the page you're
reading: leave it on the page as a whole, or **select a sentence first**
(a tip above the button says the same thing) and then click the button to
attach your comment to exactly that quoted passage. After submitting, "Give
more feedback" lets you leave another, separate comment on the same page
without reloading.

This is a different control from the two above, and they don't overlap:
"Suggest improvement" is a general note about the book's quality on any
page (a typo, a factual issue, a pedagogy concern); "Give feedback on this
activity" is a teacher-only rating of one specific activity/formative/
summative page. "Give feedback" here is neither - it's a reader's comment on
a specific piece of page content, and the curriculum owner triages it
separately from both of the others. You can see your own submitted items and
their status by returning to the same account, but a comment can't be edited
once it's sent.

**Signed out?** The same button still works, with an email field in place
of an account - fill it in, submit, then click the confirmation link that
address is sent before your comment reaches the curriculum owner's queue.
That address is used only for that confirmation and any follow-up the owner
chooses to make; it's never shown to any other reader.
