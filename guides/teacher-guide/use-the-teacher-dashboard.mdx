# Use the teacher dashboard

Your teacher dashboard is reachable from the "Teacher Dashboard" link in the
navbar once you're signed in as a teacher, and has several areas:

## Overview

The first screen you see: an ungraded-submission count for each of your
classes shown separately (never combined into one total), the 5 soonest-due
assignments across all your classes, and a feed of your students' 10 most
recent submissions and quiz attempts. If nothing is pending, it says so
plainly instead of showing a blank area.

## Classes and Grading

These reuse the same Classes area covered in
[Manage classes and assignments](../manage-classes-and-assignments) and
[Grade submissions](../grade-submissions) — the Overview's links take you
straight there.

## My Teaching Log

Log a classroom activity in one quick flow — which unit/activity, which
class, the date, how long it took, and a short reflection — and see your own
log ordered most-recent-first. Logging is independent of assignments; you
can log an activity you never assigned formally.

## Feedback & Suggestions

"My Suggestions" lists every improvement suggestion you've filed against the
book, with its current status (submitted, under review, accepted, rejected,
or published) and any note an admin left. Alongside it, your own Activity
Feedback history shows every book activity you've rated.

## Analytics

Open Analytics for one class to see the score distribution per assignment,
each student's trend over time, a unit-by-unit class average, and an
advisory flag on any student who appears to be struggling — each flag comes
with a plain-language reason (either two or more missed deadlines, or a
run of three declining scores). This flag is visible only to you; it never
appears on a student's own dashboard.

## Student drill-down

From within a class's Analytics, open one student's record to see their
submissions, grades, and unit coverage for that class together, with nothing
from any other class mixed in.
