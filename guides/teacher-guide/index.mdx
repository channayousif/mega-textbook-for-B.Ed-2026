# Teacher Guide

This guide explains how to use the B.Ed Mega Textbook platform as a teacher -
from managing classes and assignments, to grading, using your teacher
dashboard, giving feedback and suggesting improvements to the book, and what
becoming a verified teacher unlocks.

- [Manage classes and assignments](./manage-classes-and-assignments)
- [Grade submissions](./grade-submissions)
- [Use the teacher dashboard](./use-the-teacher-dashboard)
- [Give feedback and suggest improvements](./give-feedback-and-suggest-improvements)
- [Verified-teacher material](./verified-teacher-material)

If something in this guide doesn't match what you see on screen, the app
itself is always the source of truth - this guide describes the intended
behaviour, not every detail of the interface.
